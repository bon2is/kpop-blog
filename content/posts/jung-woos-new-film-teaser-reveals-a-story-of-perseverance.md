With its inspiring storyline, 'Jjanggu' is set to captivate audiences with its heartfelt portrayal of determination and courage.

The release of 'Jjanggu' teaser is significant as it highlights the often-overlooked struggles of aspiring actors in the K-Pop and Korean entertainment industry.
