## Our Take

The allure of '97-liners in K-Pop not only revolves around their looks but also encapsulates the rising star power of that generation. Artists like BTS's Jungkook, ASTRO's Cha Eun-woo, and NCT's Jaehyun have carved their identities, blending remarkable talent with striking visuals. This poll isn’t just about who is deemed the most handsome; it reflects the ongoing evolution of idol culture, where aesthetics and talent intertwine to create multi-faceted stars. Fans’ loyalty and emotional investment in these idols underscore the importance of interpersonal connections in K-Pop, where online polls serve as platforms for community engagement. As we observe this trend, it raises questions about how these idols will navigate their careers in a competitive landscape where looks often overshadow artistry. Furthermore, the '97-liners are stepping into pivotal moments in their careers, hinting at possible solo projects or group dynamics shifts. Their collective impact on the industry is significant, marking a generational shift in both fan expectations and the K-Pop narrative overall.
