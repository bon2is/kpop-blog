Shin Hae Sun, the talented South Korean actress known for her versatile performances in dramas such as 'Still 17' and 'More Than Friends', is set to make a return appearance on the popular tvN variety program 'You Quiz On The Block'. The news, which was first reported by News1 on April 27, has generated significant excitement among her fans, who are eager to see her participate in the show's unique and entertaining format. With her episode scheduled to air on May 6, anticipation is building for what promises to be a fun and engaging appearance.

## Background

Shin Hae Sun has established herself as one of the most promising young actresses in the Korean entertainment industry, with a career spanning several years and a wide range of notable roles. Born on August 10, 1989, she began her acting career in the early 2010s, initially appearing in minor roles in films and television dramas. However, it was not long before she started to gain recognition for her talent, landing more substantial parts in dramas such as 'School 2013' and 'Angel Eyes'. Her breakthrough role came in 2018 with the drama 'Still 17', where she played the lead character of Woo Seo-ri, a young woman who wakes up from a coma after 13 years. This performance earned her critical acclaim and numerous award nominations, solidifying her position as a rising star in the industry.

Shin Hae Sun's recent releases include the dramas 'More Than Friends' and 'Hometown Cha-Cha-Cha', both of which showcased her ability to portray complex, multifaceted characters. In 'More Than Friends', she played the role of Kyung Woo-yeon, a woman who falls in love with her best friend, while in 'Hometown Cha-Cha-Cha', she starred alongside Kim Seon-ho as Yoon Hye-jin, a dentist who returns to her hometown and becomes involved in a romantic relationship with a handsome and kind-hearted man. These performances have further cemented her reputation as a talented and versatile actress, capable of taking on a wide range of roles.
