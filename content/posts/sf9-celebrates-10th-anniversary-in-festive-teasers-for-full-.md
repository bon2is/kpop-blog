## Background

SF9, short for Sensational Feeling 9, is a South Korean boy group formed by FNC Entertainment in 2016. The group consists of nine members: Youngbin, Inseong, Jaeyoon, Dawon, Zuho, Rowoon, Taeyang, Hwiyoung, and Chani. SF9 made their debut on October 5, 2016, with the single 'Fanfare', and have since released several successful albums and singles, including 'Easy Love', 'Roar', and 'Good Guy'. The group is known for their high-energy performances, catchy songs, and impressive choreography. Over the years, SF9 has built a loyal fan base, both in Korea and internationally, and has won several awards for their music.
