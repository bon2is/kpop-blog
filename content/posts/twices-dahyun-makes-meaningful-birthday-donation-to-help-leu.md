TWICE's Dahyun is known for her bright personality and energetic performances, but on her recent birthday, she showed a more compassionate side by making a significant donation to help those in need. In a heartwarming gesture, Dahyun donated 30 million won (approximately $20,000) to the Ulsan Community Chest of Korea, an organization that provides support to leukemia patients in the Ulsan area. This selfless act not only celebrates her birthday but also highlights her commitment to giving back to the community.

## Background

TWICE is one of the most popular and successful K-Pop groups in recent years, known for their catchy songs, intricate choreography, and charming personalities. The group, formed by JYP Entertainment in 2015, consists of nine members: Nayeon, Jeongyeon, Momo, Sana, Jihyo, Mina, Dahyun, Chaeyoung, and Tzuyu. With numerous hit songs like 'Cheer Up', 'TT', and 'Fancy', TWICE has established themselves as a household name in the K-Pop industry. Dahyun, in particular, has gained a lot of attention for her powerful dancing skills, impressive rap verses, and bubbly personality.

Dahyun has been an integral part of TWICE's success, and her contributions to the group's music and performances have been invaluable. Born on May 28, 1998, Dahyun has been training since a young age and was eventually selected to join JYP Entertainment as a trainee. She officially debuted as a member of TWICE in 2015, and since then, the group has released numerous successful albums and singles, including 'The Story Begins', 'Page Two', and 'Yes or Yes'. With their unique blend of genres, ranging from pop and rock to hip-hop and R&B, TWICE has managed to captivate audiences worldwide and build a massive fan base.

## What Happened

On June 1, JYP Entertainment announced that Dahyun had made a generous donation of 30 million won to the Ulsan Community Chest of Korea in honor of her birthday on May 28. The donation will be used to support medical expenses and treatment for leukemia patients in the Ulsan area, providing them with the necessary resources to receive proper care and treatment. This meaningful gesture showcases Dahyun's kindness and compassion towards those in need, and her commitment to using her platform to make a positive impact. By donating to such a worthy cause, Dahyun is not only celebrating her birthday but also inspiring her fans to follow in her footsteps and give back to the community.

The Ulsan Community Chest of Korea is a non-profit organization that provides support to underprivileged individuals and families in the Ulsan area. The organization focuses on various initiatives, including poverty alleviation, education, and healthcare, and relies on donations and volunteer work to carry out its mission. Dahyun's donation will undoubtedly make a significant difference in the lives of leukemia patients and their families, providing them with the necessary support and resources to overcome their challenges. This act of kindness also highlights the importance of social responsibility and the role that celebrities can play in making a positive impact on their communities.
