TXT has made history by achieving a music show grand slam with their fifth win for 'Stick With You' on SBS's 'Inkigayo'. This impressive feat marks a perfect week of music show wins for the group, solidifying their position as one of the most popular and talented K-Pop groups today. With this achievement, TXT has proven their growing influence and dedication to their craft.

## Background

TXT, also known as Tomorrow X Together, is a South Korean boy group formed by Big Hit Music in 2019. The group consists of five members: Soobin, Yeonjun, Beomgyu, Taehyun, and Hueningkai. Since their debut, TXT has been making waves in the K-Pop industry with their unique sound, energetic performances, and thought-provoking lyrics. Their debut single 'Crown' was a massive hit, topping various music charts and earning them several awards, including the Best New Male Artist award at the Melon Music Awards and the Mnet Asian Music Awards.

The group's career has been marked by a series of successful releases, including their debut album 'The Dream Chapter: Star', which debuted at number one on the US Billboard 200 chart, making them the first K-Pop group to do so. Their subsequent releases, such as 'The Dream Chapter: Magic' and 'Minisode1: Blue Hour', have also been well-received by fans and critics alike. TXT's music often explores themes of youth, identity, and self-discovery, resonating with fans of all ages.

One of the key factors that sets TXT apart from other K-Pop groups is their unique sound, which blends elements of pop, rock, and hip-hop to create a distinctive and captivating style. Their music videos are also highly stylized and visually striking, often featuring intricate choreography and stunning visuals. With their impressive discography and captivating performances, TXT has built a loyal fan base and established themselves as one of the most exciting and innovative K-Pop groups today.

## What Happened

On the April 26 episode of SBS's 'Inkigayo', TXT was announced as the winner of the music show, marking their fifth win for 'Stick With You'. The candidates for first place were AKMU's 'Joy, Sorrow, A Beautiful Heart', AKMU's 'Paradise of Rumors', and TXT's 'Stick With You'. TXT ultimately took the win with a total of 8,070 points, beating out the other competitors. This achievement marks a perfect week of music show wins for the group, with previous wins on 'Music Bank', 'Show! Music Core', and 'M Countdown'.

The episode also featured performances by other popular K-Pop groups, including LE SSERAFIM, who performed their latest single 'No Celestial'. The group's energetic and captivating performance showcased their impressive vocals and choreography, highlighting their growing talent and popularity. Other performers on the episode included NewJeans, THE BOYZ, and DreamCatcher, among others.

TXT's win on 'Inkigayo' was met with excitement and congratulations from fans and fellow K-Pop groups alike. The group's members took to social media to express their gratitude and appreciation for their fans' support, with Soobin stating, 'We're so grateful for this amazing achievement! We couldn't have done it without our fans, who have been supporting us every step of the way.' This heartfelt message highlights the group's dedication to their fans and their passion for their craft.
