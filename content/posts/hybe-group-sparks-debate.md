---

## Our Take

The criticism of HYBE Group's visuals is not an isolated incident, as K-Pop idols are constantly under the microscope for their appearance. 

 This level of scrutiny can have a significant impact on an idol's mental health and self-esteem, emphasizing the need for a more supportive and positive environment. 

 The fact that the post has gained significant traction, with over 98,000 views, suggests that there is a segment of the population that resonates with the criticism, sparking a larger conversation about beauty standards in the K-Pop industry. 

 As HYBE Group is home to popular acts like BTS and New Jeans, this controversy may have broader implications for the company's reputation and its artists' careers.
