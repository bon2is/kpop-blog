Park Ji Hoon, Lee Sang Yi, and more star in the upcoming military fantasy-comedy drama “The Legend of Kitchen Soldier”. The drama is based on a popular web novel and webtoon and tells the story of army cook Kang Sung Jae. A new behind-the-scenes video has been shared, showing the cast's playful and creative side during the poster shoot.

## Our Take

The release of “The Legend of Kitchen Soldier” is highly anticipated, especially among fans of Park Ji Hoon, who have been waiting for his next project. 

 The drama's unique blend of military and fantasy elements, combined with its comedy genre, is expected to attract a wide range of viewers. The cast's chemistry and playfulness, as seen in the behind-the-scenes video, will likely be a major draw for fans. 

 Park Ji Hoon's career has been on the rise, with notable roles in various dramas and projects. His involvement in “The Legend of Kitchen Soldier” is likely to further boost his popularity and solidify his position as a leading actor in the K-drama industry. 

 The drama's success will also depend on its ability to balance its various genres and appeal to a broad audience, but with its talented cast and interesting premise, it has the potential to make a significant impact on the K-drama scene.
