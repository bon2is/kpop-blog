With numerous hit songs like "Fantastic Baby," "Bang Bang Bang," and "Losers," BIGBANG has consistently pushed the boundaries of K-Pop, exploring new sounds and styles that have inspired a generation of artists. Over the years, the group has undergone several lineup changes due to military service and personal reasons, with Seungri leaving the group in 2019. Despite these challenges, the remaining members have continued to work on solo projects, with G-Dragon and Taeyang releasing solo albums and collaborating with other artists.




The group's history is marked by incredible achievements, including winning numerous awards at the Mnet Asian Music Awards, Melon Music Awards, and Seoul Music Awards.
