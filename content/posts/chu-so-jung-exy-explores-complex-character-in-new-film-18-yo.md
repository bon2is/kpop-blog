The upcoming film '18 Youth' has revealed new stills of WJSN's Chu So Jung (Exy) in character, showcasing her portrayal of a high school student with a perfect image that hides her true struggles. Based on a novel, the film tells the story of an unconventional teacher and her encounter with students, including Exy's character. This role marks a significant departure from Exy's typical persona as a K-Pop idol.
---

## Our Take

The news of Exy's involvement in '18 Youth' is significant because it highlights the versatility of K-Pop idols as actors, exploring complex characters and storylines. 

 Exy's participation in this film also underscores the growing trend of K-Pop idols venturing into acting, leveraging their massive fan base to promote Korean cinema globally. 

 This film can be a pivotal moment in Exy's career, demonstrating her range as a performer and potentially opening up more opportunities in the acting world. 

 For fans of WJSN and Exy, '18 Youth' offers a unique chance to see their favorite idol in a new light, tackling a character that resonates with the struggles of adolescence and the pressures of societal expectations.
