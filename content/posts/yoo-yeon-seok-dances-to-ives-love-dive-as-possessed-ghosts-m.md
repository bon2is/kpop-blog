Yoo Yeon Seok will showcase his dance skills to IVE's “LOVE DIVE” in the legal drama “Phantom Lawyer”. The drama follows the story of a lawyer who can see ghosts and an elite attorney as they resolve lingering grievances. Yoo Yeon Seok's character, Shin I Rang, will be possessed by a ghost's memories, leading to the entertaining dance scene.
---

## Our Take

This news matters because it highlights the growing trend of K-Pop collaborations in Korean dramas. 

 The K-Pop industry has seen a significant increase in drama cameos and soundtracks, making it a great way for artists to reach a broader audience. 

 IVE's “LOVE DIVE” is a popular song, and its feature in “Phantom Lawyer” will likely introduce the group to new fans. 

 For Yoo Yeon Seok, this dance scene could be a career-defining moment, showcasing his versatility as an actor and potentially leading to more opportunities in the entertainment industry.
