PENTAGON is marking a significant milestone in their career - their 10th anniversary. To celebrate this occasion, the group has announced a special tour titled '101010', which promises to be an unforgettable experience for their fans. The tour will take PENTAGON to several cities in Japan, Korea, and Hong Kong, giving fans in these regions a chance to see the group perform live.

## Background

PENTAGON is a South Korean boy group formed by Cube Entertainment in 2016. The group consists of nine members: Jinho, Hui, Hongseok, Shinwon, Yeo One, Yan An, Yuto, Kino, and Wooseok. Over the years, PENTAGON has released numerous successful albums and singles, showcasing their versatility and talent in various genres of music. Their discography includes popular songs like 'Gorilla', 'Can You Feel It', and 'Shine', which have gained them a significant following worldwide. PENTAGON has also been part of several music shows and concerts, collaborating with other artists and performing at notable events.

PENTAGON's career has been marked by their energetic performances, catchy songs, and dedication to their craft. ## What Happened

On August 10, PENTAGON officially announced their 10th anniversary tour '101010', revealing the dates and cities they will visit. The tour is scheduled to start on October 1 in Osaka, Japan, followed by a stop in Tokyo on October 3. PENTAGON will then perform in Seoul, Korea on October 9 and 10, before concluding the tour in Hong Kong on November 1. This announcement has generated excitement among fans, who are eager to see the group perform live and celebrate their 10th anniversary together. The '101010' tour promises to be a memorable experience, featuring PENTAGON's hit songs, impressive choreography, and engaging stage presence.
