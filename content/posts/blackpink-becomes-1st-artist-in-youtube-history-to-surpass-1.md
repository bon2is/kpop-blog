On February 20, BLACKPINK officially became the first artist in YouTube history to surpass 100 million subscribers on their official artist channel. YouTube celebrated this monumental milestone by presenting the group with a custom Red Diamond Creator Award, marking a significant achievement in their career.
---

## Our Take

This achievement matters immensely, not only for BLACKPINK but for the entire music industry. Being the first artist to reach 100 million subscribers showcases their profound impact on global pop culture and highlights the changing dynamics of music consumption in the digital age.

In the broader context of the K-Pop industry, BLACKPINK’s milestone underscores the genre's massive global appeal. It reflects how K-Pop has transcended cultural boundaries, attracting a dedicated international fanbase that actively engages with artists through platforms like YouTube. This is a testament to the hard work and innovative marketing strategies employed by both the group and their management.

For fans, this success is a source of immense pride and a validation of their support. They have played a crucial role in making BLACKPINK a household name, and this milestone further strengthens their community. As for the artists, this achievement could lead to more lucrative opportunities, including partnerships and endorsements, solidifying BLACKPINK's position as trailblazers in the music industry.
