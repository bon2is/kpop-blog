In a recent exclusive interview, ZELO, the talented rapper and former member of the K-Pop group B.A.P, sat down to talk about his journey, his music, and his passion for reconnecting with fans. With a career spanning over a decade, ZELO has undergone significant transformations, from being the youngest member of B.A.P to establishing himself as a successful solo artist. His story is one of perseverance, creativity, and a relentless pursuit of artistic expression.


## Background


ZELO, whose real name is Choi Jun-hong, debuted in 2012 as a member of B.A.P, a group known for their high-energy performances, powerful lyrics, and unique blend of genres. As the youngest member, ZELO quickly gained attention for his sharp rap delivery, impressive stage presence, and undeniable charisma. During his time with B.A.P, the group released several successful albums and singles, including 'Warrior', 'Power', and 'One Shot'. Despite their popularity, the group faced numerous challenges, including a highly publicized lawsuit against their management agency, TS Entertainment, which ultimately led to their disbandment in 2019.


Following the disbandment of B.A.P, ZELO embarked on a solo career, determined to carve out his own lane in the K-Pop industry. With a renewed sense of purpose and creativity, he began to explore different sounds, styles, and themes in his music. His solo releases, such as 'Questions' and 'Lost', showcased his growth as an artist, featuring more mature and introspective lyrics, as well as a greater emphasis on melody and rhythm. Through his music, ZELO has demonstrated his ability to adapt, evolve, and push boundaries, solidifying his position as a talented and innovative solo artist.


ZELO's journey has not been without its challenges, however. The K-Pop industry is highly competitive, and the pressure to constantly produce new content, maintain a strong public image, and connect with fans can be overwhelming. Nevertheless, ZELO has persevered, using his experiences, both positive and negative, as inspiration for his music and his personal growth. His dedication to his craft and his passion for reconnecting with fans have earned him a loyal following, both domestically and internationally.
