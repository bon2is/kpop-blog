BTS's Jungkook has reached a new milestone with his solo debut track 'Seven' featuring Latto, surpassing 600 million views on YouTube. This achievement marks Jungkook's first solo music video to hit the 600 million view mark. The music video achieved this feat on April 10 at approximately 4:45 p.m. KST.
