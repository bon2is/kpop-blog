In a significant development for K-Pop fans, especially those who have been following the careers of Wanna One members after the group's disbandment, Lee Dae Hwi of AB6IX has made a notable move by signing with Off The Record Entertainment. This decision not only marks a new chapter in Lee Dae Hwi's career but also reunites him with his former Wanna One bandmate, Kim Jae Hwan, who is already under the same agency.


## Background


Lee Dae Hwi first gained widespread recognition as a member of the project boy group Wanna One, formed through the 2017 survival show 'Produce 101 Season 2'. After Wanna One's disbandment in 2019, Lee Dae Hwi went on to debut as a member of AB6IX, a group known for their energetic performances and catchy songs. Throughout his career, Lee Dae Hwi has showcased his versatility as a singer, songwriter, and performer, contributing to his group's success and building a loyal fan base.


The agency, Off The Record Entertainment, has been making waves in the K-Pop scene with its strategic management and support for its artists. By signing Lee Dae Hwi, the agency further solidifies its position as a hub for talented artists looking to expand their creative horizons. Kim Jae Hwan, who has been with the agency, has seen significant success, releasing solo music that has been well-received by fans and critics alike. The dynamic between Kim Jae Hwan and Lee Dae Hwi, both as former bandmates and now agency mates, is anticipated to foster a collaborative and supportive environment that could lead to exciting new projects.


## What Happened


On August 7, Off The Record Entertainment officially announced the signing of Lee Dae Hwi, expressing their enthusiasm for the addition of such a talented artist to their roster. The agency highlighted Lee Dae Hwi's 'outstanding musical talent and a diverse array of charms', indicating their commitment to supporting his growth and exploring the various facets of his artistry. This announcement was met with excitement from fans, who are eager to see how this new partnership will influence Lee Dae Hwi's future endeavors.


The details of the contract, including its duration and specific terms, have not been publicly disclosed. However, the move is seen as a strategic one for both Lee Dae Hwi and Off The Record Entertainment. For Lee Dae Hwi, it offers the potential for more solo activities and collaborations, possibly even with his labelmate Kim Jae Hwan. For the agency, signing Lee Dae Hwi strengthens its lineup of talented artists and positions it for further growth in the competitive K-Pop industry.
