Ha Ji Won and Nana have reunited on the film set of “Climax”, sparking an intense showdown. The series follows the story of prosecutor Bang Tae Seop as he navigates a power-driven cartel. The reunion of Ha Ji Won and Nana adds a new layer of complexity to the story.

## Our Take

The reunion of Ha Ji Won and Nana on the set of “Climax” is a significant moment in the series, as it brings to the forefront their troubled past. 

 This development matters because it adds a personal dimension to the story, making the characters' motivations more relatable and nuanced. 

 In the context of the K-Pop and K-Drama industry, this reunion is notable as it highlights the versatility of these actresses in taking on complex roles. 

 For fans of Ha Ji Won and Nana, this reunion is a treat, as it allows them to see their favorite actresses in a new and exciting storyline, and it may also attract new fans to the series.
