Kim Ji Woong, the charismatic vocalist of rookie boy group ZEROBASEONE, has just been announced as the lead of JTBC’s upcoming police investigation drama “Siren”. The news, confirmed by his agency Nest Management and reported by JTBC Entertainment News on September 4, has set off a wave of anticipation among both K‑Pop and K‑Drama fans eager to see the idol transition to a major acting role.

## Background

ZEROBASEONE debuted in August 2023 under the joint venture of RBW and Fave Entertainment, quickly gaining a dedicated fanbase with their polished choreography and catchy tracks like “Ready to Love” and “Back To The Future”. The nine‑member group has already charted on the Circle Album Chart, with their first EP peaking inside the top 10 and accumulating over 100 million streams on Spotify and Apple Music combined. While the group’s music continues to climb the charts, individual members have been exploring acting opportunities to broaden their careers.

Kim Ji Woong, born in 2001, first caught public attention as a trainee on the reality program “Boys Planet”. Though he didn’t make the final lineup, his strong stage presence and expressive vocals earned him a spot in ZEROBASEONE. Since debut, Ji Woong has contributed to songwriting and has been praised for his emotive delivery, earning him a reputation as the group’s “visual and vocal powerhouse”. Outside music, he made a brief cameo in the 2024 web drama “Love in the Time of Wi‑Fi”, but “Siren” marks his first leading role in a prime‑time television series.

The drama “Siren” is being produced by JTBC, a network known for hit series such as “Sky Castle”, “Itaewon Class”, and “The World of the Married”. The working title suggests a focus on a high‑stakes investigation involving a mysterious signal that leads detectives down a dangerous path. While plot specifics remain under wraps, the series is slated to blend thriller elements with social commentary, a formula that has resonated with global audiences on platforms like Netflix and Viki for previous JTBC dramas.

## What Happened

On September 4, JTBC Entertainment News released a casting announcement stating that Kim Ji Woong would headline “Siren”. The report highlighted that filming is set to commence in early October, with a tentative broadcast window in the first half of 2025. Nest Management quickly issued an official confirmation, stating, “We are thrilled to announce that Ji Woong has been cast as the lead in JTBC’s upcoming drama ‘Siren’. He is eager to take on this challenging role and showcase his acting abilities.”

The drama’s production team, led by veteran director Lee Hyun‑woo—known for his work on “Voice” and “Stranger”—has not yet disclosed the full cast, but rumors suggest that seasoned actors such as Kim Hye‑jae and Park Hoon‑joo may join the project. The series is expected to consist of 12 episodes, each running approximately 60 minutes. While a streaming partner has not been officially announced, JTBC’s recent collaborations with Netflix and Disney+ for international distribution hint that “Siren” could become available on those platforms shortly after its Korean broadcast.

## Why This Matters

Kim Ji Woong’s casting signals a strategic move for both the idol and his agency. Historically, successful transitions from idol to actor have propelled careers forward—think of members like Suzy (formerly of Miss A) and IU, who now dominate both music and drama charts. For a rookie group like ZEROBASEONE, having a member headline a prime‑time drama elevates the group’s visibility beyond the music sphere, potentially attracting new fans who discover the group through the drama.

From an industry perspective, JTBC continues its trend of integrating fresh faces from the K‑Pop world into its dramas, a tactic that boosts cross‑media synergy and drives streaming numbers. Recent data shows that dramas featuring idols experience a 15‑20% increase in overseas viewership on platforms like Viki and Kocowa, translating to higher ad revenue and global brand partnerships. Moreover, the drama’s thriller genre aligns with the growing appetite for high‑concept Korean series, which have consistently topped the Netflix Global Top 10 list.

The announcement also underscores the expanding role of agencies like Nest Management in managing multi‑talent careers. By securing a lead role for Ji Woong, the agency demonstrates its capability to negotiate high‑profile projects, positioning its artists for longevity in the highly competitive entertainment market.

## What Fans Can Expect

Fans of ZEROBASEONE can look forward to seeing Ji Woong tackle a complex character—likely a determined detective or a morally ambiguous figure entangled in the “Siren” mystery. Early teasers suggest a gritty aesthetic, with dark cityscapes and intense investigative sequences, promising Ji Woong an opportunity to display a range of emotions beyond his stage persona.

The drama is expected to air on JTBC’s primetime slot, with potential simul‑casts on international streaming services. Keep an eye on official JTBC and Nest Management channels for trailer drops, behind‑the‑scenes content, and possible pre‑release screenings. For those eager to stream, the series will likely become available on Netflix or Disney+ within weeks of the Korean broadcast, making it accessible to fans across the US, UK, Canada, and Australia.
