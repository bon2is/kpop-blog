TV5 and Viu are launching a new culinary series, Kumusta, which will premiere on May 24, 2026, and feature South Korean actor Ji Chang Wook. The series aims to bring together the rich flavors of the Philippines and South Korea, offering a fresh take on culinary storytelling. By exploring the intersection of Korean and Filipino cuisine, Kumusta promises to be an exciting cross-cultural experience.

This news matters because it highlights the growing interest in cross-cultural collaborations between South Korea and the Philippines, particularly in the food and entertainment industries.
