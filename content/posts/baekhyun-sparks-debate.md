Galaxy Corporation has denied rumors of recruiting singer Baekhyun, stating that there are no discussions of signing an exclusive contract. The news comes after a media outlet reported that Baekhyun was in talks to join the agency, which currently represents G-Dragon. This denial has left fans wondering about Baekhyun's future plans.
