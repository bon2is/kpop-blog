BTS's Jin was unexpectedly left out of the credits for 'Arirang', sparking widespread outrage among fans and K-netizens. This incident has raised questions about the fairness and transparency of credit attribution in K-Pop. The omission has significant implications for Jin's career and the broader K-Pop industry.
---

## Our Take

The exclusion of Jin from the 'Arirang' credits matters because it reflects a deeper issue of recognition and fairness in the K-Pop industry. This incident highlights the need for greater transparency in credit attribution, ensuring that all contributors receive the recognition they deserve. 

 The K-Pop industry has long been criticized for its strict hierarchical structures and often opaque credit attribution processes. The omission of Jin's credits for 'Arirang' serves as a stark reminder of these systemic issues, underscoring the need for reform and greater accountability. 

 For fans of BTS and Jin, this incident is not only a matter of principle but also a concern for the artist's career. The lack of recognition for Jin's contributions to 'Arirang' could potentially impact his future collaborations and projects, making it essential for the industry to address these issues promptly. 

 Ultimately, this controversy surrounding Jin's credits for 'Arirang' presents an opportunity for the K-Pop industry to reflect on its practices and strive for greater fairness and transparency. By doing so, the industry can work towards creating a more equitable environment for all artists and contributors.
