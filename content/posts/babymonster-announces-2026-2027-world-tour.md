BABYMONSTER has officially announced their 2026-2027 world tour, which will start in Seoul and then visit six cities in Japan, including Kobe, Fukuoka, and Osaka. The tour is a significant milestone for the group, marking their first major international tour. With this announcement, BABYMONSTER is poised to take their music to a global audience, further solidifying their position in the K-Pop scene.
