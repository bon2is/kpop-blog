## Background

aespa is a South Korean girl group formed by SM Entertainment, consisting of four members: Karina, Giselle, Winter, and NingNing. The group debuted in November 2020 with the single "Black Mamba" and has since released several successful singles and EPs, including "Forever" and "Savage". Known for their unique blend of genres and captivating music videos, aespa has quickly gained a significant following worldwide. NingNing, in particular, has been praised for her impressive vocal range and emotional delivery, making her a fan favorite within the group.

aespa's career has been marked by a series of achievements, from topping various music charts to collaborating with international artists. Their music often incorporates elements of EDM, hip-hop, and pop, showcasing the group's versatility and experimentation. With their thought-provoking lyrics and stunning visuals, aespa has established itself as a force to be reckoned with in the K-Pop scene. Despite their relatively short career, the group has already made a lasting impact on fans and the industry, with many looking forward to their future releases and activities.

## What Happened

In a recent interview, NingNing spoke candidly about the pressures and challenges of being an idol, revealing the emotional toll it can take on her mental health.
