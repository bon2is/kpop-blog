NCT member Doyoung has spoken out about the rumors of discord within the group following Mark's departure. Doyoung shared his thoughts on social media, addressing the situation directly. This move comes as Doyoung is currently serving in the military, showing his commitment to transparency with fans.
---

## Our Take

Doyoung's decision to address the rumors directly is significant, as it shows his dedication to maintaining a positive and honest relationship with fans. 

 The K-Pop industry often sees groups dealing with internal conflicts or member departures, and how these situations are handled can greatly impact the group's reputation and fan loyalty. 

 By speaking out, Doyoung aims to reassure fans that despite changes, the bond between members remains strong, which is crucial for NCT's future projects and comeback plans. 

 This move also highlights Doyoung's maturity and leadership within the group, setting a positive example for other idols in similar situations.
