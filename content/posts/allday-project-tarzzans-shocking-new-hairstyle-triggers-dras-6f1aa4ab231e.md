Tarzzan's new hairstyle is more than just a personal aesthetic choice; it's a reflection of the evolving standards of self-expression within the K-Pop industry. Ultimately, Tarzzan's hairstyle could pave the way for other artists to take similar risks, fostering a more diverse range of styles in the K-Pop landscape.
