BLACKPINK's Jennie has just achieved a remarkable feat in her music career, becoming the 2nd female K-Pop artist to break into the top 5 of Billboard's prestigious Hot 100 chart. This accomplishment is a testament to her talent, hard work, and the ever-growing popularity of K-Pop worldwide. With her remix of 'Dracula' alongside Tame Impala, Jennie has solidified her position as a leading figure in the K-Pop scene.

## Background

Jennie, whose full name is Kim Jennie, is a South Korean singer, rapper, and model. She debuted as a member of the highly successful girl group BLACKPINK in 2016 under YG Entertainment. Since then, she has been an integral part of the group's success, known for her powerful vocals, impressive rap skills, and captivating stage presence. In addition to her work with BLACKPINK, Jennie has also pursued a solo career, releasing her debut single 'SOLO' in 2018, which garnered significant attention and praise from fans and critics alike. Her career highlights include numerous awards and nominations with BLACKPINK, including the Melon Music Award for Best New Artist and the Mnet Asian Music Award for Best Female Group.

Jennie's recent releases and collaborations have further cemented her status as a versatile and talented artist. Her participation in the remix of 'Dracula' with Tame Impala is a prime example of her ability to explore different genres and work with international artists. This collaboration not only showcases her musical range but also her appeal to a broader audience. The history of BLACKPINK, the group Jennie is a part of, is also marked by numerous achievements and records. Since their debut, they have become one of the most successful and influential K-Pop groups globally, known for hits like 'DDU-DU DDU-DU', 'Kill This Love', and 'How You Like That'. Their music often features a unique blend of genres, from hip-hop and electronic dance music to pop and R&B, which has contributed to their widespread appeal.

## What Happened

The news of Jennie's achievement on the Billboard Hot 100 chart was announced on July 13, local time, marking a significant milestone in her solo career. The remix of 'Dracula', on which she collaborated with Tame Impala, rose to a new peak of No. 5 on the chart, which ranks the most popular songs in the United States. This feat makes Jennie the 2nd female K-Pop artist to enter the top 5 of the Billboard Hot 100, a testament to her growing influence and popularity in the global music scene. The Billboard Hot 100 chart is one of the most authoritative charts in the music industry, taking into account sales, airplay, and streaming activity. Jennie's entry into the top 5 is a reflection of her song's impressive performance across these metrics, indicating a strong reception from both critics and the general public.

The collaboration with Tame Impala, an Australian psychedelic rock band known for their dreamy and psychedelic sound, brought a unique flavor to 'Dracula'. The remix blend's Jennie's powerful vocals and rap with Tame Impala's distinctive sound, creating a captivating and addictive track. This collaboration is part of a growing trend of K-Pop artists working with international musicians, which has helped bridge the gap between different music genres and fan bases. The success of 'Dracula' and its remix is a prime example of the potential for such collaborations to yield exciting and commercially successful outcomes.
