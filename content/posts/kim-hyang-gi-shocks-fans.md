Coupang Play's upcoming drama “Absolute Value of Romance” has shared new stills featuring Kim Hyang Gi and her handsome teachers. The drama tells the story of a high school student who secretly writes a BL novel featuring her teachers as the main characters. The show promises to bring a fresh take on the romance genre with its unique plot and charming cast.

## Our Take

The upcoming drama “Absolute Value of Romance” is generating buzz among K-Pop fans and drama enthusiasts alike. 

 The show's unique plot, which features a high school student writing a BL novel about her teachers, offers a fresh take on the traditional romance genre. This bold move is likely to spark interesting discussions and debates among viewers. 

 The cast, including Kim Hyang Gi, Cha Hak Yeon, and Kim Jae Hyun, brings their distinct charms to the show, making it a must-watch for fans of the actors and the genre. 

 With the rise of K-Dramas globally, “Absolute Value of Romance” has the potential to reach a wide audience and leave a lasting impact on the industry, especially if it tackles themes and storylines that resonate with international viewers.
