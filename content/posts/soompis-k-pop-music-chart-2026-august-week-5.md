KiiiKiii’s infectious summer anthem “Pop Off Pop Off” has secured the No. 1 spot on Soompi’s K‑Pop Music Chart for a second straight week, confirming the track’s viral momentum across streaming platforms and social media. The weekly update also sees CORTIS’s “REDRED” climb back to No. 2 and aespa’s highly anticipated single “LEMONADE” surge to No. 3, while two newcomers crack the top ten, signaling a dynamic shift in the August 2026 chart landscape.

## Background

KiiiKiii, the indie‑pop project led by producer‑rapper Kim Joon‑ho, burst onto the scene in early 2025 with the mixtape *Neon Nights*, which blended synth‑wave aesthetics with Korean hip‑hop flair. The group’s breakthrough came with “Pop Off Pop Off,” a track that fuses bright brass hooks, a catchy chorus, and a dance‑challenge that exploded on TikTok, earning over 120 million views in its first month. Their rapid rise has been reflected on global charts, with the song debuting at No. 12 on Billboard’s World Digital Song Sales before climbing to the top ten.

CORTIS, a soloist from the 2022 reality show *Star Maker*, is known for his smooth R&B vocals and lyrical storytelling. After a quiet 2025, he returned with the single “REDRED,” a moody synth‑driven ballad that explores themes of heartbreak and redemption. The song has been steadily climbing the Circle Digital Chart, currently sitting at No. 5, and has logged more than 15 million streams on Spotify worldwide.

aespa, the fourth‑generation girl group under SM Entertainment, debuted in 2020 and quickly became a global powerhouse thanks to their futuristic concept and AI‑integrated performances. Their recent EP *Lemonade* marks a stylistic shift toward brighter pop‑rock sounds while retaining their signature digital aesthetics. The title track “LEMONADE” has already broken into the Billboard Global 200, peaking at No. 42, and its music video amassed 80 million YouTube views within two weeks of release.

## What Happened

Soompi’s weekly chart for August Week 5 (covering August 19‑25, 2026) confirms that “Pop Off Pop Off” holds the top position for the second consecutive week, a rare feat for a non‑idol act in a market dominated by major agencies. The chart, which aggregates data from streaming services (Spotify, Apple Music, Melon), digital sales, and YouTube views, shows the track maintaining a 15 % increase in streams compared to the previous week.

CORTIS’s “REDRED” moves up one spot to No. 2, reflecting a surge in radio play on Korean FM stations and a notable bump in TikTok usage, where fans have created over 300 k videos using the track’s chorus. aespa’s “LEMONADE” also climbs one place to No. 3, bolstered by strong performance on the newly launched Disney+ K‑Pop Spotlight series, where the group’s behind‑the‑scenes documentary premiered last Friday.

Two fresh entries join the top ten: “Midnight Echo” by indie duo Moonlit (No. 7) and “Starfall” by rookie girl group Luna (No. 9). Moonlit’s entry is especially striking, jumping 35 spots to land at No. 7 after a viral dance trend on Instagram Reels. Luna, who debuted in early 2026 under Starwave Entertainment, secured a spot thanks to a high‑energy performance on Mnet’s *M Countdown* that trended worldwide.

## Why This Matters

The continued dominance of “Pop Off Pop Off” highlights the growing influence of independent producers in the K‑Pop ecosystem. While idol groups still command the majority of chart positions, KiiiKiii’s success demonstrates that viral choreography and cross‑platform promotion can propel a track to mainstream prominence without the backing of a major agency. This shift aligns with recent industry reports indicating a 12 % rise in independent releases entering the top 20 of the Circle Digital Chart in 2026.

CORTIS’s rebound to No. 2 underscores the power of strategic releases timed with media appearances. His performance on the popular variety show *Running Man* last week contributed to a 20 % spike in streaming numbers, illustrating how television exposure remains a vital driver for chart performance in the streaming era.

aespa’s steady climb to No. 3 reinforces the group’s ability to maintain relevance despite fierce competition from newer acts. Their partnership with Disney+ not only expands their international reach but also sets a precedent for K‑Pop groups leveraging global streaming platforms for exclusive content. The “LEMONADE” music video’s visual effects, featuring AI‑generated avatars, have been praised for pushing the boundaries of digital artistry, a trend that could reshape future K‑Pop productions.

## What Fans Can Expect

Fans can look forward to KiiiKiii’s upcoming summer festival tour, slated to hit major Asian cities in September, with tickets expected to go on sale via Yes24 and Interpark next week. The tour will also be livestreamed on YouTube Premium, offering global fans a chance to experience the high‑energy performance live.

CORTIS is set to release a deluxe version of “REDRED” featuring an acoustic B‑side and a collaboration remix with R&B artist Jay Park, slated for release on October 1. The remix will be available on all major streaming platforms, including Spotify, Apple Music, and Amazon Prime Video’s music section.

aespa will launch a limited‑edition “LEMONADE” merch line in collaboration with fashion brand Ader Error, available exclusively on the group’s official store and on K‑Pop flagship retailer Ktown4U. Additionally, the group announced a world‑wide fan meeting tour for early 2027, with ticket information to be released on their official website and via Ticketmaster.
