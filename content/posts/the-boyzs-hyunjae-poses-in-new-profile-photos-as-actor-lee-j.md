THE BOYZ’s Hyunjae has just unveiled a series of sleek, black‑and‑white profile photos that spotlight his acting alter‑ego, Lee Jae Hyun. The images, released by his agency Management SEESUN on September 1, contrast sharply with the vibrant stage looks fans associate with the idol, signaling a new chapter in his burgeoning acting career.

## Background

Formed by IST Entertainment (formerly Play M Entertainment) in 2017, THE BOYZ quickly rose to prominence with their powerful performances and eclectic concepts. The nine‑member group—comprising Sangyeon, Jacob, Younghoon, Hyunjae, Ju Haknyeon, Kevin, New, Q, and Juhak—earned a reputation for tight choreography, strong vocal harmonies, and a dedicated global fanbase known as “THE B.” Their breakout hits such as “The Stealer,” “Bloom Bloom,” and the chart‑topping “The Steal” cemented them as one of the leading fourth‑generation acts. In recent years, members have diversified into solo music, variety shows, and acting, following a trend among K‑pop idols to broaden their personal brands.

Hyunjae, born Kim Hyun‑jae on March 21, 2001, debuted as the group’s main vocalist and visual. Beyond his on‑stage charisma, he has steadily built an acting résumé, appearing in web dramas like “My Perfect Stranger” (2022) and the SBS drama “The Secret of My Heart” (2023). Critics praised his natural screen presence, noting his ability to convey subtle emotions—a skill that set him apart from many idol‑actors. Fans have long speculated about his next acting move, and the new profile photos finally give a visual cue to his evolving persona.

## What Happened

On September 1, Management SEESUN posted a carousel of four high‑contrast portrait shots on THE BOYZ’s official Instagram and Twitter accounts. Each photo features Hyunjae in a minimalist black suit, stark lighting, and a serious, almost brooding expression. The caption reads, “Lee Jae Hyun – A new chapter begins,” followed by a brief note that the images are part of the promotional material for his upcoming drama series. While the exact title of the drama has not been disclosed, industry insiders suggest it will air on a major Korean broadcast network in the fall season, potentially on SBS or tvN.

The agency also released a short behind‑the‑scenes video showing Hyunjae preparing for a photo shoot, offering fans a glimpse of his professionalism and dedication. In the clip, Hyunjae mentions, “I’m excited to show a different side of myself. Acting allows me to explore emotions that I can’t always express on stage.” No official release date for the drama has been announced, but the agency hinted that a teaser will drop within the next two weeks, with the full series premiering early next year.

## Why This Matters

Hyunjae’s fresh visual campaign underscores a broader trend: K‑pop idols transitioning into mainstream acting roles with equal seriousness. When idols successfully cross over, they bring their massive fanbases to the drama, boosting viewership ratings and streaming numbers on platforms like Viki, Netflix, and Disney+. For example, BTS’s Jungkook’s cameo in “The King: Eternal Monarch” contributed to a 30% spike in the series’ global streaming figures. Hyunjae’s move could similarly elevate the drama’s profile, especially among international fans who follow THE BOYZ on YouTube and Spotify.

From an industry standpoint, the timing is strategic. The Korean drama market is currently in a renaissance, with global streaming services investing heavily in Korean content. By positioning Hyunjae as “Lee Jae Hyun,” Management SEESUN aligns the idol’s brand with the gravitas of seasoned actors, potentially opening doors to higher‑budget projects and award‑season consideration. Moreover, the stark black‑and‑white aesthetic signals a shift from the typical idol‑drama promotional style, hinting at a more mature, perhaps thriller‑oriented series that could attract a broader demographic beyond the typical teen fanbase.

## What Fans Can Expect

The immediate buzz centers on the upcoming drama teaser, expected to drop on THE BOYZ’s official channels within the next fortnight. Fans should keep an eye on YouTube Shorts, the group’s V‑Live streams, and the agency’s Twitter for the first glimpse of Lee Jae Hyun in character. In addition, the drama is likely to be available for streaming on platforms that license Korean series internationally—most notably Viki, Netflix, and possibly Disney+ depending on the network partner. When the series premieres, fans can also anticipate special behind‑the‑scenes content, possibly released on the group’s Instagram Reels and TikTok, offering a deeper look at Hyunjae’s acting process.

Beyond the drama, Hyunjae is slated to join THE BOYZ’s upcoming world tour, scheduled to kick off in Seoul in early 2025. The tour will feature a new setlist that includes solo stages, giving Hyunjae a chance to showcase both his vocal prowess and his newfound acting confidence on stage. Ticket sales for the tour will open on Ticketmaster and Yes24 later this month, with pre‑sale opportunities for fan club members.
