'Our Universe' premiered on February 4, achieving an average nationwide viewership rating of 1.9 percent. The series, featuring Bae In Hyuk, Roh Jeong Eui, and Park So Ham, explores the romantic dynamics between two in-laws initially at odds.

---

## Our Take

'Our Universe' stepping into the spotlight as the only drama airing on Wednesday and Thursday is significant for several reasons. Firstly, it creates a unique space for the show to capture an audience without direct competition, potentially allowing it to build a dedicated viewer base. The initial 1.9 percent viewership rating suggests a solid start, but it will be interesting to see how this figure evolves as word-of-mouth spreads and viewer engagement grows. The drama's premise, focusing on the complexities of in-law relationships, taps into relatable themes of familial bonds and personal growth, which are often well-received in the K-Drama landscape. Given the current trend of K-Dramas exploring diverse narratives beyond typical love stories, 'Our Universe' could resonate with audiences looking for more depth in character development. Additionally, the success of this show could prompt networks to reconsider their scheduling strategies, perhaps leading to more dramas aired during traditionally quieter times. This situation offers a fascinating case study on how programming choices can shape viewer habits and influence the overall success of a series in the competitive K-Drama market.
