Before rising to fame with 2NE1, Sandara Park had already gained recognition in the Philippines, where she began her entertainment career. She appeared in various Filipino television shows and films, eventually earning the nickname 'Pambansang Krung-Krung ng Pilipinas,' which translates to 'The National Crazy Girl of the Philippines.' Her journey to stardom, however, was not without its obstacles. The transition to a new country, learning a new language, and adapting to a different culture were just a few of the hurdles she faced.
