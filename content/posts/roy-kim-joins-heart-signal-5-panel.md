Heart Signal 5 has released a poster featuring its celebrity prediction panel, including Yoon Jong Shin, Lee Sang Min, Kim Eana, Tsuki, and Roy Kim. The show is a hit dating series where singles live together and a celebrity panel attempts to deduce the contestants' interests. This season's panel brings together a diverse group of celebrities to guess which singles will form couples.
---

## Our Take

The inclusion of Roy Kim in the 'Heart Signal 5' panel is significant, as it brings a fresh perspective to the show. 

 The combination of experienced celebrities like Yoon Jong Shin and Lee Sang Min with younger talents like Tsuki will likely create an engaging dynamic, allowing for a wide range of opinions and insights. 

 As a popular dating show, 'Heart Signal 5' is expected to draw in a large audience, and the celebrity panel will play a crucial role in keeping viewers entertained and invested in the contestants' journeys. 

 With its unique format and charismatic panel, 'Heart Signal 5' is poised to be a standout series in the Korean entertainment landscape, offering fans a mix of romance, drama, and humor.
