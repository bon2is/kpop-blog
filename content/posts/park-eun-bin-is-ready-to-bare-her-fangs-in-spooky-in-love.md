Park Eun‑bin is set to electrify audiences with her latest role as a ghost‑seeing hotel heiress in tvN’s highly anticipated occult romance, **‘Spooky In Love’**. The drama, a modern remake of the 2011 hit film *Spellbound*, pairs the actress’s sharp wit with a terrified prosecutor, promising a blend of chills and chemistry that has fans buzzing.

## Background

Park Eun‑bin first captured the public’s heart with her breakout performance in the 2015 melodrama *Hello, My Twenties!*, where her nuanced portrayal of a young woman navigating love and friendship earned her the Baeksang Arts Award for Best New Actress. Since then, she has built a versatile résumé, starring in the crime thriller *Doctor Prisoner* (2019), the period romance *The King's Affection* (2021), and the fantasy romance *The King's Affection* (2022), each showcasing her ability to shift seamlessly between intense drama and light‑hearted charm. Her recent success in the romantic comedy *The Glory* (2023) cemented her status as one of the most reliable leading ladies in Korean television.

The original *Spellbound* (2011) was a cult favorite, blending supernatural elements with a quirky love story. While the film never made a major splash overseas, its cult status grew through streaming platforms like Netflix, where it gathered a dedicated fanbase. tvN’s decision to reimagine the story for a 2024 audience reflects the growing appetite for genre‑bending K‑dramas that mix romance, horror, and mystery—a trend evident in hits like *Extraordinary Attorney Woo* and *All of Us Are Dead*.

Fans of Park Eun‑bin will recognize her talent for portraying strong, complex women. In *The King's Affection*, she played a queen navigating palace intrigue, while in *Doctor Prisoner* she embodied a determined lawyer fighting corruption. These roles have honed her skill at balancing vulnerability with fierce resolve—qualities that are essential for the new character, a hotel heiress who can see spirits but must also protect her family’s legacy.

## What Happened

tvN released the first teaser for **‘Spooky In Love’** on July 15, 2024, unveiling a moody hotel lobby shrouded in mist and a hauntingly beautiful soundtrack. The teaser revealed Park Eun‑bin, dressed in an elegant yet eerie gown, confronting a translucent apparition while delivering the line, “I’ve always lived with ghosts. Now I’ll make them my allies.” The series is slated to premiere on September 2, 2024, with episodes airing every Monday and Tuesday at 21:00 KST.

The drama pairs Park Eun‑bin with rising actor **Kim Jae‑hyun** (known for his role in *All of Us Are Dead*) as the “passionate prosecutor” who is terrified of ghosts. In a press conference on July 20, Kim described his character, Lee Min‑ho, as “a man who fights for justice by day, but whose nights are haunted by the very spirits he once dismissed.” He added, “Working with Park Eun‑bin is a dream—her energy lifts the whole set, and I can’t wait for viewers to see how our unlikely partnership unfolds.”

The production team, led by veteran director **Lee Seok‑woo** (who helmed *Hotel del Luna*), promises a visual style that mixes gothic architecture with modern sleekness. Filming took place at the historic Gyeongju Hotel, which provides authentic period details while allowing for high‑tech CGI to render the supernatural elements. According to a statement from the network, the series will feature original music by composer **Jung Jae‑il**, whose haunting melodies previously scored *Stranger*.

## Why This Matters

‘Spooky In Love’ marks a significant moment for both Park Eun‑bin’s career and the broader K‑drama market. Firstly, the series showcases Park’s willingness to tackle darker, more supernatural material—a departure from her recent romance‑driven projects. This diversification could open doors to international genre festivals, where Korean horror dramas have recently garnered critical acclaim (e.g., *Kingdom* on Netflix).

Secondly, the drama taps into the rising global demand for K‑dramas that blend genres. According to a recent Nielsen report, viewership for K‑dramas with horror or thriller elements grew 27% year‑over‑year in the U.S. and U.K. markets. By positioning ‘Spooky In Love’ on tvN, a channel known for high‑quality productions, the series is likely to secure placement on major streaming platforms such as **Netflix**, **Viki**, and **Amazon Prime Video** shortly after its broadcast, expanding its international reach.

Finally, the collaboration between Park Eun‑bin and director Lee Seok‑woo signals a strategic partnership that could set a new standard for production values in occult romances. Lee’s experience with *Hotel del Luna*—itself a massive hit that combined ghostly lore with romance—means audiences can expect meticulous set design, atmospheric lighting, and a compelling narrative rhythm that keeps viewers hooked episode after episode.

## What Fans Can Expect

Fans can look forward to a richly layered story that balances romance, mystery, and scares. The plot centers on **Han Ji‑woo** (Park Eun‑bin), the heir to a centuries‑old hotel chain, who inherited the ability to see spirits after a tragic accident in her childhood. When a string of unexplained deaths occurs at the hotel, she teams up with prosecutor **Lee Min‑ho** (Kim Jae‑hyun) to uncover a hidden conspiracy that ties the hauntings to a dark family secret.

The series promises several “must‑watch” moments: a climactic showdown in the hotel’s abandoned wing, a tender scene where Ji‑woo teaches Min‑ho to confront his fear of ghosts, and a surprise cameo by veteran actress **Kim Hye‑sun**, who will portray the hotel’s enigmatic matriarch. Moreover, the original OST, featuring a ballad by **IU**, is expected to drop alongside the finale, adding an emotional punch that often drives streaming numbers.

For viewers eager to stream the drama, it will likely become available on **Netflix** and **Viki** within a month of its final episode, with subtitles in multiple languages. Those who prefer to watch live can tune into tvN’s official YouTube channel, which historically streams episodes with English subtitles for international fans.
