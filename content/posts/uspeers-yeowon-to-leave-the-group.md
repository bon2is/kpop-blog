USPEER, a K-Pop group that debuted in June under WM Entertainment, has faced its first major lineup change with the departure of member Yeowon. The news, announced by MW Entertainment on May 6, comes after Yeowon had been on hiatus since September due to health concerns. This sudden departure raises questions about the future of the group and the well-being of its members.

The statement from MW Entertainment expressed support for Yeowon's decision, emphasizing the importance of her health and recovery.
