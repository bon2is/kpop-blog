I.O.I, the iconic K-Pop girl group, is set to perform in Hong Kong for the first time as part of their '2026 LOOP Tour'. The concert, taking place on June 20-21, 2026, at AsiaWorld-Expo, Hall 10, celebrates the group's 10th anniversary. This historic debut marks a significant milestone in the group's career, offering fans a chance to relive cherished memories and experience new performances.

## Our Take

I.O.I's historic debut in Hong Kong is a testament to the group's enduring popularity and influence in the K-Pop industry. 

 The '2026 LOOP Tour' is a highly anticipated event, especially since it coincides with the group's 10th anniversary, allowing fans to reminisce about their favorite moments and songs. 

 This concert also marks a significant milestone in I.O.I's career, as they continue to break new ground and push the boundaries of K-Pop's global reach. 

 For fans, this concert is a rare opportunity to see I.O.I perform live, and it's likely to be an unforgettable experience, with the group's energetic performances and heartfelt interactions with the audience.
