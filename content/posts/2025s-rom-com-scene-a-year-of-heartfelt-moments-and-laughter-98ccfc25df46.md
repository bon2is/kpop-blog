Soompi has released a comprehensive masterlist of over 45 romantic-comedy dramas from 2025, showcasing a vibrant year in K-Drama filled with humor, romance, and engaging chemistry. This compilation reflects the genre's popularity and its ability to blend elements from various genres, appealing to a wide audience.

---

## Our Take

The release of Soompi's rom-com masterlist for 2025 is a significant marker not just for fans, but for the K-Drama industry as a whole. In a landscape where high-stakes thrillers and fantasy dramas have dominated, rom-coms continue to provide a refreshing escape. This genre's blend of humor and heartfelt moments resonates particularly well with viewers looking for comfort content, especially in challenging times. Moreover, rom-coms often serve as a gateway for international fans to explore Korean culture and language, further expanding the Hallyu wave. With over 45 titles included, the diversity in storytelling, character development, and chemistry showcased reflects an evolving industry that is keenly aware of audience preferences. Industry players should take note: the success of these dramas can lead to increased investments in similar projects, as the demand for lighthearted, feel-good narratives shows no signs of waning. As we look forward, it will be interesting to see how these dramas evolve and whether they can maintain their charm in an ever-changing market.
