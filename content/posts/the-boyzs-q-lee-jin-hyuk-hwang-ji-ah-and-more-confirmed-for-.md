The group consists of Sangyeon, Jacob, Younghoon, Hyunjae, Juyeon, Kevin, New, Q, JuHaknyeon, Hwall, Sunwoo, and Eric. Since their debut, THE BOYZ has released several successful albums and singles, including 'Reveal', 'DreamLike', and 'Giddy Up'.
