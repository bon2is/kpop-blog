CLOSE YOUR EYES' Ma Jingxiang will not participate in today's comeback promotions due to health concerns. The group's agency, UNCORE, announced that Ma Jingxiang visited the emergency room after complaining of a headache. An in-depth examination did not indicate any particular issues, but he will sit out promotions as a precaution.
