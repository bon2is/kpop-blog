## Our Take

Jennie's inclusion on TIME's list is a testament to her immense influence and popularity worldwide. 

 As a member of BLACKPINK, Jennie has been at the forefront of the K-Pop wave, breaking cultural and linguistic barriers with her music and style. 

 This recognition will likely bolster her solo career and further cement BLACKPINK's status as one of the most successful K-Pop groups globally. 

 For fans, this is a moment of pride, validating their dedication to Jennie and BLACKPINK's music, and reinforcing the group's position as a cultural phenomenon.
