Jisoo is set to star in the upcoming drama 'Boyfriend on Demand' alongside Seo In Guk. The show, directed by Kim Jung Sik, centers on the character Seo Mi Rae, who subscribes to a virtual dating simulation service amidst her hectic work life.

---

## Our Take

The announcement of 'Boyfriend on Demand' is significant as it highlights K-Pop idols venturing into acting, particularly Jisoo, whose transition from music to drama is being closely watched. K-Pop fans often cherish their idols’ multifaceted talents, and Jisoo’s role could further solidify her position in the entertainment industry beyond music. The premise itself taps into contemporary themes of technology and romance, reflecting society's growing interest in virtual connections, especially post-pandemic. This could resonate with younger audiences who are increasingly accustomed to digital interactions in their daily lives. Moreover, the romantic comedy genre is a staple in K-Dramas, and with a reputable director like Kim Jung Sik at the helm, expectations are high for both comedic and heartfelt moments. As Jisoo navigates this new role, it will be interesting to see how her fanbase responds and if this drama will open doors for more K-Pop stars to explore acting, potentially altering perceptions of idols as mere entertainers.
