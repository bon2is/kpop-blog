Sullyoon from NMIXX and singer Parc Jae Jung are set to collaborate on a new duet titled 'Always,' which will officially drop on February 25. The song is personally composed by Parc Jae Jung, adding a personal touch to the upcoming release.

---

## Our Take

The collaboration between NMIXX’s Sullyoon and Parc Jae Jung is noteworthy for several reasons within the K-Pop landscape. Firstly, it underscores the growing trend of cross-genre collaborations, as artists from different backgrounds come together to create music that appeals to diverse audiences. Sullyoon, representing a rising girl group, and Parc Jae Jung, known for his solo artistry, can potentially attract both their fanbases, which could lead to greater visibility for both artists. Furthermore, the personal composition aspect of the song adds a layer of authenticity that resonates well in an industry increasingly focused on originality and emotional connection. As K-Pop continues to evolve, collaborations like this not only foster creativity but also bridge the gap between established and emerging talents. Additionally, with the release scheduled for late February, it will be interesting to see how this track performs in the competitive landscape of K-Pop releases, especially as fans eagerly anticipate new music after the typically busy debut season of the year.
