## Our Take

This incident highlights the often-blurred lines between idols and their fans, with some idols prioritizing fansite owners or certain groups of fans over others. 

 The K-Pop industry has seen numerous cases of idols showing favoritism, which can lead to feelings of exclusion and disappointment among fans. 

 The consequences of such actions can be severe, damaging the idol's reputation and eroding trust between the artist and their fanbase. 

 Moving forward, it is essential for idols and their management teams to prioritize fairness, transparency, and inclusivity in all fan interactions to maintain a positive and respectful relationship with their supporters.
