Nine members of THE BOYZ, excluding New, have reportedly sent a certificate of contents to ONE HUNDRED CEO Cha Ga Won, notifying her of their contract termination. This move has significant implications for the group's future. The members who notified the agency of contract termination include Sangyeon, Jacob, Younghoon, Hyunjae, Juyeon, Kevin, Q, Sunwoo, and Eric.
