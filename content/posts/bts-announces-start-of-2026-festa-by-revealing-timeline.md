BTS has officially kicked off the countdown to their 2026 FESTA, a highly anticipated annual celebration that commemorates the anniversary of their debut in June 2013. As part of the festivities, the group has revealed a timeline of exciting content and surprises that fans can look forward to in the coming weeks. From new music and photos to behind-the-scenes videos, BTS is set to delight their devoted fanbase, known as the ARMY, with a wide range of exclusive releases.

## Background

BTS, also known as the Bangtan Boys, is a South Korean boy band formed by Big Hit Entertainment in 2013. The group consists of seven members: RM, Jin, Suga, J-Hope, Jimin, V, and Jungkook. Since their debut, BTS has achieved immense success and has become one of the most popular and influential K-Pop groups worldwide. With numerous chart-topping hits like "DNA," "Mic Drop," and "Boy With Luv," BTS has broken multiple records and has won numerous awards, including the Melon Music Award for Artist of the Year and the Mnet Asian Music Award for Best Male Group.

BTS is known for their energetic live performances, thought-provoking lyrics, and genre-bending music style, which blends elements of hip-hop, rock, pop, and R&B. The group has released several successful albums, including "The Most Beautiful Moment in Life" series, "Wings," "Love Yourself: Her," and "Map of the Soul: 7." Their music often deals with themes of self-empowerment, love, and social issues, resonating with fans of all ages and backgrounds. With their unique sound and message, BTS has attracted a massive global following and has collaborated with international artists like Halsey, Ed Sheeran, and Lauv.

The group's history is marked by numerous milestones, including their debut single "No More Dream" in 2013, their first music show win on "The Show" in 2015, and their historic performance at the Grammy Awards in 2020. BTS has also been recognized for their philanthropic efforts, particularly in the areas of education and mental health awareness. Through their music and activities, the group has inspired fans to embrace their individuality and to strive for positive change in the world.

## What Happened

On June 1 at midnight KST, BTS announced the start of their 2026 FESTA by revealing a timeline of upcoming content and surprises. The timeline includes the release of new photos, videos, and music, as well as behind-the-scenes content and special messages from the members. According to the revealed schedule, fans can expect a new photo collection on June 5, a dance practice video on June 10, and a special music release on June 15. The group has also promised to share exclusive content on their social media channels, giving fans a glimpse into their daily lives and creative processes.

With the revealed timeline, fans now have a clear idea of what to expect in the coming weeks, and the excitement is building up to a fever pitch.
