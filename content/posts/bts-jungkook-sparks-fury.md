A new documentary featuring BTS's Jungkook has ignited fury among fans due to his alleged mistreatment. The documentary has sparked a heated debate about the K-Pop industry's treatment of idols. Fans are demanding better treatment for Jungkook and other idols.
---

## Our Take

The outrage over Jungkook's treatment in the documentary highlights the darker side of the K-Pop industry. 

 The K-Pop industry is known for its high demands and strict training regimens, but the alleged mistreatment of Jungkook has crossed a line. 

 This incident has sparked a wider conversation about the industry's treatment of idols and the need for better protection and support. 

 For Jungkook and BTS, this incident may impact their future projects and public appearances, as fans are likely to be more vigilant about their well-being.
