One such intersection is the upcoming tvN drama "My Bias, My Boss", which promises to delve into the complexities of fandom, romance, and professional life. At the center of this drama is Kim Hye Joon, an actress known for her versatility and depth in portraying a wide range of characters.


## Background


Kim Hye Joon has been making waves in the Korean entertainment scene with her impressive acting skills and charming on-screen presence. Before her role in "My Bias, My Boss", Kim Hye Joon has appeared in various dramas and films, showcasing her ability to adapt to different genres and roles. Her career trajectory is marked by significant milestones, including her early beginnings in modeling and her eventual transition into acting. With each project, she has demonstrated growth and a keen sense of character interpretation, making her a favorite among audiences and critics alike.


The drama "My Bias, My Boss" is based on a webtoon of the same name, which already has a dedicated fan base. The story follows Nam Da Reum, played by Kim Hye Joon, as she joins a company with the primary motivation of meeting her longtime idol bias, Lee Chan, portrayed by Cha Woo Min. This setup allows for an exploration of themes such as fandom, obsession, and the blurred lines between personal and professional life. The inclusion of Kang Hoon as her boss adds another layer of complexity, as Nam Da Reum finds herself caught between her admiration for Lee Chan and her growing feelings for her superior.


The cast of "My Bias, My Boss" brings together talented actors who are known for their performances in various K-Dramas. Cha Woo Min, who plays the role of Lee Chan, is an actor who has been gaining recognition for his roles in recent dramas. Kang Hoon, on the other hand, has established himself as a versatile actor capable of playing a wide range of characters. The chemistry between the leads is expected to be a highlight of the drama, as their characters navigate the intricate web of relationships and emotions.
