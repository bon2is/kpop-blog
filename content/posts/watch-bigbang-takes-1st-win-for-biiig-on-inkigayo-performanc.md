BIGBANG finally broke their post‑comeback win drought on September 6, when “BiiiG” captured the top spot on SBS’s Inkigayo. The veteran quartet out‑scored ATEEZ’s “BAD” and Alpha Drive One’s “BORN DIRE,” earning 5,351 points and sparking a wave of celebration across social media.

## Background

Since debuting under YG Entertainment in 2006, BIGBANG has become one of K‑pop’s most influential acts, known for chart‑topping hits like “Fantastic Baby,” “Bang Bang Bang,” and “Loser.” Their blend of hip‑hop, EDM, and emotive ballads helped define the Hallyu wave, and each member—G‑Dragon, T.O.P, Taeyang, Daesung, and Seungri—pursued successful solo careers. After a three‑year hiatus caused by military service and legal controversies, the group returned in 2022 with the single “Still Life,” which topped the Circle Digital Chart and entered Billboard’s World Digital Song Sales chart.

In early 2024, BIGBANG announced a new digital single titled “BiiiG,” a bold, synth‑driven track that showcases G‑Dragon’s experimental production and Taeyang’s soaring vocals. The release was accompanied by a high‑budget music video that premiered on YouTube, quickly amassing millions of views and trending worldwide. While “BiiiG” performed well on Korean streaming platforms—reaching the top five on the Circle Streaming Chart—it had yet to secure a music‑show win, a key metric of success in the K‑pop industry.

## What Happened

On September 6, SBS’s Inkigayo aired its weekly chart showdown featuring three contenders: BIGBANG’s “BiiiG,” ATEEZ’s comeback “BAD,” and Alpha Drive One’s “BORN DIRE.” After a live tally of digital sales, streaming points, broadcast scores, and fan voting, BIGBANG emerged victorious with a total of 5,351 points. The win was announced by host Jang Won-young, who praised the group’s “timeless charisma.” The moment was captured in a short clip that quickly circulated on Twitter, Instagram, and the Soompi community, where fans flooded the comments with emojis and heartfelt messages.

The Inkigayo episode also featured performances from MONSTA X, who delivered a high‑energy stage of “Bicycle,” and soloist Chungha, who performed her latest single “Play.” Both acts received positive buzz, but the spotlight remained on BIGBANG’s historic win. While the show did not disclose exact streaming numbers, industry insiders noted that “BiiiG” saw a spike in Spotify and Apple Music streams following the broadcast, contributing to its chart dominance.

## Why This Matters

Securing a first‑place trophy on Inkigayo is more than a vanity metric; it signals a successful comeback in the highly competitive Korean music market. For BIGBANG, the win validates their return after years of absence and proves that their fanbase—known as V.I.Ps—remains fiercely supportive. It also demonstrates YG Entertainment’s effective promotional strategy, which leveraged teasers, a visually striking MV, and strategic appearances on variety shows.

In the broader K‑pop landscape, BIGBANG’s victory underscores a shift toward veteran groups reclaiming relevance alongside newer acts like ATEEZ and Alpha Drive One. The win may encourage other second‑generation idols to pursue comebacks, knowing that chart success is still attainable. Moreover, the achievement contributes to the group’s legacy on international charts; “BiiiG” has already entered Billboard’s World Digital Song Sales Top 10 and is slated for inclusion on upcoming global playlists on Spotify’s “K‑Pop Rising” and Apple Music’s “K‑Pop Hits.”

## What Fans Can Expect

Following the Inkigayo win, BIGBANG is expected to appear on additional music programs such as Mnet’s M Countdown and KBS’s Music Bank, where they will likely continue to vie for the coveted “triple crown.” The group has also hinted at a possible summer concert series, though dates have not been confirmed. International fans should keep an eye on YG’s official channels for ticket announcements, which will likely be sold through platforms like Interpark and Yes24 for Korean dates, and via Ticketmaster for any overseas stops.

Meanwhile, “BiiiG” will remain available on all major streaming services—Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, and YouTube Music—allowing global listeners to add the track to their playlists. The music video stays on YouTube, and a behind‑the‑scenes “making‑of” clip is scheduled for release next week, offering fans deeper insight into the song’s production.

## 💭 Editor's Take

💭 Editor's Take: Watching BIGBANG finally clinch an Inkigayo win felt like a nostalgic punch of pure K‑pop joy. After years of waiting, the group’s triumphant moment proves that true artistry can weather any storm—be it military service, scandal, or a shifting industry. Their ability to still dominate a live chart showdown against fierce competition speaks volumes about their timeless appeal and the unwavering devotion of V.I.Ps. As a journalist who grew up on “Fantastic Baby,” I’m thrilled to see BIGBANG not only back on the stage but also setting the bar for comeback strategies. Expect more wins, more performances, and perhaps a world tour that finally gives long‑awaiting fans the chance to experience “BiiiG” live. The future looks bright for these legends, and we’re all here for the ride.
