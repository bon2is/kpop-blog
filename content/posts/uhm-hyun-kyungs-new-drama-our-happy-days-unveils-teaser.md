The upcoming daily drama 'Our Happy Days' has released a new teaser featuring Uhm Hyun Kyung as an intern with ulterior motives at Yoon Jong Hun's company. The drama is a warm family series that explores the stories of multiple generations. It centers around the clash between a perfect man and a clumsy woman as they strive to become the main characters of their own lives.

## Our Take

This news matters because it highlights the diverse range of roles that Uhm Hyun Kyung is taking on in her career, showcasing her versatility as an actress. 

 The K-Pop and K-Drama industry is highly competitive, and the success of a drama often depends on the chemistry between its leads and the overall storyline. In this context, 'Our Happy Days' seems to have an interesting premise that could attract a wide range of viewers. 

 For fans of Uhm Hyun Kyung, this drama is a great opportunity to see her in a new light, and her character's ulterior motives add an intriguing layer to the story. 

 The drama's focus on family and multiple generations could also resonate with audiences looking for heartwarming stories, making 'Our Happy Days' a potentially impactful series in the K-Drama landscape.
