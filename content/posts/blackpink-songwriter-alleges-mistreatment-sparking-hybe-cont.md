A BLACKPINK songwriter recently posted a TikTok video hinting at the mistreatment of group members, specifically mentioning the continuous involvement of HYBE. This has sparked widespread outrage and concern among fans. The allegations have raised questions about the treatment of artists under HYBE's management.
---

## Our Take

The news of a BLACKPINK songwriter alleging mistreatment of the group's members is significant, as it shines a light on the often-hidden dynamics between K-Pop idols and their management companies. 

 The controversy surrounding HYBE's involvement is particularly noteworthy, given the company's influential position in the K-Pop industry. As one of the largest entertainment companies in Korea, HYBE's actions can have far-reaching consequences for both artists and fans. 

 This development has major implications for BLACKPINK's career, as well as the broader K-Pop landscape. Fans are eagerly awaiting a response from HYBE and BLACKPINK's management team, hoping for transparency and accountability. 

 The situation also underscores the importance of artist welfare and advocacy in the K-Pop industry, highlighting the need for greater support and protection for idols navigating the challenges of their profession.
