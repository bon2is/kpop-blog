Hyoyeon of Girls’ Generation and Jeong Eun Ji of Apink are set to light up the next episode of the beloved reality series “The Manager.” A freshly released teaser shows the two idols laughing, sharing stories, and bonding with their bandmates, giving fans a rare glimpse of camaraderie behind the stage lights.

## Background

Girls’ Generation (SNSD) debuted in 2007 under SM Entertainment and quickly rose to become one of K‑pop’s most iconic girl groups. With hits like “Gee,” “I Got a Boy,” and “Lion Heart,” they have amassed a global fanbase and broken numerous records, including multiple entries on the Billboard World Albums chart. After a brief hiatus, the members have pursued solo projects, acting, and variety show appearances, while still reuniting for special events and anniversaries.

Apink, formed in 2011 under Play M Entertainment (now IST), carved a niche with their sweet, melodic sound and consistent line‑up. Known for tracks such as “NoNoNo,” “Mr. Chu,” and “Dumhdurum,” they have maintained a steady presence on the Circle (formerly Gaon) charts and earned a reputation for strong vocal harmonies and heartfelt lyrics. Lead vocalist Jeong Eun Ji, also known as “Eunji,” has showcased her versatility through solo ballads and musical theatre roles.

Both groups have been featured on reality and variety programs throughout their careers, helping fans see the personalities behind the polished performances. Shows like “Running Man,” “Weekly Idol,” and “Law of the Jungle” have contributed to their lasting popularity, and “The Manager” continues that tradition by focusing on the day‑to‑day lives of idols and their support staff.

## What Happened

Soompi reported that a sneak peek of the upcoming episode of “The Manager” has been released, confirming Hy Hyoyeon and Apink’s Jeong Eun Ji as special guests. The preview, posted on the show’s official YouTube channel, shows Hyoyeon arriving with fellow Girls’ Generation members, sharing a light‑hearted moment with Taeyeon and Tiffany, while Eun Ji is seen chatting with Apink’s Chorong, Namjoo, and Yoon Bo. Host Jun Hyun‑Moo, known for his witty banter, introduces the guests and sets the stage for a series of fun challenges and candid conversations.

The episode is scheduled to air next week on KBS2, with a simultaneous broadcast on the streaming platform V Live for international fans. While exact airtime varies by region, the show typically streams at 22:00 KST. The preview highlights a segment where Hyoyeon teaches the group a dance routine from her recent solo project, while Eun Ji leads a karaoke session featuring classic Apink ballads. Both idols also discuss upcoming schedules, offering fans a sneak peek of future comebacks and promotional activities.

No official statements from the agencies were released beyond the teaser, but the preview includes brief quotes: Hyoyeon says, “It’s been a while since we’ve all been together, and I’m excited to share some laughs with my sisters,” and Eun Ji adds, “Being on ‘The Manager’ feels like a family reunion – I can’t wait for the fans to see us having fun.”

## Why This Matters

The appearance marks a significant moment for both groups, reinforcing the collaborative spirit that has become a hallmark of the K‑pop industry. For Girls’ Generation, Hyoyeon’s participation underscores the members’ continued relevance in variety programming, keeping the group in the public eye despite individual pursuits. It also serves as subtle promotion ahead of the group’s 15th‑anniversary activities, rumored to include a special album or concert tour later in the year.

For Apink, Eun Ji’s guest spot highlights the group’s steady popularity and the agency’s strategy to leverage reality TV for fan engagement. Apink’s recent releases have performed well on the Circle Digital Chart, and the exposure on “The Manager” could boost streaming numbers on platforms like Spotify and Apple Music, especially in Western markets where reality show clips often go viral.

The episode also reflects a broader trend of legacy idols using variety shows to maintain relevance and connect with newer audiences. By featuring both veteran groups on the same episode, “The Manager” taps into nostalgia while introducing younger fans to the enduring charm of first‑generation idols.

## What Fans Can Expect

Viewers can look forward to a mix of light‑hearted games, behind‑the‑scenes footage, and heartfelt moments. The dance‑challenge segment with Hyoyeon promises a glimpse of her choreography skills, potentially hinting at a future solo performance or collaboration. Eun Ji’s karaoke segment may feature unreleased acoustic versions of Apink songs, offering fans exclusive content.

Beyond the episode, fans should keep an eye on official social media channels for follow‑up clips and behind‑the‑scenes photos. Both groups are expected to tease upcoming projects in the near future, so the episode could serve as a subtle platform for announcements. Additionally, the show’s international streaming on V Live means fans outside Korea can watch live or catch up later, ensuring global accessibility.
