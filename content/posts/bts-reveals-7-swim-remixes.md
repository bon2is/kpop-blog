BTS will release seven 'SWIM' remixes, one for each member, as part of their new remix album 'KEEP SWIMMING'. The album will contain nine tracks and will be released at 1 p.m. KST. This move showcases the group's creativity and dedication to their fans.
---

## Our Take

The release of seven 'SWIM' remixes, each tailored to a specific member, highlights BTS's innovative approach to music production. 

 This strategy allows fans to experience the song from different perspectives, potentially attracting new listeners and deepening existing fan engagement. 

 By doing so, BTS continues to push the boundaries of K-Pop, exploring new sounds and styles that set them apart from other groups. 

 The 'KEEP SWIMMING' remix album demonstrates BTS's commitment to their craft and their fans, offering a unique and exciting experience that will undoubtedly generate significant buzz in the K-Pop community.
