SF9’s ten‑year anniversary is more than a milestone—it’s a celebration of resilience, evolution, and the fierce spirit the group calls “TENACITY.” In an exclusive interview with HelloKpop, the nine‑member boy band opened up about their decade‑long journey, the deeper meaning of their new concept, and the upcoming release of their highly anticipated album “Without Wings.”

## Background

Formed by FNC Entertainment, SF9 (Star Force 9) debuted on October 5, 2016 with the single album “Feeling Sensation.” The group quickly distinguished itself with a blend of powerful choreography, rock‑infused tracks, and a charismatic stage presence that set them apart from contemporaries. Early hits like “Fanfare” and “Mamma Mia” showcased their ability to fuse pop sensibility with bold, experimental sounds, earning them a dedicated fanbase known as “Fantasy.”

Over the years, SF9 has built a solid discography, including full‑length albums such as “First Collection” (2018), “Turn Over” (2020), and “The Wave” (2022). Their 2020 single “Good Guy” topped the Circle Digital Chart, while “Mamma Mia” earned a nomination at the 2018 Mnet Asian Music Awards. Internationally, they have performed on Billboard’s “World Stage” and made appearances on YouTube’s “K‑Pop Central,” expanding their reach to fans across North America, Europe, and Southeast Asia. Their recent releases, “The One” (2023) and the digital single “Mouth” (2024), have continued to chart on Hanteo and show steady growth in streaming numbers on platforms like Spotify and Apple Music.

## What Happened

During the HelloKpop interview, SF9 members explained that “TENACITY” is the thematic core of their ten‑year celebration. The term, a blend of “ten” (for ten years) and “tenacity,” reflects the perseverance that has carried them through lineup changes, intense competition, and the evolving K‑pop landscape. Leader Youngbin said, “We wanted a word that captures both the number and our relentless drive. Ten years is a milestone, but the real story is how we kept pushing forward together.”

The group also announced the upcoming release of their ninth mini‑album, titled “Without Wings,” slated for early November 2024. While the exact release date remains under wraps, they confirmed that the album will be available on all major streaming services—including Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music—shortly after the digital drop. Physical copies will ship worldwide through K‑pop retailers such as Yes24, Ktown4u, and Amazon, with pre‑orders already opening on their official website. “Without Wings” will feature a lead single that embodies the “TENACITY” concept, blending rock‑driven instrumentals with soaring vocal harmonies, a sound SF9 has been perfecting since their debut.

## Why This Matters

Reaching a decade in K‑pop is a rare feat, especially for groups that debuted in the highly competitive second generation wave. SF9’s longevity signals both strong agency support and a loyal fanbase that has grown alongside them. Their emphasis on “TENACITY” resonates with a global audience that values authenticity and perseverance—traits that have become increasingly marketable in the age of streaming and social media.

From an industry perspective, SF9’s upcoming album arrives at a time when K‑pop acts are leveraging multi‑platform releases to dominate charts worldwide. By ensuring “Without Wings” lands on Spotify’s “New Music Friday” playlists and Apple Music’s “K‑Pop Essentials,” the group positions itself for potential entries on the Billboard Global 200 and even the Hot 100, should the lead single gain traction in the U.S. market. Moreover, the physical album’s collectible photobooks and limited‑edition photocards cater to the still‑robust K‑pop merchandise market, driving sales that could boost their standing on the Circle Album Chart.

## What Fans Can Expect

Fans can look forward to a series of teaser videos and concept photos beginning next week, each highlighting a different facet of the “TENACITY” narrative. According to member Inseok, the choreography for the lead track will incorporate aerial elements—symbolic “wings” that are later “removed” to represent growth without reliance on external validation. The group also hinted at a possible comeback showcase streamed live on YouTube and V Live, offering international fans a chance to experience the performance in real time.

Beyond the album, SF9 announced a world‑wide fan meeting tour titled “TENACITY: The 10‑Year Tour,” set to kick off in Seoul in December 2024 before traveling to major cities like Los Angeles, London, and Tokyo. Ticket sales will open through Ticketmaster and Interpark, with early‑bird packages that include exclusive “Without Wings” merch bundles. The tour will not only celebrate their past achievements but also serve as a platform to debut new stage designs and live renditions of the upcoming tracks.
