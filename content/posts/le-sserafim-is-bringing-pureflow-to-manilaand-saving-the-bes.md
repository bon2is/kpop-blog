LE SSERAFIM is set to ignite Manila’s K‑pop fever this winter. The five‑member girl group will headline two nights at the SM Mall of Asia Arena on December 5‑6, 2026, bringing their high‑energy PUREFLOW tour to the Philippines for the first time since their 2023 debut.

## Background

Formed by Source Music in 2022, LE SSERAFIM quickly rose from a rookie act to a global sensation. The group—Sakura, Kim Chaewon, Huh Yunjin, Kazuha, and Hong Eunchae—debuted with the EP *FEARLESS* and the title track “FEARLESS,” which topped the Circle Digital Chart and entered Billboard’s World Digital Song Sales chart at No. 3. Their follow‑up releases, including the critically acclaimed *ANTIFRAGILE* and the chart‑breaking single “ANTIFRAGILE,” solidified their reputation for bold concepts and powerful performances.

In 2023, LE SSERAFIM made history as the first K‑pop girl group to debut on the Billboard 200 with *ANTIFRAGILE*, peaking at No. 7. Their rapid rise was further highlighted by a sold‑out arena tour across Asia and North America, and a record‑setting appearance on *The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon*, where they performed “FEARLESS” live.

The group’s lineup is notable for its diverse backgrounds: Japanese member Sakura brings a J‑pop flair, Korean members Chaewon and Yunjin contribute strong vocal and dance skills, while Kazuha’s Japanese‑Korean heritage adds a bilingual edge. Hong Eunchae, the newest addition in 2024, completed the quintet, expanding their vocal palette and stage presence.

## What Happened

On September 12, 2026, PULP Live World released an official press statement confirming LE SSERAFIM’s Manila stop as part of the 2026 PUREFLOW tour. The concert will be held at the SM Mall of Asia Arena, a 15,000‑seat venue known for hosting international superstars. Dates are set for December 5 and December 6, 2026, with doors opening at 6 PM local time and the show kicking off at 7 PM.

The announcement was accompanied by a teaser video featuring snippets of the group’s new stage design, which blends futuristic LED walls with traditional Korean motifs. In the video, Chaewon says, “We can’t wait to feel the love from Manila. This city has always been special to us, and we’re bringing the purest flow of our music to you.” The teaser also hinted at a special encore song exclusive to the Manila shows, sparking speculation among fans.

Ticket sales will commence on October 1, 2026, through local platforms such as Ticket2Me, SM Tickets, and the international service Ticketmaster. Early‑bird packages include a limited‑edition PUREFLOW photocard set and a digital download of the group’s upcoming single, expected to drop in November.

## Why This Matters

LE SSERAFIM’s Manila dates mark a strategic push into the Southeast Asian market, where K‑pop fandoms have shown exponential growth over the past decade. According to the Korea Creative Content Agency (KOCCA), the Philippines ranks among the top five countries for K‑pop streaming, with a 42 % YoY increase in Spotify plays for Korean artists in 2025.

The PUREFLOW tour is the group’s first world tour since the *ANTIFRAGILE* era, featuring a revamped setlist that includes tracks from their latest EP *PUREFLOW* (released August 2026) and a brand‑new title track “WAVE.” The tour’s production budget reportedly exceeds $10 million, making it one of the most lavish K‑pop tours of the year. Success in Manila could influence future tour routing, potentially adding more Southeast Asian stops in 2027.

From an industry perspective, the concert underscores the growing clout of newer fourth‑generation groups in commanding arena venues traditionally dominated by first‑generation acts like BIGBANG or Girls’ Generation. It also highlights the effectiveness of cross‑regional promotion—LE SSERAFIM’s management leveraged partnerships with local brands such as Jollibee and Globe Telecom for co‑branded content, amplifying reach ahead of the shows.

## What Fans Can Expect

Fans attending the PUREFLOW concerts can look forward to a multi‑sensory experience. The stage design incorporates immersive AR visuals that react to live audience sound levels, creating a “pure flow” of light and sound that changes with each song. Choreography will blend the group’s signature sharp moves with traditional Korean dance elements, a nod to their cultural roots.

The setlist is expected to feature fan favorites like “FEARLESS,” “ANTIFRAGILE,” and “UNFORGETTABLE,” alongside the new single “WAVE.” Rumors suggest an exclusive Manila‑only performance of a Korean‑English mashup of “FEARLESS” and a local Filipino pop hit, a gesture that would cement the group’s connection with Filipino fans.

Merchandise stalls will offer limited‑edition items, including a Manila‑themed PUREFLOW hoodie, a signed poster, and a special “Manila Night” photocard set. Additionally, a post‑concert meet‑and‑greet package will be available for a limited number of fans, providing a chance for a brief interaction and photo with the members.
