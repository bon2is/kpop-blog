## Background


The Idol Star Athletics Championships (ISAC) has been a beloved event in the K-Pop calendar, held annually during the Chuseok holiday. The event has been running for over a decade, with the first ISAC taking place in 2010. Over the years, ISAC has become a platform for idols to showcase their athletic skills, bond with fellow artists, and entertain fans with their hilarious antics. The event typically features a range of sports and games, including track and field, archery, and bowling, among others.


The history of ISAC is closely tied to the growth of the K-Pop industry. As K-Pop gained popularity globally, ISAC became an integral part of the K-Pop experience, providing fans with a unique opportunity to see their favorite idols in a different light.
