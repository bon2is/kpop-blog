NCT WISH has kicked off the countdown to their April comeback with the release of the first teaser for their upcoming album 'Ode to Love'. The album, which is NCT WISH's very first full-length album, is set to drop on April 20 at 6 p.m. KST. This debut full-length album is a major milestone in the group's career, showcasing their growth and artistic evolution.

## Our Take

The release of 'Ode to Love' is a highly anticipated event in the K-Pop scene, with fans eagerly awaiting the group's first full-length album. 

 NCT WISH's debut full-length album is significant not only for the group but also for the K-Pop industry as a whole, as it represents a new chapter in the evolution of NCT's sub-units and their unique sound. 

 The teaser release has generated immense excitement among fans, who are looking forward to seeing the group's artistic growth and the musical direction they will take with 'Ode to Love'. 

 As NCT WISH prepares for their comeback, the success of 'Ode to Love' is expected to have a significant impact on their career, potentially paving the way for future collaborations, concerts, and awards.
