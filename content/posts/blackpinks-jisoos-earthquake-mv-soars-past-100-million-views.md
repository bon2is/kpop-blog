BLACKPINK’s Jisoo has achieved a remarkable milestone with her music video for 'earthquake,' surpassing 100 million views on YouTube. The video, released on February 14, 2025, reached this impressive mark on February 7, 2025, highlighting Jisoo's growing presence as a solo artist.

## Our Take

This news matters as it underscores Jisoo's ability to stand out as a solo artist amidst the global K-Pop phenomenon that BLACKPINK represents. Achieving over 100 million views not only showcases her individual artistry but also reaffirms her strong fanbase's commitment, reflecting the power of dedicated fandom in today's music industry.

In the context of the K-Pop industry, this milestone is significant as it illustrates the increasing trend of solo projects from group members who seek to explore their individuality. As K-Pop evolves, having members like Jisoo successfully branch out into solo careers can pave the way for others in the group, enhancing their versatility and marketability as artists.

For fans, this achievement is a source of pride and validation for their support of Jisoo's solo endeavors. Additionally, this success could lead to more personalized content and opportunities for interaction, fostering a deeper connection between Jisoo and her fans, who eagerly anticipate her future projects.

For Jisoo's career, crossing the 100 million view mark can be a significant turning point. It not only increases her visibility but also strengthens her position as a leading figure in the K-Pop scene, potentially opening doors for future collaborations, endorsements, and even international opportunities as she continues to carve out her niche.
