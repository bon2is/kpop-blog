Lee Chae Yeon is set to make her first comeback under her new agency, DOD. The announcement was made on April 6, with the comeback scheduled for April. This will be her first release since parting ways with WM Entertainment and signing with DOD, the parent company of BTOB Company.

## Our Take

Lee Chae Yeon's upcoming comeback is a significant moment in her career, marking her first release under a new agency. 

 This move to DOD, the parent company of BTOB Company, could bring new opportunities and collaborations for the artist. 

 The K-Pop industry is highly competitive, and a successful comeback can make all the difference in an artist's career, so fans are eagerly awaiting Lee Chae Yeon's new music. 

 With this comeback, Lee Chae Yeon has the chance to reconnect with her fans and potentially attract new ones, which could lead to a resurgence in her popularity.
