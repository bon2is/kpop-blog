Over the years, Taecyeon has also pursued a career in acting, showcasing his versatility in various dramas and films. Some of his notable roles include 'Dream High', 'Let's Fight, Ghost', and 'Save Me'. With his experience in both music and acting, Taecyeon has built a strong fan base and is widely recognized as a talented artist in the Korean entertainment industry.

So Ji Sub, on the other hand, is a veteran actor with a career spanning over two decades. He has appeared in numerous dramas and films, including 'I'm Sorry, I Love You', 'Cain and Abel', and 'Master's Sun'. So Ji Sub's ability to portray complex characters has earned him critical acclaim and a loyal fan base. In 'Agent Kim Reactivated', he plays the role of Manager Kim, a seemingly ordinary father who will stop at nothing to save his beloved daughter. The drama's premise, combined with So Ji Sub's acting prowess, has generated significant buzz among fans and critics alike.

The webtoon 'Agent Kim', on which the drama is based, has also garnered a significant following. With the addition of Taecyeon to the cast, the drama is expected to appeal to a wider audience, including fans of K-Pop and action genres.
