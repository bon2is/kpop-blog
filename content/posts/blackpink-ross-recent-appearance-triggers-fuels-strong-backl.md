With hits like 'DDU-DU DDU-DU' and 'Kill This Love', BLACKPINK has not only dominated the K-Pop scene but has also made significant inroads into the global music market, including charting on the Billboard Hot 100 and Billboard 200.




As a group, BLACKPINK has been praised for their high-energy performances and thought-provoking music videos, addressing themes of female empowerment and self-love. Rosé, along with her bandmates Jennie, Lisa, and Jisoo, has been instrumental in BLACKPINK's success, contributing to the group's songwriting and choreography. Their influence extends beyond music, with the group members often featured in fashion campaigns and collaborations with international brands. This level of success and visibility means that their actions, both on and off stage, are subject to intense scrutiny and can have far-reaching consequences.
