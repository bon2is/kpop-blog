Han Sun Hwa stars in the high school comedy-horror 'Teaching Practice: Idiot Girls and School Ghost 2', a standalone sequel to the 2024 film. The movie follows Eun Gyeong, a passionate student teacher, as she battles against an exam ghost. The film also features Hong Ye Ji, Yeoreum, and more in key roles.

## Our Take

The release of 'Teaching Practice: Idiot Girls and School Ghost 2' marks an exciting moment for fans of Korean comedy-horror films. 

 The movie's blend of genres is likely to appeal to a wide range of audiences, from fans of lighthearted comedies to those who enjoy supernatural thrillers. 

 Han Sun Hwa's leading role in the film is a significant milestone in her career, showcasing her versatility as an actress. 

 The success of 'Teaching Practice: Idiot Girls and School Ghost 2' could also pave the way for more standalone sequels in the Korean film industry, offering new opportunities for emerging actors and filmmakers.
