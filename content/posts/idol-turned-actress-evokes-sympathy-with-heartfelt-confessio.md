## Background

Son Naeun, whose real name is Son Na-eun, was born on February 10, 1994, in Seoul, South Korea. She rose to fame in 2011 as a member of the K-Pop group Apink, which was formed by Cube Entertainment. Alongside her group members, Son Naeun quickly gained popularity for her charming on-stage presence, captivating vocals, and stunning visuals. Throughout her career with Apink, the group released numerous hit songs, including 'NoNoNo', 'Luv', and 'Eung Eung'. As a member of Apink, Son Naeun participated in various music shows, concerts, and variety programs, solidifying her position as a beloved K-Pop idol.

As Apink's popularity grew, so did Son Naeun's individual career. She began to explore acting, making her debut in the 2012 drama 'The Great Seer'. Her acting career took off, with notable roles in dramas such as 'Cinderella and Four Knights' and 'When the Devil Calls Your Name'. In 2021, Son Naeun announced her departure from Apink, choosing to focus on her acting career. Despite her departure from the group, Son Naeun remains a cherished figure in the K-Pop world, with fans continuing to support her endeavors.
