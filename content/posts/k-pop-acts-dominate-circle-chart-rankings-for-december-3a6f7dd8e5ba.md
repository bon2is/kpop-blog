CORTIS topped the Circle Chart for December with their debut EP 'COLOR OUTSIDE THE LINES,' while SAY MY NAME's new EP '&Our Vibe' debuted at No. 2. Notable mentions also include artists like Hwasa, Doyoung, and fromis_9 making significant placements on the charts.

## Our Take

The recent success of CORTIS on the Circle Chart underscores the growing trend of debuting groups making immediate impacts in the K-Pop industry. It reflects a dynamic shift where fresh talent is not only entering a saturated market but also carving a niche for themselves with unique concepts and sounds. Their debut topping the chart signifies a potential change in consumer preferences, moving towards more diversified offerings rather than established acts dominating the scene. This is particularly relevant in a landscape increasingly characterized by rapid content consumption and the rise of social media, where visibility and engagement can propel new acts to the forefront quickly. The presence of well-known groups like TWICE and emerging acts like SAY MY NAME alongside each other on the charts illustrates a healthy competition and showcases the vast array of talent K-Pop has to offer. It will be interesting to observe how these trends evolve in the coming months and whether newer groups can maintain their momentum against longstanding favorites.
