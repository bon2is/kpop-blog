Ong Seong Wu will star in the upcoming occult romance drama “Spellbound” alongside Park Eun Bin and Yang Se Jong. The drama is a remake of the 2011 film of the same name. It will be an investigative occult romance drama, exploring themes of mystery and love.
---

## Our Take

Ong Seong Wu's involvement in “Spellbound” marks an exciting development in his career, as he takes on a role in a highly anticipated drama. 

 The drama's blend of occult themes and romance is likely to captivate audiences, especially given the success of similar genres in recent years. 

 For fans of Ong Seong Wu, this news is a welcome announcement, as they will get to see him in a new and challenging role. 

 The pairing of Ong Seong Wu with Park Eun Bin and Yang Se Jong promises a compelling dynamic, and their combined talent will likely bring depth and nuance to the story.
