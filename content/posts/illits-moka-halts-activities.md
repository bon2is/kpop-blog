ILLIT's Moka has temporarily halted all activities due to health issues, just 10 days before the group's comeback. Moka recently received medical treatment and has been advised to continue treatment and rest. This decision may impact ILLIT's upcoming comeback plans.

## Our Take

ILLIT's comeback was highly anticipated, and Moka's temporary halt of activities may cause concern among fans. 

 The K-Pop industry is notorious for its demanding schedule, and health issues are not uncommon among idols. Moka's decision to prioritize her health is commendable, but it may also affect the group's momentum. 

 This setback could be an opportunity for ILLIT to re-evaluate their comeback strategy and ensure that Moka's health is prioritized. 

 Fans will be eagerly waiting for updates on Moka's condition and the group's revised plans, hoping that ILLIT can make a successful comeback soon.
