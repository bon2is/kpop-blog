## Background

## What Happened


On August 11, Billboard announced that 'SWIM' had tied the record for BTS's longest-charting song on the Hot 100, spending its 20th consecutive week on the chart at number 91. This achievement marks a significant milestone in the group's career, as 'SWIM' is now tied with BTS's 2021 smash hit 'Butter' for the group's longest-charting song on the Hot 100. The news has been met with excitement from fans, who have taken to social media to congratulate the group on their achievement.
