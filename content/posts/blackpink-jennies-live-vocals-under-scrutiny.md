## Our Take

The news of Jennie's live vocals being ridiculed matters because it highlights the high expectations placed on K-Pop idols to deliver flawless performances. 

 The K-Pop industry is known for its highly produced music and choreographed performances, making live vocals a crucial aspect of a successful show. In this context, Jennie's struggles with live vocals may impact her reputation as a skilled performer. 

 For fans, this news may be disappointing, especially if they have come to expect a certain level of quality from BLACKPINK's live performances. 

 The criticism of Jennie's live vocals also raises questions about the pressure on K-Pop idols to maintain a perfect image, and whether this pressure can be detrimental to their well-being and artistic growth.
