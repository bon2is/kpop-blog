Min Hee Jin's new boy group, OOAK, has released a debut trailer that seemingly hints at the identity of a possible member. The trailer raises questions about whether OOAK will adopt a similar debut strategy as the successful girl group NewJeans.

---

## Our Take

The debut trailer for OOAK marks an intriguing development in the K-Pop landscape, especially given the high expectations set by Min Hee Jin's previous work with NewJeans. NewJeans revolutionized the norm with their innovative marketing strategy that emphasized a low-profile buildup, allowing them to create an organic buzz prior to their official debut. If OOAK is indeed following this pattern, it could signify a shift in how boy groups are introduced to the market, prioritizing subtler engagement over traditional, grandiose promotional campaigns. This approach could also indicate a broader industry trend toward authenticity and relatability, which resonates well with younger audiences. Furthermore, the potential reveal of a group member through the trailer adds an element of intrigue and engagement, creating a direct line of connection with fans even before the group officially debuts. In a time when the K-Pop industry is saturated with new acts, such innovative strategies may play a critical role in distinguishing OOAK and potentially reshaping the trajectory of boy group debuts in the future.
