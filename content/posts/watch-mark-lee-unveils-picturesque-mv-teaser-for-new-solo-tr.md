Mark Lee, the charismatic rapper of Stray Kids, has set the K‑pop world buzzing with a breathtaking teaser for his upcoming solo single “My Friend.” The short clip, released on September 2, showcases cinematic visuals and a melodic hook that hints at a more introspective side of the artist. Fans can expect the full track to drop on September 10, marking Lee’s first solo effort since leaving SM Entertainment.

## Background

Mark Lee debuted in 2018 as the main rapper and sub‑vocalist of Stray Kids, the boy group formed by JYP Entertainment that quickly rose to global prominence. Known for his powerful rap verses, energetic stage presence, and charismatic personality, Mark has contributed to the group’s signature sound, co‑writing many of their hits such as “God’s Menu,” “Back Door,” and the emotionally charged “Haven.” Stray Kids have amassed a dedicated international fanbase, breaking into the Billboard 200 with their 2022 album *Oddinary* and topping South Korea’s Circle Album Chart multiple times. Their 2023 world tour, *5‑STAR*, sold out arenas across North America, Europe, and Asia, cementing their status as one of the era’s most influential K‑pop acts.

In early 2024, Mark announced his departure from JYP Entertainment, citing a desire to explore solo artistic avenues. While the news initially shocked fans, it also opened the door for a new chapter in his career. Since parting ways, Mark has been teasing solo material on his personal social media, hinting at a more personal, melodic direction that contrasts with Stray Kids’ high‑octane tracks.

The upcoming single “My Friend” is the first official release under his own agency, marking a pivotal moment for both the artist and the industry. In an era where K‑pop idols increasingly pursue solo projects, Mark’s move reflects a broader trend of group members branching out to showcase individual artistry while still maintaining group ties.

## What Happened

On September 2, Mark’s newly formed agency released an official statement confirming that “My Friend” will be released on September 10. Alongside the announcement, a 30‑second music video teaser dropped on YouTube and Instagram, instantly garnering millions of views. The teaser features Mark walking through a misty forest, intercut with close‑up shots of him playing an acoustic guitar—a striking visual departure from Stray Kids’ high‑energy choreography. The background score hints at a mellow R&B groove, with a soft piano melody that builds into a soaring chorus.

In the agency’s press release, Mark expressed his excitement: “‘My Friend’ is a song about the people who stand by you when the world feels overwhelming. I wanted to capture that feeling of comfort and gratitude through both the music and the visuals.” The statement also confirmed that the full music video will premiere on Mark’s official YouTube channel at 6 PM KST on September 10, with the single available on all major streaming platforms, including Spotify, Apple Music, and Amazon Music.

While exact chart projections are unavailable, industry insiders predict a strong debut on the Circle Digital Chart, given Mark’s established fanbase and the buzz generated by the teaser. The song is also expected to enter Billboard’s World Digital Song Sales chart, following the pattern of previous solo releases from Stray Kids members.

## Why This Matters

Mark Lee’s solo debut is significant on multiple fronts. First, it showcases the evolving landscape of K‑pop where idols are no longer confined to group activities alone. Artists like BTS’s J‑Hope, (G)I‑DLE’s Soyeon, and NCT’s Taeyong have successfully launched solo projects that complement their group work, expanding their artistic portfolios and revenue streams. Mark’s move aligns with this trajectory, offering fans a fresh perspective on his musicality.

Second, the timing of the release—just months after his departure from JYP—signals confidence from his new agency in his marketability. The swift rollout of a high‑production teaser suggests substantial backing and a strategic push to capture both domestic and international attention. If “My Friend” performs well on the Circle and Billboard charts, it could set a precedent for other idols transitioning to independent agencies while still achieving mainstream success.

Finally, the song’s theme of friendship resonates deeply with the K‑pop community, which often emphasizes fan‑artist bonds. By framing his solo debut around gratitude toward supporters, Mark reinforces the reciprocal relationship that fuels K‑pop’s global expansion. This narrative may also inspire other artists to craft personal, fan‑centric releases rather than purely commercial tracks.

## What Fans Can Expect

Beyond the September 10 release, Mark has hinted at a possible follow‑up EP later this year, though details remain under wraps. The teaser’s visual style suggests a more cinematic approach to his solo concepts, potentially featuring narrative-driven music videos and collaborations with indie producers. Fans should also keep an eye on Mark’s social media for behind‑the‑scenes content, lyric snippets, and possible live‑stream Q&A sessions that could deepen engagement.

In terms of accessibility, “My Friend” will be available for streaming worldwide on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, and YouTube Music immediately after its release. The full music video will premiere on YouTube, and a lyric video is expected to follow on the same platform. For those who prefer physical media, a limited edition CD single may be announced closer to the release date, a common practice for solo K‑pop releases targeting collectors.
