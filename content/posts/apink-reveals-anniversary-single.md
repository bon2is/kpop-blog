Apink is celebrating their 15th anniversary with a new digital single titled '15th Season', set to release on April 19. The group announced the single on April 16, along with a teaser. This release marks a significant milestone in the group's career, showcasing their enduring presence in the K-Pop scene.
---

## Our Take

Apink's 15th-anniversary single is a testament to the group's lasting impact on the K-Pop industry. 

 The release of '15th Season' is highly anticipated by fans, who have been loyal to the group since their debut. This single will likely feature a nostalgic sound, blending Apink's signature style with modern elements. 

 For Apink, this anniversary single is an opportunity to reconnect with their fans and potentially attract new listeners. The group's ability to adapt and evolve over the years has contributed to their longevity, making them one of the most beloved and enduring K-Pop groups. 

 The success of '15th Season' will be a significant indicator of Apink's continued relevance in the K-Pop scene, and fans are eagerly awaiting the single's release to celebrate this milestone with the group.
