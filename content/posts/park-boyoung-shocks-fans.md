Disney+ has unveiled its new Korean crime thriller 'Gold Land', starring Park Boyoung and Kim Sung Cheol, set to premiere on April 29. The series is written by acclaimed 'Old Boy' screenwriter Hwang Joyoon. The story follows a young airport employee whose life takes a dramatic turn.
---

## Our Take

The premiere of 'Gold Land' on Disney+ marks a significant milestone in the streaming platform's expansion into Korean content. 

 The involvement of Hwang Joyoon, known for his work on 'Old Boy', brings a level of excitement and anticipation among fans of the thriller genre. 

 For Park Boyoung, this series could be a career-defining moment, showcasing her range as an actress in a high-stakes crime thriller. 

 The success of 'Gold Land' could also pave the way for more Korean original content on Disney+, catering to the growing global demand for K-Dramas and thrillers.
