ENA's upcoming drama “The Scarecrow” has released new stills featuring its supporting cast, including Song Geon Hee, Seo Ji Hye, and Jung Moon Sung. The drama follows a detective investigating a series of murders and his unlikely partnership with a man he despises. The story spans from 1988 to 2019, tracking two men bound by misfortune and hatred.
---

## Our Take

The release of new stills for “The Scarecrow” has generated excitement among K-Drama fans, who are eager to see the diverse charms of the supporting cast in action. 

 The drama's unique storyline, which spans multiple decades, is also sparking interest and curiosity about how the plot will unfold. 

 The inclusion of talented actors like Song Geon Hee, Seo Ji Hye, and Jung Moon Sung is likely to bring depth and complexity to the story, making it a must-watch for fans of the genre. 

 As the drama's release date approaches, fans can expect to see more promotional materials and teasers, building anticipation for what promises to be a thrilling and suspenseful ride.
