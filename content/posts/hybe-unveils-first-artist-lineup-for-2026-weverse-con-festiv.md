HYBE has announced the initial lineup for the 2026 ‘Weverse Con Festival’, featuring 10 diverse artists. The festival is scheduled to take place on June 6-7 at the KSPO Dome and the nearby 88 Lawn Field in Seoul. This event marks the fourth edition of the global music festival.
---

## Our Take

The announcement of the first artist lineup for the 2026 ‘Weverse Con Festival’ is significant as it sets the stage for a highly anticipated event in the K-Pop calendar. 

 The diverse lineup showcases HYBE's commitment to promoting a wide range of talents, which is crucial in the current K-Pop industry where fans are always looking for new and exciting music. 

 This festival will undoubtedly be a major highlight for fans, offering them a unique opportunity to see their favorite artists perform live. 

 The success of the ‘Weverse Con Festival’ will also have implications for the artists involved, potentially boosting their global recognition and career prospects.
