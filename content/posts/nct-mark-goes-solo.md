## Our Take

Mark's departure from NCT marks a significant change in the K-Pop landscape, as fans have grown accustomed to his presence in the group. 

 This move may be seen as an opportunity for Mark to explore his own artistic style and sound, potentially attracting a new fan base. 

 The K-Pop industry has seen numerous members leave their groups to pursue solo careers, and Mark's decision may spark debate about the future of NCT and its remaining members. 

 For fans, this change may be bittersweet, as they will no longer see Mark perform with NCT, but they can look forward to his upcoming solo projects and music releases.
