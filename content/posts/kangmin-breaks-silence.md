VERIVERY's youngest member KANGMIN has made his solo debut with the single album 'Free Falling', a self-penned work that explores themes of growth, letting go, and moving forward. This milestone marks both his solo debut and the first solo release from a VERIVERY member. The album is a candid and atmospheric reflection of KANGMIN's emotional journey.

## Our Take

KANGMIN's solo debut is a significant moment for VERIVERY fans, as it showcases the member's growth and versatility as an artist. 

 The K-Pop industry has seen a surge in solo releases from group members, allowing them to explore different sounds and styles. KANGMIN's 'Free Falling' is a notable example of this trend, as it highlights his ability to create emotive and introspective music. 

 For KANGMIN, this solo debut is an opportunity to connect with fans on a deeper level and demonstrate his artistry beyond his work with VERIVERY. The success of 'Free Falling' could pave the way for future solo projects and collaborations, further solidifying his position in the K-Pop scene.
