Kim Se Jeong has officially left Jellyfish Entertainment after 10 years, as announced by the agency on March 23. Earlier reports suggested she signed an exclusive contract with BH Entertainment, although this has not been confirmed by the new agency. This move marks a significant change in Kim Se Jeong's career.
