## Background

BTS, also known as the Bangtan Boys, is a seven-member South Korean boy band formed by Big Hit Entertainment in 2013. The group consists of RM, Jin, Suga, J-Hope, Jimin, V, and Jungkook, each bringing their unique talents and personalities to the table. With their debut single 'No More Dream' in 2013, BTS quickly gained attention for their energetic performances, thought-provoking lyrics, and genre-bending sound, which blends elements of hip-hop, rock, pop, and R&B. Over the years, the group has released several successful albums and singles, including 'The Most Beautiful Moment in Life' series, 'Wings', 'Love Yourself: Her', 'Love Yourself: Tear', 'Map of the Soul: Persona', and 'Map of the Soul: 7', cementing their position as one of the most popular and influential K-Pop groups globally.

The group's career highlights include numerous awards and accolades, such as the Melon Music Award for Artist of the Year, the Mnet Asian Music Award for Best Male Group, and the Billboard Music Award for Top Duo/Group. They have also broken numerous records, including the most-watched music video in 24 hours on YouTube and the first K-Pop group to perform at the Grammy Awards. With their recent releases, such as 'Butter' and 'Permission to Dance', BTS has continued to push the boundaries of K-Pop and expand their global reach. Their music often incorporates themes of self-empowerment, love, and social issues, resonating with fans from diverse backgrounds and age groups.
