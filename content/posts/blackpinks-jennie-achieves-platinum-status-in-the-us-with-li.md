BLACKPINK's Jennie has made history by becoming the first K-Pop solo artist to have a non-collaborative song certified platinum in the United States. Her song 'like JENNIE' has sold over 1 million units in the U.S., earning her the prestigious platinum certification from the Recording Industry Association of America (RIAA). This achievement showcases Jennie's growing global influence and popularity as a solo artist.
