## Our Take

The criticism of V's English skills is not an isolated incident, as many K-Pop idols face similar scrutiny for their language abilities. 

 This backlash highlights the high expectations placed on K-Pop idols to be proficient in multiple languages, which can be overwhelming and unfair. 

 V's response to the criticism has sparked a debate about the importance of language skills in the K-Pop industry and how it affects the mental health of idols. 

 The support from fans and fellow idols has been overwhelming, with many speaking out in defense of V and emphasizing that language skills do not define an idol's worth or talent.
