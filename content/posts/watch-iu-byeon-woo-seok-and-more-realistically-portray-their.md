## Background

IU, whose real name is Lee Ji-eun, is a renowned South Korean singer-songwriter and actress. She has been active in the entertainment industry since 2008 and has released several successful albums, including 'Growing Up' and 'Palette'. IU has also made a name for herself in the acting world, with notable roles in dramas such as 'Dream High' and 'My Mister'. Her versatility and range have earned her a large following in Korea and internationally.

Byeon Woo Seok, on the other hand, is a rising star in the K-Drama world. He made his acting debut in 2016 and has since appeared in several dramas, including 'Record of Youth' and 'Dal-li and Gamjatang'. Byeon Woo Seok's breakout role came in 2020 when he played the lead in the drama 'Record of Youth', which gained him widespread recognition and praise. His portrayal of Grand Prince Ian in 'Perfect Crown' is his latest project, and he is impressing audiences with his charming and nuanced performance.

The two actors star alongside each other in 'Perfect Crown', a romantic drama set in an alternate universe where modern-day Korea is a constitutional monarchy. The show follows the story of Seong Hui Ju, a chaebol heiress played by IU, and Grand Prince Ian, played by Byeon Woo Seok, as they navigate their complicated relationships and royal duties. With its unique premise and talented cast, 'Perfect Crown' is generating significant buzz in the K-Drama community.
