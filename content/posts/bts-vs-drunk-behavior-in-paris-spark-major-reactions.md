## Background

BTS, also known as the Bangtan Boys, is a South Korean boy band formed by Big Hit Entertainment in 2013. The group consists of seven members: RM, Jin, Suga, J-Hope, Jimin, V, and Jungkook. With their unique blend of genres, thought-provoking lyrics, and captivating performances, BTS has become a global phenomenon, breaking numerous records and winning countless awards. V, whose real name is Kim Tae-hyung, is a vocalist and visual of the group, known for his deep, soulful voice and striking appearance. Throughout his career, V has been involved in various solo activities, including photography and acting, showcasing his versatility and creativity.

V's career highlights include his solo song 'Stigma' from the Wings album, which debuted in 2016, as well as his role in the K-Drama 'Hwarang: The Poet Warrior Youth'. His recent releases, such as 'Christmas Tree' and 'Our Beloved Summer' soundtrack, have also gained significant attention and praise from fans and critics alike. As a member of BTS, V has been part of numerous chart-topping albums and singles, including 'Map of the Soul: 7', 'Butter', and 'Permission to Dance'. The group's latest album, 'Proof', has also been a huge success, featuring a collection of hits that showcase the members' growth and maturity as artists.

BTS has a long history of breaking cultural and linguistic barriers, using their music as a tool to promote self-empowerment, love, and acceptance. With their dedicated fan base, known as the ARMY, the group has become a symbol of hope and inspiration for millions of people around the world. Throughout their career, BTS has collaborated with various artists, including Halsey, Ed Sheeran, and Coldplay, further expanding their musical horizons and exploring new sounds.
