## Background


For those who may be new to the K-Pop scene, the cast of "ALL or NOTHING" is comprised of some of the most recognizable and beloved names in the industry. Leeteuk, as the leader of SUPER JUNIOR, has been a staple in K-Pop for over 15 years, known for his charismatic stage presence and versatile talents. Johnny and Jisung, part of the highly successful group NCT, have also made waves with their unique blend of music and performance styles. DAWN, formerly of PENTAGON, has been making a name for himself as a solo artist, pushing the boundaries of K-Pop with his innovative sound. Together, these artists, along with the rest of the cast, bring a dynamic mix of experiences and skills to the table, ensuring that "ALL or NOTHING" will be an unforgettable ride.


The concept of "ALL or NOTHING" is built around the idea of a winner-take-all competition, where the cast members will embark on a seven-day, six-night journey across Egypt, visiting iconic cities like Cairo, Luxor, and Hurghada. The goal is to claim the title of "prince" by completing a series of missions and challenges, each designed to test their physical and mental limits. With its unique blend of travel, competition, and camaraderie, "ALL or NOTHING" has the potential to appeal not only to K-Pop fans but also to a broader audience interested in travel and adventure.
