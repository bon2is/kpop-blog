NCT 127 has once again set the internet ablaze with the release of the official dance practice video for their title track “Blingy.” Dropped on September 5, the full‑length footage gives fans an unfiltered look at the group’s razor‑sharp choreography, intricate formations, and the sheer power behind the song that anchors their upcoming studio album of the same name.

## Background

Formed in 2016 under SM Entertainment, NCT 127 is the Seoul‑based unit of the larger NCT project, which is known for its rotating lineup and global concept. Since debut, the nine‑member (now ten with the addition of new members) ensemble has built a reputation for experimental music, high‑concept visuals, and some of the most technically demanding dance routines in K‑pop. Their discography spans chart‑topping releases such as *Regular* (2018), *Neo Zone* (2020), and *Sticker* (2021), each solidifying their presence on both the Korean Circle Chart and Billboard’s World Albums chart.

The group’s recent activities include the highly successful *Dreams Come True* world tour, which sold out arenas across North America, Europe, and Asia. Their last major release, the EP *Fact Check* (2023), saw the title track peak at #2 on the Billboard World Digital Song Sales chart and earned them a nomination at the 2024 MAMA Awards. With a dedicated fanbase—known as NCTzens—NCT 127 has consistently leveraged social media, YouTube, and streaming services like Spotify and Apple Music to maintain a global footprint.

## What Happened

On September 5, SM Entertainment uploaded the official dance practice video for “Blingy” to NCT 127’s YouTube channel. The video, running just under six minutes, presents a full‑view camera angle that captures every nuance of the choreography, from the synchronized footwork to the dynamic group formations. The members—Taeyong, Taeil, Johnny, Yuta, Doyoung, Jungwoo, Mark, Haechan, and the newest addition—deliver a performance that feels both polished and raw, highlighting the song’s blend of futuristic synths and hip‑hop‑infused beats.

“Blingy” serves as the title track for NCT 127’s forthcoming studio album, also titled *Blingy*, slated for release later this year. While SM has not yet announced an exact drop date, the label confirmed that the album will be available on all major streaming platforms, including Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, and YouTube Music, as well as for purchase on physical formats through retailers like Amazon and YesAsia. The dance practice video also includes a brief teaser of the album’s visual concept—neon‑lit cityscapes and metallic textures that echo the song’s high‑energy vibe.

In a statement released alongside the video, NCT 127’s leader Taeyong said, “We wanted to share the pure energy of ‘Blingy’ with our fans. Watching the choreography come together in one take shows how much we’ve grown as performers, and we hope you feel that excitement too.” The post quickly amassed over 2 million views within 24 hours, with fans flooding the comments section with praise for the intricate footwork and the group’s chemistry.

## Why This Matters

The release of a dance practice video is more than just a behind‑the‑scenes treat; it’s a strategic move that fuels anticipation for the full album and reinforces NCT 127’s reputation for performance excellence. Historically, dance practice videos have driven streaming numbers for title tracks, as fans replay the choreography while streaming the song on platforms like Spotify and Apple Music. This synergy often translates into higher chart placements on the Circle Digital Chart and Billboard’s Global 200.

For the K‑pop industry, NCT 127’s continued emphasis on high‑budget, high‑quality visual content underscores the competitive nature of the market, where visual storytelling is as crucial as the music itself. The group’s ability to consistently deliver complex choreography sets a benchmark for peers and keeps the global conversation focused on Korean pop’s artistic depth.

Moreover, the timing aligns with a broader wave of K‑pop releases scheduled for the fourth quarter, a period traditionally dominated by year‑end award shows and chart battles. By dropping “Blingy” practice footage now, SM Entertainment positions NCT 127 to capture early streaming momentum, potentially influencing year‑end chart tallies and award nominations.

## What Fans Can Expect

With the dance practice video already generating buzz, fans are eagerly awaiting the full *Blingy* album. SM has hinted that the record will feature a mix of genre‑bending tracks—from trap‑heavy bangers to melodic R&B—showcasing each member’s vocal and rap strengths. The group is also expected to promote the comeback with a series of televised performances on Korean music shows such as *M Countdown*, *Music Bank*, and *Inkigayo*.

In addition, NCT 127 is rumored to embark on a second world tour later next year, potentially covering new territories in South America and the Middle East. Ticket sales for past tours have consistently sold out within minutes on platforms like Ticketmaster and Yes24, so fans should keep an eye on official announcements for pre‑sale dates.
