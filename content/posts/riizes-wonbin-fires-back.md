## Our Take

Wonbin's decision to address the hater's comment is significant, as it shows he won't tolerate bullying. 

 This situation reflects the larger issue of body shaming and online harassment in the K-Pop industry, where idols often face criticism about their appearance. 

 For fans, Wonbin's response is a powerful statement about self-acceptance and confidence, and may inspire them to stand up against bullying as well. 

 The support from fans and possibly other idols could further amplify the message that height or any physical attribute does not define a person's worth or talent.
