## Background


TWICE is a South Korean girl group formed by JYP Entertainment in 2015. The group consists of nine members: Nayeon, Jeongyeon, Momo, Sana, Jihyo, Mina, Dahyun, Chaeyoung, and Tzuyu. Since their debut, TWICE has achieved immense success and has become one of the most popular and influential K-Pop groups globally. With hit songs like 'Cheer Up', 'TT', and 'Fancy', TWICE has consistently topped music charts and has won numerous awards, including several Melon Music Awards, Mnet Asian Music Awards, and Gaon Chart Music Awards. Jeongyeon, as a vital member of the group, has played a significant role in TWICE's success, showcasing her exceptional vocal and performance skills.


Jeongyeon's career with TWICE has been nothing short of remarkable. From their early days as trainees to their current status as K-Pop idols, Jeongyeon has been an integral part of the group's journey. Her dedication, hard work, and passion for music have earned her a special place in the hearts of fans worldwide. As a member of TWICE, Jeongyeon has had the opportunity to perform on some of the world's most prestigious stages, including the Melon Music Awards, the Mnet Asian Music Awards, and the Billboard Music Awards. With TWICE, Jeongyeon has also broken numerous records, including becoming the first K-Pop girl group to have a music video surpass 500 million views on YouTube.


## What Happened


On August 10, Jeongyeon took to Instagram to share a handwritten letter, announcing her departure from JYP Entertainment. This news sent shockwaves through the K-Pop community, with fans expressing their surprise and concern for Jeongyeon's future. However, in the same breath, VARO Entertainment announced that they had signed an exclusive contract with Jeongyeon, signaling a new chapter in her career. The agency expressed their excitement and anticipation for working with Jeongyeon, stating that they would support her in all her future endeavors. For global fans, this news means that they can continue to support Jeongyeon through her upcoming projects, which will be available to stream on major platforms like Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music.
