## Our Take

Jin Young's return to the big screen is a significant move in his career, especially after 15 years in the industry. 

 This film, 'Mr Shota’s Last Business Trip', is expected to bring a fresh perspective to his acting career, and his collaboration with Otani Ryohei will likely attract a wide range of audiences. 

 The K-Pop industry has seen many idols transition into acting, and Jin Young's experience will be closely watched by fans and critics alike. 

 With the film's release next month, fans are eagerly anticipating Jin Young's performance and the impact it will have on his future projects.
