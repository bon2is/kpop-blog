ILLIT has confirmed their April comeback with 'It’s Me' set to release on April 30. The announcement was made during the second night of their 'PRESS START♥︎' concert in Seoul on March 15. This highly anticipated comeback is expected to generate significant buzz in the K-Pop scene.

## Our Take

ILLIT's decision to announce their comeback at their concert adds a personal touch, showcasing their appreciation for their fans' support. 

 The K-Pop industry is known for its fast-paced and competitive environment, with numerous groups and artists vying for attention. ILLIT's strategic announcement during their concert may help them stand out amidst the April comeback rush. 

 This comeback is crucial for ILLIT's career, as it will demonstrate their ability to produce consistent and engaging content. Fans are eagerly awaiting the release of 'It’s Me', hoping it will live up to their expectations. 

 The success of 'It’s Me' may also impact ILLIT's future tour and concert schedule, potentially leading to more opportunities for fans to see them perform live.
