## Background

I.O.I, which stands for IOI or Individuality of Idol, is a South Korean girl group formed by CJ E&M through the reality show 'Produce 101' in 2016. The group consisted of 11 members, each from different entertainment companies, and was active for about a year before disbanding in 2017. Despite their short time together, I.O.I made a significant impact on the K-Pop scene, releasing hit singles like 'Dream Girls' and 'Very Very Very'. The group's members have since pursued solo activities, with some joining new groups or focusing on acting careers.

I.O.I's career highlights include their debut single 'Crush', which topped various music charts, and their follow-up single 'Whatta Man', which showcased the group's energetic and playful side. In recent years, the members have been busy with their individual activities, with some releasing solo music and others appearing in dramas and variety shows.

## What Happened

On the July 12 episode of SBS's 'Inkigayo', I.O.I took the win for 'Suddenly', beating out strong competitors aespa and RESCENE. The group scored a total of 5,343 points, with aespa's 'LEMONADE' coming in second and RESCENE's 'LOVE ATTACK' in third. The win marks I.O.I's fourth music show trophy for 'Suddenly', following wins on 'Show! Music Core', 'Music Bank', and 'The Show'.

The episode of 'Inkigayo' featured performances by various artists, including i-dle, Yeonjun, and BABYMONSTER.
