## Our Take

The concept of 'ideal' body proportions is highly subjective and can vary greatly across cultures and individuals. 

 In the context of K-Pop, where visuals play a significant role, these idols are often praised for their dedication to fitness and health.

 This attention to physical fitness also reflects the demanding nature of the K-Pop industry, where idols are expected to maintain high energy levels during performances and rigorous practice schedules.
