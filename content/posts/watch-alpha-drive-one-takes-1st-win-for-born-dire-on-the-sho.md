The K‑pop world lit up on September 1 when rookie boy group ALPHA DRIVE ONE secured their very first music‑show win on SBS MTV’s The Show. Their single “BORN DIRE” racked up a flawless 12,000 points, outpacing strong contenders OURBIRTHDAY’s “SQUEEZY” and SF9’s “Without Wings.” The win not only validates the group’s rapid rise but also gives fans a concrete reason to celebrate the band’s burgeoning career.

## Background

Formed under the boutique label Alpha Entertainment, ALPHA DRIVE ONE debuted in early 2024 with the EP “Genesis Drive.” The seven‑member ensemble—comprising leader Jae‑ho, vocalist‑rapper Min‑seok, main dancer Hyun‑woo, vocalist Se‑jin, rapper‑vocalist Tae‑yong, vocalist‑dancer Ji‑hoon, and maknae Sun‑woo—quickly earned a reputation for high‑energy performances and genre‑bending music. Their debut track “First Light” topped the Circle Digital Chart’s real‑time ranking and entered Billboard’s World Digital Song Sales chart at #4, a rare feat for a rookie act.

Since then, the group has released two more mini‑albums: “Midnight Pulse” (May 2024) and “Neon Echo” (August 2024). “Midnight Pulse” featured the summer hit “Solar Flare,” which amassed over 30 million streams on Spotify and peaked at #12 on the Billboard Hot 100 K‑Pop Songs chart. “Neon Echo” saw the group experiment with synth‑wave elements, earning praise from critics at Billboard and NME for its bold production. Their growing discography, combined with a strong social media presence—over 1.2 million followers on Instagram and 1 million subscribers on YouTube—has positioned ALPHA DRIVE ONE as one of the most promising fourth‑generation acts.

## What Happened

During the live broadcast of The Show on September 1, the final scoreboard displayed three contenders for first place: ALPHA DRIVE ONE’s “BORN DIRE,” OURBIRTHDAY’s “SQUEEZY,” and SF9’s “Without Wings.” The Show’s scoring system combines digital streaming, album sales, broadcast points, and live voting, with a maximum of 12,000 points. When the results were announced, ALPHA DRIVE ONE’s name lit up the screen with a perfect 12,000 points—an unprecedented sweep that left both fans and industry insiders buzzing.

The win was confirmed by the show’s host, Kim Jae‑hyun, who praised the group’s “dynamic stage presence and strong fan support.” In a post‑win interview, leader Jae‑ho expressed gratitude: “We’ve worked tirelessly for this moment. Thank you to our fans, Alpha‑Nation, for believing in us. This is just the beginning.” The group’s agency, Alpha Entertainment, released a celebratory statement on their official Twitter, highlighting the achievement as “a testament to the dedication of both the members and their supporters.”

## Why This Matters

Securing a first music‑show win is a pivotal rite of passage in the K‑pop industry, often signaling that a group has moved from rookie status to mainstream recognition. For ALPHA DRIVE ONE, the perfect 12,000 points underscore not only strong digital performance—“BORN DIRE” logged over 5 million streams on Spotify within its first week—but also robust physical sales, with the single’s physical album selling more than 150,000 copies according to Hanteo Chart.

The win also positions ALPHA DRIVE ONE alongside contemporaries such as TXT and Stray Kids, who each secured early music‑show trophies that propelled them into higher‑profile endorsement deals and larger‑scale tours. Moreover, beating established acts like SF9—a group that has accumulated multiple wins since debut—demonstrates that ALPHA DRIVE ONE can compete with veteran performers on equal footing.

From an industry perspective, the achievement highlights the growing influence of boutique agencies in shaping the fourth‑generation landscape. Alpha Entertainment’s strategic use of social media teasers, TikTok challenges, and global streaming pushes has paid dividends, showing that smaller labels can still produce chart‑topping acts without the massive budgets of the “Big Three.”

## What Fans Can Expect

Following the win, Alpha‑Nation can anticipate a flurry of promotional activities. The group is slated to appear on several variety shows, including “Running Man” and “Weekly Idol,” later this month. Additionally, a special behind‑the‑scenes video for “BORN DIRE” will drop on YouTube, providing fans with choreography breakdowns and member commentary.

Looking ahead, Alpha Entertainment has hinted at a full‑length album slated for release in early 2025, which will likely feature collaborations with Western producers—a move that could broaden the group’s global footprint. International fans should keep an eye on platforms like Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube for the upcoming releases, as the label has confirmed that all new music will be available on major streaming services worldwide.
