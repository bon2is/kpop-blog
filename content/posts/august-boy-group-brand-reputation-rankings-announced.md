Their music often focuses on themes of self-empowerment, love, and social issues, resonating with fans worldwide. ## What Happened

The rankings are determined through an analysis of consumer participation, media coverage, interaction, and community indexes, using big data collected from July 8 to August 8. The institute analyzes data from various sources, including news articles, blogs, and social media platforms, to determine the rankings.
