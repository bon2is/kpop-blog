## Background

SEVENTEEN is one of the most popular and influential K-Pop groups in recent years, known for their high-energy performances, catchy songs, and talented members. The group, formed by PLEDIS Entertainment, debuted in 2015 and has since released numerous successful albums and singles, including 'Adore U', 'Mansae', and 'Don't Wanna Cry'. Dino, whose real name is Lee Chan, is the group's main dancer and vocalist. He has been an integral part of SEVENTEEN's success, showcasing his impressive dance skills and charming stage presence.

SEVENTEEN has explored various genres and concepts throughout their career, from hip-hop and rock to pop and R&B. Their music often deals with themes of love, youth, and self-empowerment, resonating with fans of all ages. The group has also been praised for their energetic live performances, which often feature intricate choreography and impressive vocal harmonies. As a member of SEVENTEEN, Dino has had the opportunity to work with top producers and songwriters, honing his craft and developing his unique artistic voice.

In recent years, Dino has been exploring his creative side through various solo activities, including photography and art. His alter ego, Picheolin, is an extension of this creativity, allowing him to express himself in new and innovative ways. The name 'Picheolin' is derived from the French word 'pichenette', meaning 'a small pinch', and the suffix '-lin', which is often used in French names. This unique moniker reflects Dino's desire to create something fresh and distinctive as Picheolin.

## What Happened

On May 11, PLEDIS Entertainment announced that Dino would be debuting as Picheolin with the mini album 'Gilboard'. The title 'Gilboard' is described as a mix of the Chinese character '吉' (gil), meaning 'good luck' or 'auspicious', and the English word 'board', referring to a platform or stage. This title reflects the themes of good fortune, opportunity, and creative expression that are woven throughout the album. 'Gilboard' is set to feature a range of genres and styles, from hip-hop and electronic to pop and R&B, showcasing Picheolin's versatility and musicality.

The announcement of Picheolin's debut has generated significant buzz among K-Pop fans, who are eager to see what Dino has in store for them as his alter ego. The debut is seen as a bold move, allowing Dino to explore new artistic avenues and push the boundaries of his creativity. As Picheolin, Dino will have the freedom to experiment with different sounds, styles, and concepts, potentially appealing to a wider audience and expanding his fan base.
