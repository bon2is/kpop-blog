ENA's upcoming drama 'The Scarecrow' stars Park Hae Soo as a detective investigating a series of murders. The story follows an unlikely partnership between the detective and a man he despises. Set between 1988 and 2019, the drama tracks two men bound by misfortune and hatred.
---

## Our Take

Park Hae Soo's new drama 'The Scarecrow' is generating buzz for its unique storyline and suspenseful plot. 

 The K-Drama industry has seen a rise in crime thrillers, and 'The Scarecrow' is set to join the ranks with its gripping narrative. 

 As a detective returning to his hometown, Park Hae Soo's character will face a troubling case that will test his skills and push him to form an uneasy alliance. 

 The drama's success will depend on its ability to balance suspense and character development, but with Park Hae Soo at the helm, fans are eagerly anticipating its premiere.
