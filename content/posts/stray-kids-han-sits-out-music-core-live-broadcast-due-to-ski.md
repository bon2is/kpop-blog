Stray Kids' Han was unexpectedly absent from the live broadcast of 'Music Core' today, August 15, due to health concerns. The news was announced by JYP Entertainment, the group's agency, which explained that Han had visited a hospital earlier that morning after experiencing itching on his facial skin. This sudden development has sparked concern among fans, who are wishing Han a speedy recovery.

## Background

Stray Kids is a South Korean boy group formed by JYP Entertainment in 2017. The group consists of nine members: Bang Chan, Woojin, Lee Know, Changbin, Hyunjin, Han, Felix, Seungmin, and I.N. Known for their energetic performances and catchy songs, Stray Kids have gained a significant following worldwide. Their discography includes several successful albums and singles, such as 'District 9', 'Clé 1: Miroh', and 'Oddinary'. ## What Happened

According to JYP Entertainment, Han visited a hospital on the morning of August 15 after experiencing itching on his facial skin. The agency announced that Han would not be attending the live broadcast of 'Music Core' that day, citing health concerns and the need for further treatment. The exact nature of Han's condition has not been disclosed, but it is understood that he is undergoing treatment and is expected to make a full recovery.
