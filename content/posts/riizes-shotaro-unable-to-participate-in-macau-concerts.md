Shotaro from RIIZE will not attend the group's concerts in Macau during their first world tour 'RIIZING LOUD'. The announcement was made by the production company, but did not specify the reason behind his absence.

---

## Our Take

Shotaro's absence from the Macau concerts is significant for both RIIZE and the wider K-Pop landscape, as it underscores the pressures and challenges that idols face in balancing health, schedules, and public expectations. The fact that no specific reason was provided for his absence invites speculation, which can sometimes lead to unfounded rumors and fan anxiety. This incident is a reminder of the rigorous demands placed on artists in the K-Pop industry, where attendance is often viewed as a commitment to fans. Moreover, it highlights the importance of mental and physical health for idols, a topic increasingly discussed in the industry as many artists have begun to advocate for their wellbeing. As RIIZE is still in its formative stage, maintaining a positive public image while managing individual challenges will be crucial for the group's future success. This situation not only affects the group's dynamic but also raises awareness about the need for better support systems within the industry to ensure artists can perform at their best without compromising their health.
