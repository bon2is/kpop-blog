BOYNEXTDOOR, the rising K-Pop group, has made history with their latest album 'HOME', which has become their fourth million-seller album in a remarkably short period. The group's highly anticipated comeback, which included the emotional title track 'VIRAL', took place on June 8, and in less than five days, they achieved the impressive milestone of selling over 1 million copies.


## Background


BOYNEXTDOOR is a South Korean boy group formed by KOZ Entertainment, a subsidiary of ADOR, which is also the label behind NewJeans. The group consists of six members: Sunwoo, Riwoo, Jaehyun, Taesan, Woonhak, and Huijun. They made their debut on May 30, 2023, with the single album 'WHO!', which was a moderate success. However, it was their subsequent releases, such as 'WHY!' and 'SEOUL CITY', that gained them significant attention and praise from fans and critics alike. Their unique blend of genres, energetic performances, and catchy hooks have set them apart in the competitive K-Pop scene.


BOYNEXTDOOR's career has been marked by a steady rise in popularity, with each release surpassing the previous one in terms of sales and chart performance. Their music often explores themes of youth, love, and self-discovery, resonating with the younger generation. The group's members have also been active on social media, engaging with fans and sharing their creative process, which has helped build a loyal and dedicated fan base. With 'HOME', BOYNEXTDOOR aimed to showcase their growth and maturity as artists, and the response has been overwhelmingly positive.


The K-Pop industry has witnessed a surge in new talent in recent years, with many rookie groups making a name for themselves. ## What Happened


On June 8, BOYNEXTDOOR released their highly anticipated first full-length album 'HOME', along with the title track 'VIRAL'. The album features a diverse range of genres, from pop and rock to hip-hop and R&B, showcasing the group's versatility and musical growth. The title track 'VIRAL' is an emotional and uplifting song that explores the theme of going viral and becoming famous, but also touches on the pressures and expectations that come with it. The song's music video, which features the members performing intricate choreography and showcasing their vocal and rap skills, has gained millions of views on YouTube.


On June 12, Hanteo Chart announced that 'HOME' had sold over 1 million copies, making it BOYNEXTDOOR's fourth million-seller album. This achievement is a significant milestone for the group, solidifying their position as one of the most successful and popular K-Pop acts of the year. The album's success can be attributed to the group's dedicated fan base, as well as the album's high-quality production, catchy songs, and meaningful lyrics. BOYNEXTDOOR's achievement is also a testament to the power of K-Pop, which continues to break records and push boundaries in the music industry.
