Byeon Woo Seok, the talented South Korean actor known for his captivating performances in dramas like 'Record of Youth' and 'Twenty-Five Twenty-One', has taken on a new role as the ambassador for the renowned Italian luxury brand BVLGARI. This exciting announcement was made on May 11, marking a significant collaboration between the actor and the high-end jewelry brand. With his sophisticated visuals and undeniable charm, Byeon Woo Seok is set to embody the essence of BVLGARI, representing the brand's commitment to excellence and style.

## Background

Byeon Woo Seok has been making waves in the Korean entertainment industry with his impressive acting skills and distinctive style. Born on October 16, 1991, Byeon Woo Seok began his career in the early 2010s, initially landing small roles in films and television dramas. However, it wasn't until his breakout performance in the 2019 drama 'Record of Youth' that he gained widespread recognition, earning praise for his portrayal of Won Hae-hyo, a model struggling to find his place in the world. This role not only showcased his acting abilities but also highlighted his unique sense of style, which has since become a hallmark of his public persona.

Byeon Woo Seok's career has been on a steady rise since his debut, with notable appearances in dramas like 'Twenty-Five Twenty-One' and 'If You Wish Upon Me'. His ability to convey complex emotions and his undeniable on-screen presence have made him a favorite among audiences and critics alike. As a result, he has become a sought-after brand ambassador, with several high-profile partnerships under his belt. His appointment as BVLGARI's new ambassador is a testament to his growing influence and status as a style icon in the Korean entertainment industry.

The news of Byeon Woo Seok's appointment as BVLGARI's ambassador was announced on May 11, with the brand releasing a statement praising the actor's 'sophisticated visuals, unparalleled presence, and sincere attitude'.
