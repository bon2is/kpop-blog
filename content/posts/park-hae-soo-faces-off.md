Park Hae Soo and Lee Hee Joon star in ENA's upcoming drama “The Scarecrow”, a suspenseful story of an unlikely partnership between a detective and his past tormentor. The drama follows a series of murders and spans from 1988 to 2019. The two characters are bound by misfortune and an uneasy alliance of hatred as they chase a killer.
---

## Our Take

The pairing of Park Hae Soo and Lee Hee Joon in “The Scarecrow” promises to bring a unique dynamic to the screen, pitting two characters with a troubled past against each other in a fight for justice. 

 This drama matters because it explores themes of unlikely alliances and the complexities of human relationships, set against the backdrop of a gripping murder mystery. 

 For fans of K-Dramas, “The Scarecrow” offers a fresh take on the suspense genre, with its non-linear storytelling and complex character development. 

 The success of this drama could also impact the careers of its leads, potentially opening up new opportunities for Park Hae Soo and Lee Hee Joon in the K-Drama scene.
