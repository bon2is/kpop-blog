THE L1VE, the agency behind ZEROBASEONE's Sung Han Bin, has released a statement threatening legal action against malicious rumors and violations of privacy. This move aims to protect their artist from online harassment. The agency's statement serves as a warning to those spreading false information and invading the artist's personal life.
