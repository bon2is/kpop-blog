Lisa’s latest solo music video, “SaWaDika,” exploded onto YouTube, surpassing 100 million views within days of its release. The milestone, confirmed at 1 p.m. KST on September 6, makes it the fastest‑growing MV of 2026, underscoring the BLACKPINK member’s solo star power and the growing appetite for K‑pop content worldwide.

## Background

Lisa, born Lalisa Manoban in 1997, debuted as the main dancer and rapper of BLACKPINK in 2016 under YG Entertainment. The quartet quickly rose to global prominence, breaking records with hits like “Ddu‑du Ddu‑du,” “Kill This Love,” and the 2020 YouTube milestone of 1 billion views for the “How You Like That” MV. Individually, Lisa has cultivated a massive following through fashion collaborations, brand ambassadorships, and a vibrant social media presence that boasts over 80 million Instagram followers.

In August 2023, Lisa released her first solo single, “Lalisa,” which debuted at No. 84 on the Billboard Hot 100 and topped the Billboard World Digital Song Sales chart. The song’s music video set a new YG record for first‑day YouTube views, confirming that Lisa’s solo appeal extends far beyond the group’s fanbase. Since then, she’s dropped several promotional tracks and performed at major events such as Coachella 2024, further solidifying her status as a global pop icon.

## What Happened

On September 6, 2024, YG Entertainment announced that the “SaWaDiKa” MV had crossed the 100 million‑view threshold, making it the fastest music video of the year to do so. The video, released on September 4, amassed roughly 70 million views within the first 24 hours and continued to surge, reaching the landmark in just 48 hours. In a brief statement, YG praised Lisa’s “unparalleled global reach” and highlighted the song’s blend of Latin‑inspired beats with Lisa’s signature rap flow.

“SaWaDiKa” serves as a pre‑release single for Lisa’s upcoming solo EP, slated for early 2025. The track, produced by longtime collaborator Teddy Park, features a catchy chorus that mixes Korean and English lyrics, a strategy that has proven successful for K‑pop artists targeting Western markets. The MV, directed by the acclaimed Korean‑American filmmaker Kim Ji‑young, showcases high‑octane choreography filmed across Seoul’s neon‑lit streets and a rooftop studio in Los Angeles, reinforcing Lisa’s cross‑cultural appeal.

Fans worldwide have been quick to stream the song on major platforms. “SaWaDiKa” is already available on Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music, while the MV can be viewed on YouTube and the official YG channel. The track is also being promoted on TikTok, where a dance challenge has already generated millions of user‑generated videos, further amplifying its viral potential.

## Why This Matters

Lisa’s rapid 100 million‑view achievement is significant for several reasons. First, it demonstrates the growing power of solo projects within K‑pop groups. While BLACKPINK’s collective releases dominate charts, individual members like Lisa, Jennie, and Rosé have shown that solo ventures can command comparable streaming numbers, challenging the traditional group‑centric model.

Second, the record reflects the expanding global appetite for K‑pop content on mainstream platforms. In the United States and Europe, “SaWaDiKa” entered the Billboard Hot 100’s “Bubbling Under” chart and debuted on the Billboard Global 200 at No. 12, indicating strong cross‑border consumption. The swift viewership also highlights YouTube’s continued role as the primary discovery hub for K‑pop, outpacing even Spotify in terms of first‑day impact for many Korean artists.

Finally, the achievement underscores YG Entertainment’s strategic push to position its artists on Western‑friendly streaming services. By releasing a bilingual track with a Latin‑infused production, Lisa taps into the current global music trend that favors genre‑blending hits—a move that could influence future K‑pop releases to adopt more multicultural sounds.

## What Fans Can Expect

The momentum from “SaWaDiKa” sets the stage for Lisa’s full solo EP, expected in early 2025. YG has hinted at a multi‑track project that will feature collaborations with both Korean and international producers, potentially including a feature from a Western pop star. In addition, a teaser for a second music video is slated for release in November, and a world‑wide promotional tour is rumored to be in planning, with dates likely to be announced via Lisa’s official channels and major ticket platforms such as Ticketmaster.

Fans should also keep an eye on streaming playlists. “SaWaDiKa” is already featured on Spotify’s “New Music Friday” and Apple Music’s “K‑Pop Hits,” ensuring continued visibility. The ongoing TikTok challenge is expected to drive further streams, and the song will likely be performed at upcoming award shows, including the Mnet Asian Music Awards (MAMA) in December.
