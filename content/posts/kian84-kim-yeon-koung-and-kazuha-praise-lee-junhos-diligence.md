Lee Junho’s dedication to the quirky guesthouse project has become the talk of the town as Netflix’s “Kian’s Bizarre B&B” returns for a second season. The actor‑singer, known for his roles in dramas like *While You Were Sleeping* and as a former member of SS501, impressed fellow cast members with his tireless work ethic, sparking excitement among fans eager for more behind‑the‑scenes fun.

## Background

“Kian’s Bizarre B&B” is a Netflix original variety series that first aired in early 2023. Created by internet personality Kian84 (real name Kian Kwon), the show follows a group of celebrities who transform an abandoned guesthouse on a remote island into a one‑of‑a‑kind lodging experience for tourists. Season 1 featured a star‑studded lineup including BTS’s Jin, actress‑singer Ji Ye‑eun, and Kian84 himself. The premise blends travel, comedy, and the unpredictable chemistry that emerges when idols and actors are forced to manage daily operations together.

The series quickly became a fan‑favorite for its light‑hearted take on hospitality, its stunning island scenery, and the genuine moments of camaraderie among the cast. It also earned solid viewership numbers on Netflix, ranking in the platform’s top‑10 non‑English shows in several regions during its debut week. The success prompted Netflix to green‑light a second season, expanding the cast to include Lee Junho, actress‑singer Kim Yeon‑koung, and former IZ*ONE member Kazuha, among others.

Lee Junho, whose real name is Lee Jun‑ho, debuted in 2005 with SS501 and later built a reputable acting career with lead roles in dramas such as *The Heirs*, *My Love from the Star* (special cameo), and *While You Were Sleeping*. He is also known for his charismatic variety appearances, most notably on *Running Man* and *Law of the Jungle*. His involvement in “Kian’s Bizarre B&B” marks his first major foray into a travel‑hospitality variety format, adding a fresh dynamic to the ensemble.

## What Happened

During a recent interview with Soompi, Lee Junho opened up about his experience filming Season 2, emphasizing the “hard work” required to keep the guesthouse running smoothly. He described long days of cleaning, cooking, and greeting guests, noting that the physical labor was far more demanding than his usual acting schedules. “I was surprised how much effort goes into even the smallest details—making the beds, preparing meals, and ensuring every guest feels welcome,” Junho said.

His diligence did not go unnoticed. Kian84, the show’s mastermind, publicly praised Junho on social media, calling him “the most reliable member of the team.” Kim Yeon‑koung echoed the sentiment, highlighting Junho’s willingness to step in whenever a task needed an extra pair of hands, even when it meant staying late after filming wrapped. Kazuha, the former IZ*ONE idol, added that Junho’s calm demeanor helped keep the group’s morale high during hectic moments.

Season 2 is set to premiere on Netflix worldwide on **October 12, 2024**, with new episodes dropping weekly. The series will continue to be available on all major Netflix regions, including the US, UK, Canada, and Australia, making it easily accessible for international fans. No additional platforms have been announced, but the show’s popularity suggests it may also be featured in Netflix’s “K‑Pop Variety” promotional banners.

## Why This Matters

Lee Junho’s involvement brings several layers of significance to the series. First, his reputation as a hardworking idol‑actor adds credibility to the show’s premise of “real work” in hospitality. Fans of SS500 and Korean dramas now have a fresh reason to tune in, expanding the show’s audience beyond variety‑show enthusiasts.

Second, the praise from fellow cast members underscores a growing trend in Korean variety programming: the celebration of work ethic and teamwork over mere star power. In an industry where scripted drama often dominates, “Kian’s Bizarre B&B” offers a genuine glimpse into the effort behind service‑industry jobs, resonating with viewers who appreciate authenticity.

Third, the series continues Netflix’s strategy of investing in niche Korean content that blends travel, lifestyle, and K‑pop culture. By securing high‑profile participants like Junho, the platform strengthens its position against competitors such as Disney+ and Prime Video, which have yet to launch comparable Korean variety titles. The show’s strong performance on Netflix’s global charts also contributes to the broader Hallyu wave, reinforcing Korean entertainment’s export power.

## What Fans Can Expect

Fans can look forward to a blend of familiar island scenery and new challenges for the cast. Season 2 introduces a “local cuisine” episode where Junho leads a cooking segment, showcasing traditional Korean dishes with a twist. There will also be a “guest surprise” segment featuring surprise visits from other K‑pop idols, adding an extra layer of excitement.

Beyond the episodes, Netflix has hinted at exclusive behind‑the‑scenes clips and a possible interactive fan‑Q&A session after the season finale, where viewers can ask Junho and the rest of the cast about their favorite moments. While ticketed live events are not part of the show’s format, fans can stay updated on any pop‑up fan meetings through the official Netflix social channels.
