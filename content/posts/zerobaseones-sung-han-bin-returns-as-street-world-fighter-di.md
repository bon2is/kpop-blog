## What Happened

On July 14, Mnet announced that Sung Han Bin would return as the MC of Street World Fighter: Directors' War, following his successful stint on World of Street Woman Fighter. This news was met with enthusiasm from fans, who are eager to see him bring his energy and charisma to the new season. Street World Fighter: Directors' War is the latest expansion of the Street Woman Fighter series, promising to deliver high-quality performances, intense competition, and a fresh perspective on the world of dance. The show's format, which involves directors competing against each other, is expected to bring a new level of excitement and unpredictability to the series.

His dedication to his craft and his passion for the show are evident in his words, and fans are excited to see him in action once again.
