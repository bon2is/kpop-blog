## Our Take

This plot twist will likely increase the drama's viewership as fans are eager to see how Han Ji Min's character navigates her new living situation. 

 The K-Pop and K-Drama industries often feature storylines that focus on family and relationships, making this show relatable to a wide audience. 

 Han Ji Min's decision to live with Park Sung Hoon will probably lead to comedic moments and heartwarming interactions between the two characters. 

 As the story unfolds, fans can expect to see character growth and development, particularly in Han Ji Min's portrayal of Lee Ui Yeong.
