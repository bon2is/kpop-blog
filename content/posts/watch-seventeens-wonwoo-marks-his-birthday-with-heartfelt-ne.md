SEVENTEEN's Wonwoo has given fans a special gift on his 30th birthday, releasing a heartfelt new song titled 'Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter'. By sharing this song, Wonwoo not only celebrates his own birthday but also expresses his gratitude to the fans who have been with him throughout his journey.

## Background

SEVENTEEN is a South Korean boy group formed by Pledis Entertainment in 2015. The group consists of thirteen members: S.Coups, Jeonghan, Joshua, Jun, Hoshi, Wonwoo, Woozi, DK, Mingyu, The8, Seungkwan, Vernon, and Dino. Wonwoo, whose real name is Kim Wonwoo, is one of the group's main rappers and has been an integral part of SEVENTEEN's music and performances since the group's debut. With a career spanning over seven years, SEVENTEEN has released numerous successful albums and singles, including 'Adore U', 'Mansae', 'Don't Wanna Cry', and 'Left & Right', showcasing their versatility and talent in the K-Pop scene.

SEVENTEEN has been known for their energetic performances, catchy songs, and heartfelt ballads. The group has won numerous awards, including several Melon Music Awards, Mnet Asian Music Awards, and Gaon Chart Music Awards. Their achievements are a testament to their hard work, dedication, and the love they receive from their fans, known as CARATs. Wonwoo, as a member of SEVENTEEN, has been part of this journey, contributing his rap skills and charming stage presence to the group's success.

This heartfelt track is a departure from SEVENTEEN's usual upbeat and energetic style, showcasing Wonwoo's ability to convey deep emotions through his music. The song reflects on the passing of time and the memories made with loved ones through every season, making it a poignant and relatable listen. By releasing this song on his 30th birthday, Wonwoo shares a personal and intimate moment with his fans, expressing his gratitude for the love and support he has received throughout his career.
