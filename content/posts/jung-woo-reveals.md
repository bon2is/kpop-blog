Jung Woo's 'Audition 109' is a highly anticipated film, especially given its connection to his earlier work 'Wish'. 

 The poster's release sparks excitement among fans, who are eager to see Jung Woo's portrayal of a determined and passionate individual. 

 This film's themes of perseverance and chasing one's dreams are likely to resonate with audiences, particularly in the context of the competitive K-Pop and K-Drama industries. 

 As 'Audition 109' gears up for its release, fans can expect a heartfelt and inspiring story that highlights Jung Woo's acting prowess and dedication to his craft.
