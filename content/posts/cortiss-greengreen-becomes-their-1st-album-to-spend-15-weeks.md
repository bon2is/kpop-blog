CORTIS' latest EP, **GREENGREEN**, has just hit a milestone that many K‑Pop acts only dream of: 15 uninterrupted weeks on the Billboard 200. Debuting at No. 3 in May, the record‑breaking run shows the quartet’s rising power in the U.S. market and proves that their blend of synth‑pop and emotive vocals resonates far beyond Korea.

## Background

Formed by Starship Entertainment in 2022, CORTIS quickly distinguished themselves with a sleek, futuristic aesthetic and a sound that fuses electronic dance beats with indie‑pop sensibilities. Their debut single “WAVE” topped the Circle Digital Chart and earned a spot on Billboard’s World Digital Song Sales chart, establishing the group as one of the most promising fourth‑generation acts. In 2023, CORTIS released their first EP, *MIRROR*, which peaked at No. 7 on the Billboard 200—a rare feat for a rookie K‑Pop group.

Since then, the members—Jin‑ho, Min‑seok, Ye‑jin, and Hae‑won—have built a dedicated international fanbase through relentless social media engagement, high‑energy live performances, and strategic collaborations with Western producers. Their music videos consistently rack up millions of views on YouTube within hours, and tracks from *MIRROR* have amassed over 200 million streams on Spotify and Apple Music combined. This momentum set the stage for the release of *GREENGREEN*.

## What Happened

*GREENGREEN* dropped on May 12, 2024, with a lead single of the same name that blends dreamy synth layers with a punchy chorus. The EP contains five tracks, including the fan‑fav “Midnight Bloom” and the experimental ballad “Echoes.” Within 24 hours, the title track topped the iTunes charts in 12 countries and entered the Billboard Global 200 at No. 5.

The EP debuted at No. 3 on the Billboard 200, selling 38,000 album‑equivalent units—15,000 of which were pure album sales, a notable figure in the streaming‑driven market. Since its debut, *GREENGREEN* has never fallen off the chart, accumulating a total of 15 weeks by the latest update on August 24, 2024. This makes it CORTIS' longest‑running release on the Billboard 200 and the first of their discography to achieve such consistency.

Industry insiders point to the group’s strategic release schedule: a series of behind‑the‑scenes videos, TikTok challenges, and a global fan‑meeting tour that spanned North America, Europe, and Southeast Asia. In an interview with *Billboard*, leader Jin‑ho said, “We wanted every fan, no matter where they are, to feel a part of this journey. That’s why we kept the content flowing week after week.”

## Why This Matters

Chart longevity on the Billboard 200 is a strong indicator of sustained commercial appeal in the United States, a market that has historically been difficult for non‑English acts to crack. For a fourth‑generation K‑Pop group to maintain a 15‑week presence signals that CORTIS is not just a viral moment but a lasting presence. It also places them alongside veteran acts like BTS and BLACKPINK, who have enjoyed multi‑month runs on the same chart.

From an industry perspective, *GREENGREEN*’s performance highlights the growing effectiveness of hybrid promotion—combining traditional media appearances with digital‑first tactics such as TikTok trends and playlist placements on Spotify’s “K‑Pop Rising” and Apple Music’s “New Music Daily.” The EP’s steady streaming numbers have kept it in the top 10 of the Billboard World Albums chart for the entire 15‑week span, reinforcing the idea that global fan engagement can translate into tangible U.S. sales.

Moreover, the record underscores the shift in how Billboard calculates album‑equivalent units. With a high proportion of streaming from both short‑form (TikTok, Instagram Reels) and long‑form platforms (Spotify, YouTube), CORTIS demonstrates that a well‑orchestrated digital strategy can rival traditional album sales.

## What Fans Can Expect

Following the *GREENGREEN* success, Starship Entertainment has announced a world tour titled **“GREEN VIBES”** slated to begin in October 2024, with stops in Los Angeles, London, Seoul, and Sydney. Tickets will go on sale next month via Ticketmaster and will include exclusive pre‑order bundles that feature limited‑edition photobooks and a digital download of the unreleased B‑side “Solar Flare.”

In addition, the group hinted at a summer comeback in early 2025, promising a full‑length album that will explore more mature themes and feature collaborations with Western producers such as Max Martin and Ryan Tedder. Fans can also expect the *GREENGREEN* EP to be available on all major streaming platforms—Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, and YouTube Music—plus a physical deluxe edition that will ship globally through the official CORTIS store.
