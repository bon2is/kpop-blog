CRAVITY’s rising star Serim will be unable to attend the highly anticipated fan meeting in Qingdao after the sudden passing of his grandfather. The news, confirmed by STARSHIP Entertainment on August 28, has sparked an outpouring of sympathy across social media, with fans rallying to support the idol during his time of grief.

## Background

Formed by STARSHIP Entertainment in 2020, CRAVITY quickly earned a reputation for powerful performances and a diverse musical palette that blends EDM, hip‑hop, and pop-rock influences. The nine‑member boy group—Serim, Allen, Jungmo, Woobin, Wonjin, Minhee, Hyeongjun, Taeyoung, and Seongmin—debuted with the EP *Season 1. Hideout: Remember Who We Are* and has since amassed a dedicated global fanbase known as LUVITY. Their discography includes chart‑topping releases such as *Season 2. Hideout: The New Day We Step Into* (2021) and *The Awakening: Written in the Stars* (2023), both of which placed within the top ten of South Korea’s Circle Album Chart and entered Billboard’s World Albums chart.

CRAVITY’s recent activities have centered on a world tour that began in early 2024, covering North America, Europe, and Asia. The group’s energetic stage shows and tight vocal harmonies have earned them spots on major festival line‑ups and nominations at the 2024 Mnet Asian Music Awards. In addition to touring, the members have been active on variety shows and YouTube, where their behind‑the‑scenes content garners millions of views, further expanding their international reach.

Fan meetings have become a cornerstone of CRAVITY’s interaction strategy, offering LUVITY a chance to meet members up close, participate in games, and hear exclusive performances. The upcoming Qingdao fan meeting, scheduled for early September, was advertised as a “special summer celebration” with a limited‑edition photo book and a live‑stream component for fans unable to travel.

## What Happened

On August 28, STARSTAR Entertainment released an official statement: “Hello, this is STARSHIP Entertainment. We regret to inform you that CRAVITY member Serim’s grandfather has passed away. As a result, Serim will unfortunately be unable to attend the upcoming fan meeting in Qingdao.” The agency added that the group’s schedule would proceed as planned, with the remaining eight members participating in the event. No further details about a possible reschedule for Serim’s segment were provided.

The announcement quickly circulated on Korean entertainment portals such as Soompi, Allkpop, and the agency’s own social media channels. Fans responded with an outpouring of condolence messages, using hashtags like #SerimCondolences and #RestInPeaceGrandfather. Several fan‑run accounts organized virtual candle‑lighting ceremonies on Twitter and Instagram, while others compiled supportive video montages to be played during the Qingdao event.

Serim, known for his charismatic stage presence and strong vocal line, has been a pivotal figure in CRAVITY’s public image. In past interviews, he has spoken about the importance of family and the influence of his grandfather on his musical aspirations. Though no direct quote was released regarding his personal feelings, the agency’s statement emphasized respect for his privacy during this difficult period.

## Why This Matters

The incident underscores the delicate balance K‑pop agencies must maintain between rigorous promotional schedules and the personal well‑being of their artists. While fan meetings are lucrative—often selling out within minutes and generating additional merchandise sales—the health and emotional state of idols remain a growing concern among industry observers.

From a commercial perspective, CRAVITY’s Qingdao fan meeting is part of a broader Asian tour that supports the group’s latest EP, *Infinity: The Dream*. The event is expected to draw thousands of attendees, with tickets sold through platforms like Ticketmaster China and local ticketing services. The absence of Serim may affect fan sentiment, but the agency’s transparent communication aims to mitigate potential backlash and preserve the group’s reputation.

Historically, similar situations have occurred with other idols—most notably when EXO’s Kai missed a concert due to a family emergency, or when NCT’s Taeyong postponed a showcase for health reasons. In each case, agencies have faced scrutiny over scheduling flexibility, prompting a gradual shift toward more humane management practices, such as allowing members to take bereavement leave without penalty.

## What Fans Can Expect

Despite Serim’s absence, the Qingdao fan meeting will proceed with the remaining members delivering a full program. Fans can anticipate a mix of live performances of recent hits like “Flame” and “Hush,” interactive games, and a Q&A session. The event will also be streamed live on CRAVITY’s official YouTube channel and V Live, providing international fans the chance to participate virtually.

Ticket holders who purchased packages that included a meet‑and‑greet with Serim will receive a refund or a credit toward future events, according to the agency’s FAQ. STARSHIP also announced that a special tribute segment will be aired during the fan meeting, featuring a video montage of Serim’s past moments with LUVITY and a heartfelt message from the group.

Looking ahead, CRAVITY is slated to continue its world tour in October, with stops in Seoul, Los Angeles, and London. The group’s schedule remains intact, and Serim is expected to rejoin the lineup once he has taken the necessary time to mourn and support his family.
