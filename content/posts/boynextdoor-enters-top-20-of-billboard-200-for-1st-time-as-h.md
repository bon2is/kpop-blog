BOYNEXTDOOR has made history by entering the top 20 of the Billboard 200 chart for the first time with their latest studio album 'HOME'. This impressive feat is a testament to the group's dedication, hard work, and the unwavering support of their fans. As the K-Pop industry continues to expand its global reach, BOYNEXTDOOR's achievement serves as a shining example of the genre's growing influence and popularity.


## Background


BOYNEXTDOOR is a South Korean boy group formed by KOZ Entertainment in 2022. The group consists of six members: Sunwoo, Riwoo, Jaehyun, Taesan, Woonhak, and Hyunjun. Although they are a relatively new group, BOYNEXTDOOR has already gained significant attention for their unique sound, which blends genres such as pop, rock, and hip-hop. Their debut single 'BOYNEXTDOOR' was released in May 2022, followed by their first EP 'WHO!' in September of the same year. Since then, the group has been actively promoting their music, releasing several successful singles and EPs, including 'Be My Lover' and 'I'm Da One'.


BOYNEXTDOOR's music style is characterized by their energetic and addictive melodies, combined with meaningful lyrics that often focus on themes of youth, love, and self-empowerment. The group's members are also known for their impressive vocal and dance skills, which have been showcased in their high-energy performances and music videos. With their growing popularity, BOYNEXTDOOR has been able to collaborate with other notable artists and participate in various music shows and festivals, further solidifying their position in the K-Pop scene.


In recent months, BOYNEXTDOOR has been gearing up for their comeback with the release of their new studio album 'HOME'. The album features a total of 12 tracks, including the title song 'One and Only', which showcases the group's signature sound and style. The album's lyrics explore themes of identity, belonging, and the search for one's true home, resonating with listeners of all ages and backgrounds. With 'HOME', BOYNEXTDOOR aims to take their music to the next level, experimenting with new sounds and genres while maintaining their unique identity.


## What Happened


On June 23, Billboard announced that BOYNEXTDOOR's new studio album 'HOME' had debuted at No. 16 on its Top 200 Albums chart. This achievement marks a significant milestone in the group's career, as it is their first time entering the top 20 of the Billboard 200 chart. The album's success can be attributed to the group's dedicated fan base, as well as their growing popularity in the US and other international markets. According to Billboard, 'HOME' achieved BOYNEXTDOOR's best US sales week yet, with over 20,000 equivalent album units earned in the week ending June 22.


The news of BOYNEXTDOOR's achievement was met with excitement and congratulations from fans and fellow artists alike. The group's members took to social media to express their gratitude and thank their fans for their unwavering support. The achievement is a testament to the group's hard work and dedication to their craft, as well as the power of K-Pop to transcend cultural and linguistic barriers. As BOYNEXTDOOR continues to make waves in the music industry, their fans can expect even more exciting releases and performances in the future.
