MBC's new drama 'Fifties Professionals' is generating significant buzz with its unique concept and talented cast. The latest stills released by the network showcase the drama's supporting cast, including Han Ji Eun, Hyun Bong Sik, and Kim Sang Ho, as they become entangled in different power groups. With its blend of action and comedy, 'Fifties Professionals' promises to be an exciting watch.

## Background

'Fifties Professionals' is an action-comedy that tells the story of three middle-aged men who are brought back into action by destiny. The drama follows the lives of these men, who were once at the top of their respective fields but are now exiled to a remote area. The cast includes talented actors such as Jung Woo, Kwon Yul, and Lee Kyu Hyung, who play the lead roles. Han Ji Eun, Hyun Bong Sik, and Kim Sang Ho are part of the supporting cast, bringing their own unique energy to the drama.

The lead actors have an impressive track record in the K-drama industry. Jung Woo is known for his roles in dramas like 'Reply 1988' and 'Youth Over Flowers', while Kwon Yul has appeared in 'Let's Eat' and 'Dalja's Spring'. Lee Kyu Hyung has also had notable roles in 'Prison Playbook' and 'Hi Bye, Mama!'. The combination of these talented actors is expected to create a compelling and engaging storyline.
