BLACKPINK’s Lisa has once again become the center of a global conversation, this time not for her dance moves or fashion but for a cryptic comment that has fans buzzing about a possible past romance with a BTS member. The surprise revelation, made during a recent interview, has sparked a wave of speculation across social media, Korean entertainment news sites, and fan communities worldwide.

## Background

Lisa, born Lalisa Manoban in 1997, debuted with BLACKPINK under YG Entertainment in August 2016. The Thai-born rapper, vocalist, and main dancer quickly rose to international stardom, becoming one of the most recognizable faces in K‑pop. Her solo debut "Lalisa" in September 2021 shattered YouTube records, amassing over 73 million views within 24 hours and cementing her status as a global icon. Beyond music, Lisa’s influence extends to fashion—she’s a brand ambassador for Celine, Saint Laurent, and Bulgari—and to social media, where she boasts over 90 million followers on Instagram.

BLACKPINK themselves have achieved unprecedented milestones: multiple Billboard Hot 100 entries, a number‑one spot on the Billboard 200 with "The Album" (2020), and a historic performance at the Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival in 2022. Their YouTube channel is the most‑subscribed for a K‑pop group, and each member’s solo projects continue to break streaming records on platforms like Spotify and Apple Music.

BTS, meanwhile, has dominated the global music landscape for nearly a decade. With seven Billboard Hot 100 number‑ones, a Grammy nomination, and record‑breaking album sales on the Circle (formerly Gaon) and Hanteo charts, the group has set a benchmark for K‑pop’s worldwide reach. Their members are known for close friendships within the group, but rumors of external relationships have historically been tightly guarded, given the industry’s strict dating policies.

## What Happened

During a televised interview on a popular Korean variety show (the exact program name was not disclosed in the source), Lisa responded to a question about past relationships with a brief, “I once had a boyfriend who was a member of another group.” The camera cut away before she could elaborate, but the comment was enough to ignite a firestorm. Korean entertainment outlet Koreaboo reported that the remark “sparked major reactions,” and fans immediately began connecting the dots, pointing to BTS as the most likely candidate due to the group’s massive global profile.

The interview clip quickly spread across YouTube, Twitter, and TikTok, gathering millions of views within hours. While the original broadcast aired on Korean television, the segment was uploaded to YouTube by the network’s official channel, making it accessible to international fans. No official statement from YG Entertainment or BTS’s label, Big Hit Music (now HYBE), has been released, and both parties have remained silent, fueling further speculation.

## Why This Matters

If the comment is indeed referencing a BTS member, it would be one of the few public acknowledgments of a cross‑group romance in the tightly managed K‑pop industry. Historically, dating rumors have led to intense scrutiny, sometimes resulting in contract renegotiations or hiatuses. For Lisa, who has built a brand around independence and artistic freedom, the revelation could add a new dimension to her public persona, potentially influencing future solo projects and endorsements.

For BTS, the implication could be equally significant. The group has consistently emphasized unity and a collective identity, and any hint of individual relationships outside the group could challenge that narrative. However, BTS members have also been praised for their openness about mental health and personal growth, suggesting that a respectful acknowledgment could be handled with maturity.

From an industry standpoint, the story highlights the shifting power of fan‑driven narratives. In the age of YouTube and TikTok, a single off‑hand comment can dominate headlines worldwide within minutes, prompting media outlets to chase the story before official confirmations appear. This rapid cycle underscores how K‑pop agencies now must balance transparency with protecting their artists’ privacy.

## What Fans Can Expect

In the coming weeks, fans will likely scour every interview, live stream, and social media post for additional clues. Watch for Lisa’s upcoming appearances on platforms like YouTube and Instagram Live, where she often interacts directly with fans. Additionally, any new BLACKPINK or solo releases from Lisa could contain lyrical hints or visual Easter eggs that fans will dissect.

If the speculation proves true, both YG and HYBE may release carefully crafted statements to manage the narrative. Until then, the story will dominate trending topics on Twitter, Reddit’s r/kpop, and fan‑run Discord servers. For those eager to follow every development, subscribing to the official YouTube channels of YG Entertainment and HYBE, as well as setting alerts for Korean entertainment news on platforms like Naver and Soompi, will ensure you don’t miss any official updates.
