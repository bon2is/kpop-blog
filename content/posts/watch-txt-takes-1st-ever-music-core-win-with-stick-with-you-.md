## What Happened

On the April 25 episode of MBC's 'Music Core', TXT competed against AKMU and PLAVE for the top spot. The candidates for first place were AKMU's 'Joy, Sorrow, A Beautiful Heart', TXT's 'Stick With You', and PLAVE's 'Born Savage'. After a tense competition, TXT ultimately took the win with a total of 8,308 points, marking their first-ever win on the show. The group's victory was met with excitement and congratulations from fans and fellow artists, who took to social media to express their support and admiration.
