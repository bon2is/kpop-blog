ENHYPEN's Sunghoon recently made a fan call referencing 'OT6', which has sparked divided reactions among netizens following Heeseung's departure from the group. The move has been met with both support and criticism. Netizens are discussing the implications of this statement on the group's dynamics and fan community.
---

## Our Take

The fan call has ignited a heated debate about the concept of 'OT6' and its relevance to ENHYPEN's current situation. 

 ENHYPEN's fanbase is split, with some interpreting Sunghoon's statement as a sign of loyalty to the original lineup, while others see it as insensitive to Heeseung's departure. 

 This controversy highlights the complexities of group dynamics and the challenges that K-Pop groups face when dealing with lineup changes. 

 The reaction to Sunghoon's fan call also underscores the passionate engagement of ENHYPEN's fanbase, who are invested in the group's narrative and eager to discuss its developments.
