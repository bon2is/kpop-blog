## Our Take

The news of VIVIZ, Lee Mujin, and BE'O terminating their contracts with Big Planet Made Entertainment matters because it highlights the evolving dynamics between K-Pop artists and their management agencies. 

 This move by the artists reflects the growing trend of K-Pop idols taking control of their careers and seeking better opportunities that align with their artistic visions and personal goals. 

 In the context of the K-Pop industry, contract terminations are not uncommon, but they often signify significant changes in an artist's career trajectory. For VIVIZ, Lee Mujin, and BE'O, this could mean exploring new musical directions or collaborating with different agencies that can offer them the support and resources they need to thrive.
