Hello82 has turned up the heat on the U.S. K‑Pop festival circuit by unveiling the full lineup for 82WORLD: LA, a two‑day, fan‑built extravaganza slated for October 17‑18, 2026 at the historic LA State Historic Park. The announcement follows a buzz‑building early‑bird sale that sold out within hours, signaling massive demand from the global fan community.

## Background

Hello82 began as a grassroots movement in 2022, aiming to give K‑Pop fans a voice in curating large‑scale events outside of Korea. After a successful pop‑up showcase in Seoul and a modest pilot festival in New York last year, the organization launched its flagship project, 82WORLD, a series of fan‑driven festivals that blend live performances, interactive booths, and cultural experiences. The “82” refers to the iconic $82 early‑bird ticket price that sparked a viral frenzy on social media, proving that fans are eager to invest in immersive, community‑centric events.

The U.S. market has seen a surge in K‑Pop festivals, from KCON’s multi‑city tours to the massive “K‑Pop World Tour” hosted by SM Entertainment. However, most of these events are curated solely by agencies or promoters, leaving little room for fan input. Hello82’s model flips that script: fans vote on lineups, suggest stage concepts, and even help design merch. This democratic approach has resonated strongly, especially among younger fans who crave a sense of ownership over the culture they love.

In addition to the festival itself, Hello82 has partnered with local venues, food vendors, and cultural institutions to create a “K‑Pop village” atmosphere. Attendees can expect Korean street food, language workshops, and exclusive photo zones—making 82WORLD more than just a concert series, but a cultural immersion.

## What Happened

On Tuesday, September 10, Hello82 released the official roster for 82WORLD: LA. The lineup balances chart‑topping superstars with up‑and‑coming acts, ensuring a broad appeal. Headliners include BLACKPINK, who will close the first night, and Stray Kids, slated to headline the second night. Supporting performers feature ATEEZ, (G)I‑DLE, ENHYPEN, and soloist IU, alongside rising groups like STAYC, TXT, and the newly debuted girl group LE SSERAFIM’s sub‑unit. International collaborations are also on the bill, with K‑Pop‑influenced EDM act KREAM and a surprise appearance by American pop‑rock band Imagine Dragons slated for a crossover stage.

The festival will occupy the expansive grounds of LA State Historic Park, offering multiple stages, a main arena, and intimate “pop‑up” venues for fan‑talks and workshops. Tickets went on sale in three tiers: the $82 early‑bird, a $150 general admission, and a $300 VIP package that includes backstage access, exclusive merch, and a meet‑and‑greet lounge. According to Hello82’s CEO, Jae‑Hyun Lee, “We wanted to keep the pricing accessible while rewarding the most dedicated fans with unique experiences. The early‑bird price proved that fans will rally behind a community‑first vision.”

Promotional videos released on YouTube and Instagram showcase snippets of rehearsals, behind‑the‑scenes planning meetings, and fan reactions to the lineup reveal. The teaser has already amassed over 2 million views, and the hashtag #82WORLDLA is trending across Twitter and TikTok, with fans posting countdown videos and outfit ideas.

## Why This Matters

82WORLD: LA marks a pivotal moment for fan‑driven festivals in the K‑Pop ecosystem. By giving fans a decisive role in lineup selection, Hello82 challenges the traditional top‑down model that agencies have dominated for decades. If successful, this could inspire other promoters to adopt more participatory approaches, potentially reshaping how tours and festivals are booked worldwide.

From an industry standpoint, the inclusion of both established acts like BLACKPINK—who recently topped the Billboard Hot 100 with “Pink Venom”—and newer groups such as LE SSERAFIM underscores the growing depth of K‑Pop’s global reach. The festival also serves as a strategic platform for agencies to test new material ahead of official comebacks, as several groups have hinted that they will debut unreleased songs during their sets.

Economically, the event is projected to generate over $15 million in local revenue, according to a preliminary impact study by the Los Angeles Convention & Visitors Bureau. The festival’s timing—mid‑October—fills a seasonal gap in major U.S. K‑Pop events, providing fans with a high‑profile gathering before the holiday concert rush.

## What Fans Can Expect

Beyond the music, 82WORLD: LA promises a suite of fan‑centric experiences. The “K‑Pop Academy” will host language lessons, dance workshops led by choreographers from the featured groups, and panels discussing the evolution of K‑Pop aesthetics. A dedicated “Merch Market” will feature limited‑edition apparel co‑designed by fans, including a collaborative line between BLACKPINK and local streetwear brand Supreme.

Ticket holders can also look forward to a digital companion app that streams live backstage cams, offers real‑time setlist updates, and provides a virtual meet‑and‑greet for those unable to attend in person. While the main performances will be streamed exclusively on Hello82’s YouTube channel after the festival, VIP attendees will receive a private link to an on‑demand archive on the platform, ensuring fans worldwide can relive the experience.
