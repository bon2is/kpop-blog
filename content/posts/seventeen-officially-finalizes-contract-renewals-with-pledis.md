SEVENTEEN, one of the most beloved and successful K-Pop groups of the past decade, has made a monumental decision that will undoubtedly thrill their devoted fan base, CARATs. As announced at their 10th anniversary fan meeting event last April, all members of SEVENTEEN have officially renewed their contracts with PLEDIS Entertainment, the agency that has guided them since their debut in 2015. This significant development marks a new chapter in the group's illustrious career, filled with promise and anticipation for what's to come.


## Background


SEVENTEEN, composed of 13 members - S.Coups, Jeonghan, Joshua, Jun, Hoshi, Wonwoo, Woozi, DK, Mingyu, The8, Seungkwan, Vernon, and Dino - has been a powerhouse in the K-Pop scene since their debut with the single 'Adore U' in 2015. Over the years, they have consistently pushed the boundaries of musical excellence, experimenting with a wide range of genres and styles. From the energetic and addictive 'Pretty U' and 'Mansae' to the more mature and introspective 'Home' and 'Left & Right', SEVENTEEN has showcased their versatility and depth, appealing to a broad audience and garnering numerous awards and accolades. Their recent releases, such as 'Attacca' and 'Face the Sun', have further solidified their position as one of the leading K-Pop groups, with 'Face the Sun' topping various music charts and achieving impressive sales figures.


The group's history is marked by dedication, hard work, and a strong bond between members, which has been a key factor in their success. SEVENTEEN has always been known for their tight-knit unity, often referring to themselves as a 'team' rather than just a group. This camaraderie has allowed them to navigate the challenges of the competitive K-Pop industry, supporting each other through the highs and lows. Their commitment to their craft and to their fans has earned them a loyal following worldwide, with CARATs eagerly anticipating each new release and project.


PLEDIS Entertainment, founded in 2007, has played a pivotal role in SEVENTEEN's journey, providing the necessary support and resources for the group to flourish. The agency has a reputation for nurturing talented artists, with SEVENTEEN being one of their most successful acts. The decision by all members to renew their contracts reflects the trust and satisfaction they have in the agency's management and vision for their future.


## What Happened


On July 13, PLEDIS Entertainment released an official statement confirming that all 13 members of SEVENTEEN have renewed their contracts. This announcement follows the group's 10th anniversary fan meeting event in April, where they first shared their intention to continue with the agency. The statement from PLEDIS Entertainment expressed gratitude to the fans for their unwavering support and announced the agency's commitment to supporting SEVENTEEN's future endeavors, promising to work tirelessly to ensure the group's continued success and growth. While specific details about the contract renewal, such as the duration of the new contracts, were not disclosed, the news has been met with excitement and relief from fans, who were eager to learn about the group's plans.


The contract renewal is a significant development for both SEVENTEEN and PLEDIS Entertainment, marking a new era in their partnership. It signals the group's confidence in the agency's ability to guide them through the ever-evolving K-Pop landscape, ensuring they remain at the forefront of the industry. For PLEDIS Entertainment, retaining SEVENTEEN is a major achievement, underscoring the agency's capability to foster long-term relationships with their artists and support their artistic visions.
