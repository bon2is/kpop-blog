BTS has broken their personal record for first-week sales with their new album 'ARIRANG', selling 3,981,507 copies on its first day alone. This surpasses their previous record, showcasing the group's enduring popularity. The album's title track 'SWIM' has also topped charts worldwide, further solidifying BTS's global influence.
