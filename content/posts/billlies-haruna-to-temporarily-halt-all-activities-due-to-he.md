Haruna, the charismatic vocalist and dancer of rising K‑pop girl group Billlie, has been placed on a temporary hiatus due to health concerns, prompting an outpouring of support from fans worldwide. Mystic Story confirmed the news on August 22, emphasizing the agency’s priority to safeguard her well‑being before any further promotions.

## Background

Billlie debuted in November 2021 under Mystic Story, quickly gaining a reputation for their eclectic sound that blends dream‑pop, synth‑wave, and experimental R&B. The seven‑member lineup—Siyoon, Moon Sua, Suhyeon, Haram, Tsuki, and Haruna—has been praised for their distinctive concepts and strong vocal harmonies. Their debut EP *The Billage of Perception* topped the Circle Album Chart, and the follow‑up *The Billage of Vision: Chapter One* secured a spot on Billboard’s World Albums chart, signaling their growing global footprint.

Haruna, born in 2001, joined the group after training for several years at Mystic’s academy. Known for her ethereal tone and sharp dance lines, she quickly became a fan favorite, often highlighted in variety shows and live performances. In 2023, Billlie’s single “GingaMingaYo (the strange world)” earned a place on the Billboard Global Excl. US chart, and the group’s YouTube view counts have consistently surpassed the million‑mark within days of release, cementing their status as an emerging act with international appeal.

## What Happened

Mystic Story released an official statement on August 22, stating: “We are announcing that Billlie member Haruna, an artist under our agency, will be halting all activities for the time being due to health reasons.” The agency did not disclose specific medical details, respecting Haruna’s privacy, but assured fans that she is receiving proper care and that the group’s upcoming schedules will be adjusted accordingly. The announcement appeared on the agency’s website and was simultaneously shared across Billlie’s official Twitter, Instagram, and Weverse accounts, where fans quickly began trending supportive hashtags.

While no concrete timeline for her return was provided, Mystic Story indicated that Haruna will re‑join activities when her health permits. In the meantime, Billlie will continue group promotions without her, focusing on pre‑recorded performances and digital content. The group’s upcoming reality show *Billlie: Dreamscape*—set to premiere on V Live and later on YouTube—will feature the remaining members, with the agency promising that Haruna’s presence will be felt through special video messages.

## Why This Matters

Haruna’s hiatus underscores the intense physical and mental demands placed on K‑pop idols, especially those in groups that emphasize intricate choreography and frequent comebacks. The industry has seen a rise in public discussions about artist welfare, with recent high‑profile cases—such as members of Aespa and Stray Kids—prompting agencies to adopt more transparent health policies. Mystic Story’s swift announcement and emphasis on recovery reflect a growing trend of prioritizing member health over relentless promotion cycles.

From a commercial standpoint, Billlie’s momentum on international charts means that any disruption could affect streaming numbers and ticket sales. However, the group’s loyal fanbase, known as *Billlie’s Fans* (or *Billlie’s Starlight*), has historically rallied behind members during similar challenges, often boosting digital sales and streaming counts as a show of solidarity. This phenomenon was observed when former member Tsuki took a short break in 2022, resulting in a surge of streams on Spotify and Apple Music.

Moreover, the news arrives just weeks before the group’s scheduled appearance at the *K‑Pop Wave* festival in Los Angeles, an event streamed live on YouTube and later available on Disney+. While Haruna will miss the live stage, the festival’s organizers have confirmed that Billlie will perform a shortened set, and a pre‑recorded segment featuring Haruna will be aired during the broadcast, ensuring her fans worldwide still get a glimpse of her talent.

## What Fans Can Expect

In the short term, Billlie will focus on group activities that do not require Haruna’s physical presence, such as variety show recordings, fan‑signed events (virtual), and the upcoming *Dreamscape* series. Fans can also look forward to exclusive behind‑the‑scenes footage released on the group’s YouTube channel, where Haruna’s candid moments and messages will be highlighted.

Looking ahead, the agency hinted at a possible solo project for Haruna once she recovers, a notion that has ignited speculation across fan forums. While no details are confirmed, industry insiders suggest that a solo single could be slated for a mid‑2025 release, potentially dropping on major platforms like Spotify, Apple Music, and Amazon Music. Until then, supporters are encouraged to stream Billlie’s existing discography, as increased streaming numbers often translate into better chart performance on Circle and Billboard, indirectly supporting the group’s overall visibility.
