The cast of 'Guardian: The Lonely and Great God', also known as 'Goblin', has concluded their 10th anniversary trip, leaving fans with a mix of emotions. The special program, created to celebrate the decade-long impact of the drama, brought together the main cast, including Gong Yoo, Kim Go-eun, Lee Dong-wook, and Yoo In-na, for a meaningful journey down memory lane. As the trip came to a close, the cast members shared heartfelt farewells, reminiscing about their time on set and the unforgettable experience of working together.

## Background

'Guardian: The Lonely and Great God' premiered in 2016 and quickly gained a massive following worldwide. The fantasy romance drama, written by Kim Eun-sook, told the story of a goblin and a high school student who fall in love, despite the danger and obstacles that surround them. The show's unique storyline, paired with its stunning visuals and memorable characters, captivated audiences and solidified its place as one of the most beloved K-dramas of all time. The cast, comprising talented actors Gong Yoo, Kim Go-eun, Lee Dong-wook, and Yoo In-na, delivered impressive performances that earned them widespread recognition and praise.

The drama's success can be attributed to the impeccable chemistry between the lead actors, as well as the show's thought-provoking themes and emotional depth. 'Guardian: The Lonely and Great God' explored complex issues such as mortality, sacrifice, and the consequences of playing with fate. The show's soundtrack, featuring songs by renowned artists like Crush, Lasse Lindh, and Ailee, also played a significant role in enhancing the viewing experience. As the drama approached its 10th anniversary, fans around the world eagerly awaited a reunion or special celebration, and tvN's 'Guardian: The Lonely and Great God 10th Anniversary' special did not disappoint.

The cast members have gone on to achieve great success in their respective careers, with Gong Yoo starring in films like 'Train to Busan' and 'Seobok', while Kim Go-eun appeared in dramas such as 'The King: Eternal Monarch' and 'Yumi's Cells'. Lee Dong-wook and Yoo In-na have also maintained a strong presence in the K-drama scene, with notable roles in 'Hell Is Other People' and 'Touch Your Heart', respectively. Despite their busy schedules, the cast came together for the 10th anniversary special, rekindling the magic that made 'Guardian: The Lonely and Great God' so beloved.

## What Happened

The 'Guardian: The Lonely and Great God 10th Anniversary' special took the cast on a meaningful trip, where they revisited memorable locations from the drama and shared behind-the-scenes stories. The program featured heartfelt interviews, hilarious games, and touching moments of reunion, giving fans an intimate look into the cast's relationships and experiences during filming. As the trip progressed, the cast members reflected on their time working together, expressing gratitude for the opportunity to have been a part of such an iconic drama. Gong Yoo, in particular, shared his appreciation for the show's impact on his career, stating that 'Guardian: The Lonely and Great God' marked a significant turning point in his life as an actor.

The special program also included special appearances by supporting actors and crew members, who shared their own stories and anecdotes about working on the drama. The cast's chemistry and camaraderie were palpable throughout the trip, as they laughed, cried, and reminisced together. As the anniversary trip came to a close, the cast members took to social media to express their heartfelt farewells, thanking fans for their unwavering support over the years. Kim Go-eun, in an emotional post, wrote about the drama's lasting impact on her life, stating that 'Guardian: The Lonely and Great God' will always hold a special place in her heart.
