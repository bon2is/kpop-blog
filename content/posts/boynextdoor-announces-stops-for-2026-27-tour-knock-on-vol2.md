## Background

BOYNEXTDOOR is a K-Pop group formed by KOZ Entertainment, a subsidiary of ADOR, a label under HYBE. The group consists of six members: Sunwoo, Riwo, Jaehyun, Taesan, Woonhak, and Hyeongjun. Since their debut, BOYNEXTDOOR has been making waves in the K-Pop industry with their unique sound, captivating music videos, and synchronized choreography. Their career highlights include the release of their debut single, 'Attention', and their first EP, 'Who!', which garnered significant attention from fans and critics alike. ## What Happened

On May 13, BOYNEXTDOOR announced their new 2026-27 tour, 'KNOCK ON Vol.2', which will kick off in Seoul and Busan, Korea. The tour will then head to various cities in Japan, including Kanagawa, Saga, Osaka, Miyagi, Nagano, and Chiba. BOYNEXTDOOR will also perform in major cities across the US, including Dallas, Pompano, Chicago, New York, and Los Angeles. Additionally, the group will visit Toronto and Vancouver in Canada, Mexico City in Mexico, and Jakarta and Kuala Lumpur in Indonesia. This extensive tour schedule demonstrates BOYNEXTDOOR's commitment to connecting with fans worldwide and sharing their music with a broader audience.
