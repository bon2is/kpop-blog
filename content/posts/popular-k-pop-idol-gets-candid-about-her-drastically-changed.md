Chungha’s recent appearance on KBS Radio’s Gabee’s Super Radio has set fans buzzing. The 30‑year‑old solo star opened up about the deliberate lifestyle changes that have reshaped her look, offering a rare glimpse into the personal side of aging in the hyper‑visual K‑Pop industry.

## Background

Born Kim Mi‑ju on February 9, 1996, Chungha first rose to fame as the top vote‑getter on Mnet’s survival show *Produce 101* (Season 1) in 2016, earning a spot in the project girl group I.O.I. After I.O.I’s disbandment, she launched a solo career under MNH Entertainment, debuting with the single “Why Don’t You Know” in June 2017. Over the past six years, Chungha has built a reputation for powerful choreography, versatile vocals, and a polished visual aesthetic.

Her discography includes hits such as “Roller Coaster,” “Snapping,” and the chart‑topping “Stay Tonight,” which peaked at #4 on the Gaon Digital Chart and entered Billboard’s World Digital Song Sales chart. She has also made notable strides on the Billboard 200, with her 2022 EP *Flourishing* debuting at #12, a first for a solo female K‑Pop artist in the United States. Beyond music, Chungha has become a fashion icon, fronting campaigns for brands like Dior and Calvin Klein, and appearing on variety shows ranging from *Running Man* to *Weekly Idol*.

In recent years, Chungha has faced the inevitable transition from her twenties into her thirties—a period that often brings heightened scrutiny of appearance for idols. While many peers have opted for cosmetic procedures or drastic image overhauls, Chungha’s recent radio confession suggests a more holistic approach.

## What Happened

During the September 2 episode of Gabee’s Super Radio, host Gabee asked Chungha directly about the noticeable shift in her facial contours and overall glow compared to her early‑career photos. Chungha responded that the change is not the result of any surgery but stems from a “new health regimen” she adopted after turning 30. She explained that she now prioritizes a balanced diet rich in antioxidants, regular yoga, and a consistent sleep schedule—habits she previously neglected due to relentless promotional schedules.

She also mentioned that her agency, MNH, encouraged her to take a short hiatus from intensive choreography to focus on “self‑care and mental well‑being.” Chungga shared a quote from her personal trainer: “When you respect your body, the confidence shows on camera.” The interview aired live on KBS Radio, and a replay is available on KBS’s official YouTube channel and the station’s website for fans who missed the original broadcast.

No concrete dates for upcoming releases were disclosed, but Chungha hinted that the newfound energy will feed into her next musical project, slated for later this year. She also thanked fans for their “unwavering support” and urged them to “embrace their own journeys, no matter the age.”

## Why This Matters

Chungha’s openness challenges a long‑standing K‑Pop narrative that idols must maintain a youthful, often artificially preserved image to stay relevant. By attributing her changed appearance to lifestyle choices rather than cosmetic alteration, she normalizes aging and self‑care for idols in their thirties—a demographic that is still relatively small in the industry.

The interview also arrives at a time when the global K‑Pop market is expanding, with more Western audiences embracing older solo acts. Billboard recently highlighted a trend of “30‑plus idols breaking into the U.S. charts,” citing artists like HyunA and Taeyeon. Chungha’s candidness may inspire agencies to support mature concepts rather than pushing younger‑only aesthetics, potentially reshaping promotional strategies.

From a commercial standpoint, Chungha’s transparent narrative can boost her brand partnerships. Companies focusing on wellness, skincare, and lifestyle (e.g., Amorepacific’s “Time‑Less” line) often seek authentic ambassadors. Her story aligns with consumer demand for genuine, relatable role models, which could translate into lucrative endorsement deals.

## What Fans Can Expect

Fans can look forward to Chungha’s next musical comeback, which she hinted will reflect her renewed mindset. While no title or release date has been confirmed, industry insiders speculate a summer 2024 EP that blends her signature dance‑pop with more introspective lyricism about growth and self‑acceptance.

In addition, MNH Entertainment is expected to roll out a series of behind‑the‑scenes content—potentially a mini‑documentary on her health journey—available on platforms like YouTube and V‑Live. This type of content not only deepens fan engagement but also serves as a subtle promotion for her upcoming music.

For those eager to revisit the interview, the full episode will be streamed on KBS Radio’s official website and YouTube channel, with subtitles in English expected within a week. Keep an eye on Chungha’s official Instagram and Twitter for announcements about the new release and any related live‑stream events.
