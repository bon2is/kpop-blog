IU is preparing to star in MBC's upcoming drama 'Perfect Crown' as Sung Hee Joo, a chaebol heiress in a modern-day Korea that is a constitutional monarchy. The drama tells the love story of Sung Hee Joo and a grand duke. IU shared her thoughts on why she chose to star in the drama and what makes her character unique.
---

## Our Take

IU's decision to star in 'Perfect Crown' is significant, as it marks her return to the small screen after a series of successful music releases. 

 The K-Pop industry has seen a surge in artists exploring acting roles, and IU's choice to star in 'Perfect Crown' is a testament to her versatility as an artist. 

 The drama's unique premise, set in an alternate universe, offers a fresh take on traditional romance dramas, and IU's character, Sung Hee Joo, is expected to bring a new level of complexity to the story. 

 With IU's massive fan base and the drama's intriguing plot, 'Perfect Crown' is highly anticipated, and fans are eagerly waiting to see IU bring her character to life on screen.
