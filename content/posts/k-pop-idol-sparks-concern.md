## Our Take

The K-Pop industry is known for its high beauty standards, often emphasizing slender figures and flawless appearances. 

 This incident underscores the potential health risks associated with extreme dieting and the pressure to conform to unrealistic beauty ideals. 

 For fans, this situation serves as a reminder of the importance of prioritizing idols' health and wellbeing over physical appearance. 

 The concern raised by this idol's condition may prompt a reevaluation of beauty standards and health support within the K-Pop industry.
