## Our Take

The premiere of 'The Scarecrow' is a significant milestone for Park Hae Soo, who has been gaining popularity in recent years. 

 The K-Drama industry is highly competitive, and achieving a No. 1 rating is a notable achievement, especially for a new series. 

 This success can be attributed to the talented cast, including Lee Hee Joon, and the intriguing storyline that has piqued the interest of audiences. 

 For fans of Park Hae Soo, this is an exciting development, as it cements his status as a leading actor in the K-Drama scene.
