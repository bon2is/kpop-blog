BTS has broken records for the highest 1st-day streams of any pop group or K-Pop artist in Apple Music history with their new album 'ARIRANG'. The album and its title track 'SWIM' were released on March 20, and by the next day, Apple Music announced that 'ARIRANG' had achieved this impressive feat. This milestone marks a significant achievement in BTS's career and a testament to their global popularity.
---

## Our Take

The news of BTS breaking records for the highest 1st-day streams is a significant indicator of the group's enduring popularity and influence in the music industry. 

 This achievement is particularly noteworthy given the highly competitive nature of the K-Pop industry, where numerous talented groups and artists continually strive to outdo one another. 

 The record-breaking streams of 'ARIRANG' will likely have a positive impact on BTS's career, further solidifying their position as one of the most successful K-Pop groups globally. 

 For fans, this news is a celebration of BTS's hard work and dedication to their craft, and it reinforces the special bond between the group and their loyal fan base, known as the ARMY.
