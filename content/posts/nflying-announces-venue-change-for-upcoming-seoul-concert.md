N.Flying, a popular South Korean rock band formed by FNC Entertainment, has been making waves in the K-Pop scene with their unique sound and energetic performances. Recently, the band announced a venue change for their upcoming Seoul concert, leaving fans eager to know more about the new location. The concert, titled 'LIVE ‘&CON5 : into REM’ IN SEOUL,' was initially set to take place at Ticketlink Live Arena from July 31 to August 2, 2026.


## Background


N.Flying is a South Korean rock band that debuted in 2013 under FNC Entertainment, a renowned entertainment company in Korea. The band consists of five members: Lee Seung-hyub, Cha Hun, Kim Jae-hyun, Yoo Hwe-seung, and Seo Dong-sung. Over the years, N.Flying has released several successful albums and singles, showcasing their musical versatility and talents. Their music style, which combines elements of rock, pop, and electronic genres, has captivated fans worldwide and set them apart from other K-Pop groups. In recent years, the band has been focusing on releasing new music and experimenting with different sounds, resulting in hits like 'Rooftop' and 'Oh Really.'.
