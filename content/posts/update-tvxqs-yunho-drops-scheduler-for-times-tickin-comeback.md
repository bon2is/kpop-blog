Yunho, in particular, has been praised for his versatility as a performer, showcasing his skills as a singer, rapper, and actor in various projects.


Yunho's solo career has been equally impressive, with his debut solo album 'True Colors' released in 2019 to critical acclaim. The album featured a mix of genres, from pop and rock to R&B and hip-hop, demonstrating Yunho's vocal range and artistic growth. As a solo artist, Yunho has collaborated with numerous musicians and producers, resulting in a distinctive sound that blends his TVXQ roots with modern styles and trends. His upcoming single album 'Time's Tickin'' is expected to continue this trend, offering fans a fresh and exciting musical experience.
