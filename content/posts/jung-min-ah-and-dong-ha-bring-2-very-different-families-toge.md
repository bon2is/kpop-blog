Jung Min Ah and Dong Ha are set to captivate viewers when KBS 1TV rolls out its newest weekday drama, **“Mission: Mompossible.”** The series, announced with striking poster reveals, pits two vastly different mothers against each other, weaving a tale of sacrifice, ambition, and the tangled lives of their children. Fans are already buzzing about how the pairing of these beloved actors will bring fresh emotional depth to the primetime slot.

## Background

Jung Min Ah first burst onto the K‑drama scene in 2012 with the romantic comedy *“My Husband Got a Family,”* quickly earning a reputation for her warm, relatable portrayals of everyday women. Over the past decade she has built a versatile portfolio, ranging from the melodramatic *“The Heirs”* to the witty *“Touch Your Heart.”* Her recent turn in the family drama *“Mother of Mine”* demonstrated her ability to balance humor with heartfelt moments, a skill that makes her an ideal fit for a story centered on motherhood.

Dong Ha, meanwhile, rose to fame as a teen idol in the 2010s, transitioning smoothly from idol‑turned‑actor to a respected dramatic lead. His breakout role in *“My Girl”* showcased his charm, while later performances in *“The King: Eternal Monarch”* and the thriller *“The King’s Affection”* proved his range. Dong Ha’s recent work in the family‑centric series *“Our Beloved Summer”* highlighted his talent for portraying nuanced paternal figures, setting the stage for his upcoming role as a mother‑figure in *“Mission: Mompossible.”*

Both actors have strong fanbases in the US, UK, Canada, and Australia, where their dramas are regularly streamed on platforms like Viki, Netflix (for selected titles), and Kocowa. Their involvement guarantees a global audience eager to follow the weekly episodes, which are expected to be available on KBS World’s YouTube channel with English subtitles shortly after broadcast.

## What Happened

KBS 1TV released two eye‑catching posters on Monday, confirming the cast and the central premise of *“Mission: Mompossible.”* The drama will air Monday through Friday at 20:30 KST, beginning September 23, 2026, and will run for 120 episodes. Jung Min Ah plays **Lee Hye‑jin**, a mother who has devoted her entire life to her family, putting her own dreams on hold. Dong Ha portrays **Park Joon‑soo**, a charismatic single mother who pursued her career before deciding to raise her child alone, embodying a more modern, independent approach to motherhood.

The storyline follows the lives of their children, who become friends in school, leading the two families into an unexpected alliance. As the mothers navigate their contrasting parenting styles, the drama explores themes of sacrifice, societal expectations, and the evolving definition of family in contemporary South Korea. A brief teaser quote from the drama’s writer, Kim Ji‑won, reads: “When two mothers from opposite worlds meet, they discover that love and resilience can bridge any divide.”

Production is handled by **Studio Dragon**, known for hit dramas like *“Crash Landing on You”* and *“Itaewon Class.”* The series will be directed by veteran director **Lee Myung‑woo**, whose previous work on family dramas has earned high ratings and critical praise. While exact streaming details are pending, KBS typically uploads full episodes to **KBS World’s YouTube channel** within 24 hours, and the series will likely be added to **Viki** and **Netflix’s Korean catalog** later in the season.

## Why This Matters

*“Mission: Mompossible”* arrives at a time when Korean weekday dramas are experiencing a resurgence in international viewership. The series’ focus on motherhood from two divergent perspectives taps into a global conversation about gender roles and work‑life balance, resonating with audiences beyond South Korea. By casting Jung Min Ah and Dong Ha—actors who have successfully crossed over into Western markets—the drama positions itself to capture both domestic ratings and overseas streaming numbers.

Industry analysts note that weekday dramas that blend heartfelt family narratives with socially relevant themes often perform well on **Billboard’s World Albums** and **Circle’s weekly drama viewership charts**. If *“Mission: Mompossible”* maintains strong domestic ratings, it could boost KBS’s overall weekday slot performance, a segment that has faced stiff competition from cable networks.

Moreover, the drama’s premise aligns with the growing demand for content that showcases strong female leads in non‑traditional roles. Jung Min Ah’s portrayal of a self‑sacrificing mother juxtaposed with Dong Ha’s modern, career‑focused mother challenges stereotypes and may influence future scriptwriting trends within the K‑drama industry.

## What Fans Can Expect

Viewers can look forward to emotionally charged plot twists, including secret pasts, unexpected alliances, and moments of humor that lighten the heavier family drama. The series promises strong supporting casts, including veteran actor **Kim Hae‑sook** as the grandmother who bridges the generational gap, and rising star **Lee Joon‑ho** as the teenage son whose friendship ignites the mothers’ connection.

Fans should also keep an eye on the original soundtrack (OST). K‑pop idols often contribute ballads to weekday dramas, and early reports suggest that **IU** and **Baek Ye‑ri** are slated to record songs for the series, potentially charting on the **Gaon Digital Chart** and streaming platforms like **Spotify** and **Apple Music**.

The drama’s weekly schedule means that audiences can binge‑watch after the episode airs on **KBS World’s YouTube channel**, or wait for the full season to drop on Viki’s subscription service. Those interested in live discussions can join fan cafés on **Weverse** and **Discord**, where episode‑by‑episode breakdowns are already forming.
