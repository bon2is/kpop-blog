Jungkook, the main vocalist and youngest member of the group, has been particularly popular among fans for his captivating stage presence and versatility as a performer. In recent years, BTS has released several successful albums, including 'Map of the Soul: 7' and 'Proof', which have topped charts globally and solidified the group's position as one of the most influential K-Pop acts.

BTS's career has been marked by numerous milestones, including their debut single 'No More Dream' in 2013, their first Billboard Music Award in 2017, and their historic performance at the Grammy Awards in 2020. The group's music often focuses on themes of self-empowerment, love, and social issues, resonating with fans from diverse backgrounds. As a result, BTS has built a dedicated fan base, known as the ARMY, which has been instrumental in promoting the group's music and message. However, with fame comes the downside of obsessive fan behavior, which can be detrimental to both the artists and the fans themselves.
