i-dle secured their first win for their latest title track, 'Mono (Feat. skaiwater),' on the February 5 episode of 'M Countdown.' The group triumphed over competitors, earning a total of 7,693 points to take home the trophy, marking a significant milestone for the group.

i-dle's victory for 'Mono' on 'M Countdown' represents not just a win in terms of points, but a testament to their enduring popularity and musical evolution. With this momentum, i-dle is likely to continue pushing boundaries, setting new standards for performance and artistry in the industry. Their success not only adds to their accolades but also reinforces the importance of fresh music and meaningful collaborations in sustaining a group's legacy.
