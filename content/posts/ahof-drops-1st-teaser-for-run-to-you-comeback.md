AHOF has officially kicked off the countdown to their summer comeback, and fans couldn't be more excited. On June 22 at midnight KST, the group released their first teaser for their upcoming return next month, titled 'RUN TO YOU'. This will be AHOF's third mini album, and it's set to drop on July 8 at 6 p.m. KST. The 'Universe League' boy group has been making waves in the K-Pop scene, and their summer comeback is expected to be one of the most highly anticipated releases of the season.

## Background

AHOF, short for 'Always Hope Of Freedom', is a South Korean boy group formed by KOZ Entertainment. The group consists of seven members: Jinsung, Jeongin, Yaebin, Hwan, Won, Min, and Siyoon. AHOF made their official debut on May 27, 2022, with their single album 'The Billage of Perception: Chapter One'. Since then, they have been steadily releasing new music and performing at various events. One of their most notable releases is their second mini album, 'The Billage of Perception: Chapter Two', which dropped in February 2023. The album featured a mix of genres, from hip-hop to EDM, and showcased the group's versatility and talent.

AHOF's music style is a unique blend of genres, with a focus on powerful vocals and intricate choreography. Their lyrics often touch on themes of hope, freedom, and self-empowerment, resonating with fans of all ages. The group's name, 'AHOF', represents their desire to bring hope and freedom to their listeners through their music. With their 'Universe League' concept, AHOF aims to create a sense of community and connection with their fans, encouraging them to join forces and support one another.

The group's career highlights include performances at major music festivals, such as the '2022 Seoul Music Festival', and collaborations with other notable K-Pop artists. AHOF has also been gaining attention for their impressive dance skills, with many fans praising their synchronized choreography and high-energy performances. As AHOF continues to grow and evolve as a group, fans are eager to see what they have in store for their summer comeback.
