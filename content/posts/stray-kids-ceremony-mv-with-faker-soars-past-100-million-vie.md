Stray Kids' music video for their track “CEREMONY” has officially surpassed 100 million views on YouTube as of February 1, making it another significant achievement for the group. The video is notable for featuring popular esports player Faker, which has likely contributed to its successful reach.

---

## Our Take

The achievement of Stray Kids reaching 100 million views with their music video for “CEREMONY” underscores their growing influence and popularity not just within the K-Pop scene but also in broader pop culture. Collaborating with a figure like Faker, a well-known esports icon, bridges the gap between music and gaming, two of the most consumed forms of entertainment today. This crossover appeal not only enhances Stray Kids’ visibility but also showcases the potential for synergies between different entertainment sectors. In an era where digital content is king, accumulating viewership numbers like this can significantly impact a group's marketability and fan engagement. The rapid growth in views also highlights the effectiveness of their marketing strategies and the dedicated fanbase they have cultivated. As K-Pop continues to expand globally, such partnerships may become more common, indicating a shift in how artists and brands approach audience engagement in a multimedia landscape.
