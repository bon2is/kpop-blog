MBC's upcoming drama “The Judge Returns” features Ji Sung as a corrupt judge who travels back in time to 2025, where he meets Oh Se Young's character. A sneak peek reveals a dramatic first encounter marked by a slap, highlighting the tension between the characters.

## Our Take

The premise of 'The Judge Returns' taps into a popular narrative device in K-Drama, where time travel allows for character development and moral reckoning. Ji Sung's role as a corrupt judge presents a compelling opportunity to explore themes of justice and redemption. The slap from Oh Se Young symbolizes a clash of values, emphasizing the internal and external conflicts both characters will face. This drama's focus on legal themes reflects a growing trend in K-Pop and K-Drama where societal issues are increasingly tackled, resonating with a young audience that seeks depth in entertainment. Moreover, Ji Sung's return to the small screen adds to his impressive catalog, and fans are eager to see how this narrative unfolds. As K-Pop and K-Drama continue to gain international traction, the success of shows like 'The Judge Returns' will likely influence global perceptions of Asian storytelling, proving that complex narratives can attract diverse audiences.
