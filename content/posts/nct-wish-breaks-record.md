NCT WISH has achieved over 1 million first-day sales with their new album 'Ode to Love', marking a career high for the group. The album's title track also reached a new peak on the Melon Top 100 chart. This milestone comes just one day into their comeback, showcasing the group's growing popularity.

 As NCT WISH continues to break records, fans can expect even more exciting projects from the group in the future, solidifying their position as one of the leading K-Pop acts.
