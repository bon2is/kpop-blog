Former NCT member Mark is embarking on a new journey with the launch of his own label, Upper Room, marking a significant milestone in his career. This move comes as no surprise, given Mark's passion for music and his desire for creative expression. With Upper Room, Mark is poised to take his career to new heights, exploring new sounds and collaborating with like-minded artists.




## Background
Mark, whose full name is Mark Lee, is a Canadian singer, rapper, and songwriter who rose to fame as a member of the popular K-Pop group NCT. Formed by SM Entertainment in 2016, NCT is known for its unique concept and rotational unit system, which allows members to participate in various sub-units and collaborations. Mark was an active member of NCT's sub-units NCT 127 and NCT U, and has been instrumental in the group's success, showcasing his versatility as a performer and songwriter.




Mark's career with NCT has been marked by numerous achievements, including the release of several successful albums and singles, such as "Superhuman" and "Kick It." He has also participated in various music shows, concerts, and variety programs, cementing his status as a talented and charismatic performer. In recent years, Mark has been focusing on his solo activities, releasing his own music and collaborating with other artists. The launch of Upper Room is a natural progression of his career, allowing him to take full control of his creative vision and pursue new opportunities.




As a member of NCT, Mark has been part of a larger cultural phenomenon, with the group's music and message resonating with fans worldwide. NCT's innovative approach to K-Pop has helped to push the genre's boundaries, experimenting with new sounds and styles. Mark's own music has reflected this eclecticism, incorporating elements of hip-hop, R&B, and electronic dance music into his songs. With Upper Room, Mark is poised to continue this trend, exploring new sounds and collaborating with artists from diverse backgrounds.




## What Happened
On June 4, Upper Room announced Mark's new venture, stating that the label would serve as the starting point for his music, creative vision, and various projects. The announcement was met with excitement from fans, who have been eagerly anticipating Mark's next move. Mark has also launched official social media channels for Upper Room, providing a platform for him to connect with fans and share updates about his projects. The launch of Upper Room marks a significant milestone in Mark's career, offering him the freedom to pursue his passions and create music on his own terms.
