## Our Take

The appeal of K-Dramas often lies in their ability to create relatable, heartwarming moments that resonate with audiences. In ‘Positively Yours’, Kang Du Jun's sweet gestures towards Jang Hui Won serve not only to develop their romantic storyline but also to highlight a growing trend in K-Dramas where male leads express their emotions more openly. This shift reflects a broader cultural change within the K-Pop and K-Drama industries, where vulnerability and emotional intelligence are increasingly celebrated, breaking away from traditional stoic masculinity. Furthermore, the popularity of such moments can be attributed to the rise of ‘healing dramas’ in recent years, which provide audiences with comfort and positivity amidst global uncertainties. As ‘Positively Yours’ continues to unfold, it will be interesting to see how these themes evolve and whether they spark new expectations for character development in future K-Dramas, making viewers crave not just romantic tension but also genuine emotional connections.
