## Our Take

MONSTA X's upcoming album, Unfold, is highly anticipated by fans and marks a significant milestone in the group's career. 

 The K-Pop industry has seen a surge in global popularity, and MONSTA X's English album is a strategic move to cater to their international fanbase. 

 This new era for MONSTA X is expected to bring a fresh sound and style, showcasing the group's growth and versatility as artists. 

 With Unfold, MONSTA X is poised to make a lasting impact on the music scene, and fans are eagerly awaiting the release to experience the group's new musical direction.
