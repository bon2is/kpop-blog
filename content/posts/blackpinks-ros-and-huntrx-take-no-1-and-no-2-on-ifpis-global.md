BLACKPINK’s Rosé and the rising girl group HUNTR/X have claimed the top two spots on the IFPI’s Global Single Chart for 2025. Their singles, including Rosé's collaboration with Bruno Mars, showcase the growing influence of K-Pop in the global music landscape.

## Our Take

This news is significant as it highlights the impact of K-Pop on the global music industry, with Rosé and HUNTR/X demonstrating that the genre continues to break barriers and set records. Dominating the IFPI's chart not only underscores their popularity but also reinforces K-Pop's status as a major player in the global music market.

The context of this achievement cannot be overlooked, especially as the K-Pop genre has been expanding its reach over the past few years. Rosé's success with her solo endeavors, combined with HUNTR/X's fresh appeal, indicates a shift in audience preferences and a growing demand for diverse sounds within the industry. This trend might encourage other K-Pop artists to explore collaborations and innovative concepts to capture a wider audience.

For fans, this accomplishment is a source of pride as it reflects the hard work and creativity of their favorite artists. As for the artists' careers, this milestone could lead to more opportunities for international promotions, collaborations, and potentially, awards in the coming year.

Overall, the success of Rosé and HUNTR/X on this prestigious chart signals a promising future for them and the K-Pop industry as a whole. It opens doors for further exploration of global markets, ensuring that K-Pop continues to resonate with audiences worldwide.
