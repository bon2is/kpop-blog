KiiiKiii achieved their first win on a music show with their title track “404 (New Era)” during the February 4 episode of “Show Champion.” They competed against popular tracks from artists like i-dle, EXO, POW, and WHIB.

---

## Our Take

KiiiKiii’s victory on 'Show Champion' represents a significant milestone not only for the group but also for the K-Pop landscape. The competition for first place is ever-increasing, given the presence of established groups like EXO and rising stars like i-dle. Winning against such formidable contenders highlights KiiiKiii's unique sound and growing popularity among fans. This win could potentially elevate their visibility in the industry and encourage more engagement with their music. Furthermore, '404 (New Era)' likely resonates with themes of innovation and freshness, aligning with a broader trend in K-Pop where groups seek to differentiate themselves through unique concepts. The success of newer groups like KiiiKiii signals a diversification of the genre, showing that audiences are increasingly open to new talent. This win is not just a validation of their hard work but also a message to the industry that fresh voices can rise to prominence, fostering a competitive yet dynamic environment for all K-Pop artists.
