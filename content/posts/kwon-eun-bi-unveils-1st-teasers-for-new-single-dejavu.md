Kwon Eun Bi, former CLC member and beloved soloist, has ignited the K‑pop community with the release of her first teaser photos for the upcoming digital single “DEJAVU.” The images, posted on August 23, confirm a September 3, 6 p.m. KST drop, marking her debut under the new agency RBW and promising a fresh musical direction for the artist.

## Background

Born on September 27, 1995, Kwon Eun Bi first entered the public eye as a contestant on Mnet’s survival show *Produce 101* in 2016, where she quickly became a fan favorite for her powerful vocals and charismatic stage presence. She subsequently debuted as the main vocalist of CLC under Cube Entertainment, contributing to the group’s evolving sound from cute concepts to more mature, EDM‑infused tracks such as “No” and “Me.”

After CLC’s disbandment in early 2022, Eun Bi embarked on a solo career, releasing her first solo mini‑album *Open* in August 2021. The project showcased her versatility, featuring the sultry R&B‑flavored title track “Open” and the introspective ballad “Killer.” Her follow‑up digital single “Door” (2022) further cemented her reputation as a solo artist capable of delivering emotive performances and chart‑worthy songs. Despite a busy schedule, Eun Bi remained active on variety shows and collaborations, keeping her fanbase—known as *EunBi’s*—engaged and growing.

In March 2023, Eun Bi announced her departure from Cube Entertainment, citing a desire for new artistic challenges. After a brief hiatus, the singer signed with RBW, the home of groups such as MAMAMOO and ONEUS, in early 2024. RBW is known for its strong vocal focus and global promotion strategies, which has raised expectations for Eun Bi’s next musical chapter.

## What Happened

On August 23, 2024, RBW’s official Instagram and Twitter accounts released the first set of teaser photos for Eun Bi’s upcoming digital single “DEJAVU.” The visuals feature Eun Bi in a sleek, monochrome aesthetic—half‑face makeup split between dark smokey eyes and a bright, ethereal glow—suggesting a dual‑natured concept that plays on the idea of déjà vu, the feeling of experiencing something familiar yet new.

The caption confirmed the release date: September 3 at 6 p.m. KST on all major streaming platforms, including Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube. While no full tracklist or detailed concept description was provided, RBK’s press release highlighted that “DEJAVU” is a digital single album, meaning it will be a standalone track accompanied by a possible instrumental version. The agency also hinted that a music video will accompany the release, promising high‑production visuals that align with Eun Bi’s reputation for striking stage aesthetics.

In a brief statement, Eun Bi expressed her excitement: “I’ve been working hard on this song, and I can’t wait for you all to hear it. ‘DEJAVU’ feels like a fresh start while still holding onto the emotions that have shaped my journey.” The teaser rollout will continue over the next week, with a concept video and a short audio snippet slated for release on August 28.

## Why This Matters

Eun Bi’s comeback is significant for several reasons. First, it marks her first official release under RBW, a label that has successfully propelled vocal‑centric acts onto the global stage. By aligning with RBW, Eun Bi gains access to a broader international distribution network, which could translate into higher chart placements on Billboard’s World Digital Song Sales chart and Korea’s Circle Digital Chart.

Second, the timing of the release places “DEJAVU” in a competitive summer‑fall window, where many soloists and groups drop summer anthems and comeback tracks. If the song resonates, it could secure a spot on year‑end lists and potentially break streaming records, similar to how other RBW artists have performed. For example, MAMAMOO’s “Starry Night” achieved over 30 million streams on Spotify within its first month, setting a benchmark for vocal‑driven tracks.

Third, the teaser’s artistic direction suggests a mature, concept‑driven approach that may attract not only existing fans but also new listeners who appreciate sophisticated visual storytelling—a hallmark of successful K‑pop releases. This could boost Eun Bi’s visibility on platforms like YouTube, where music video views heavily influence algorithmic recommendations and ad revenue.

## What Fans Can Expect

Fans can anticipate a polished production that blends Eun Bi’s signature vocal prowess with contemporary electronic and R&B elements. Given RBW’s history, the arrangement is likely to feature layered harmonies and a dynamic bridge that showcases her range. The music video, hinted to be “high‑budget,” may incorporate narrative motifs of repetition and memory, aligning with the “déjà vu” theme.

In addition to the single, RBW has hinted at possible promotional activities, including a live showcase streamed on YouTube and a series of behind‑the‑scenes clips on V‑Live. While no tour dates have been announced, the agency may schedule a mini‑fan‑meeting tour in South Korea and key overseas markets such as the United States, Japan, and Southeast Asia, aligning with typical promotional cycles for digital singles.
