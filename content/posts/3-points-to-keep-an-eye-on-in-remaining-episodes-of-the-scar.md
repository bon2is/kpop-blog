## Background

The K-Drama “The Scarecrow” is set between 1988 and 2019, and tells the story of two men who are bound together by misfortune and an uneasy alliance of hatred. The series follows Detective Cha Gi-Hyun, a seasoned and skilled investigator who is tasked with solving a series of murders that have shaken the community. However, as he delves deeper into the case, he is forced to work with a man named Kim Tae-Yeong, who is a suspect in the murders and someone that Detective Cha has a deep-seated hatred for. Despite their differences, the two men must put aside their animosity and work together to uncover the truth behind the murders.

The series boasts a talented cast, including veteran actors who bring depth and complexity to their roles. The chemistry between the leads is undeniable, and their characters' tumultuous relationship is a highlight of the series. The drama also features a talented supporting cast, who add to the richness and diversity of the story. With its strong cast and engaging storyline, “The Scarecrow” has become a fan favorite among K-Drama enthusiasts.
