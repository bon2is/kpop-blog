The Asia Star Entertainer Awards 2026 has announced its first MC lineup, with MONSTA X's Hyungwon and IVE's Rei set to host the first day of the ceremony on May 16. The ASEA awards ceremony has become a prominent event in the entertainment industry since its launch in 2024.
