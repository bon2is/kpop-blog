## Background

TXT, short for Tomorrow X Together, is a South Korean boy band formed by Big Hit Entertainment in 2019. The group consists of five members: Soobin, Yeonjun, Beomgyu, Taehyun, and Hueningkai. Since their debut, TXT has been making waves in the K-Pop scene with their unique sound, energetic performances, and thought-provoking lyrics. With hits like '9 and Three Quarters (Run Away)' and 'Blue Hour', the group has built a strong following not only in Korea but also worldwide. Their music often explores themes of youth, identity, and self-discovery, resonating with fans of all ages.

TXT's career has been marked by numerous achievements, including topping various music charts, winning awards at prestigious award shows, and collaborating with international artists. Their recent releases, such as 'Still Dreaming' and 'minisode1: Blue Hour', have further solidified their position as one of the leading K-Pop groups today. With their versatile discography and captivating stage presence, TXT has become a household name in the K-Pop industry.

Yoajung, on the other hand, is a popular premium dessert franchise that has been gaining traction in recent years. Known for their high-quality desserts and unique flavors, Yoajung has become a favorite among dessert lovers in Korea and beyond. With a strong brand image and a commitment to using only the finest ingredients, Yoajung is the perfect partner for TXT, who share similar values of quality, creativity, and passion.

## What Happened

According to the official announcement, TXT has been chosen as Yoajung's first-ever global models due to their unique identity and wholesome energy, which aligns perfectly with the brand's values. As quoted by Yoajung, 'Through this partnership, we will strengthen our brand image and expand our global reach.' This exciting collaboration is set to bring together the best of both worlds, with TXT's massive following and Yoajung's premium desserts.
