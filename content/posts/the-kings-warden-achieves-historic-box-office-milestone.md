The King's Warden has become the 5th biggest box office hit in Korean film history, with a total of 13,467,838 moviegoers as of March 15 KST. This milestone marks a significant achievement for the film, which has been attracting large audiences since its release. The film's success is a testament to its engaging storyline and talented cast.
