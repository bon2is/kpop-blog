HUNTR/X, a fictional girl group from the series 'KPop Demon Hunters,' will have its singing voices, EJAE, Audrey Nuna, and Rei Ami, performing the song 'Golden' at the upcoming BAFTA Film Awards on February 3. This marks a significant crossover between K-Pop and major international film events.
