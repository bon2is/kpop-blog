## Our Take

Chenle's statement is significant as it acknowledges the fans' emotional investment in the group and its members. 

 The K-Pop industry is known for its highly produced and tightly controlled image, making it rare for idols to speak candidly about sensitive topics. 

 Chenle's words may help to alleviate some of the uncertainty and disappointment felt by fans, demonstrating a more personal and empathetic side to the group. 

 This moment could be a turning point for NCT, as they navigate the challenges of lineup changes and evolving fan expectations, potentially impacting their future music and performances.
