## What Happened

On the July 18 episode of MBC's 'Music Core', Yeonjun's 'Ice Cream' was nominated for first place alongside RESCENE's 'Pretty Girl' and aespa's 'LEMONADE'. After a tense competition, Yeonjun emerged as the winner with a total of 7,125 points. This win marks Yeonjun's second music show trophy for 'Ice Cream', following his first win on another popular music show.
