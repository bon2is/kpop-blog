KPop Demon Hunters, an animated film featuring a fictional K-Pop girl group, has won two Oscars at the 2026 Academy Awards. The film took home the award for Best Animated Feature Film and Best Original Song for 'Golden', performed by EJAE, Audrey Nuna, and Rei Ami. This historic win marks a significant milestone for K-Pop's global recognition and influence in the film industry.

## Our Take

The win of KPop Demon Hunters at the Oscars is a testament to the growing global influence of K-Pop, with the genre's unique sound and energetic performances captivating audiences worldwide. 

 The context of this win is particularly significant, as it highlights the increasing crossover between K-Pop and the global film industry, with K-Pop idols and groups contributing to soundtracks and appearing in films. 

 This historic win is expected to have a profound impact on the careers of the artists involved, particularly EJAE, Audrey Nuna, and Rei Ami, who lent their voices to the film's fictional girl group HUNTR/X. 

 For fans of K-Pop, this win is a moment of pride and validation, demonstrating the genre's ability to transcend cultural boundaries and achieve mainstream recognition, and potentially paving the way for more K-Pop collaborations in film and beyond.
