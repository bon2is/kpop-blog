Hwang Minhyun, the charismatic vocalist best known for his work with NU’EST and his solo success, has just unveiled the schedule for his 2026 fan‑meeting tour titled “PEACH‑BLOSSOM.” The first leg will launch in Seoul from October 16‑18 at COEX Artium’s Woori Bank Hall, and fans can already feel the excitement rippling through social media.

## Background

Minhyun first rose to fame as a member of NU’EST, debuting under PLEDIS Entertainment in 2012. The group quickly earned a reputation for strong vocal performances and experimental concepts, scoring hits such as “Face,” “Sleep Talking,” and the chart‑topping “Bet Bet.” After a brief hiatus, Minhyun gained wider recognition by winning the survival show *Produce 101 Season 2* in 2017, subsequently joining the project group Wanna One. During his two‑year run with Wanna One, he topped the *Gaon* Digital Chart multiple times and solidified his status as a top‑tier solo performer.

Following Wanna One’s disbandment in 2019, Minhyun returned to NU’EST for a final group comeback before the members fulfilled their mandatory military service. He completed his service in 2022 and has since focused on solo activities, releasing the EP *Truth* in 2023, which peaked at #3 on the Circle Album Chart and entered Billboard’s World Albums chart at #12. His solo single “Blue Moon” broke into the Billboard Hot 100’s “Bubbling Under” section, marking a rare achievement for a solo K‑pop artist.

Minhyun’s fan‑meeting history is equally impressive. In 2020, he held a sold‑out fan‑meeting in Seoul that sold out within minutes, and a subsequent Asia tour in 2021 covered Japan, Thailand, and the Philippines, drawing over 30,000 attendees in total. Those events set a high bar for his upcoming “PEACH‑BLOSSOM” series, which promises a more intimate, spring‑themed experience.

## What Happened

On August 21, PLEDIS Entertainment released an official statement confirming the dates and venues for Minhyun’s 2026 fan‑meeting tour. The tour, named “PEACH‑BLOSSOM,” will span six cities across Asia, beginning with three consecutive days in Seoul (October 16, 17, and 18). Each Seoul show will be held at the 1,500‑seat Woori Bank Hall inside COEX Artium, a venue known for its high‑tech staging and comfortable audience layout.

Following the Seoul leg, the itinerary includes stops in Tokyo (November 2‑3), Bangkok (November 10‑11), Kuala Lumpur (November 18‑19), Singapore (December 1‑2), and Manila (December 8‑9). The announcement also noted that tickets will go on sale via the official fan‑meeting website and major Korean ticket platforms such as Interpark and Yes24, with a pre‑sale window for members of Minhyun’s official fan club, MINHYO. While exact pricing has not been disclosed, past events suggest a range of ₩120,000‑₩200,000 (approximately $100‑$170 USD) depending on seat tier.

Minhyun shared a short video teaser on his Instagram, standing beneath a blooming peach tree with the caption, “Spring is coming, and so is PEACH‑BLOSSOM 🌸✨.” In the clip, he hinted at special stages, a brand‑new acoustic performance, and an exclusive photo‑book that will be available for purchase only at the venues. The agency also confirmed that a live‑stream option will be offered for international fans through the K‑Live platform, with a separate ticket tier for the digital audience.

## Why This Matters

The “PEACH‑BLOSSOM” fan‑meeting marks Minhyun’s first large‑scale fan‑engagement event since his military discharge, signaling his full return to the entertainment circuit as a solo act. In an industry where many idols focus on album releases and world tours, fan‑meetings serve as a crucial touchpoint for deepening fan loyalty and generating additional revenue streams. For Minhyun, whose solo discography has already charted internationally, the tour provides a platform to showcase unreleased material and gauge fan reaction ahead of his anticipated 2027 album.

From a market perspective, the decision to include a live‑stream component reflects the growing demand for hybrid events. According to a 2024 IFPI report, 38% of K‑pop fans outside of Asia prefer digital attendance options, and platforms like K‑Live have seen a 22% YoY increase in ticket sales. By offering a global streaming ticket, Minhyun’s team is tapping into that lucrative market while maintaining the intimacy of a physical fan‑meeting.

The tour also underscores PLEDIS’s strategy to diversify its artists’ activities post‑NU’EST. With NU’EST now on an indefinite hiatus, the label is positioning its soloists—Minhyun, Baekho, and others—for independent success. A successful fan‑meeting series can boost Minhyun’s brand equity, making him a more attractive collaborator for endorsements, variety shows, and future overseas promotions.

## What Fans Can Expect

Fans attending the Seoul shows can look forward to a curated setlist that blends Minhyun’s NU’EST classics (“Sleep Talking,” “Love Paint”) with his solo tracks (“Blue Moon,” “Truth”). The teaser video hinted at a brand‑new acoustic rendition of a yet‑to‑be‑released song, which many speculate could be the title track of his next EP. Additionally, Minhyun will host a Q&A segment, a game segment with fan‑club members, and a surprise collaboration with a fellow PLEDIS artist—rumors point to a possible appearance by former Wanna One member Kang Dong‑won.

The exclusive photo‑book, titled *Peach Blossom Memories*, will feature behind‑the‑scenes shots from the preparation process, handwritten notes, and limited‑edition postcards. Merchandise such as enamel pins, scented candles, and a “PEACH‑BLOSSOM” tote bag will also be sold at each venue. For international fans, the K‑Live stream will include multi‑language subtitles and a virtual meet‑and‑greet lobby where ticket holders can send real‑time questions to Minhyun.
