The world of K-Pop is no stranger to crossovers into the acting realm, with many idols pursuing careers in film and television. One of the most highly anticipated projects to come out of this trend is the new horror-thriller film 'Shaving', which boasts an impressive cast including ITZY's Ryujin, Roh Jeong Eui, and Kasamatsu Sho. The movie has just completed filming and has unveiled its first stills, giving fans a glimpse into the terrifying world that awaits them.


## Background


ITZY is a South Korean girl group formed by JYP Entertainment in 2019. The group consists of five members: Yeji, Lia, Ryujin, Chaeryeong, and Yuna. Since their debut, ITZY has been known for their unique sound, which blends genres such as pop, hip-hop, and electronic dance music. They have released several successful singles and EPs, including 'Dalla Dalla', 'Icy', and 'Wannabe'. Ryujin, in particular, has been gaining attention for her versatile talents, showcasing her acting skills in various projects. Roh Jeong Eui is a South Korean actress who has appeared in several films and dramas, including '18 Again' and 'Our Beloved Summer'. Kasamatsu Sho is a Japanese actor who has also made a name for himself in the Korean entertainment industry, with roles in 'The Package' and 'My ID is Gangnam Beauty'.


The cast of 'Shaving' brings together a diverse range of talents, each with their own unique experiences and strengths. Roh Jeong Eui has already proven her acting chops in various genres, from romance to drama. Kasamatsu Sho has shown his ability to adapt to different roles, from comedy to horror. ITZY's Ryujin, on the other hand, is relatively new to the acting scene, but has already demonstrated her potential in previous projects. The combination of these actors promises to bring a fresh and exciting dynamic to the film.


## What Happened


The production company behind 'Shaving', Star Platinum Co., Ltd., announced on May 8 that the film has completed filming and has now entered post-production. The company also unveiled new stills from the movie, giving fans a first look at the horror-thriller. According to the announcement, 'Shaving' is set to release in the second half of this year, although an exact date has not been confirmed. The film's plot revolves around the theme of trauma, with the characters confronting their dark pasts and inner demons. The stills released so far show a haunting and atmospheric setting, with the cast members displaying intense emotions and fear.


The announcement of 'Shaving' has generated significant buzz among fans of the cast members, as well as horror enthusiasts. The film's premise and setting have drawn comparisons to other popular horror movies, such as 'Train to Busan' and 'The Silenced'. The fact that the movie has completed filming and is now in post-production suggests that the release date is not too far away, and fans are eagerly awaiting more updates and teasers.

The release of 'Shaving' is significant not only for the cast members but also for the K-Pop industry as a whole. ITZY's Ryujin is one of the first idols from the group to venture into acting, and her performance in the film will be closely watched by fans and critics alike.
