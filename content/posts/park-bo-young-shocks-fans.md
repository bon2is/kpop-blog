Park Bo Young and Lee Kwang Soo will guest star on the final episode of Season 4 of 'Whenever Possible'. The show follows hosts Yoo and other cast members as they complete various missions. The episode is expected to be an exciting conclusion to the season.
---

## Our Take

This news matters because it brings together two beloved Korean celebrities in a unique and entertaining way. 

 The K-Pop and K-Drama industries often overlap, with actors and idols frequently appearing on variety shows like 'Whenever Possible'. 

 For fans of Park Bo Young and Lee Kwang Soo, this appearance is a rare treat, allowing them to see their favorite stars in a more casual and humorous setting. 

 The guest appearance may also boost the show's ratings and attract new viewers who are fans of the two celebrities.
