The recent performance by BTS at the 2026 FIFA World Cup was highly anticipated and widely covered, but what caught the attention of many was not just their energetic stage presence, but an incident that occurred after the show. The group's behavior towards member Jimin during a meeting with an influencer has sparked controversy, with many fans and netizens criticizing the members for what they perceive as disrespect towards Jimin.


## Background


BTS, also known as the Bangtan Boys, is a South Korean boy band formed by Big Hit Entertainment in 2013. The group consists of seven members: RM, Jin, Suga, J-Hope, Jimin, V, and Jungkook. Over the years, BTS has become one of the most popular and influential K-Pop groups globally, known for their energetic live performances, meaningful lyrics, and genre-bending music style. With numerous chart-topping hits like 'DNA', 'Mic Drop', and 'Boy With Luv', BTS has broken multiple records and won numerous awards, including the Melon Music Award for Artist of the Year and the Mnet Asian Music Award for Best Male Group.


The group's career highlights include their debut single 'No More Dream' in 2013, followed by their first studio album 'The Most Beautiful Moment in Life, Part 1' in 2015. Since then, BTS has released several successful albums, including 'Wings' in 2016, 'Love Yourself: Her' in 2017, 'Love Yourself: Tear' in 2018, and 'Map of the Soul: 7' in 2020. Their most recent release is the album 'Proof', which debuted at number one on the US Billboard 200 chart. BTS has also collaborated with international artists like Halsey, Lauv, and Charli XCX, further expanding their global reach.


Jimin, whose full name is Park Ji-min, is one of the main vocalists and dancers of BTS. Born on October 13, 1995, Jimin is known for his powerful vocals, impressive dance skills, and captivating stage presence. He has been an integral part of BTS since the group's debut and has participated in the creation of many of their songs. Jimin has also released solo tracks, including 'Promise' and 'Christmas Love', showcasing his vocal range and emotional delivery.


## What Happened


The controversy surrounding BTS members' behavior towards Jimin began after a video surfaced online, showing the group meeting an influencer after their performance at the 2026 FIFA World Cup. In the video, Jimin appears to be standing at the back, looking uncomfortable, while the other members are chatting and laughing with the influencer. Some fans have pointed out that the members seemed to be ignoring Jimin or not including him in the conversation, which has led to accusations of disrespect and bullying. The incident has sparked a heated debate among fans and netizens, with many calling out the group's alleged behavior towards Jimin.


The influencer who posted the video has since deleted it, but not before it was shared and spread widely on social media. The incident has also drawn attention to the dynamics within the group, with some fans speculating about the relationships between the members. While some have defended the group, saying that the video was taken out of context or that Jimin was just shy, others have expressed concern and disappointment at the alleged behavior of the members. BTS's management team, Big Hit Entertainment, has not yet commented on the incident.
