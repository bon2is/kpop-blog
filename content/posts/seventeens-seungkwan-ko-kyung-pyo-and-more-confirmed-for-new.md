## Background


SEVENTEEN is a popular South Korean boy group formed by Pledis Entertainment in 2015. The group consists of 13 members: S.Coups, Jeonghan, Joshua, Jun, Hoshi, Wonwoo, Woozi, DK, Mingyu, The8, Seungkwan, Vernon, and Dino. Over the years, SEVENTEEN has released several successful albums and singles, including 'Adore U', 'Mansae', 'Pretty U', and 'Don't Wanna Cry'. The group has won numerous awards, including the Melon Music Award for Best New Male Artist, the Mnet Asian Music Award for Best Male Group, and the Gaon Chart Music Award for Artist of the Year. SEVENTEEN's music style is a unique blend of genres, including pop, rock, hip-hop, and R&B, which has helped the group gain a massive following worldwide.


Ko Kyung Pyo is a South Korean actor who has appeared in several popular dramas, including 'Reply 1988', 'Strong Girl Bong-soon', and 'Private Lives'. He has also starred in films like 'Horror Stories' and 'Seondal: The Man Who Sells the River'. Ko Kyung Pyo has won several awards for his acting, including the Korea Drama Award for Best New Actor and the APAN Star Award for Best New Actor. He is known for his versatility and range as an actor, and his fans are excited to see him take on a new challenge in 'The Great Escape'.


'The Great Escape' is an adventure variety show that premiered on tvN in 2018. The show features a cast of celebrities who work together to solve elaborate escape room challenges. The show has gained a huge following worldwide, thanks to its unique concept and exciting gameplay. In each episode, the cast members are 'trapped' in a themed room and must solve puzzles and clues to escape within a certain time limit. The show requires teamwork, communication, and problem-solving skills, making it a thrilling and entertaining watch.
