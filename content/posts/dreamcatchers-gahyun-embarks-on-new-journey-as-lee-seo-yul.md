Dreamcatcher's Gahyun has signed an exclusive contract with Hogiroun Company to pursue an acting career under the new stage name Lee Seo Yul. This marks a significant shift in her career, as she transitions from being the youngest member of the K-Pop group Dreamcatcher to focusing on acting. The announcement was made on March 24, signaling an exciting new chapter for the artist.
---

## Our Take

Gahyun's decision to change her stage name to Lee Seo Yul for her acting career is a strategic move, allowing her to differentiate her acting and music personas. 

 This change is significant in the context of the K-Pop industry, where idols often pursue multiple careers, and having a distinct stage name can help manage different public images. 

 As Lee Seo Yul, she will likely appeal to a broader audience, including those who may not be familiar with her work as a member of Dreamcatcher. 

 For fans of Dreamcatcher and Gahyun, this development means they will have the opportunity to see their favorite artist in a new light, tackling acting roles and potentially expanding her creative horizons.
