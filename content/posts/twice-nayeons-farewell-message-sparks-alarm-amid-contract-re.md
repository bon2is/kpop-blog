## Background

TWICE is a South Korean girl group formed by JYP Entertainment in 2015, through the reality show Sixteen. The group consists of nine members: Nayeon, Jeongyeon, Momo, Sana, Jihyo, Mina, Dahyun, Chaeyoung, and Tzuyu. Since their debut, TWICE has released numerous hit songs, including 'Cheer Up', 'TT', 'Knock Knock', and 'Fancy'. The group has also won numerous awards, including the Melon Music Award for Artist of the Year and the Mnet Asian Music Award for Best Female Group. Nayeon, in particular, has been a key member of the group, known for her powerful vocals and captivating stage presence.

Nayeon has been with JYP Entertainment since 2010, training for five years before debuting with TWICE. She has been an integral part of the group's success, participating in the creation of many of their songs and music videos. In recent years, TWICE has been on a roll, releasing hit after hit and embarking on successful world tours. The group's latest album, 'Between 1&2', was released in August 2022 and features the hit single 'Talk That Talk'. The album was a commercial success, topping the charts in Korea and abroad.
