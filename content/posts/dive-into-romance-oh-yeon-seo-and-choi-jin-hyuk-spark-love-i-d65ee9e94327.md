The excitement is building as the highly anticipated drama "Positively Yours" has just dropped a captivating new teaser! This romantic comedy, inspired by a beloved webtoon, promises to take viewers on a delightful ride through unexpected love and humorous twists.

At the heart of the story are Oh Yeon Seo and Choi Jin Hyuk, who play the charming leads navigating the complexities of modern relationships. Choi Jin Hyuk takes on the role of Kang Du Jun, a suave CEO of Taehan Liquor, while Oh Yeon Seo portrays a strong-willed woman who finds herself in a whirlwind romance following a fateful one-night stand. Both characters have made vows to avoid marriage, setting the stage for a comedic exploration of love, responsibility, and the surprises that life throws at us.

The teaser hints at the intriguing premise where a casual encounter leads not only to unexpected pregnancy but also to a heartfelt proposal. This dynamic twist will keep viewers on the edge of their seats, eager to see how Kang Du Jun and Oh Yeon Seo's character navigate their evolving relationship. With both actors bringing their A-game, fans can anticipate a mix of laughter, warmth, and emotional depth as they embark on this unexpected journey together.

"Positively Yours" is not just another romantic comedy; it reflects the modern-day dilemmas faced by many young adults today, making it relatable and engaging. Audiences can look forward to the chemistry between Oh Yeon Seo and Choi Jin Hyuk, both of whom have garnered attention for their previous work in the K-drama scene. With a stellar cast and an engaging storyline, this drama is poised to capture the hearts of K-drama enthusiasts worldwide.

Mark your calendars for the premiere of "Positively Yours" and prepare to join Kang Du Jun and his love interest on a rollercoaster of emotions that meld together romance and comedy in the most delightful way.
