## Our Take

The release of these dance practice videos is a treat for fans who have been dissecting every move in the official music videos. 

 This move is also strategic, as it allows BTS to engage with their fans and encourage user-generated content. By sharing their choreography, the group is inviting fans to learn and share their own dance covers. 

 In the context of the K-Pop industry, dance practice videos have become a staple of fan engagement, and BTS is no exception. The group has consistently used these videos to connect with their fans and showcase their musicality. 

 For fans, this means a deeper level of appreciation for the group's artistry and dedication to their craft. It also underscores the significance of 'ARIRANG' as an album, which has already made waves with its unique sound and themes.
