## Background

Jun Ji Hyun is a South Korean actress born on October 30, 1981, in Seoul, South Korea. She rose to fame with her debut film 'White Valentine' in 1999, but it was her role in the 2001 film 'My Sassy Girl' that catapulted her to stardom. The film's massive success not only established her as a leading lady but also paved the way for her future projects. Throughout her career, Jun Ji Hyun has demonstrated her acting prowess by taking on diverse roles in films like 'The Thieves', 'The Berlin File', and 'Assassination'. Her ability to effortlessly transition between genres has earned her widespread recognition and acclaim.

Jun Ji Hyun's career is marked by numerous notable performances, including her portrayal of Cheon Song-yi in the 2013 television series 'My Love from the Star', which became a cultural phenomenon in Korea and beyond. Her on-screen chemistry with co-star Kim Soo-hyun was undeniable, and the show's massive success can be attributed in part to her exceptional acting skills. In addition to her film and television work, Jun Ji Hyun has been a long-time ambassador for several high-profile brands, further solidifying her status as a household name in Korea.

## What Happened

On July 13, film distributor SHOWBOX announced that Jun Ji Hyun had received the Extraordinary Star Asia Award at the 25th New York Asian Film Festival (NYAFF). The award ceremony, which took place in New York City, was attended by prominent figures from the film industry, all gathered to celebrate the achievements of Asian cinema's finest talents. The Extraordinary Star Asia Award is presented to actors who have made significant contributions to the Asian film industry, and Jun Ji Hyun's win is a testament to her enduring impact on the world of cinema.
