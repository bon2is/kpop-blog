In a move that has sent shockwaves of excitement through the K-Pop community, BLACKPINK's Jennie has announced the release date for her highly anticipated new single, 'Less than a Lover'. This solo project is particularly noteworthy as it showcases Jennie's multifaceted talents, with her not only performing but also directing, writing, and creating the music for the single. The announcement, made on July 21, revealed that 'Less than a Lover' will be available to fans on July 24 at 1 p.m. KST, marking a significant moment in Jennie's solo career.

## Background

Jennie, whose full name is Kim Jennie, is a member of the immensely popular K-Pop girl group BLACKPINK, formed by YG Entertainment. Since her debut with BLACKPINK in 2016, Jennie has been recognized for her unique vocal style, captivating stage presence, and versatility as a performer. Over the years, BLACKPINK has achieved unparalleled success, breaking numerous records and becoming one of the most influential K-Pop groups globally. Jennie's solo endeavors, including her 2018 single 'SOLO', have also garnered significant attention and acclaim, demonstrating her ability to shine outside of the group context.

The path to Jennie's solo work has been marked by her consistent contribution to BLACKPINK's discography, which includes hit songs like 'DDU-DU DDU-DU', 'Kill This Love', and 'How You Like That'. These songs, along with the group's albums and EPs, have not only topped Korean music charts but have also made significant inroads into the global music scene, including the US Billboard charts. Jennie's decision to explore solo projects reflects her artistic growth and the desire to express herself in different musical dimensions, a trend that is increasingly popular among K-Pop idols seeking to expand their creative horizons.

## What Happened

The announcement of 'Less than a Lover' was accompanied by a teaser, offering fans a glimpse into what they can expect from the new single. The fact that Jennie is credited with directing, writing, and creating the music for 'Less than a Lover' underscores her commitment to the project and suggests a deeply personal and artistic endeavor. This level of involvement is rare, even among seasoned artists, and indicates that 'Less than a Lover' is a labor of love for Jennie, allowing her to express her creativity in a holistic manner. The release strategy, including the timing and the platform, suggests a well-planned approach to maximize the single's reach and impact, leveraging Jennie's and BLACKPINK's substantial global fan base.

The teaser release and subsequent announcement have generated considerable buzz, with fans and media alike speculating about the theme, sound, and style of 'Less than a Lover'. Given Jennie's past work and her influence on contemporary K-Pop, expectations are high, with many anticipating that the single will not only showcase her musical talent but also her maturity as an artist. The involvement of Jennie in all aspects of the single's production adds an extra layer of excitement, as it promises a work that is quintessentially hers, reflecting her vision, emotions, and experiences.

## Why This Matters

The release of 'Less than a Lover' is significant for several reasons. Firstly, it marks an important milestone in Jennie's solo career, demonstrating her capacity to conceive and deliver a project that is entirely her own. This level of creative control and involvement is a testament to her growth as an artist and her desire to explore different facets of her talent.
