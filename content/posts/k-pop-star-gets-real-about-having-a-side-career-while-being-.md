Kang Daniel, one of K‑pop’s most charismatic soloists, stunned fans in May when he posted a heartfelt, multi‑part personal essay titled “Personal life story I’ve never spoken of (Part 1).” In the essay, the former Wanna One member opened up about a side career he’s quietly building – acting – and how he’s learning to juggle the demanding schedules of both worlds.

## Background

Born on December 10, 1996, Kang Daniel rose to fame after winning the reality competition *Produce 101 Season 2* in 2017. The massive exposure catapulted him into the project group Wanna One, where he quickly became the group’s visual and main vocalist. After Wanna One disbanded in early 2019, Daniel launched a solo career under Konnect Music, delivering chart‑topping hits like “What Are You Doing,” “2U,” and the recent EP *YELLOW* that debuted at #2 on the Circle Album Chart.

Beyond music, Daniel has cultivated a reputation as a versatile entertainer. He’s hosted popular variety shows such as *Talk Talk Talk* and *Star Dance Battle*, and he’s frequently invited as a guest on Korean talk shows, showcasing his quick wit and charm. His social media following exceeds 20 million on Instagram, making him one of the most influential K‑pop idols in the West. These achievements have earned him spots on Billboard’s World Albums chart and regular mentions in the Korean Hanteo real‑time rankings.

## What Happened

In a 3,500‑word Instagram post on May 12, Daniel confessed that he has been auditioning for drama roles behind the scenes for the past year. He revealed that he recently landed a supporting role in the upcoming SBS drama *“City Lights”*, set to air in September 2026. The series follows a group of young professionals navigating love and ambition in Seoul’s fast‑paced tech industry. Daniel will portray “Ji‑hoon,” a charismatic start‑up founder whose storyline intersects with the main lead’s romance arc.

Daniel’s post included a short video clip of his first on‑set rehearsal, where he practiced a serious monologue in front of the director. He wrote, “I’ve always wanted to tell stories beyond the stage, and acting gives me a new way to connect with people.” He also addressed the inevitable question of time management, stating that his agency, Konnect Music, has arranged his schedule so that promotional activities for his next single, slated for a July release, will not clash with the drama’s filming timetable.

Fans quickly responded, flooding the comment section with supportive messages and speculation about how his acting debut might affect his music releases. Major Korean entertainment news outlets, including *Koreaboo* and *Soompi*, picked up the story, noting that Daniel’s move mirrors a growing trend of idols branching into acting to diversify their portfolios.

## Why This Matters

Kang Daniel’s foray into acting is significant on several levels. First, it underscores the evolving career pathways for K‑pop idols. While many idols have historically taken on acting gigs, the intensity of modern idol schedules often makes it difficult to commit fully to a drama. Daniel’s ability to secure a role while maintaining a solo music schedule signals that agencies are becoming more flexible, allowing talent to explore multiple avenues without sacrificing their core brand.

Second, his involvement in a prime‑time SBS drama could boost viewership internationally. *City Lights* is expected to be available on Viki and Kocowa shortly after its broadcast, with subtitles in English, Spanish, and French. For Western fans, this means an accessible way to watch Daniel’s acting debut without a cable subscription. The drama may also be added to Netflix’s Asian catalog later in the year, a common practice for high‑profile Korean series.

Third, the move may influence chart performance. Historically, idols who act in successful dramas see a spike in digital sales and streaming numbers for their music. For example, IU’s drama appearances often lead to a surge in Billboard Hot 100 placements for her OST tracks. If Daniel’s drama garners strong ratings, we could see a similar boost for his upcoming single and the *YELLOW* EP, potentially pushing it onto the Billboard 200.

## What Fans Can Expect

Fans should keep an eye on two major releases this summer. Daniel’s new single “Midnight Dream” is slated for a July 15 digital drop on all major platforms, including Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music. The track will be accompanied by a visually striking MV that hints at a cinematic aesthetic, possibly foreshadowing his acting role.

The drama *City Lights* will premiere on September 5 on SBS, with episodes airing twice a week. International viewers can stream the series on Viki (free with ads, premium for ad‑free) and Kocowa (subscription required). A subtitled version is expected to land on Netflix’s global library by early 2027, expanding its reach to the U.S., Canada, the U.K., and Australia.

Additionally, Daniel hinted at a possible fan‑meeting tour in late 2026, pending his filming schedule. He promised “more personal moments” for fans, suggesting a blend of live performances and Q&A sessions that could include behind‑the‑scenes stories from *City Lights*.
