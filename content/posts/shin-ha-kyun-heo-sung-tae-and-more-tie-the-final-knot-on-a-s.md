'Fifties Professionals' is a story about three seemingly ordinary but secretly dangerous middle-aged men who are brought back into action by destiny. The show features an all-star cast, including Shin Ha Kyun, Heo Sung Tae, and more, who bring to life the characters of three men who once reigned at the top of their respective fields. Shin Ha Kyun, a veteran actor known for his versatility, plays the lead role of a former spy who is forced back into action. Heo Sung Tae, on the other hand, is a talented actor who has gained recognition for his roles in various K-dramas and films.

The show's premise is centered around the three men, who find themselves exiled to a remote island after their respective careers are cut short. However, their peaceful lives are disrupted when they are brought back into action by a series of events that unfold on the island. With their unique skills and experiences, the three men must work together to overcome the challenges that lie ahead. Throughout the series, the characters face various obstacles, including action-packed sequences, comedic moments, and heartfelt drama.
