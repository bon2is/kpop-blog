Circle Chart has announced its latest rankings, highlighting that ATEEZ, ENHYPEN, Hwasa, and Car, The Garden have all earned double crowns for their recent releases. ENHYPEN's mini album 'THE SIN : VANISH' and its title track 'Knife' led the way, while ATEEZ and others made significant marks as well.
