NCT members Jeno and Jaemin are set to debut as a new unit called NCT JNJM, with their first mini album titled "BOTH SIDES" scheduled for release on February 23. The announcement was made by SM Entertainment on January 26, accompanied by a teaser poster.

---

## Our Take

The debut of Jeno and Jaemin as NCT JNJM is a significant development in the constantly evolving landscape of the K-Pop industry. This unit not only showcases the versatility of NCT members but also highlights SM Entertainment's strategy of utilizing sub-units to keep their fanbase engaged. By forming smaller groups, they allow members to explore different musical styles and concepts, which can resonate with a broader audience. The mini album "BOTH SIDES" suggests a duality in themes and sound, which could offer a fresh take on the group's established identity. Additionally, Jeno and Jaemin's individual charms and talents have already garnered substantial attention, making this unit's debut highly anticipated. Their synergy could also pave the way for more collaborations within NCT, enhancing the group's overall appeal. As K-Pop continues to grow globally, the strategic formation of units like NCT JNJM exemplifies how agencies are innovating to maintain relevance and excitement in a competitive market.
