JTBC's upcoming drama "We Are All Trying Here" has released a new teaser, showcasing the story of Hwang Dong Man, a character who feels his life isn't working out amidst successful friends. The drama explores themes of anguish, envy, and jealousy. The teaser focuses on the bond between Hwang Dong Man and another character, becoming each other's sanctuary.

## Our Take

The release of this teaser matters because it indicates a shift in K-Drama storytelling, focusing more on the inner struggles of characters. 

 This drama is set to resonate with audiences who have felt like they don't measure up to societal expectations, a common feeling in today's competitive world. By exploring these themes, "We Are All Trying Here" could start important conversations about mental health and support. 

 In the context of the K-Pop and K-Drama industry, this drama's approach to sensitive topics could influence future productions, encouraging more realistic portrayals of life's challenges. 

 For fans of Koo Kyo Hwan and Go Youn Jung, this drama presents an opportunity to see these actors in deeper, more complex roles, potentially elevating their careers and fan appreciation.
