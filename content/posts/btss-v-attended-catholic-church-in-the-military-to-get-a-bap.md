## Background

V, whose real name is Kim Tae-hyung, is a vocalist and visual of the globally renowned K-Pop group BTS. BTS has been on an indefinite hiatus since 2022, with members pursuing solo projects and military service. V, being one of the older members, began his military service in June 2023, following the mandatory conscription laws in South Korea.

As a member of BTS, V has been part of numerous chart-topping albums, including 'Map of the Soul: 7', 'BE', and 'Proof'.
