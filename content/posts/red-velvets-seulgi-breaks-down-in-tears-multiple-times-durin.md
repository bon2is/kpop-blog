## Background


Red Velvet, formed by SM Entertainment, debuted in 2014 and quickly rose to fame with their unique sound that blends genres like pop, rock, and R&B. The group consists of Irene, Seulgi, Wendy, Joy, and Yeri, each bringing their distinct talents and personalities to the table. Seulgi, in particular, has been praised for her powerful vocals and impressive dance skills, making her a standout performer in the group. Over the years, Red Velvet has released numerous hit songs and albums, including 'Bad Boy,' 'Psycho,' and 'The ReVe Festival' series, which have topped various music charts, including the Billboard World Albums chart and the Circle Chart.


The group's success is not limited to their music; they have also made waves in the fashion and beauty worlds, with endorsements and appearances in high-profile events. Individually, the members have pursued solo activities, with Seulgi being part of the sub-unit Red Velvet - Irene & Seulgi, which debuted in 2020 with the single 'Monster.' This versatility and the group's ability to experiment with different concepts have contributed to their enduring popularity among K-Pop fans worldwide.


Seulgi's personal life, while largely private, has been subject to public scrutiny, especially in times of sorrow. The recent loss of her cousin has undoubtedly been a challenging time for her, and fans have been watching with concern as she navigates her grief amidst a busy schedule of performances and promotions. Social media platforms like Instagram and Twitter have been filled with messages of condolence and support for Seulgi, demonstrating the strong bond between K-Pop idols and their fans.
