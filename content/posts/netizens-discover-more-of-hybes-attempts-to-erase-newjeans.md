Recent discussions among netizens have spotlighted HYBE’s alleged efforts to downplay the rising popularity of NewJeans. This situation has ignited debates about the management practices within one of K-Pop's most powerful companies.
---

## Our Take

The significance of this news cannot be overstated, as it raises serious questions about the ethics of artist promotion and company competition in the K-Pop industry. NewJeans has emerged as a formidable group, quickly gaining a massive following and influencing trends, which may have prompted HYBE to consider more aggressive tactics to protect its own artists or image.

This situation also reflects a broader context within the K-Pop industry, where management companies often engage in cutthroat competition. The idea of 'erasing' a rival group points to the intensity of the race for dominance in a market that is increasingly saturated with talent. Such strategies may undermine the collaborative spirit that K-Pop fans often celebrate, leading to a more toxic environment among fans of different groups.

For fans, this news is concerning as it highlights the complicated relationship between management decisions and artist development. NewJeans' supporters are likely to rally around the group even more fiercely, viewing these attempts as an attack on their favorite idols. As for NewJeans' career trajectory, overcoming such adversity could ultimately strengthen their brand and fan loyalty, turning the narrative in their favor.

In the end, how HYBE responds to these revelations will be crucial. The company must navigate these waters carefully, as the backlash from netizens could not only affect its reputation but also have lasting consequences for NewJeans and any future groups under their management.
