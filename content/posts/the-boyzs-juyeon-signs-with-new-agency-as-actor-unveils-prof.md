Juyeon, the charismatic vocalist of THE BOYZ, has just taken a bold step in his career. On April 14, ACE FACTORY announced an exclusive contract with the idol, promising agency backing for both his group work and burgeoning acting projects. Alongside the news, the agency dropped a set of sleek profile photos that instantly ignited fan excitement across social media.

## Background

THE BOYZ debuted in 2017 under Cre.ker Entertainment and quickly rose to fame with their powerful performances and diverse concepts. The nine‑member group—originally formed through the reality show "Boys24"—has delivered hits such as "The Stealer," "D.D.D," and the chart‑topping "Maverick" which secured a top‑10 placement on the Circle Album Chart. Their 2022 EP "THE BOYZ 5th Mini Album: BE AWARE" showcased a mature sound and earned them a spot on Billboard’s World Albums chart, confirming their growing international footprint.

Juyeon, born Kim Ju‑yeon on June 23, 2002, has been praised for his smooth vocal tone and stage presence. While primarily known for his singing, he has dabbled in acting before, appearing in web dramas like "Love & Love Like a Summer" (2022) and the SBS drama "The Dreaming Boy Is a Realist" (2023). His acting chops have been noted by critics, but a dedicated agency could provide the structure needed for larger roles.

In recent years, K‑pop idols have increasingly pursued parallel acting careers, with agencies playing a crucial role in securing scripts, managing schedules, and negotiating contracts. Agencies such as SM Entertainment and JYP have dedicated acting divisions, and the trend has proven profitable for both idols and production houses.

## What Happened

ACE FACTORY, a boutique agency known for nurturing young talent in music and acting, released an official statement on April 14: “We are thrilled to announce that we have signed an exclusive contract with BOYZ member Juyeon. Our agency will fully support his activities with THE BOYZ while also expanding his career into acting.” The press release emphasized a collaborative approach, noting that the agency will coordinate with Cre.ker to ensure Juyeon’s group commitments remain uninterrupted.

The announcement was accompanied by a series of high‑resolution profile photos featuring Juyeon in casual streetwear, a sleek black suit, and a minimalist studio backdrop. Fans quickly shared the images on Twitter, Instagram, and Weverse, flooding the hashtag #JuyeonACEFACTORY with over 20,000 posts within hours. In the photos, Juyeon’s confident stare and refined styling hint at a more mature public image, aligning with his upcoming acting ambitions.

While specific drama titles have not been disclosed, ACE FACTORY hinted that negotiations are already underway for a supporting role in a mid‑year K‑drama slated for a 2026 release on a major streaming platform. The agency also confirmed that Juyeon will continue to promote with THE BOYZ’s upcoming comeback, expected later this year, though exact dates remain unannounced.

## Why This Matters

Juyeon’s move reflects a broader shift in the K‑pop industry, where idols are increasingly positioning themselves as multi‑talented entertainers. By securing a dedicated agency for acting, Juyeon can access a wider network of casting directors and production companies, potentially landing higher‑profile roles that could elevate his personal brand beyond the music sphere.

For THE BOYZ, the news reinforces the group’s stability and growth. Fans often worry when members sign with external agencies, fearing scheduling conflicts. ACE FACTORY’s explicit commitment to coordinate with Cre.ker eases those concerns, suggesting that Juyeon’s acting schedule will be carefully balanced with group promotions, comebacks, and tours. This cooperative model could become a template for other groups navigating similar dual‑career paths.

From an industry standpoint, the partnership highlights the rising importance of boutique agencies that specialize in cross‑media talent management. ACE FACTORY’s reputation for personalized career development may attract other idols seeking more focused support, challenging the dominance of the big three agencies.

## What Fans Can Expect

In the near term, fans should keep an eye out for teaser images and concept photos for THE BOYZ’s next comeback, likely slated for the latter half of 2026. The group’s comeback will probably be available on major streaming platforms such as Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music, with the music video premiering on YouTube.

On the acting front, while exact titles are still under wraps, industry insiders predict Juyeon could appear in a romantic‑comedy or a youth‑drama, genres that align with his previous roles. Once confirmed, the drama will likely stream on platforms popular with international audiences—Netflix, Disney+, or Viu—making it easily accessible for global fans.

Additionally, ACE FACTORY hinted at a possible fan‑meeting or live‑stream event to introduce Juyeon’s new agency affiliation, which could be hosted on platforms like YouTube or V Live. Such events often include behind‑the‑scenes footage, giving fans a glimpse into his upcoming acting projects.
