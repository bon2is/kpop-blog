The story follows a detective who is forced to work with a man he despises to solve a series of serial murders. Set between 1988 and 2019, the series promises to be a gripping tale of misfortune and pursuit.

The series brings together two talented actors, Park Hae Soo and Lee Hee Joon, in a unique and captivating storyline. With its chilling teaser, 'The Scarecrow' is set to keep viewers on the edge of their seats.

The teaser for 'The Scarecrow' hints at a dark and suspenseful plot, with the two leads embarking on a dangerous mission to uncover the truth behind the serial murders.
