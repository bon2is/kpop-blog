Dayoung, a member of the K-Pop group WJSN, is scheduled to release a solo comeback in April. The news was announced through a teaser image on her official social media, hinting at a new release with a striking visual and the phrase 'What’s a girl to do'. This comeback follows the success of her previous solo release 'Body'.
---

## Our Take

The news of Dayoung's solo comeback is significant as it showcases her growth as an artist beyond her group activities. 

 This move is also contextually relevant in the K-Pop industry, where solo comebacks have become a trend, allowing artists to explore different genres and styles. 

 For fans of WJSN and Dayoung, this comeback is highly anticipated, especially after the success of 'Body', which demonstrated her potential as a solo artist. 

 The upcoming release is expected to further establish Dayoung's presence in the K-Pop scene, both as a member of WJSN and as a solo artist, and fans are eagerly waiting to see what she has in store for them in April.
