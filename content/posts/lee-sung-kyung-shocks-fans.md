MBC’s “In Your Radiant Season” has unveiled new stills ahead of its final episode, showing Lee Sung Kyung learning the truth from Lee Joo Yeon. The romance drama tells the love story between Sunwoo Chan and Song Ha Ran. The finale is expected to bring closure to the story.
---

## Our Take

The finale of “In Your Radiant Season” is highly anticipated as it will reveal the truth to Lee Sung Kyung's character, bringing a conclusion to the story. 

 The drama has been a heartwarming tale of love and self-discovery, with Lee Sung Kyung's character learning to open up and trust again. 

 This finale will be crucial in determining the career trajectory of Lee Sung Kyung, as it will showcase her acting range and ability to carry a drama. 

 The success of the finale will also impact the K-Drama industry, as it will set a precedent for future romance dramas and their ability to captivate audiences.
