In the upcoming tvN drama “Undercover Miss Hong,” Park Shin Hye stars as an elite financial officer who disguises herself as a 20-year-old. ITZY’s Yuna will play a crucial role in assisting her character’s transformation. The series is set in the late 1990s, blending humor and a unique storyline.

## Our Take

The collaboration between ITZY’s Yuna and established actress Park Shin Hye in 'Undercover Miss Hong' reflects a growing trend in the K-Pop industry where idols are increasingly featured in acting roles, especially in significant dramas. This crossover not only showcases Yuna's versatility beyond music but also bridges the gap between K-Pop and K-Drama, attracting diverse audiences. The premise of an elite officer going undercover as a younger version of herself introduces a comedic yet relatable narrative that can resonate with viewers facing age-related workplace challenges. Furthermore, by setting the drama in the late 1990s, it taps into nostalgia, appealing to both younger viewers curious about past eras and older audiences reminiscing about them. Overall, this project could set a precedent for future collaborations between K-Pop idols and seasoned actors, potentially reshaping audience expectations for talent within the entertainment industry.
