## Background


CrazAngel debuted in July 2025 with their single 'I'm Just Me', a dance pop track that introduced the group's clear and distinctive sound. The group's members, Solmi, Daze, Shannie, and Ahon, each bring their own unique personality and style to the table, making CrazAngel a compelling and charismatic group. Prior to their debut, the members underwent rigorous training, honing their skills in singing, dancing, and rapping. Solmi, the leader of the group, has a strong background in dance, having trained in various styles including hip-hop and contemporary. Daze, on the other hand, has a passion for music production and has been involved in the creation of many of the group's songs.


The group's history is still relatively short, but they have already made a significant impact on the K-Pop scene. Their debut single 'I'm Just Me' was well-received by fans and critics alike, with many praising the group's energy and chemistry. Since then, CrazAngel has released several singles, including 'Picasso', which has further solidified their position in the industry. The group's music style is a fusion of different genres, including pop, rock, and hip-hop, making them a unique and exciting addition to the K-Pop landscape.


In addition to their music, CrazAngel is also known for their strong bond and camaraderie. The members have spoken about the importance of teamwork and communication in their group, and it is clear that they have a deep affection and respect for one another. This chemistry is evident in their performances, where they exude confidence and enthusiasm, drawing in fans and leaving a lasting impression.


## What Happened


In the exclusive interview, CrazAngel shared their thoughts on their latest release 'Picasso'. The song, which features a catchy and upbeat melody, is a testament to the group's growth and maturity. According to Solmi, the leader of the group, 'Picasso' is a song that showcases the group's true color and personality. 'We wanted to create a song that would reflect our growth and maturity as artists,' Solmi explained. 'We're really proud of how 'Picasso' turned out, and we're excited for our fans to hear it.'


The interview also touched on the group's experiences since their debut. The members shared stories about their training, performances, and interactions with fans. Daze spoke about the challenges of being a K-Pop idol, including the intense practice schedules and constant scrutiny. However, the members also expressed their gratitude for the opportunity to pursue their passion and share their music with the world. 'We're just grateful to be able to do what we love,' Ahon said. 'We're thankful for our fans and for the support they've given us.'
