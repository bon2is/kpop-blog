# Introduction


K-Pop fans around the world are always excited to see their favorite artists perform live, but sometimes unforeseen circumstances can get in the way. For NMIXX, a rising star in the K-Pop scene, their highly anticipated concert in Kaohsiung had to be postponed due to the threat of Typhoon Bavi. The concert, which was part of their 'EPISODE 1 : ZERO FRONTIER' tour, was initially scheduled to take place on July 11 but has been rescheduled for July 13.


## Background


NMIXX is a South Korean girl group formed by JYP Entertainment, one of the most renowned entertainment companies in the K-Pop industry. The group debuted in February 2022 with their single 'O.O' and has since gained a significant following worldwide. With their unique sound and captivating performances, NMIXX has been making waves in the K-Pop scene. Their recent releases have showcased their versatility and talent, solidifying their position as one of the most promising new groups in the industry.


The group's history is marked by their rigorous training and dedication to their craft. As part of JYP Entertainment, NMIXX has had the opportunity to work with some of the best producers and choreographers in the industry, honing their skills and preparing them for their debut. Since their debut, NMIXX has been actively promoting their music, performing on various music shows and concerts, and engaging with their fans through social media and fan meetings.


NMIXX's 'EPISODE 1 : ZERO FRONTIER' tour is a significant milestone in their career, marking their first solo concert tour. The tour has been highly anticipated by fans, who have been eagerly waiting to see the group perform their hits live. With their high-energy performances and impressive stage presence, NMIXX is expected to deliver an unforgettable experience for their fans.


## What Happened


On July 10, JYP Entertainment announced that the first day of NMIXX's concert in Kaohsiung, originally scheduled for July 11, would be postponed due to the threat of Typhoon Bavi. The local government had declared July 11 a 'typhoon day,' prompting the entertainment company to reschedule the concert for July 13. This decision was made to ensure the safety of the fans, staff, and the group members themselves, as the typhoon was expected to bring heavy rain and strong winds to the area.


The postponement of the concert was met with understanding from fans, who prioritized the safety of everyone involved. While the news may have been disappointing for some, fans were relieved that the concert was not canceled altogether. The new schedule for the concert has been announced, and fans are eagerly waiting for July 13 to see NMIXX perform live.


The impact of Typhoon Bavi on the concert is a reminder of the unpredictable nature of natural disasters and their potential to disrupt planned events. The K-Pop industry, in particular, has experienced its fair share of concert cancellations and postponements due to weather conditions, highlighting the importance of safety and flexibility in event planning.

The postponement of NMIXX's concert in Kaohsiung due to Typhoon Bavi is significant not only for the group but also for the K-Pop industry as a whole.
