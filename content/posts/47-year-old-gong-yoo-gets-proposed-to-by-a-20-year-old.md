In a shocking turn of events, renowned South Korean actor Gong Yoo found himself at the receiving end of a marriage proposal from a fan who is a staggering 27 years his junior. This unexpected moment was captured on the tvN program Goblin 10th Anniversary Trip, which aired on July 12th. The proposal has since become a topic of interest, sparking various reactions from the public.

## Background

Gong Yoo is a household name in South Korea, celebrated for his versatile acting skills and captivating on-screen presence. Born on July 10, 1979, Gong Yoo has been active in the entertainment industry for over two decades, with his career spanning a wide range of genres, from romantic comedies to historical dramas. One of his most notable roles to date is his portrayal of Kim Shin in the hit television series Goblin, alongside Kim Go-eun, Lee Dong-wook, and Yoo In-na. The drama, which aired from 2016 to 2017, was a massive success, not only in Korea but also internationally, and it played a significant role in cementing Gong Yoo's status as a leading actor in K-drama history.

Gong Yoo's career is marked by a series of critically acclaimed performances. Before Goblin, he gained widespread recognition for his roles in Coffee Prince and Silenced. More recently, he has appeared in the films Seobok and The Silent Sea, further diversifying his filmography. Throughout his career, Gong Yoo has received numerous awards and nominations, a testament to his dedication and talent as an actor. His ability to connect with audiences of all ages has made him a beloved figure in Korean entertainment, with fans spanning across different generations.

## What Happened

The proposal took place during the filming of the Goblin 10th Anniversary Trip, a program that brought the cast of Goblin back together to celebrate the drama's decade-long impact. The scene in question involved interactions between the cast members and their fans, leading to the unexpected moment when a 20-year-old fan mustered the courage to propose to Gong Yoo. The actor, known for his gracious and humble demeanor, was visibly flustered by the sudden proposal, showcasing a mix of surprise and appreciation for the fan's bold gesture. Although the details of the proposal, including the fan's identity and the actor's full response, have not been fully disclosed, the incident has already generated significant buzz among fans and media outlets.
