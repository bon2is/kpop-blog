Kim Bum has been unveiled in new stills for the upcoming SBS drama 'Sold Out On You', where he plays the role of an executive director of a global skincare brand. The new stills offer a glimpse into Kim Bum's character and his potential interactions with the lead characters.

## Our Take

The news of Kim Bum's role in 'Sold Out On You' matters because it highlights his versatility as an actor, taking on a wide range of characters throughout his career.
