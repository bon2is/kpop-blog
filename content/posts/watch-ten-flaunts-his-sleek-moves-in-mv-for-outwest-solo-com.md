TEN’s solo debut has finally arrived, and the K‑pop world is buzzing. On September 4, the former NCT member unveiled “OUTWEST,” a sleek, genre‑blending single accompanied by a high‑octane music video that highlights his signature dance precision. The track not only marks TEN’s first solo effort after parting ways with SM Entertainment, but also showcases his growth as a songwriter and producer, making it a must‑watch for fans worldwide.

## Background

Born Choi Seung‑min in 1996, TEN first entered the public eye as a member of SM Entertainment’s ambitious project group NCT, debuting with NCT U’s “The 7th Sense” in 2016. Over the next several years, he became known for his powerful vocals, dynamic rap verses, and, perhaps most memorably, his flawless dance performances on tracks like “Cherry Bomb,” “Kick It,” and the viral “Make A Wish (Birthday Song).” His versatility earned him a spot in multiple NCT sub‑units—U, Dream, and WayV—allowing him to explore a range of musical styles from hip‑hop to R&B.

In 2023, TEN made headlines when he announced his departure from SM Entertainment, citing a desire for artistic freedom and the opportunity to co‑found his own label, ILLIMNT. The move sparked speculation about his next steps, with fans hoping he would finally release solo material that truly reflects his personal taste. While he has contributed to songwriting for NCT projects, a full solo release had remained elusive—until now.

Since leaving SM, TEN has been active behind the scenes, collaborating with producers in Seoul and Los Angeles, and honing his production skills. He has also hinted at a more experimental sound, blending trap beats, synth‑wave textures, and melodic hooks. This background sets the stage for “OUTWEST,” a track that feels both familiar to longtime fans and fresh enough to attract new listeners.

## What Happened

On September 4, TEN dropped “OUTWEST” alongside a visually striking music video on YouTube, Vevo, and major streaming platforms such as Spotify, Apple Music, and Amazon Music. The single runs 3 minutes and 12 seconds, featuring a pulsating bass line, atmospheric synths, and a chorus that showcases TEN’s soaring falsetto. Notably, TEN is credited as a co‑composer and lyricist, confirming his hands‑on involvement in the creative process.

The MV opens with a neon‑lit cityscape, quickly transitioning to a series of meticulously choreographed dance sequences that highlight TEN’s trademark sleek moves. The visual aesthetic draws inspiration from cyber‑punk cinema, with glitch effects and mirrored reflections that echo the song’s title—a play on “outwest,” suggesting a journey beyond conventional borders.

In a brief press release, TEN explained, “‘OUTWEST’ is about stepping out of the familiar and embracing the unknown. I wanted the music and the visuals to feel like a portal to a new world, one where I can be completely myself.” The release has already amassed over 2 million views within 48 hours, and streaming numbers indicate a strong global response, with the track entering the iTunes K‑Pop chart in several countries.

## Why This Matters

TEN’s solo debut is significant on multiple fronts. First, it signals a successful transition from idol group member to independent artist—a path that few SM alumni have navigated smoothly. By releasing a self‑produced track under his own label, TEN demonstrates that artists can retain creative control while still achieving commercial impact.

Second, the song’s performance on streaming platforms hints at broader industry trends. Early data shows “OUTWEST” entering the Billboard World Digital Song Sales chart’s top 10, a notable achievement for a debut solo effort without the backing of a major label’s promotional machine. This mirrors recent successes from other former SM artists, such as EXO’s Kai and Red Velvet’s Wendy, who have also found chart traction with independent releases.

Third, the MV’s high production value underscores the evolving standards for solo K‑pop visuals. Despite operating under a smaller label, ILLIMNT invested heavily in cinematography, choreography, and post‑production, proving that indie projects can compete with major‑company releases in terms of visual impact. This could encourage more artists to pursue independent routes without sacrificing quality.

Finally, TEN’s involvement in songwriting adds to the growing narrative of idols becoming true musicians. Fans increasingly value authenticity, and TEN’s lyrical contribution—exploring themes of self‑discovery and boundary‑pushing—resonates with a global audience seeking deeper connection.

## What Fans Can Expect

Following “OUTWEST,” TEN hinted at a forthcoming EP slated for early 2025, which will likely expand on the experimental sound introduced in the single. He also announced a limited‑edition merch drop, featuring apparel designed in collaboration with streetwear brand Ader Error, available through ILLIMNT’s official store.

In terms of live performances, TEN is set to appear on major music shows such as Mnet’s “M Countdown” and SBS’s “Inkigayo” later this month, as well as a special showcase streamed on YouTube Live for international fans. While ticketed concerts have not yet been announced, industry insiders suggest a solo tour could be on the horizon once pandemic‑related restrictions fully lift.

Fans should keep an eye on TEN’s social media channels for updates on the EP release date, teaser images, and potential collaborations with other artists—rumors already point to a possible feature with a Western producer.
