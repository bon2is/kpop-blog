MBC's 'Perfect Crown' has released a teaser featuring IU as Seong Hui Ju, a chaebol heiress, proposing marriage to Grand Prince Ian, played by Byeon Woo Seok. The series is set in an alternate universe where Korea is a constitutional monarchy, telling the love story of the two characters. IU's bold proposal has garnered attention and excitement for the show's release.

## Our Take

The teaser for 'Perfect Crown' showcases IU's ability to take on strong and daring roles, which is a significant factor in her enduring popularity.
