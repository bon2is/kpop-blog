The buzz around Netflix’s upcoming **Solo Leveling** live‑action series has taken a fresh turn: South Korean actors **Sung Joon** and **Kim Si Ah** are reportedly joining the cast alongside **Byeon Woo Seok**. The news, first reported by News1 on September 3, has sparked a wave of speculation about the characters they might portray and how their addition could shape the series.

## Background

**Solo Leveling** began as a South Korean web novel in 2016, later evolving into a wildly popular web‑toon that amassed millions of readers worldwide. Its story follows Sung Jin‑woo, a low‑ranked hunter who gains the unique ability to “level up” after a mysterious event, turning him into the strongest hunter in a world plagued by deadly dungeons. The series’ blend of high‑stakes action, game‑like progression systems, and striking artwork made it a global phenomenon, topping platforms like **Webtoon** and **Tapas**, and earning a spot on **Billboard’s** “Top 10 Most‑Streamed Korean Songs” playlist after the OST releases.

In early 2024, Netflix announced a live‑action adaptation, marking one of the biggest Western‑K‑Pop‑culture collaborations to date. The streaming giant confirmed **Byeon Woo Seok**—known for his roles in *The King: Eternal Monarch* and *Vincenzo*—as the lead, playing the titular Sung Jin‑woo. The casting choice generated excitement because Byeon’s charismatic screen presence aligns well with the character’s evolution from weak to unstoppable.

The series is part of Netflix’s broader push into Asian content, joining titles like *All of Us Are Dead* and *Squid Game* in its slate of Korean productions. While the exact release window remains “expected on major streaming platforms” later this year, the anticipation is already palpable among international fans who have been tracking every casting rumor.

## What Happened

On September 3, News1 published a report stating that **Kim Si Ah** and **Sung Joon** are set to join the **Solo Leveling** cast. The article cited unnamed insiders but did not specify which characters the actors would portray. Kim Si Ah, best known for her supporting roles in *The School Nurse Files* and *Love Alarm*, is represented by **VARO Entertainment**, while Sung Joon, a rising star from dramas like *My Name* and *Vincenzo*, is managed by **moahn Entertainment**.

Both agencies issued brief statements acknowledging the reports. VARO Entertainment said, “We appreciate the interest in Ms. Kim Si Ah’s upcoming projects and will provide official updates when available.” Moahn Entertainment responded similarly, noting, “Mr. Sung Joon is currently reviewing the offer and will confirm his participation through official channels.” No further details were released, and Netflix has not yet issued a formal announcement confirming the additions.

The timing of the report aligns with Netflix’s recent pattern of releasing casting news in phases to maintain momentum. Earlier this year, the platform revealed Byeon Woo Seok’s involvement, followed by a teaser showcasing the series’ visual style—dark, gritty dungeons contrasted with sleek, modern Seoul. The inclusion of Sung Joon and Kim Si Ah could signal an expansion of the story’s ensemble, possibly introducing new hunters, guild leaders, or pivotal antagonists.

## Why This Matters

Adding two recognizable actors to the **Solo Leveling** roster serves multiple strategic purposes. First, it broadens the series’ appeal beyond the core fanbase of the web‑toon. Both Sung Joon and Kim Si Ah have cultivated dedicated followings through their previous Netflix dramas, which can translate into higher viewership numbers for the new series. In the streaming era, star power often drives subscriber retention, especially for high‑budget adaptations.

Second, the casting hints at Netflix’s commitment to delivering a faithful yet expansive adaptation. The original web‑toon features a large cast of hunters, each with distinct abilities and backstories. By bringing in additional talent, the production can explore sub‑plots that were previously limited to the source material, potentially enriching the narrative and offering fresh content for both newcomers and longtime fans.

Finally, the news underscores the growing synergy between Korean entertainment agencies and global streaming platforms. Agencies like VARO and moahn are increasingly open to negotiating with Netflix, recognizing the platform’s reach. This partnership model mirrors successful collaborations such as **BTS**’s partnership with Disney+ for *BTS: Burn the Stage* and **BLACKPINK**’s documentary on Hulu, setting a precedent for future K‑Pop and K‑Drama cross‑overs.

## What Fans Can Expect

While official character assignments remain unconfirmed, fans can speculate based on the actors’ previous roles. Kim Si Ah’s talent for portraying nuanced, emotionally layered characters could see her cast as a key ally or a complex antagonist—perhaps a high‑ranked hunter with a hidden agenda. Sung Joon’s action‑oriented background makes him a strong candidate for a rival hunter or a guild leader who challenges Jin‑woo’s ascent.

Netflix has indicated that **Solo Leveling** will debut later in 2026, with a global rollout across all regions where the service operates. Subscribers can expect the series to be available on the platform’s standard catalog, meaning no extra purchase is required beyond a Netflix subscription. As the release date approaches, Netflix typically releases a trailer and a full cast list, so fans should keep an eye on the platform’s official YouTube channel and social media for updates.

## 💭 Editor's Take

💭 **Editor’s Take:** The rumored addition of Sung Joon and Kim Si Ah feels like Netflix is playing a smart game of “level‑up”—just like the series’ protagonist. By bolstering the cast with actors who already have a rapport with the streaming audience, Netflix not only guarantees a built‑in viewership but also promises a richer, more diversified storyline. As someone who followed the web‑toon from its early days, I’m thrilled to see the adaptation potentially expand beyond the core Jin‑woo arc. If the chemistry between Byeon Woo Seok, Sung Joon, and Kim Si Ah clicks, we could be looking at a series that satisfies both die‑hard fans and newcomers alike. Stay tuned, because the next teaser might finally reveal who these new faces will become in the world of **Solo Leveling**.
