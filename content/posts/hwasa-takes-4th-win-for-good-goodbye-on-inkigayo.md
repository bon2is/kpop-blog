On the January 11 episode of 'Inkigayo', Hwasa's 'Good Goodbye' triumphed against tough contenders including LE SSERAFIM's 'SPAGHETTI' and ILLIT's 'NOT CUTE ANYMORE', earning her a commendable total of 7,249 points. This win marks her fourth music show trophy for this track.

Hwasa's fourth win for 'Good Goodbye' is a significant milestone not only for her as a solo artist but also for MAMAMOO as a group. This achievement underscores her ability to stand out in a competitive landscape, especially amidst the rising popularity of groups like LE SSERAFIM. This win not only benefits Hwasa's career but also elevates the MAMAMOO brand, showcasing its versatility and the strength of its members as solo performers. Overall, 'Good Goodbye' is a reflection of Hwasa's artistic evolution and her ability to connect with fans, emphasizing the importance of personal expression in K-Pop.
