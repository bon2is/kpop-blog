JTBC's new fantasy rom-com drama, 'Human X Gumiho', has confirmed its cast lineup, including Jun Ji Hyun and Ji Chang Wook. The drama is about a seductive being and a human who attracts mythical creatures. The cast also includes Cha Joo Young and Kang You Seok.
---

## Our Take

This news matters because it brings together two of Korea's most beloved stars, Jun Ji Hyun and Ji Chang Wook, in a unique fantasy rom-com setting. 

 The K-Pop and K-Drama industries are closely intertwined, with many idols crossing over into acting and vice versa, making this drama a highly anticipated event. 

 For fans of Jun Ji Hyun and Ji Chang Wook, this drama is a dream come true, offering a chance to see their favorite stars in a new and exciting context. 

 The addition of Cha Joo Young and Kang You Seok to the cast adds depth and complexity to the story, making 'Human X Gumiho' a must-watch for fans of Korean drama and fantasy romance.
