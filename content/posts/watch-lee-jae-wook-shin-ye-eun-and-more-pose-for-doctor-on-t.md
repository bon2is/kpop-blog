The world of K-Dramas is abuzz with the latest news from ENA, as the network gears up to release its highly anticipated drama “Doctor on the Edge”. The show has been making waves with its unique premise, which follows the love story of a public health doctor and a nurse on the notorious island of Pyeondong. At the heart of this compelling narrative are its leads, Lee Jae Wook and Shin Ye Eun, who recently posed for the drama's poster shoot, giving fans a glimpse into the exciting project.


## Background


Lee Jae Wook is a South Korean actor who has been steadily rising through the ranks of the K-Drama world. With a career spanning over a decade, he has appeared in a variety of roles, showcasing his versatility as a performer. Some of his notable works include “When the Devil Calls Your Name”, “Extraordinary You”, and “Do Do Sol Sol La La Sol”. His ability to portray complex characters with depth and nuance has earned him a loyal fan base, who are eagerly anticipating his latest outing in “Doctor on the Edge”. Shin Ye Eun, on the other hand, is a talented young actress who has already made a name for herself in the industry. With roles in dramas like “A-TEEN”, “He Is Psychometric”, and “More Than Friends”, she has demonstrated her range and chemistry with her co-stars. The pairing of Lee Jae Wook and Shin Ye Eun in “Doctor on the Edge” promises to bring a fresh and exciting dynamic to the small screen.


The drama “Doctor on the Edge” is set on the island of Pyeondong, a place notorious for its rugged landscape and harsh living conditions. The story follows Do Ji Eui, a public health doctor who is assigned to the island, and Yook Ha Ri, a nurse who is already stationed there. As they navigate the challenges of their jobs and the isolation of the island, they find themselves drawn to each other, sparking a romance that will change their lives forever. With its unique blend of medical drama, romance, and stunning scenery, “Doctor on the Edge” is shaping up to be one of the most compelling K-Dramas of the year.
