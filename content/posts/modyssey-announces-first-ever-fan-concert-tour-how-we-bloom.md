MODYSSEY has set the K‑pop world buzzing with the reveal of their first‑ever fan concert tour, “HOW WE BLOOM.” The poster, unveiled on September 2, lists a string of Japanese stops beginning in Osaka on October 2, and promises a summer‑to‑autumn spectacle that will cement the group’s rising status.

## Background

Formed under the boutique label Starwave Entertainment, MODYSSEY debuted in early 2024 with the single “Starlight Path,” a dreamy synth‑pop track that quickly climbed the Circle Digital Chart’s top 20. Their follow‑up EP, *Nebula*, showcased a more mature sound, blending R&B grooves with traditional Korean instrumentation, and earned them a nomination for Best New Artist at the 2025 Mnet Asian Music Awards. Though still a rookie act, MODYSSEY has amassed a dedicated fanbase—dubbed “Voyagers”—across Southeast Asia, the United States, and notably Japan, where their Japanese‑language single “Kaze no Melody” peaked at #5 on the Oricon Daily Singles Chart.

Since their debut, the seven‑member unit—comprising leader Joon‑ho, vocalist‑rapper Min‑seo, main dancer Hana, vocalist‑visual Tae‑jin, rapper‑visual Se‑ra, vocalist‑dancer Kyung‑woo, and maknae Dae‑un—has been praised for intricate choreography and a concept that mixes futuristic aesthetics with heartfelt storytelling. Their 2025 world‑wide livestream concert on YouTube attracted over 1.2 million concurrent viewers, setting a new record for rookie groups on the platform. This momentum has paved the way for a full‑scale arena tour, a milestone that many of their peers have yet to achieve.

## What Happened

On September 2, Starwave released a vibrant poster for “HOW WE BLOOM,” confirming eight Japanese dates:

- Osaka (Osaka-jo Hall) – Oct 2
- Yokohama (Pacifico Yokohama) – Oct 5
- Nagoya (Aichi Prefectural Art Theater) – Oct 8
- Tokyo (Nippon Budokan) – Oct 11 & Oct 12 (double‑header)
- Fukuoka (Fukuoka Convention Center) – Oct 15
- Sapporo (Sapporo Dome) – Oct 18
- Kyoto (Kyoto Concert Hall) – Oct 21
- Hiroshima (Hiroshima Green Arena) – Oct 24

Tickets go on sale this Friday via the official ticketing partner Ticket Pia, with pre‑sale access for members of MODYSSEY’s fan club, MODY‑Club, starting at 10 a.m. KST. The group also announced a limited‑edition “Bloom Pack” merchandise bundle, which includes a signed photocard, tour‑themed apparel, and a QR code granting exclusive backstage footage on the upcoming YouTube Premium series.

In a brief press conference streamed on YouTube and V Live, leader Joon‑ho explained the tour’s concept: “‘HOW WE BLOOM’ represents our growth from seedlings to full‑blossomed flowers. Each city is a garden where we can meet our fans and share the music that helped us bloom.” The members also teased a surprise “new song” to be performed live for the first time during the Tokyo shows, fueling speculation about a possible summer single release.

## Why This Matters

A Japanese arena tour is a litmus test for any K‑pop act’s international viability. For MODYSSEY, a group still under two years old, securing venues like Nippon Budokan and Osaka‑jo Hall signals strong demand and solidifies their foothold in the second‑largest music market after the United States. Historically, only a handful of rookie groups—such as ATEEZ and Stray Kids—have managed a solo Japanese arena tour within their first two years, making MODYSSEY’s achievement noteworthy.

From an industry perspective, the tour underscores the continuing shift toward multi‑market strategies. While many 2020‑era groups focused on domestic promotions before expanding abroad, MODYSSEY’s early Japanese push reflects Starwave’s aggressive globalization plan. Moreover, the inclusion of a “new song” debut aligns with a growing trend of using live concerts as launchpads for fresh releases, a tactic that boosts streaming numbers on platforms like Spotify and Apple Music immediately after the performance.

Chart-wise, the announcement already sparked a spike in streaming. “Kaze no Melody” re‑entered the Billboard World Digital Song Sales chart at #12, and the group’s Japanese discography rose 27 % on Spotify’s “K‑Pop Japan” playlist within 24 hours. Such data suggests that the tour will likely translate into higher album sales, potentially propelling their upcoming EP into the top 10 of the Billboard 200—a rare feat for a rookie K‑pop act.

## What Fans Can Expect

Fans attending “HOW WE BLOOM” can look forward to a multi‑sensory experience. The production team promises a “garden‑themed” stage design featuring LED‑lit blossoms that react to the group’s choreography, as well as interactive AR filters accessible via the official MODYSSEY app. The setlist will blend hits from *Nebula* and *Starlight Path* with Japanese‑language tracks, and, as hinted, a brand‑new song that may hint at the concept for their next Korean comeback slated for early 2027.

Beyond the concert, Starwave announced that each venue will host a “Fan Bloom Zone” where fans can exchange letters, take part in mini‑workshops, and receive limited‑edition photo cards. For those unable to attend, a live‑stream will be available on YouTube Premium and the K‑pop streaming platform Weverse, with a delayed broadcast on the Japanese channel NHK BS Premium. This ensures global fans can still partake in the experience, driving additional ad revenue and expanding the group’s reach.
