The Korean Business Research Institute has released the January brand reputation rankings for male idol groups, highlighting data collected between December 10, 2025, and January 10, 2026. BTS maintained their top position, reflecting their sustained popularity and engagement despite the ever-evolving landscape of K-Pop.

---

## Our Take

The consistency of BTS at the top of the brand reputation rankings is indicative of their unparalleled influence in the K-Pop industry. Despite the increasing competition from emerging groups, BTS’s unique ability to connect with fans and maintain a strong international presence sets them apart. Their brand power is not just a reflection of musical talent but also a robust engagement strategy that encompasses social media interaction, community involvement, and media presence. This dominance also highlights the significance of brand reputation in the K-Pop ecosystem, where public perception can directly influence a group's commercial success and opportunities. As newer groups continue to debut and challenge the status quo, it will be interesting to observe how they leverage data and fan relations to climb these rankings. The rankings serve as a litmus test for the industry, revealing shifting trends in consumer behavior and public interest. Moving forward, it will be crucial to monitor how BTS adapts to this dynamic environment and how other groups strategize to enhance their brand reputation amidst the fierce competition.
