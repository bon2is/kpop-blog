Nine members of THE BOYZ have filed a criminal complaint against their agency's CEO, Cha Ga Won, for embezzlement. This comes after their demand for contract termination from ONE HUNDRED. The complaint was filed on April 21, as reported by a media outlet.

## Our Take

This news is significant as it highlights the ongoing issues between K-Pop groups and their management agencies. 

 The move by THE BOYZ members shows their determination to take control of their careers and finances, a trend seen in recent years with other groups seeking better treatment and transparency from their agencies. 

 For fans, this development may lead to uncertainty about the group's future activities and comeback schedule. However, it also demonstrates the members' commitment to protecting their rights and interests. 

 The outcome of this complaint will be closely watched, as it could have implications for the broader K-Pop industry and the relationships between artists and their agencies.
