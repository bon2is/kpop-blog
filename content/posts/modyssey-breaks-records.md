MODYSSEY, a new group formed through the 'BOYS II PLANET' spin-off 'PLANET C : HOME RACE', has made a strong debut with their first single album '1.Got Hooked: An Addictive Symphony'. According to Hanteo Chart, the album achieved the 2nd highest 1st-week sales of any artist to debut in 2026. This milestone marks a promising start to MODYSSEY's career.
