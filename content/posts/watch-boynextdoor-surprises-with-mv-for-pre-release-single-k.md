## Background

BOYNEXTDOOR is a South Korean boy group formed by KOZ Entertainment, a subsidiary of ADOR, a label under HYBE. The group consists of six members: Sunwoo, Riwoo, Jaehyun, Taesan, Woonhak, and Hui. Although they are a relatively new group, having debuted in May 2023, BOYNEXTDOOR has quickly garnered attention for their unique sound and energetic performances. Their debut single 'Butterfly' showcased the group's potential, blending genres and styles in a way that captivated listeners. Since then, the group has been working tirelessly to hone their craft, and the release of 'Knock Knock' is a testament to their growth and dedication.

BOYNEXTDOOR's career highlights include their successful debut and the subsequent release of their first single album 'WHO!', which further solidified their position in the K-Pop landscape. The group's members have also been involved in various projects, including collaborations with other artists and appearances on music shows. This diversity in their early career has helped them build a strong foundation and garner a loyal fanbase. The upcoming release of their first studio album 'HOME' is highly anticipated, with 'Knock Knock' serving as a pre-release single that sets the tone for the album.

The group's history is marked by their rigorous training and preparation under KOZ Entertainment. Each member brings their unique talents and experiences to the table, contributing to the group's distinctive sound and style. From their early days as trainees to their current status as rising stars in the K-Pop world, BOYNEXTDOOR has demonstrated a commitment to their craft and a passion for music that resonates with fans.

## What Happened

The music video for 'Knock Knock' was released on May 11 at midnight KST, catching fans off guard and generating a buzz of excitement. The song itself is a product of collaboration between notable producers, including Block B's Zico and Pop Time, with BOYNEXTDOOR members Jaehyun and others co-composing the track. This blend of creative talents has resulted in a song that is both catchy and meaningful, with 'Knock Knock' exploring themes of love, longing, and connection. The music video, with its vibrant visuals and engaging storyline, complements the song perfectly, offering fans a comprehensive artistic experience.
