The K-Pop scene is abuzz with the news of EVAN (Heeseung), formerly of the popular group ENHYPEN, embarking on a solo journey. ## Background

EVAN, whose real name is Lee Hee-seung, rose to fame as a member of the South Korean boy group ENHYPEN. Formed by Belift Lab, a joint venture between CJ E&M and HYBE (formerly Big Hit Entertainment), ENHYPEN debuted in 2020 with the single 'Given-Taken'. The group quickly gained a massive following worldwide, thanks to their unique sound, stunning visuals, and captivating performances. As a key member of ENHYPEN, EVAN showcased his impressive vocal and dancing skills, leaving a lasting impression on fans and critics alike. With his solo debut, EVAN is poised to explore new musical territories and push the boundaries of his artistry.

ENHYPEN's discography boasts an array of hit singles and albums, including 'Border: Day One', 'Border: Carnival', and 'Dimension: Dilemma'. The group's music often incorporates genres like hip-hop, rock, and electronic dance music, making them a standout in the K-Pop scene. As a member of ENHYPEN, EVAN has been an integral part of the group's success, participating in the creation of their music and choreography. His experience and talents will undoubtedly influence his solo work, and fans are eager to see how he will express himself as a solo artist.

## What Happened

On June 11, concept posters for EVAN's solo debut single 'RIDE OR DIE' were unveiled, generating immense excitement among fans. The posters feature EVAN in various edgy, high-fashion outfits, exuding a sense of confidence and charisma. The release date for 'RIDE OR DIE' is set for June 22 at 6 p.m. KST, and fans can expect a digital single that showcases EVAN's vocal and visual prowess. A teaser clip has also been shared, offering a glimpse into the song's dark, intense atmosphere and EVAN's captivating performance.

The announcement of EVAN's solo debut has sparked a flurry of discussions among fans, with many speculating about the song's lyrics, composition, and music video.
