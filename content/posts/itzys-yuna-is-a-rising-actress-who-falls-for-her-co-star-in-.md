## Background
ITZY is a popular South Korean girl group formed by JYP Entertainment in 2019. The group consists of five members: Yeji, Lia, Ryujin, Chaeryeong, and Yuna. Since their debut, ITZY has released several successful singles and EPs, including 'Dalla Dalla', 'Icy', and 'Not Shy'. The group is known for their unique blend of genres, from hip-hop to electronic dance music, and their energetic live performances. Yuna, in particular, has been standing out for her vocal and visual talents, making her a fan favorite among the group's loyal followers.




Yuna's career has been on the rise since ITZY's debut, with the group achieving numerous milestones, including winning several music show awards and collaborating with international artists. As a member of ITZY, Yuna has also been a part of various variety shows and music programs, showcasing her charming personality and talents. With her growing popularity, it's no surprise that Yuna has been cast in her first leading drama role, marking an exciting new chapter in her career.




The webtoon 'My Bias, My Boss' has gained a significant following for its relatable storyline and engaging characters. The story follows the romance between the protagonist, who joins a company to meet her idol, and the company's CEO. The drama adaptation is expected to bring this beloved story to life, with Yuna playing the lead role of the protagonist. With her character's journey at the center of the story, fans are looking forward to seeing Yuna's portrayal of the character's emotions and experiences.




## What Happened
The upcoming drama 'My Bias, My Boss' has shared a sneak peek of Yuna in character, giving fans a glimpse of what to expect from the series. The drama is set to premiere on tvN, with a total of 12 episodes planned. The story will follow Yuna's character as she navigates her feelings for her co-star, played by Nam Da Reum, who takes on the role of the company's CEO. With the drama's release date approaching, fans are eagerly awaiting the premiere, excited to see Yuna's performance and the chemistry between the lead actors.




The drama's production team has shared that they are thrilled to have Yuna on board, praising her dedication and passion for the project. Yuna has also expressed her excitement about the role, stating that she is looking forward to bringing her character to life and working with the talented cast and crew. With the drama's unique storyline and talented cast, 'My Bias, My Boss' is expected to capture the hearts of viewers and leave a lasting impression on the K-drama scene.
