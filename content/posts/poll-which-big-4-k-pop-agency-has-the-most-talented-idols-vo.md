SM Entertainment is known for its highly produced music videos and choreographed performances, while YG Entertainment has a reputation for pushing the boundaries of K-Pop with its edgy and experimental sound. JYP Entertainment has been praised for its emphasis on artist development, with many of its groups undergoing rigorous training before debuting. Pledis Entertainment, on the other hand, has focused on creating a strong bond between its artists and fans, often incorporating fan feedback into its music and performances.
