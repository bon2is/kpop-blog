## Background

TWICE is a South Korean girl group formed by JYP Entertainment in 2015. The group consists of nine members: Nayeon, Jeongyeon, Momo, Sana, Jihyo, Mina, Dahyun, Chaeyoung, and Tzuyu. Since their debut, TWICE has released numerous hit songs, including 'Cheer Up', 'TT', and 'Fancy'. The group has won several awards, including the Melon Music Award for Artist of the Year and the Mnet Asian Music Award for Best Female Group. Recently, TWICE has been focusing on solo activities, with some members releasing solo music and participating in variety shows.

TWICE has always been known for their close relationship with their fans, known as ONCE. The group has been praised for their dedication to their fans, often engaging with them on social media and at fan meetings. However, with the increasing demands of their schedule and the pressure to constantly produce content, it's not surprising that one of the members has decided to set boundaries. The member in question has been known for her creativity and artistic skills, often sharing her designs and artwork with fans on social media.

## What Happened

In a recent message, the TWICE member announced that she would no longer be taking design requests from fans. According to the message, the member cited the increasing number of requests and the pressure to constantly produce content as the reason for her decision. The member apologized to fans who had been looking forward to receiving designs from her and expressed her gratitude for their understanding. The news has sparked a mixed reaction from fans, with some understanding the member's decision and others feeling disappointed and frustrated.

The decision has also sparked a conversation about the importance of artist boundaries and the potential implications for fan-artist interactions in the K-Pop industry. Some fans have expressed concern that the decision may set a precedent for other artists to follow, potentially limiting the interactions between fans and artists. Others have praised the member for prioritizing her mental health and well-being, citing the importance of setting boundaries in the demanding K-Pop industry.

## Why This Matters

The decision by the TWICE member to set boundaries with fans is significant for several reasons. Firstly, it highlights the importance of prioritizing mental health and well-being in the K-Pop industry. The industry is known for its demanding schedules and high expectations, and artists often face pressure to constantly produce content and engage with fans.
