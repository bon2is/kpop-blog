EXID's leader Solji surprised fans worldwide when she opened up about her past relationships in a candid YouTube interview, saying she treats ex‑boyfriends with respect and honesty. The revelation, posted on August 26 by the channel No Back Tak, has ignited conversations about dating norms in the K‑pop world, where idols often face strict image contracts.

## Background

EXID debuted in 2012 under Banana Culture and quickly rose to fame with hits like “Up & Down,” “Ah Yeah,” and the viral “DDD.” The quartet—LE, Hani, Hyelin, and Solji—has weathered lineup changes and a hiatus due to Solji’s health issues, returning stronger each time. Solji, born Heo Sol‑ji, is known for her powerful vocals and leadership, often serving as the group's spokesperson during interviews and press events.

Over the past decade, EXID has amassed a loyal international fanbase, with chart success on both South Korea’s Circle (formerly Gaon) and Billboard’s World Albums chart. Their 2023 EP “X” debuted at #3 on the Circle Album Chart and entered Billboard’s World Albums at #7, proving their staying power. Despite their musical achievements, the group has kept personal lives largely private—a common practice among K‑pop idols due to agency policies and fan expectations.

## What Happened

On August 26, the YouTube channel No Back Tak uploaded a video titled “EXID Solji, ‘That …’” (full title: “EXID Solji, ‘That’s How I Treat My Ex‑Boyfriends’”). In the 12‑minute interview, Solji explained that she maintains a polite distance from former partners, emphasizing honesty over drama. She said, “I think it’s important to be clear and kind. I don’t want to keep any resentment, so I simply wish them well and move on.”

She also addressed rumors that idols are prohibited from dating, noting that while her agency does not have an official ban, the pressure to maintain a “pure” image remains intense. Solji added that she has never hidden a relationship from fans, but she also never publicized it, choosing to keep the focus on her music.

The interview was uploaded in English subtitles, making it accessible to the global EXID fandom. Within hours, the video garnered over 300,000 views, and fans flooded the comments with supportive messages, praising Solji for her maturity and honesty.

## Why This Matters

Solji’s openness challenges a long‑standing taboo in the K‑pop industry: idols rarely discuss dating, and when they do, it often leads to backlash or contract repercussions. By speaking candidly, Solji contributes to a growing shift where idols like BTS’s Jungkook, BLACKPINK’s Rosé, and (G)I‑DLE’s Soyeon have hinted at personal relationships without severe fallout.

From an industry perspective, agencies are increasingly weighing the benefits of authenticity against the risk of alienating a fanbase that values the “idealized” image. Solji’s comments may encourage other idols to adopt a more realistic approach to personal life, potentially influencing future contract clauses.

Furthermore, the interview’s performance on YouTube highlights the power of digital platforms for fan‑artist communication. While traditional Korean media often filters such topics, global platforms like YouTube allow idols to control their narrative, reaching international audiences directly.

## What Fans Can Expect

Following the interview, EXID’s agency has not issued an official statement, but the group’s upcoming schedule suggests no immediate changes. Fans can look forward to EXID’s participation in the “K‑Pop Wave 2024” festival in Seoul this September, where they will perform new stage versions of their classic hits.

In addition, Solji hinted at a possible solo project later this year, mentioning that she is working on a “personal song” that reflects her growth. While no release date has been confirmed, the speculation has already sparked pre‑orders on platforms like Apple Music and Spotify, where her solo work will likely debut.

For those eager to watch the full interview again, it remains available on the No Back Tak YouTube channel, and a re‑upload with Korean subtitles is expected later this week.
