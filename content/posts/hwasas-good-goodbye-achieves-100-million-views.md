Hwasa's music video for 'Good Goodbye' has surpassed 100 million views on YouTube, making it her third solo MV to reach this milestone. The achievement comes just over three months after its release on October 15, 2025.

---

## Our Take

Hwasa's rapid ascent to 100 million views with 'Good Goodbye' underscores her growing influence in the K-Pop industry as a solo artist. This achievement not only highlights her individual artistry outside of MAMAMOO but also reflects the changing dynamics of K-Pop consumption, where solo artists are increasingly commanding attention and viewership comparable to their group counterparts. The swift success of 'Good Goodbye' may signal a shift in audience preferences, with fans more keen on supporting solo projects that showcase personal narratives and diverse musical styles. Additionally, as K-Pop continues to evolve, such milestones are essential for defining an artist's commercial viability and contributing to their long-term career sustainability. For Hwasa, reaching this milestone reinforces her position as a leading figure among the new generation of K-Pop soloists, paving the way for further exploration of her unique sound and artistic identity. This trend can encourage other artists to pursue solo endeavors, potentially reshaping group dynamics within the industry.
