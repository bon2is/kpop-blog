Seok Matthew and Park Gun Wook from the K-Pop group ZEROBASEONE have officially signed exclusive contracts with WAKEONE, as announced on February 9. This transition comes after Seok Matthew's tenure at MNH Entertainment, indicating a pivotal moment in their artistic paths.
---

## Our Take

This news is significant as it showcases the evolving dynamics within the K-Pop industry, particularly how artists navigate agency changes. Signing with WAKEONE could provide Seok Matthew and Park Gun Wook with fresh opportunities and resources crucial for their growth as artists.

In the context of the K-Pop industry, agency changes are not uncommon; they often signal rebranding or new strategic direction. WAKEONE has a solid reputation for nurturing talent and producing successful artists, which can enhance the group's visibility and marketability in a competitive landscape.

For fans, this means they can expect new music and possibly a shift in the group's artistic direction. The exclusive contract could also pave the way for more diverse collaborations and promotional activities, thus enriching the fan experience.

Overall, this move marks a new chapter for Seok Matthew and Park Gun Wook, potentially boosting their careers and bringing new energy to ZEROBASEONE. Fans will be keenly watching how this transition unfolds and what it means for their favorite artists' future endeavors.
