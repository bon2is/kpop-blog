Kang Dong Won, Park Ji Hyun, and Um Tae Goo star in the new comedy movie 'Wildsing' as a co-ed idol group called Triangle. The film follows their journey as they attempt to reunite and make a comeback. The movie teaser shows the group performing their comeback single, generating excitement among fans.
---

## Our Take

The news of Kang Dong Won, Park Ji Hyun, and Um Tae Goo forming a co-ed idol group for the movie 'Wildsing' has sparked interest among K-Pop fans. 

 The movie's concept of a co-ed group making a comeback is unique and intriguing, especially given the current K-Pop landscape dominated by single-gender groups. 

 This movie can be seen as an opportunity for the actors to showcase their musical talents and may even lead to real-life music collaborations. 

 For fans of Kang Dong Won, Park Ji Hyun, and Um Tae Goo, 'Wildsing' offers a fresh and entertaining storyline that combines music, comedy, and drama, making it a must-watch.
