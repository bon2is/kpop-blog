Stray Kids, one of the most popular K-Pop groups today, is gearing up for their highly anticipated comeback with the mini album 'THIS & THAT'. However, the excitement has been tempered by the news that member Seungmin will not be participating in some of the group's upcoming promotions due to health issues. This news has left fans concerned but also understanding, given the importance of prioritizing health and well-being.


## Background


Stray Kids is a South Korean boy group formed by JYP Entertainment in 2017. The group consists of nine members: Bang Chan, Woojin, Lee Know, Changbin, Hyunjin, Han, Felix, Seungmin, and I.N. Known for their energetic performances, catchy songs, and meaningful lyrics, Stray Kids have quickly gained a massive following worldwide. With hits like 'God's Menu', 'Back Door', and 'Case 143', they have proven their versatility and talent, making them one of the leading groups in the K-Pop scene. Their music often tops various charts, including the Billboard 200 and the Circle Chart, and they have broken numerous records in the process.


Their journey to stardom has been marked by hard work, dedication, and a passion for music. From their early days as trainees to their debut and subsequent rise to fame, Stray Kids have shown that they are a force to be reckoned with. Their fanbase, known as STAY, has been instrumental in their success, providing unwavering support and encouragement throughout their career. With their unique blend of genres, from hip-hop to pop, and their thought-provoking lyrics, Stray Kids have managed to stand out in a crowded industry and make a name for themselves.


## What Happened


On August 7 KST, JYP Entertainment announced that Seungmin would be sitting out certain promotions for Stray Kids' upcoming mini album 'THIS & THAT' due to his rehabilitation treatment. Although the details of his health issue were not disclosed, the agency emphasized that Seungmin's health and recovery are the top priority. This decision comes as the group is about to release new music, which is always an exciting but also demanding time for K-Pop idols. The announcement has sparked a mix of concern and understanding from fans, who wish Seungmin a speedy recovery while also looking forward to the group's new music.


The news of Seungmin's temporary absence from promotions has not dampened the excitement for 'THIS & THAT'. Fans are still eagerly awaiting the release, which is expected to be available on major streaming platforms such as Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music. For those interested in watching Stray Kids' comeback performances, they can tune into Korean music shows like Music Bank, Show! Music Core, and Inkigayo, which can be streamed on platforms like YouTube and V Live. The group's music videos and live performances are always highly anticipated, and this comeback is no exception.


## Why This Matters


Seungmin's health issue and subsequent decision to sit out certain promotions highlight the importance of prioritizing well-being in the demanding world of K-Pop. The industry is known for its rigorous training schedules, tight comeback timelines, and high performance expectations, which can take a toll on the physical and mental health of idols. By putting Seungmin's health first, Stray Kids and JYP Entertainment are setting a positive example for the industry, emphasizing that health should never be compromised for the sake of work.


This situation also underscores the strong bond between Stray Kids and their fans, STAY. Despite the disappointment of not having Seungmin participate in all promotions, fans have shown understanding and support, wishing him a speedy recovery and looking forward to his return. This kind of fan-idol relationship is a testament to the power of K-Pop fandoms, where fans not only support the music but also care deeply about the well-being of their favorite artists.


## What Fans Can Expect


While Seungmin's absence from certain promotions might be a setback for some fans, there is still a lot to look forward to with Stray Kids' comeback. The group has been teasing their new music, and based on their past releases, fans can expect high-quality songs and impressive performances. For those who want to show their support, the mini album 'THIS & THAT' will be available for streaming on platforms like Spotify and Apple Music, and fans can also purchase physical copies from online retailers or in-store at music shops.
