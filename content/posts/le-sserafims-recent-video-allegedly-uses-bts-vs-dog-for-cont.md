## Background

LE SSERAFIM debuted in 2022 under Source Music, an affiliate of HYBE (formerly known as Big Hit Entertainment), the same label that manages BTS. The group consists of five members: Sakura, Kim Chae-won, Huh Yun-jin, Kazuha, and Kim Garam. Since their debut, LE SSERAFIM has gained significant attention for their unique sound and style, which blends elements of pop, rock, and hip-hop. Their debut single, 'FEARLESS', was well-received by fans and critics, showcasing the group's potential and versatility. The members themselves come from diverse backgrounds, with some having previous experience in the entertainment industry, such as Sakura, who was a member of the Japanese girl group IZ*ONE.

LE SSERAFIM's career is still in its early stages, but they have already made a name for themselves in the competitive K-Pop landscape. Their music often explores themes of empowerment, self-discovery, and the challenges of growing up. The group's dynamic and the chemistry among its members have been praised, contributing to their growing fan base. As with any new group, there is a lot of anticipation and scrutiny over their actions, both on and off stage. This recent controversy, therefore, not only reflects on the group but also on the broader K-Pop industry and its practices regarding content creation and animal involvement.

## What Happened

The controversy began when a video surfaced allegedly showing LE SSERAFIM using BTS V's dog for content. The specifics of the video, including when it was filmed and the context in which the dog was used, are still somewhat unclear. However, the reaction from fans and the general public has been swift and decisive, with many expressing discomfort and even outrage over the situation. BTS V's dog, known for its adorable appearances in BTS content and social media, holds a special place in the hearts of the BTS fan base, known as the ARMY. The use of the dog by another group, especially without explicit consent or clear context, has been seen as a breach of privacy and an exploitation of the dog's celebrity status for the sake of content.

The incident has also sparked a wider conversation about the ethics of using pets in entertainment, particularly when those pets belong to public figures. The debate centers around the question of whether such actions constitute an invasion of privacy and if they set a problematic precedent for the treatment of celebrity pets. While some argue that the dog's appearance might have been a harmless gesture of friendship or collaboration between BTS V and LE SSERAFIM, others see it as a deeper issue that reflects the sometimes-blurred lines between personal and public life in the K-Pop industry.
