Jang Yoon Joo, Lee Jong Won, and TXT's Yeonjun are set to star as mentors in the new fashion survival show 'Kill It: Style Creator War'. The show will premiere in May on tvN. The lineup also includes Cha Jung Won, Shin Hyun Ji, Ahn Ah Reum, and Yanggaeng as mentors.
---

## Our Take

This news matters as it signals a growing trend of K-Pop idols venturing into the fashion industry. 

 The K-Pop industry has seen a surge in fashion-related collaborations and shows, with many idols showcasing their personal style and flair. Yeonjun's participation in 'Kill It: Style Creator War' is a testament to this trend. 

 For fans of TXT and Yeonjun, this is an exciting opportunity to see their favorite idol in a new and different role. The show promises to bring together a unique blend of fashion and competition, making it a must-watch for fans of the genre. 

 The show's premise and mentor lineup also raise questions about the current state of the fashion industry in Korea and how K-Pop idols are influencing it.
