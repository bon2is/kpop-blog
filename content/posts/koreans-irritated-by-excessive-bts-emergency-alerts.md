Koreans are expressing outrage over the excessive emergency alert text messages they have been receiving about BTS. The messages, which are typically reserved for critical information such as natural disasters or missing persons, have been used to inform the public about BTS's activities. This has led to widespread frustration and criticism from the Korean public, who feel that these alerts are an abuse of the emergency alert system.
---

## Our Take

The news of Koreans being bombarded with emergency alerts about BTS has sparked a heated debate about the appropriate use of the emergency alert system. 

 The K-Pop industry, known for its highly dedicated fanbase and extensive media coverage, has often pushed the boundaries of what is considered acceptable in terms of promotional activities. 

 This incident highlights the blurred lines between what constitutes a genuine emergency and what is simply a publicity stunt, raising questions about the responsibility of the entertainment industry and the government in regulating such messages. 

 For BTS fans, the issue may seem trivial, but for the general public, it underscores the potential for the emergency alert system to be exploited for non-essential purposes, potentially desensitizing people to genuine emergencies.
