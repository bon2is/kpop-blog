## Background

NewJeans is a South Korean girl group formed by ADOR, a subsidiary of HYBE (formerly Big Hit Entertainment). The group consists of five members: Minji, Hanni, Danielle, Haerin, and Hyein. NewJeans debuted on July 22, 2022, with their self-titled extended play, which featured the hit singles 'Attention' and 'Hype Boy'. The group's unique sound, which blends genres like pop, rock, and hip-hop, quickly gained them a dedicated fan base. NewJeans' music often incorporates catchy hooks, energetic beats, and thought-provoking lyrics, setting them apart from other K-Pop groups. Since their debut, NewJeans has released several successful singles and EPs, including 'OMG' and 'Get Up', solidifying their position as one of the most promising new groups in the K-Pop scene.

NewJeans' career has been marked by numerous milestones, including their debut on various music shows, such as 'Music Bank' and 'Show! Music Core'. The group has also appeared on popular variety shows, like 'Idol Room' and 'Weekly Idol', showcasing their charming personalities and talents. Minji, in particular, has been a fan favorite due to her stunning vocals, captivating stage presence, and kind heart. As a member of NewJeans, Minji has played a vital role in the group's success, and her potential return has sparked widespread interest among fans and industry insiders alike.
