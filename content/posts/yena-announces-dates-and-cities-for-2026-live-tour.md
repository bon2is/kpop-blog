On March 3, Yena revealed the itinerary for her highly anticipated “So Near, Yet So Far, Another Wo2ld” live tour, which includes several cities across Asia. The tour kicks off with two shows in Seoul on April 4 and 5, followed by performances in Macau, Taipei, and more.

Yena's announcement is a pivotal moment as it signifies her growing presence in the K-Pop industry.
