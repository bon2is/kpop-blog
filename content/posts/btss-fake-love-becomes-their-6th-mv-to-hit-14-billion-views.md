BTS has once again proven their immense popularity and influence in the music industry, as their music video for 'Fake Love' has hit 1.4 billion views on YouTube. This milestone is a testament to the group's enduring appeal and their ability to create music that resonates with fans worldwide. With this achievement, 'Fake Love' becomes BTS's sixth music video to surpass 1.4 billion views, joining the ranks of other highly successful videos like 'Boy With Luv,' 'DNA,' 'Dynamite,' 'MIC Drop (Steve Aoki Remix),' and 'IDOL.'
