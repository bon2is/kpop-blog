ONEUS is back on the road. With vocalist Seoho completing his mandatory military service, the five‑member boy group will embark on a brand‑new international tour, kicking off with two sold‑out shows in Seoul. The announcement has sent waves of excitement through the fandom, promising a fresh chapter for the group and a chance for global fans to see the full lineup perform together again.

## Background

Formed by RBW in 2019, ONEUS quickly earned a reputation for powerful choreography, eclectic music styles, and a storytelling approach that blends Korean mythology with modern pop. Their debut EP *Light* (2019) showcased a bold, cinematic sound, while the follow‑up *Raise* (2020) cemented their place on the Billboard World Albums chart, peaking at No. 5. Over the past three years, the group has delivered hits like “LIT,” “Valkyrie,” and “No Diggity,” each earning high placements on South Korea’s Circle Digital Chart and garnering millions of streams on Spotify and Apple Music.

In early 2022, ONEUS released their first full‑length album *Trickster*, which debuted at No. 2 on the Gaon Album Chart and entered the Billboard 200 at No. 126—an impressive feat for a K‑pop act still building its overseas presence. The group’s energetic live performances have been praised on shows such as *M Countdown* and *Music Bank*, and they have already toured Asia, performing in Japan, Thailand, and the Philippines.

Seoho, the group’s main vocalist, enlisted in the Korean army in March 2023. His absence was felt deeply by fans, as his soaring vocals are a hallmark of ONEUS’s sound. While the remaining members continued promotional activities, including a digital single released during his service, the group has always emphasized a “complete comeback” once every member returns.

## What Happened

On Wednesday, B Wave Entertainment released an official statement confirming that ONEUS will embark on a new international tour beginning with two consecutive concerts at Seoul’s Olympic Gymnastics Arena on October 12 and 13, 2024. The press release highlighted that the tour will cover major cities across North America, Europe, and Asia, with additional dates slated for 2025. While the full itinerary is still being finalized, confirmed stops include Los Angeles, New York, London, Paris, and Tokyo.

The agency also announced that tickets for the Seoul shows will go on sale next Monday via Yes24 and Interpark, with a pre‑sale for fan‑club members beginning at 10 a.m. KST. International fans can expect ticket links to be posted on the group’s official website and social media channels, with options for both standard and VIP packages that include exclusive merch and a meet‑and‑greet (subject to local health guidelines).

In a short video posted to ONEUS’s YouTube channel, members expressed their excitement: “We’re finally together again, and we can’t wait to share this energy with fans worldwide,” said Seoho, smiling as he rehearsed the new choreography for their upcoming single “Eclipse.” The teaser also showed snippets of a new stage design featuring LED walls and a kinetic set that will adapt to each city’s atmosphere.

## Why This Matters

The tour marks a pivotal moment for ONEUS, signaling their transition from a rising act to a fully established global brand. Historically, K‑pop groups that successfully complete a world tour after a member’s military hiatus—such as EXO’s “EXO PLANET #5 – EXplOration” and SHINee’s “SHINee World VI: Perfect Illumination”—have seen a resurgence in album sales and streaming numbers. Industry analysts predict a similar boost for ONEUS, especially as the group will likely promote a new EP alongside the tour.

From a market perspective, the timing aligns with the growing appetite for K‑pop concerts in Western venues. According to Billboard, 2023 saw a 38% increase in K‑pop ticket sales in the United States compared to 2022. ONEUS’s inclusion of major U.S. cities positions them to tap into this expanding fanbase, while their European dates cater to a market where groups like BTS and BLACKPINK have already set records.

Furthermore, the tour serves as a litmus test for RBW’s strategic push to diversify its roster internationally. If ONEUS can sell out arenas abroad, it strengthens the label’s negotiating power with streaming platforms such as Spotify and Apple Music, potentially leading to exclusive playlist placements and promotional campaigns.

## What Fans Can Expect

Fans should anticipate a multi‑dimensional concert experience. The setlist will likely blend classic hits like “Valkyrie” and “LIT” with tracks from the upcoming EP, including the title track “Eclipse,” which blends synth‑wave elements with traditional Korean instrumentation. Choreography is expected to be more intricate, reflecting the group’s growth over the past year.

In addition to music, the tour will feature interactive segments: a “Fan Spotlight” where fan‑submitted videos are displayed on the giant screens, and a limited‑edition merch line exclusive to each city. The VIP packages will also include a backstage tour and a signed photo book—highly coveted items for collectors.

Ticket sales for the Seoul shows are expected to sell out within minutes, given the group’s strong domestic fanbase. International tickets will likely follow a similar pattern, especially in markets where ONEUS has previously held fan meetings.
