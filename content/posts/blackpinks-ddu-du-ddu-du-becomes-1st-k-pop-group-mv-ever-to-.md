# Introduction

BLACKPINK has once again broken records and made history in the K-pop scene. On July 25, the music video for their hit song 'DDU-DU DDU-DU' reached an unprecedented 2.4 billion views on YouTube, making it the first K-pop group music video to ever achieve this feat. This milestone is a testament to the group's immense global popularity and their ability to consistently produce chart-topping hits.

## Background

BLACKPINK is a South Korean girl group formed by YG Entertainment in 2016. The group consists of four members: Rosé, Jennie, Lisa, and Jisoo. Since their debut, BLACKPINK has been known for their unique sound, which blends genres such as pop, hip-hop, and electronic dance music. Their music often features strong, empowering lyrics and catchy melodies, which have resonated with fans worldwide. Some of their most notable hits include 'Whistle,' 'Playing with Fire,' and 'As If It's Your Last.'

The group has released several successful albums and EPs, including 'Square One,' 'Square Up,' and 'The Album.' Their music has topped various charts, including the Billboard Hot 100 and the UK Singles Chart. BLACKPINK has also broken numerous records in the K-pop industry, including becoming the first K-pop girl group to perform at Coachella and to have a music video reach 1 billion views on YouTube.

In recent years, BLACKPINK has been focusing on solo activities, with each member pursuing their own projects. Rosé released her solo single 'On the Ground,' while Jennie collaborated with artist G-Dragon on the song 'Lovesick Girls.' Lisa and Jisoo have also been working on their own solo music, with Lisa releasing her single 'Lalisa' and Jisoo starring in the K-drama 'Snowdrop.'

## What Happened

The music video for 'DDU-DU DDU-DU' was released on June 15, 2018, and it quickly gained popularity worldwide. The song's catchy hook and addictive beat, combined with the group's energetic and charismatic performance, made it an instant hit. The music video features the members performing intricate choreography and showcasing their individual personalities. The video's success can be attributed to the group's strong fan base, known as BLINK, who have been tirelessly streaming and promoting the song since its release.

According to YouTube, 'DDU-DU DDU-DU' is the first K-pop group music video to reach 2.4 billion views, surpassing other popular K-pop videos such as BTS's 'Boy With Luv' and 'DNA.' This achievement is a significant milestone for BLACKPINK, solidifying their position as one of the most popular and influential K-pop groups globally. The group's record label, YG Entertainment, has expressed their gratitude to fans for their support and announced that they will continue to work hard to produce high-quality music and content for fans.

## Why This Matters

BLACKPINK's achievement is significant not only for the group but also for the K-pop industry as a whole. It demonstrates the growing global popularity of K-pop and the ability of Korean artists to compete with international acts. The success of 'DDU-DU DDU-DU' has paved the way for other K-pop groups to break into the global market and has inspired a new generation of K-pop fans. The song's impact can be seen in the many covers and remixes that have been created by fans and artists alike, showcasing the song's timeless appeal and versatility.

The achievement also highlights the power of social media and online platforms in promoting K-pop. YouTube has played a crucial role in the global dissemination of K-pop, providing a platform for artists to reach a wider audience and for fans to connect with each other. The success of 'DDU-DU DDU-DU' is a testament to the effectiveness of online marketing and promotion in the K-pop industry.
