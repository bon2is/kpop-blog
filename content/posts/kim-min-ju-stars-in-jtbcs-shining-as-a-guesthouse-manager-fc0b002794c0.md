JTBC's upcoming drama 'Shining' stars Kim Min Ju as Mo Eun Ah, a guesthouse manager who reunites with her first love, Park Jinyoung, in her 30s. The series focuses on young adults navigating their past relationships and the evolving nature of trust.

---

## Our Take

The premise of 'Shining' taps into a universal theme: the bittersweet nature of first love and the concept of revisiting significant past relationships. In K-Pop and its associated dramas, nostalgia plays a crucial role in storytelling, often resonating deeply with audiences who are navigating their own life phases. Kim Min Ju's character, a guesthouse manager, symbolizes warmth and hospitality, mirroring the emotional support that friendships and love provide. This setting allows for an exploration of growth as the characters, once isolated in their youthful world, find solace and guidance in each other. The casting of Kim Min Ju and Park Jinyoung is particularly interesting, given their established fan bases and previous works that showcase their chemistry. As the K-Pop industry increasingly merges with cinematic storytelling, 'Shining' could serve as a benchmark for future dramas that blend romance with themes of maturity, offering viewers a chance to reflect on their own journeys and relationships in a relatable, engaging format.
