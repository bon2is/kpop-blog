## Our Take

The reaction to Jennie's photos highlights the double standards that female K-Pop idols often face. 

 On one hand, Jennie is praised for her confidence and self-expression. On the other hand, she is criticized for being too provocative or revealing. 

 This debate is not new in the K-Pop industry, where idols are often expected to maintain a certain image or persona. 

 For Jennie and BLACKPINK, this controversy may actually boost their popularity and reinforce their reputation as bold and unafraid to challenge norms.
