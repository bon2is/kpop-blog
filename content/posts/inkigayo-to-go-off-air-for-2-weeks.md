In a surprise move, SBS announced that its long-running music show Inkigayo will be going off the air for two consecutive weeks, leaving fans wondering what prompted this decision. Inkigayo, also known as The Music Trend, is a South Korean music program that has been broadcast by SBS since 1991. The show has a rich history, having been hosted by various K-Pop idols and celebrities over the years, including BTS's Jungkook, BLACKPINK's Jisoo, and EXO's Baekhyun. Inkigayo is known for its live performances, chart rankings, and special segments, making it a must-watch for K-Pop fans around the world. The show has played a significant role in promoting K-Pop globally, with many international fans tuning in to watch their favorite artists perform live. ## What Happened


On July 30, SBS announced through its official social media account that Inkigayo would not air on August 2 and 9. The announcement stated, "'SBS Inkigayo' will not air on August 2 and 9. We kindly ask for your understanding and look forward to seeing you again on August 16!" The reason for the hiatus was not specified, leaving fans to speculate about the cause. According to SBS's programming schedule, a rerun of a previous episode will air in place of the new episodes on August 2 and 9. This news has sparked a mix of reactions from fans, with some expressing disappointment and others understanding the need for a break.
