KISS OF LIFE's Haneul has announced changes to her schedule due to health issues, specifically a sore throat and cold symptoms. Her agency, S2 Entertainment, stated that her symptoms are gradually improving. Haneul will be rescheduling a couple of her upcoming activities to prioritize her health.
---

## Our Take

Haneul's decision to prioritize her health is a responsible move, considering the demanding nature of the K-Pop industry. 

 The K-Pop scene has seen several cases of artists pushing through illness, only to suffer from long-term consequences. Haneul's choice to reschedule her activities sets a positive example for her peers and fans. 

 This news may come as a disappointment to fans who were looking forward to Haneul's upcoming activities, but it's essential to prioritize her well-being. 

 The support from her agency and fans will be crucial in her recovery, and it's heartening to see the K-Pop community come together to promote artist health and wellness.
