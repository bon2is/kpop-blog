Seonghwa, a member of the K-Pop group ATEEZ, has been making waves in the music industry with his group's high-energy performances and catchy songs. However, in a recent interview with Arena Homme Plus, Seonghwa revealed a more personal side, discussing his love for photography and the process of building his own identity. This newfound interest in photography has not only allowed Seonghwa to express himself creatively but also to find satisfaction in the process of self-discovery.


## Background


ATEEZ, which debuted in 2018 under KQ Entertainment, has been steadily gaining popularity worldwide with their unique sound and captivating music videos. The group consists of eight members: Seonghwa, Yunho, Yeonguk, Mingi, Wooyoung, San, Jongho, and Hongjoong. With their debut single 'Pirate King', ATEEZ quickly gained attention for their powerful performances and intricate choreography. Since then, the group has released several successful albums and singles, including 'Treasure EP.1: All to Zero' and 'Wave', which have topped various music charts in Korea and beyond. Seonghwa, as a vocalist and visual of the group, has been an integral part of ATEEZ's success, known for his striking looks and impressive vocal range.


As a member of ATEEZ, Seonghwa has had the opportunity to participate in numerous photo shoots, which he has come to enjoy greatly. He has mentioned that these experiences have not only allowed him to showcase his personal style but also to learn more about himself. Seonghwa's passion for photography is evident in the way he approaches each shoot, always looking for new ways to express himself and push the boundaries of his creativity. This dedication to his craft has earned him a reputation as one of the most stylish and charismatic idols in the K-Pop scene.


## What Happened


In his interview with Arena Homme Plus, Seonghwa delved into the details of his photo shoot experiences, sharing his thoughts on the process of self-discovery and the importance of constantly reinventing oneself. He expressed his satisfaction with the creative freedom that photo shoots provide, allowing him to experiment with different styles and personas. Seonghwa also discussed the challenges of being an idol, where the pressure to maintain a certain image can be overwhelming at times. However, through his passion for photography, he has been able to find a sense of balance and fulfillment, using these experiences as an opportunity to grow and learn more about himself.


The interview with Arena Homme Plus is a testament to Seonghwa's dedication to his craft and his desire to connect with his fans on a deeper level. For fans of ATEEZ and K-Pop in general, this interview provides a fascinating insight into the world of idol culture, where the lines between reality and performance are often blurred.
