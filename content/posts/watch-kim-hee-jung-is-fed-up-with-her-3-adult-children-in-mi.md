The first glimpse of KBS 1TV’s new weekday drama **"Mission: Mompossible"** has set social media ablaze. In the teaser, veteran actress **Kim Hee Jung** delivers a blistering monologue, declaring she’s “fed up” with her three adult children’s antics, promising a roller‑coaster of family drama and humor that fans can’t wait to binge.

## Background

Kim Hee Jung is one of South Korea’s most respected screen veterans, with a career spanning over three decades. She first rose to fame in the 1990s with hit dramas like *"First Love"* and *"My Love, My Bride"*, and later cemented her status with powerful supporting roles in *"The Heirs"* (2013) and *"My Mister"* (2018). Known for her ability to oscillate between warm maternal figures and fierce, no‑nonsense characters, Kim has earned multiple Baeksang Arts Awards nominations and a reputation as a “queen of emotional depth.”

In recent years, Kim has continued to appear in high‑profile projects, most notably the 2022 drama *"The Red Sleeve"* and the 2023 series *"One the Woman"*, where her performances garnered praise from both Korean audiences and international fans on platforms like Viki and Netflix. Her consistent presence on the small screen has made her a familiar face to viewers who appreciate nuanced, age‑defying portrayals of women navigating modern Korean society.

"Mission: Mompossible" marks Kim’s first leading role in a weekday drama since the early 2010s. The series is produced by KBS Drama Division, a powerhouse behind long‑running hits such as *"What’s Wrong with Secretary Kim?"* and *"The King: Eternal Monarch"*. The show is slated for a Monday‑Friday slot at 20:30 KST, a coveted primetime window that typically garners high viewership ratings.

## What Happened

KBS released the official teaser on August 31, 2026, unveiling the series’ core premise: two mothers with starkly opposite parenting styles whose lives become entangled through their children’s relationships. Kim Hee Jung portrays **Lee Hye‑sun**, a mother who has sacrificed everything for her family, embodying the traditional, self‑effacing Korean mom archetype. Opposite her is **Jang Mi‑ra**, played by rising star **Park Ha‑neul**, a free‑spirited mother who pursued her own dreams before motherhood, often clashing with Hye‑sun’s expectations.

The teaser shows Hye‑sun confronting her three adult children—two sons and a daughter—about their lack of responsibility, delivering a line that quickly trended: *“I’m done being your safety net!”* The dramatic cut ends with a quick flash of the series title, accompanied by an upbeat, slightly mischievous soundtrack, hinting at both comedic and heartfelt moments.

The drama’s creator, writer **Lee Hyun‑soo**, known for *"My Golden Life"* and *"The World of the Married"*, explained in a press release that the series aims to explore “the evolving definition of motherhood in contemporary Korea.” Production began in early 2025, with filming locations across Seoul and the scenic countryside of Gangwon‑do, providing a visual contrast between urban hustle and familial tranquility.

## Why This Matters

"Mission: Mompossible" arrives at a time when Korean dramas are increasingly spotlighting multi‑generational narratives that resonate with both domestic and overseas audiences. The series taps into a universal theme—parent‑child tension in adulthood—while embedding uniquely Korean cultural nuances, such as the expectation of filial piety and the growing trend of adult children living independently.

Kim Hee Jung’s involvement adds gravitas and draws an older demographic that often feels under‑represented in K‑Drama viewership metrics. Her reputation for delivering emotionally resonant performances is expected to boost the drama’s ratings, potentially positioning it alongside other KBS weekday juggernauts like *"The World of the Married"* which broke viewership records in 2020.

From an industry standpoint, the drama’s focus on two contrasting mother figures reflects a broader shift in Korean media toward more diverse female representations. By juxtaposing traditional sacrifice with modern independence, the series may spark conversations about gender roles, especially as it airs during a period when South Korea is grappling with low birth rates and evolving family structures.

## What Fans Can Expect

The series will premiere on **KBS 1TV** on September 12, 2026, with episodes airing Monday‑Friday at 20:30 KST. International fans can look forward to streaming the drama on **Viu**, **Kocowa**, and **Netflix** (where available), as KBS typically licenses its weekday dramas to these platforms within a week of the Korean broadcast. Those eager to binge the series can also keep an eye on **Viki** for subtitled episodes.

Beyond the central mother‑daughter conflict, viewers can anticipate sub‑plots involving romance, career struggles, and a touch of mystery surrounding a hidden family secret revealed midway through the season. Supporting cast members include **Lee Dong‑wook** as Hye‑sun’s estranged husband and **Kim So‑hyun** as the rebellious daughter, promising strong chemistry and layered storytelling.
