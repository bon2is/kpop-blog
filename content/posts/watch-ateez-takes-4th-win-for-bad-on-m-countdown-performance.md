ATEEZ has once again proven their unstoppable momentum, taking home their fourth music show trophy for their hit song 'BAD' on the July 23 episode of M Countdown. This achievement is a testament to the group's dedication and the unwavering support of their fans, known as ATINY. With this win, ATEEZ continues to solidify their position as one of the most promising and successful K-Pop groups in recent years.


## Background


ATEEZ is an eight-member South Korean boy group formed by KQ Entertainment in 2018. The group consists of members Seonghwa, Yunho, Yeonguk, Mingi, Wooyoung, Jongho, San, and Hongjoong, each bringing their unique talents and personalities to the table. Since their debut, ATEEZ has been making waves in the K-Pop scene with their high-energy performances, catchy songs, and thought-provoking lyrics. Their discography includes a string of successful albums and singles, such as 'Treasure EP.1: All to Zero', 'Treasure EP.2: Zero to One', and 'Treasure EP.3: One to All', showcasing the group's musical growth and experimentation.


One of the key factors contributing to ATEEZ's success is their strong bond with their fans. ATINY, the group's fandom, has been instrumental in supporting ATEEZ throughout their career, from attending concerts and music shows to participating in online trends and promotions. This symbiotic relationship between the group and their fans has created a sense of community and belonging, driving ATEEZ's momentum and motivating them to continue pushing the boundaries of their music and performances.


In recent months, ATEEZ has been on a roll, releasing new music and embarking on international tours. Their latest single, 'BAD', has been a game-changer for the group, topping various music charts and earning them numerous music show wins. The song's dark, edgy concept and addictive melody have resonated with listeners worldwide, further cementing ATEEZ's status as a global K-Pop phenomenon.


## What Happened


On the July 23 episode of M Countdown, ATEEZ faced off against TXT's Yeonjun, who was promoting his solo single 'Ice Cream'. The competition was fierce, with both artists delivering impressive performances. However, in the end, ATEEZ emerged victorious, taking home the first-place trophy with a total of 6,754 points. The win was met with enthusiasm from ATINY, who took to social media to congratulate the group and celebrate their achievement.


The episode also featured performances by other notable K-Pop acts, including fromis_9 and OURBIRTHDAY. Fromis_9, a nine-member girl group formed by CJ E&M in 2017, showcased their bubbly and upbeat energy with their latest single. OURBIRTHDAY, a relatively new group, also made their presence known with a powerful and intense performance. The diversity of performances on the show highlighted the richness and variety of the K-Pop scene, with each group bringing their unique style and flair to the stage.


The winner announcement was a thrilling moment, with ATEEZ displaying their signature humility and gratitude. The group's leader, Hongjoong, expressed his thanks to the fans, staff, and members, emphasizing the importance of teamwork and perseverance in achieving their goals. The win was a testament to ATEEZ's hard work and dedication, as well as the unwavering support of their fans, who have been instrumental in driving their success.
