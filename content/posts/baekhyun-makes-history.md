Baekhyun's appearance on 'The Kelly Clarkson Show' is a notable achievement, as it showcases his growing global influence. 

 This performance will help introduce Baekhyun to a new audience, potentially expanding his fan base in the US. 

 As a member of EXO and a successful solo artist, Baekhyun's US TV debut demonstrates the increasing popularity of K-Pop worldwide. 

 This milestone is expected to Have a positive impact on Baekhyun's career, further solidifying his position as a leading K-Pop artist.
