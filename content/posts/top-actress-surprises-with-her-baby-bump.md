## Background

The actress, whose name has become synonymous with both critical acclaim and commercial success, first rose to fame in the early 2010s with a breakout role in a hit teen romance series. Since then, she has built an impressive résumé that includes award‑winning performances in dramas such as "Moonlit Dream," "City of Secrets," and the internationally streamed series "Echoes of the Past," which landed on Netflix and garnered a global fanbase. Her versatility—shifting seamlessly between melodramas, thrillers, and light‑hearted rom‑coms—has earned her multiple Baeksang Arts Awards and a reputation as one of the most respected actresses of her generation.

Beyond the small screen, she has also ventured into film, starring in the Cannes‑selected indie "Silent Horizons" and the blockbuster action‑drama "Steel Heart," both of which performed strongly on the Korean Box Office and charted on the Korean Film Council's yearly rankings. Her recent work includes a lead role in a high‑budget drama currently filming for a major Korean broadcaster, slated to air later this year on platforms like Viu and Viki, making it accessible to international viewers.

## What Happened

On August 20th, the actress uploaded a short video clip to her official Instagram account, offering fans a glimpse of the set for her upcoming drama. The camera panned over a meticulously designed living‑room set before focusing on the actress, who was wearing a comfortable, loose‑fitting top that clearly revealed her baby bump. In the caption, she wrote, "Feeling blessed and a little nervous as we count down to the most exciting chapter of my life. Thank you for all the love and support!" The post quickly amassed millions of likes and thousands of comments, with fans flooding the comment section with well‑wishes and speculation about her due date.

The production company confirmed the authenticity of the post in a brief statement, noting that filming would continue with necessary accommodations for the actress's health and safety. They also hinted that the drama will premiere in the fourth quarter of 2024, with streaming rights already secured for Netflix and Viki, ensuring global accessibility. No official birth date has been announced, but insiders suggest a delivery window between September and November, aligning with the actress's previous statements about a "fall" birth.
