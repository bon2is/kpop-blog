## Our Take

The upcoming episode of 'The Practical Guide to Love' is set to be an intense one, with Lee Ki Taek's dad and stepmom getting into a fight on set. 

 This news matters because it showcases the dramatic turn the series is taking, which could attract more viewers interested in character-driven storylines. 

 The K-Pop and K-Drama industry is known for its dramatic plot twists, and 'The Practical Guide to Love' is no exception, offering a fresh take on romance and relationships. 

 For fans of the series and Lee Ki Taek, this means a deeper exploration of his character's backstory and emotional depth, potentially leading to a more engaging viewing experience.
