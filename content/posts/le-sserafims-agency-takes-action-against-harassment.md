SOURCE MUSIC, the agency of K-Pop group LE SSERAFIM, has announced that it will restrict access to the group's Weverse DM service for users who send malicious messages. This decision comes after members received messages that violated community guidelines and caused emotional distress. The agency aims to protect its artists from online harassment.

## Our Take

This move by SOURCE MUSIC highlights the growing concern of online harassment in the K-Pop industry. 

 The K-Pop industry has seen a rise in online harassment cases, with many artists facing malicious comments and messages from fans or anti-fans.

 For fans of LE SSERAFIM, this move ensures a safer and more positive interaction with their favorite artists, while also promoting a respectful and considerate community.
