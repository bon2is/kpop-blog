TWICE's Mina has been criticized for wearing a veil during a recent tour performance. The use of a mask has sparked debate among fans and critics, with some expressing concern over the potential impact on her health and others questioning the artistic choice. This incident has drawn attention to the physical and mental demands placed on K-Pop idols.
---

## Our Take

The criticism Mina is facing highlights the intense scrutiny K-Pop idols are under, with every aspect of their performance being closely watched and judged. 

 The use of a mask or veil in performances is not uncommon in K-Pop, with some artists using it as a creative element to enhance their stage presence or convey a message. 

 This incident also raises questions about the well-being of K-Pop idols, who often push themselves to extreme limits to deliver high-energy performances, potentially compromising their health. 

 For Mina and TWICE, this controversy may impact their upcoming tour schedules and fan interactions, as they may need to address the concerns and criticisms from their fans and the broader public.
