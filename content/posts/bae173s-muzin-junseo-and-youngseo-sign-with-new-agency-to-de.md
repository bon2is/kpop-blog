Three former BAE173 members have just turned a new page in their careers, and fans are buzzing. Muzin, Junseo, and Youngseo signed exclusive contracts with Orca Music on September 4, and the agency confirmed that the trio will debut together under a brand‑new group name later this year.

## Background

BAE173 debuted in November 2020 under PocketDol Studio, quickly gaining a reputation for sharp choreography and a bright, youthful concept. The nine‑member boy band released a string of mini‑albums, including *Intersection: Blaze* (2021) and *Intersection: Trace* (2022), which charted on Korea’s Circle Album Chart and earned them a spot on Billboard’s World Albums list. Their single “Crush on You” broke into the top 10 on the Billboard World Digital Song Sales chart, cementing their global fanbase.

In early 2023, internal restructuring at PocketDol led to a hiatus for several members, and by mid‑2024 only a handful of the original lineup remained active. Muzin, Junseo, and Youngseo continued promoting as part of the group’s sub‑unit activities, but the lack of a full group comeback left fans speculating about their future. The three have built a strong individual following through variety shows, solo OST contributions, and social media engagement, making them prime candidates for a fresh project.

Orca Music, founded in 2022, has quickly become known for nurturing emerging talent. The label’s roster includes indie‑pop act LUNA and R‑&‑B trio Vivid, both of which have enjoyed strong streaming numbers on Spotify and Apple Music. Orca’s strategy focuses on artist‑centric development, guaranteeing creative input and global distribution via platforms like YouTube, Spotify, and Apple Music.

## What Happened

On September 4, Orca Music released an official statement announcing that Muzin, Junseo, and Youngseo had signed exclusive contracts with the agency. The press release read, “Through extensive discussions with the members, we have become confident in their future direction and potential for growth. We will spare no support to help them achieve their artistic vision.” The announcement was accompanied by a teaser image featuring the three idols in coordinated street‑wear, hinting at a modern, urban concept.

While the agency has not disclosed the new group’s name, it confirmed that the trio will officially debut as a unit in the fourth quarter of 2026. A pre‑debut single is slated for release in November, with a full EP expected in December. Orca Music also promised a series of behind‑the‑scenes content on YouTube and exclusive interviews on platforms such as Spotify’s “Storyline” podcast, giving international fans multiple ways to engage.

In a brief interview with Soompi, the three members expressed excitement: “We’ve always dreamed of creating music together that reflects who we are now. Joining Orca feels like the right step, and we can’t wait to share our sound with the world.” No specific chart projections were mentioned, but industry insiders predict a strong debut given the trio’s existing fanbase and Orca’s proven promotional machinery.

## Why This Matters

The move is significant on several levels. First, it underscores a growing trend in K‑Pop where members of disbanded or inactive groups re‑emerge under new agencies, often forming sub‑units or entirely new acts. This flexibility allows idols to continue their careers without the stigma of a group’s dissolution. For Muzin, Junseo, and Youngseo, the new contract provides a fresh platform to showcase their artistic growth, something that was limited under the larger BAE173 framework.

Second, Orca Music’s involvement highlights the expanding role of boutique agencies in the global market. While the “big three” (SM, YG, JYP) still dominate, smaller labels are increasingly successful in leveraging streaming services and social media to reach Western audiences. By promising distribution on Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube, Orca positions the trio for chart entries on Billboard’s World Digital Song Sales and potentially the Hot 100, should the pre‑debut single resonate.

Third, the announcement revitalizes interest in BAE173’s legacy. Fans who followed the original nine‑member lineup are now eager to support the trio’s next chapter, potentially boosting back‑catalog sales and streaming numbers. Early data from similar re‑debut projects (e.g., EXO’s Kai solo debut) show spikes of 30‑40 % in streaming of previous group releases, a pattern that could repeat here.

## What Fans Can Expect

Orca Music has hinted at a “genre‑blending” sound that mixes hip‑hop, R&B, and synth‑pop, aligning with current global trends. The November pre‑debut single is expected to drop on major platforms—Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, and Amazon Music—allowing fans worldwide to stream instantly. A music video will premiere on YouTube, with a simultaneous premiere event scheduled on V Live for Korean fans.

Beyond the music, the trio will embark on a limited‑edition fan‑sign tour across major Asian cities—Seoul, Tokyo, and Bangkok—starting in early 2027. Tickets will be sold through Yes24 and Ticketmaster, with a portion of proceeds earmarked for a charitable partnership with UNICEF Korea, reflecting Orca’s commitment to social responsibility.
