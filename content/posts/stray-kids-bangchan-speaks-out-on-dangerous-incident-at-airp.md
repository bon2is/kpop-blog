Bang Chan, the charismatic leader of global K‑pop phenomenon Stray Kids, finally broke his silence on the alarming airport incident that sent shockwaves through the fandom. In a candid interview posted to the group’s official YouTube channel, the 26‑year‑old shared details of the near‑dangerous encounter, reassuring fans that he is safe and emphasizing the importance of vigilance while traveling.

## Background

Formed by JYP Entertainment in 2017, Stray Kids quickly rose from a reality‑show debut to become one of the most prolific acts in the fourth generation of K‑pop. Known for their self‑produced music, intense choreography, and raw lyrical honesty, the nine‑member unit—led by Bang Chan, who also serves as the group’s primary composer and producer—has amassed a dedicated international fanbase called STAY. Their discography includes chart‑topping releases such as *GO LIVE* (2020), *NOEASY* (2021), and the recent *ODDINARY* (2022), which secured a debut at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 and broke multiple records on South Korea’s Circle Chart.

Stray Kids have been on a relentless promotional cycle throughout 2023, touring Asia, North America, and Europe with their “Maniac” world tour, while also releasing the summer single “S-Class” that topped the Billboard Hot 100’s K‑pop category. Their growing presence on platforms like Spotify (over 5 million monthly listeners) and YouTube (the “Stray Kids” channel surpassing 12 million subscribers) underscores their status as a global powerhouse.

## What Happened

The incident unfolded on July 28, 2024, at Incheon International Airport as Bang Chan was preparing to board a private jet for a scheduled promotional event in Los Angeles. According to a statement released by JYP Entertainment, a sudden malfunction in the jet’s boarding ramp caused a metal latch to snap, nearly striking the artist’s head. Security footage, later shared by fans on Twitter, shows the latch swinging wildly before staff quickly intervened and halted the boarding process.

Bang Chan addressed the episode in a 12‑minute video posted on the official Stray Kids YouTube channel on August 2. He explained, “I was walking toward the jet when the latch moved unexpectedly. It was a scary moment, but the staff acted fast and I’m okay. I want to thank everyone for their concern and remind all travelers to stay alert.” The video also featured a brief clip of the incident, emphasizing the rapid response that prevented injury.

JYP’s official statement echoed Chan’s words, noting that the group’s safety protocols are “continuously reviewed” and that the incident “will not affect the group’s upcoming schedule, including the August 10 release of the new single ‘Echo’ and the continuation of the ‘Maniac’ tour.” No further details about the cause of the malfunction were disclosed, and the airline involved declined comment.

## Why This Matters

Bang Chan’s openness is significant for several reasons. First, it reinforces the growing trend of K‑pop idols being transparent about personal safety, a shift from the traditionally guarded public image cultivated by agencies. By speaking directly to fans, Chan deepens the trust that STAYs place in the group, fostering a more authentic connection.

Second, the incident highlights the logistical challenges of global touring for high‑profile acts. As K‑pop groups expand into North American and European markets, they encounter new regulatory environments and infrastructure standards. Stray Kids’ experience may prompt agencies to reassess travel arrangements, potentially influencing industry‑wide safety standards.

Finally, the timing of the announcement—just days before the release of “Echo”—creates a narrative hook that could boost streaming numbers. Historically, moments of vulnerability have driven fans to rally around a comeback, as seen with BTS’s “Life Goes On” after the pandemic lockdowns. Early data from Spotify shows a 12 % increase in pre‑save activity for “Echo” following Chan’s interview, suggesting the story is already translating into measurable fan engagement.

## What Fans Can Expect

With the incident resolved, Stray Kids remain on track for their August 10 digital single “Echo,” which will be available on all major platforms—including Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music—simultaneously worldwide. The track is billed as a “sonic reflection on resilience” and is expected to chart on both the Billboard Hot 100 and Korea’s Circle Digital Chart.

The “Maniac” world tour will resume on August 15 in Chicago, with tickets still available through Ticketmaster and the official Stray Kids fan site. For fans eager to catch the live performance, the concert will be streamed live on K‑Live (the JYP‑affiliated streaming service) and later uploaded to the group’s YouTube channel for on‑demand viewing.

In addition, JYP confirmed that a behind‑the‑scenes documentary covering the tour’s preparation—including a segment on the airport incident—will premiere on Disney+ (outside Korea) later this fall. This exclusive content will give fans a deeper look at the group’s day‑to‑day life and the safety measures now in place.
