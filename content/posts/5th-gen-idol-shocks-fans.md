A 5th generation K-Pop idol has sparked controversy online after their heavily edited selfies were compared to their 'real' visuals. Netizens have been discussing the dramatic difference between the two. The idol's management team has not yet commented on the situation.
---

## Our Take

The controversy surrounding the idol's edited selfies highlights the intense pressure to conform to unrealistic beauty standards in the K-Pop industry. 

 The use of editing software to enhance one's appearance is not new, but the stark difference between the idol's online persona and real-life visuals has raised questions about authenticity. 

 This incident may have implications for the idol's career, as fans may feel deceived by the discrepancy between their online and offline image. 

 The situation also underscores the need for a more nuanced discussion about beauty standards and the impact of social media on self-perception in the K-Pop industry.
