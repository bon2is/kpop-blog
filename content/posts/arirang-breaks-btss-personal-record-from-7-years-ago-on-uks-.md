BTS has achieved another milestone in their illustrious career, breaking their 7-year personal record on the UK's Official Albums Chart with their latest album 'ARIRANG'. ## Background

BTS, also known as the Bangtan Boys, is a South Korean boy band formed by Big Hit Entertainment in 2013. The group consists of seven members: RM, Jin, Suga, J-Hope, Jimin, V, and Jungkook. With their unique blend of genres, thought-provoking lyrics, and captivating performances, BTS has become a household name, transcending cultural and linguistic barriers. Their discography includes numerous critically acclaimed albums, such as 'The Most Beautiful Moment in Life' series, 'Wings', 'Love Yourself: Her', 'Map of the Soul: Persona', and 'Map of the Soul: 7'. The group has won numerous awards, including the Melon Music Award for Artist of the Year, the Mnet Asian Music Award for Best Male Group, and the Billboard Music Award for Top Duo/Group.

The group's career has been marked by several notable milestones, including their debut on the Billboard 200 chart with 'The Most Beautiful Moment in Life, Part 2' in 2015, and their first No. 1 album on the Billboard 200 chart with 'Love Yourself: Tear' in 2018. BTS has also broken multiple records, such as the most-watched music video in 24 hours on YouTube with 'Boy With Luv' featuring Halsey, and the most-streamed K-pop group on Spotify. Their influence extends beyond the music industry, with collaborations with international artists, fashion brands, and philanthropic efforts.

## What Happened

The UK's Official Albums Chart announced that BTS's latest album 'ARIRANG' had climbed back up to No. 43 in its 20th week, breaking the group's 7-year personal record.
