BAE173’s Hangyul will be parting ways with PocketDol Studio after a decade-long relationship, with the official contract ending on February 28. The agency expressed gratitude for their shared journey and indicated that this decision comes after thoughtful consideration.
---

## Our Take

Hangyul's decision to leave PocketDol Studio is significant for both him and the agency. Ten years is a long time in the fast-paced K-Pop industry, and such a move often indicates a desire for new opportunities and personal growth. For Hangyul, this could mean a chance to explore other avenues in his career, whether that be solo activities or collaborations with different agencies.

In the context of the K-Pop industry, this departure reflects a growing trend where idols seek more control over their careers and paths. Many artists are beginning to recognize the value of exploring different management styles and creative directions. Hangyul's move may encourage others in similar situations to consider their own career trajectories and the importance of agency alignment with personal goals.

For fans, this news can be bittersweet. While there may be concerns about the transition, it also opens up possibilities for Hangyul to expand his artistry and potentially connect with a broader audience. Fans will be eager to see how this change will affect his future projects, and whether it will lead to a reinvention of his public persona.

Ultimately, this development is a reminder of the dynamic nature of the K-Pop landscape. With idols consistently evolving and reshaping their careers, Hangyul’s journey will be closely monitored by fans and industry insiders alike, as it could set a precedent for future idol transitions within the competitive market.
