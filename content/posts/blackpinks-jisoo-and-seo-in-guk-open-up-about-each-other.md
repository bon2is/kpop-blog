## Our Take

The news of Jisoo and Seo In Guk's Q&A session matters because it offers a rare glimpse into the personal dynamics between K-Pop idols and actors when they collaborate on projects. 

 This kind of openness is significant in the K-Pop industry, where idols often maintain a polished public image, making such candid discussions noteworthy. The fact that Jisoo, a member of one of the most popular K-Pop groups, and Seo In Guk, an established actor, are willing to share their honest thoughts about each other reflects the growing trend of K-Pop idols venturing into acting and the blurring of lines between the music and drama industries. 

 For fans of both Jisoo and Seo In Guk, this Q&A session provides valuable insight into their personalities and working relationship, potentially enhancing their engagement with the series 'Boyfriend On Demand'. 

 The impact of this session on Jisoo's career could be significant, as it showcases her ability to form meaningful connections with her co-stars, an important aspect of any successful acting career, and could lead to more acting opportunities in the future.
