## What Happened


According to JYP Entertainment, Yeji recently visited the hospital due to lower back pain, where she was diagnosed with a herniated disc. As a result, she has been advised to 'minimize back-straining movements' to prevent further injury and ensure a speedy recovery. This means that Yeji will be limiting her participation in ITZY's upcoming concerts, which are scheduled to take place this weekend. While the exact extent of her participation is unclear, it is likely that Yeji will be taking a more passive role in the performances, potentially sitting out certain songs or dances that may exacerbate her condition.


The news of Yeji's health issue has been met with an outpouring of support from fans, who are wishing her a speedy recovery and expressing their understanding of the situation. The group's upcoming concerts are still scheduled to take place as planned, with the other members of ITZY set to perform in Yeji's place where necessary.
