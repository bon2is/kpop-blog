## What Happened

On June 1, KOZ Entertainment announced that Jaehyun had visited a hospital for an ankle injury, which was subsequently diagnosed as a partial ligament sprain. This diagnosis necessitates a period of rest and rehabilitation to ensure proper healing and prevent further injury. As a result, Jaehyun will continue to participate in upcoming scheduled activities but with limited involvement in performances. The specifics of how his participation will be limited have not been detailed, but it is clear that his health and safety are the top priority.
