Park Hae Soo, the beloved actor known for his heartfelt performances in dramas like *Doctor Prisoner* and *It's Okay to Not Be Okay*, has just welcomed his second child. A statement from BH Entertainment on August 24 confirmed that the couple’s new baby boy arrived safely, and both mother and child are in good health. Fans across the globe flooded social media with congratulatory messages, celebrating the newest addition to the Hae Soo family.

## Background

Park Hae Soo first captured audiences’ attention in 2014 with his breakout role as the charismatic antagonist in the hit drama *Doctor Prisoner*. Since then, he has built a diverse portfolio, ranging from intense thrillers like *My Mister* to tender romance series such as *It's Okay to Not Be Okay*, where his chemistry with co‑star Kim Go‑eun earned critical acclaim and solidified his status as a top‑tier actor. In addition to television, Park has appeared in films like *The Witch: Part 1. The Subversion* and has been praised for his versatility and emotional depth.

Outside of his on‑screen career, Park is known for his low‑key personal life. He married his non‑celebrity wife in a private ceremony in 2020, a decision that reflected his desire to keep his family out of the spotlight. The couple welcomed their first son in 2021, a moment that was shared modestly through a brief agency announcement. Since then, Park has balanced his demanding filming schedule with fatherhood, often speaking about the joys and challenges of parenting in rare interviews.

## What Happened

On August 24, BH Entertainment released an official statement confirming that Park Hae Soo’s second son was born the previous day. The agency wrote, “Park Hae Soo’s second son was born yesterday. He is currently by his wife’s side, and both the mother and the baby are in good health.” No further details about the baby’s name or exact birth time were disclosed, respecting the family’s privacy. The announcement came shortly after the actor wrapped filming on the upcoming drama *The Good Bad Mother*, which is slated for a late‑year release on Korean broadcaster tvN and will likely be available on global streaming platforms such as Viki and Netflix, depending on licensing.

Fans quickly responded on platforms like Twitter, Instagram, and the Soompi community, using hashtags such as #ParkHaeSoo and #CongratsHaeSoo. Many highlighted the actor’s previous candid moments about fatherhood, recalling his heartfelt Instagram post in 2021 where he shared a photo of his first son holding a tiny plush toy. The new announcement reignited discussions about the actor’s ability to maintain a grounded family life while continuing to deliver powerful performances on screen.

Second, the timing aligns with a pivotal moment in his career. With *The Good Bad Mother* set to air later this year, the actor will be navigating the demands of a new project while adjusting to life with a newborn. Historically, actors who manage both responsibilities often experience a boost in public goodwill, translating to higher viewership numbers and stronger streaming performance. This could positively impact the drama’s ratings on domestic charts like Nielsen Korea and its eventual placement on international platforms, where fans are eager to stream fresh content from beloved stars.

Looking ahead, fans can anticipate seeing Park Hae Soo back on screen with *The Good Bad Mother*, a drama that promises a blend of family drama and suspense—an apt backdrop given his own expanding family life. The series is expected to premiere on tvN in November, with streaming rights likely secured by platforms like Viki and possibly Netflix for international audiences.
