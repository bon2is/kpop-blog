Park Hae Soo and Kwak Sun Young star in the suspenseful series 'The Scarecrow', which follows a detective investigating a series of murders. The story takes place between 1988 and 2019 and explores an unlikely partnership between two men. The series is set to keep viewers on the edge of their seats as the characters launch a high-risk bait operation to lure a serial killer.

## Our Take

The news of Park Hae Soo and Kwak Sun Young's 'trap investigation' in 'The Scarecrow' has garnered significant attention from K-Drama fans. 

 This series is particularly notable for its unique storyline, which spans multiple decades and explores the complexities of an unlikely partnership. 

 The high-risk bait operation is a bold move by the characters, and it will be interesting to see how the story unfolds and whether they are able to lure the serial killer. 

 For fans of the genre, 'The Scarecrow' promises to be a thrilling ride, and the involvement of talented actors like Park Hae Soo and Kwak Sun Young only adds to the excitement.
