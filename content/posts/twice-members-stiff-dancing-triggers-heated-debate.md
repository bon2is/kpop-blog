## Background

TWICE is a nine-member South Korean girl group formed by JYP Entertainment in 2015. The group consists of Nayeon, Jeongyeon, Momo, Sana, Jihyo, Mina, Dahyun, Chaeyoung, and Tzuyu. Since their debut, TWICE has released numerous hit songs, including 'Cheer Up', 'TT', and 'Fancy', and have won several awards for their music and performances. The group is known for their bright and cheerful image, as well as their impressive dance skills, which have been showcased in their music videos and live performances.

TWICE's career has been marked by numerous milestones, including their debut single 'The Story Begins' in 2015, which introduced the group to the K-Pop scene. Since then, they have released several successful albums and singles, including 'Page Two', 'Twicetagram', and 'Yes or Yes'. The group has also embarked on several world tours, performing in front of thousands of fans in Asia, Europe, and the Americas. With their impressive discography and energetic live performances, TWICE has established themselves as one of the leading K-Pop groups of their generation.

## What Happened

The controversy surrounding the TWICE member's dance performance began when a video of the member dancing to one of the group's songs was uploaded online. Some netizens criticized the member's dancing as 'stiff' and 'awkward', sparking a heated debate among fans and non-fans alike. The debate has highlighted the intense pressure that K-Pop idols face to conform to certain standards of beauty and talent, particularly when it comes to their dancing abilities.
