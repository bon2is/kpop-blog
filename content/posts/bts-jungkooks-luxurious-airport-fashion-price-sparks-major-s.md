## Background

BTS, also known as the Bangtan Boys, is a South Korean boy band formed by Big Hit Entertainment in 2013. The group consists of seven members: RM, Jin, Suga, J-Hope, Jimin, V, and Jungkook. With their unique blend of genres, thought-provoking lyrics, and captivating performances, BTS has become a global phenomenon, breaking cultural and linguistic barriers to amass a devoted fan base across the world. Jungkook, in particular, has been a fan favorite, known for his versatility as a singer, rapper, and dancer, as well as his endearing personality and captivating stage presence. Over the years, BTS has released numerous hit songs and albums, including 'DNA', 'Mic Drop', 'Fake Love', 'Boy With Luv', and 'Dynamite', which have topped various music charts and won numerous awards.

BTS's career highlights are too numerous to count, but some of their most notable achievements include becoming the first K-Pop group to top the US Billboard 200 chart, performing at the Grammy Awards, and winning the Melon Music Award for Artist of the Year. The group's recent releases, such as their album 'Map of the Soul: 7' and their single 'Butter', have also been hugely successful, showcasing their continued growth and evolution as artists. As individuals, the BTS members have also been exploring their own solo projects and interests, with Jungkook being one of the most active and experimental members in this regard. His fashion sense, in particular, has been a subject of interest among fans and fashion enthusiasts, who closely follow his style choices and trends.
