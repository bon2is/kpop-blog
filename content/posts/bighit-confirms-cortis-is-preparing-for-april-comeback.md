BIGHIT MUSIC has officially announced that rookie group CORTIS is preparing for their first comeback, scheduled for April. This marks a significant moment for the group, who debuted in August of last year and are now working on new material to showcase their growth as artists.
---

## Our Take

The announcement of CORTIS's upcoming comeback is a pivotal moment for the group, as it will be their first major release since their debut. This is an opportunity for them to solidify their presence in the competitive K-Pop landscape, especially as fans eagerly anticipate what new concepts and musical styles they will bring to the table.

In the current K-Pop industry, comebacks are crucial for sustaining and increasing a group's popularity. They not only allow artists to showcase their artistic evolution but also help maintain engagement with their fanbase. For CORTIS, this comeback is more than just a follow-up; it is a chance to establish their identity and differentiate themselves from other rookie groups.

Fans are likely to experience a mix of excitement and anticipation as they await the new music and performances. This comeback could significantly impact CORTIS's trajectory in the industry, potentially leading to nominations for awards or positions on music charts. For the group, this is a defining moment that could set the tone for their future releases and overall career.

As the date approaches, fans will likely rally with support, creating a buzz that could amplify CORTIS's visibility. This engagement can be pivotal in shaping their narrative within the industry, making it essential for the group to deliver an impressive comeback that resonates with their audience.
