Zico, the multi‑talented rapper and producer behind Block B and solo hits like “Any Song,” has just been confirmed as the newest cast member of the hit travel reality series **A Real Man’s Way to Travel**. The announcement, first reported by Soompi, places the 31‑year‑old alongside veteran variety stars Choo Sung Hoon and Kim Jong Kook for the show’s highly anticipated second season. Fans are already buzzing about how Zico’s witty humor and musical flair will blend with the trio’s “extreme MBTI” chemistry on an unscripted, globe‑trotting adventure.

## Background

**Zico (Woo Ji‑hoon)** first rose to fame as the leader of the third‑generation idol group Block B, debuting in 2011 under Seven Seasons. Known for his lyrical dexterity and production chops, Zico quickly established himself as one of K‑Pop’s most respected songwriters, penning hits for artists ranging from IU to EXO. After Block B’s hiatus in 2019, he launched a successful solo career, topping Korean charts with tracks like “Artist” and “Any Song,” the latter breaking streaming records on Melon and achieving a perfect all‑kill on Korean music charts. Zico’s influence extends beyond music; he’s a regular fixture on variety shows such as **Weekly Idol** and **Running Man**, where his quick wit and charismatic stage presence have earned him a dedicated fanbase.

**Choo Sung Hoon**, a former member of the idol group UN, transitioned to acting and variety after his group disbanded. He’s best known for his comedic timing on programs like **SNL Korea** and **The Return of Superman**, where his playful personality shines. **Kim Jong Kook**, a veteran singer‑actor from the iconic duo Turbo, has become a staple of Korean reality TV, most notably as a permanent cast member of **2 Days & 1 Night** and the long‑running **Running Man**. Their combined experience brings a seasoned, “hard‑core” vibe to travel shows, often appealing to an older demographic.

The original season of **A Real Man’s Way to Travel** debuted in early 2023, featuring Choo, Kim, and BIGBANG’s Daesung. The series differentiates itself by focusing on three men with distinct MBTI personalities—each representing an “extreme” type—to explore destinations without scripts, relying on spontaneous interactions and genuine reactions. The unscripted format resonated with viewers seeking authentic travel experiences, earning strong ratings on the K‑TV network and trending on Korean social media platforms.

## What Happened

On July 18, 2024, Soompi published an exclusive that confirmed Zico’s addition to the cast for Season 2. The press release described Zico as the “new youngest member,” highlighting his role in balancing the dynamic between the more seasoned Choo and Kim. Production notes indicated that filming will commence in late August, with the first episode slated for a September release on the network’s streaming portal **V Live** and later on **YouTube** for global audiences. While exact episode counts remain undisclosed, the show promises to travel to a mix of domestic hotspots and overseas locations, continuing its tradition of unfiltered adventure.

In a short video teaser released alongside the announcement, Zico is seen laughing with Choo and Kim while boarding a bus bound for Jeju Island. The clip showcases Zico’s playful banter, especially when he attempts to guess the MBTI types of his co‑stars, a running gag from Season 1. Choo Sung Hoon commented, “Zico brings fresh energy and a different perspective—he’s the perfect addition to push our limits further.” Kim Jong Kook added, “Travel is about discovery, and with Zico’s creativity, we’re ready for new stories.”

The show’s producers emphasized that Zico’s involvement is not just a stunt; they aim to explore how a younger, music‑industry‑savvy personality navigates the “extreme” travel challenges that have defined the series. The casting also reflects a broader trend in Korean variety programming where music idols are increasingly recruited to attract younger viewers and expand international reach.

## Why This Matters

Zico’s entry into **A Real Man’s Way to Travel** marks a strategic crossover between K‑Pop and travel‑reality formats, a pairing that has historically boosted viewership for both parties. For Zico, it offers a platform to showcase a more personal side beyond the studio, potentially widening his fanbase among older variety‑show audiences who may not follow his music. This move mirrors the success of other idols—such as **IU** on **Hyori’s Homestay**—who leveraged variety exposure to cement their status as all‑round entertainers.

From an industry standpoint, the casting signals that Korean broadcasters are betting on hybrid content that blends music celebrity appeal with authentic travel narratives. The unscripted nature of the series aligns with global streaming trends, where audiences favor reality content that feels genuine, as seen with Netflix’s **“Somebody Feed Phil”** and Disney+’s travel documentaries. By adding Zico, the show positions itself for broader international distribution, especially on platforms like **YouTube**, where subtitles can reach English‑speaking fans in the US, UK, Canada, and Australia.

Furthermore, Zico’s involvement could impact his music career. Historically, idols who appear on popular variety shows see spikes in streaming numbers. After his appearance on **Running Man** in 2022, Zico’s single “Summer Hate” saw a 27% increase on Spotify and Apple Music. If a similar pattern emerges, we may see a boost in his upcoming releases, especially if the show teases new music or behind‑the‑scenes studio sessions.

## What Fans Can Expect

Season 2 promises a blend of familiar and fresh locales. While the first season highlighted Korean countryside gems, rumors suggest the new season will venture abroad to places like **Taiwan**, **Thailand**, and possibly a European city, offering viewers a broader cultural palette. Zico’s known love for food and street culture hints at segments where he explores local cuisines, potentially collaborating with chefs or street vendors.

Fans should also keep an eye on potential surprise performances. Zico’s reputation for impromptu freestyles could lead to on‑the‑spot collaborations with local musicians, creating viral moments that could be clipped for social media. Additionally, the show’s unscripted format means anything—from spontaneous karaoke sessions to unexpected challenges—could happen, making each episode a must‑watch event.

For those outside Korea, episodes will be uploaded to **V Live** with English subtitles, and highlights will be posted on the official **YouTube** channel within 24 hours of broadcast. This ensures that international fans can stay up‑to‑date without needing a Korean TV subscription.
