Heeseung, formerly a member of the popular K-Pop group ENHYPEN, is gearing up for an exciting new chapter in his music career. According to an official statement from his agency BELIFT LAB, Heeseung is preparing to make his solo debut as EVAN in June, with new music slated for release next month. This announcement has sent shockwaves of excitement through the K-Pop community, with fans eagerly anticipating the artist's first solo venture.

Heeseung originally debuted in 2020 as a member of ENHYPEN, a group formed by BELIFT LAB, a joint venture between CJ E&M and Big Hit Entertainment. During his time with ENHYPEN, Heeseung quickly gained attention for his captivating stage presence, impressive vocal range, and undeniable charm. The group itself has enjoyed significant success, releasing a string of hit singles and albums, including their debut EP "Border: Day One" and the follow-up "Border: Carnival". ENHYPEN's music often explores themes of youth, identity, and self-discovery, resonating deeply with fans around the world.

ENHYPEN's career has been marked by numerous milestones, including their debut on the Billboard 200 chart and performances at prominent music festivals such as Music Bank and the Mnet Asian Music Awards.
