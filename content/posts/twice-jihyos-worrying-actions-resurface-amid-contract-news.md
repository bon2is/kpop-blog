Jihyo, in particular, has been an integral part of the group's success, showcasing her impressive vocal range and energetic performances. In recent years, TWICE has been on a roll, releasing chart-topping albums like 'Between 1&2' and 'Ready to Be', which have solidified their position as one of the leading K-Pop groups globally.
