The Hanteo Music Awards announced that Tiffany Young from Girls’ Generation and Lee Chan Won will co-host the upcoming ceremony on February 15, 2025. This marks a significant pairing, as both artists have substantial influence in the K-Pop scene.

---

## Our Take

The announcement of Tiffany Young and Lee Chan Won as hosts for the Hanteo Music Awards 2025 is noteworthy for several reasons. Firstly, Tiffany Young's legacy as a member of Girls’ Generation, a seminal group in K-Pop history, adds a layer of star power and credibility to the event. Her experience in both music and as a public figure allows her to engage the audience effectively, drawing in not only fans of Girls’ Generation but also new listeners who admire her solo work. On the other hand, Lee Chan Won, an emerging artist, represents the new generation of K-Pop idols. His involvement highlights the industry's transition and the blending of established and rising stars, which is vital for maintaining relevance in a rapidly changing musical landscape. By showcasing this diverse pair, the Hanteo Music Awards are not only honoring traditional K-Pop roots but also embracing its evolving future. This combination could potentially attract a broader audience, enhancing the awards’ viewership and engagement, which is essential in today’s competitive entertainment market.
