tvN's “Lovely Runner” has celebrated its second anniversary by releasing special posters featuring Kim Hye Yoon and Byeon Woo Seok. The time-slip romance drama is based on a popular web novel and has gained a significant following. The anniversary posters showcase the main characters, Im Sol and her love interest, in memorable scenes from the show.
---

## Our Take

The second anniversary of “Lovely Runner” is a significant milestone for the show, demonstrating its enduring popularity among K-Drama fans. 

 The release of special posters featuring Kim Hye Yoon and Byeon Woo Seok is a thoughtful gesture towards fans, who have been invested in the characters' stories and relationships. 

 This celebration also highlights the impact of time-slip romance dramas in the K-Drama landscape, with “Lovely Runner” being one of the notable examples of this genre. 

 For fans of the show, the anniversary is an opportunity to revisit favorite moments and characters, and to look forward to potential future developments or spin-offs, further solidifying the show's place in K-Drama history.
