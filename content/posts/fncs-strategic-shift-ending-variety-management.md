FNC Entertainment revealed its decision to end the management of variety show and broadcast personalities, effective January 16. Following thorough discussions with their talent, the agency will gradually conclude its involvement in this area and not renew existing contracts.

---

## Our Take

FNC Entertainment's move to end its management of variety personalities marks a significant shift in the agency's strategy, reflecting broader trends within the K-Pop industry. As the competition intensifies among entertainment companies to diversify revenue streams, FNC's decision could indicate a prioritization of core music-related endeavors over ancillary activities like variety shows. This shift may stem from the changing landscape of the entertainment industry, where social media and streaming platforms have begun to dominate traditional formats, diminishing the impact of variety shows. Furthermore, this move could allow FNC to focus more on nurturing and promoting its core talent, particularly in the music sector, where it has faced challenges in recent years. It's also notable that other agencies may follow suit, opting to streamline their operations in a bid to maintain competitiveness in a rapidly evolving market. Ultimately, FNC's decision could signal a recalibration of priorities, reflecting the ongoing transformation of entertainment consumption in the digital age.
