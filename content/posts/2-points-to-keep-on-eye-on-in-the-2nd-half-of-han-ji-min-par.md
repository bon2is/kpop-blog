The K-Drama “The Practical Guide to Love” is entering its second half, with Han Ji Min playing the lead role of Lee Ui Yeong, a woman navigating the world of blind dates. The drama features a love triangle between Han Ji Min's character and two men with different charms, played by Park Sung Hoon and Lee Ki Taek. As the series progresses, fans can expect more twists and turns in the story.
