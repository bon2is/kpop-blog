The upcoming drama 'Absolute Value of Romance' has released character posters of its five leads, including Cha Hak Yeon and N.Flying's Kim Jae Hyun. The story revolves around high school student Yeo Eui Ju, who writes a BL novel featuring her teachers as main characters. The drama explores her secret life and unexpected encounters with her teachers.
---

## Our Take

The inclusion of Cha Hak Yeon and N.Flying's Kim Jae Hyun in the drama is significant, as it brings together talented actors from different backgrounds. 

 The drama's unique plot, which features a high school student writing a BL novel, may spark debate among audiences and generate interest in the K-Pop and K-Drama communities. 

 The character posters suggest a strong visual aesthetic, which could contribute to the drama's success and appeal to fans of Korean entertainment. 

 As Kim Hyang Gi takes on the lead role, this drama may mark an important milestone in her career, showcasing her range and versatility as an actress.
