SF9’s tenth anniversary is more than a milestone—it’s a statement. The nine‑member boy group is set to unleash TENACITY, their second full‑length album, on August 26, 2026 at 6 p.m. KST, kicking off a month‑long celebration that blends nostalgia with bold new sounds. Fans worldwide are already buzzing about where to stream the album, how to watch the upcoming showcase, and what records the group might break on charts like Circle and Billboard.

## Background

Formed by FNC Entertainment in 2016, SF9 (Star Force 9) debuted with the EP “Feeling Sensation” and quickly earned a reputation for high‑energy performances and versatile concepts. Over the past decade, they’ve delivered hits such as “O Sole Mio,” “Good Guy,” and “Mamma Mia,” each showcasing a different musical facet—from retro pop to sleek R&B. Their first full‑length album, “First Collection,” dropped in 2021 and landed at #4 on the Circle Album Chart, solidifying their place among the second‑generation of K‑Pop acts that continue to thrive.

In recent years, SF9 has expanded their reach beyond Korea. They’ve appeared on American talk shows, performed at K‑Pop festivals in Europe, and secured a spot on Spotify’s “K‑Pop Rising” playlist, where they regularly rank in the top 20. Their 2024 EP “Turn On” topped the Hanteo real‑time chart and earned a Billboard World Digital Song Sales entry, proving they can still generate global streaming power.

## What Happened

The anniversary rollout was announced via a teaser video posted on SF9’s official YouTube channel on July 15, 2026. The 30‑second clip featured a montage of past performances, intercut with a new, gritty visual of the members standing amid shattered glass—symbolizing the “tenacity” they’ll showcase in the new record. The official statement from FNC read, “TENACITY is a celebration of our journey and a promise to our fans that we will keep pushing forward, no matter the obstacles.”

TENACITY will be released in three versions—“Phoenix,” “Mirror,” and “Chronicle”—each containing exclusive photobooks and QR codes that unlock behind‑the‑scenes footage. The lead single, “Resilience,” is a hybrid trap‑pop track produced by renowned composer Kenzie, featuring a soaring chorus and a rap bridge from members Inseong and Jaeyoon. A music video teaser dropped on August 10, revealing a futuristic set with neon‑lit choreography that nods to the group’s early dance style.

The comeback will be supported by a live showcase streamed globally on YouTube and V Live at 7 p.m. KST on release day. The performance will also be available later on Disney+ (in select regions) and on the K‑Pop streaming platform Weverse. Physical album sales are expected to open on major retailers such as Yes24, Ktown4u, and Amazon, while digital copies will be on Apple Music, Spotify, and Amazon Music.

## Why This Matters

A decade in K‑Pop is a rare feat, especially for a group that debuted without the massive backing of the “Big Three” agencies. SF9’s ability to sustain relevance speaks to their consistent artistic evolution and dedicated fanbase, known as “Fantasy.” By aligning the album’s theme with perseverance, they tap into a universal narrative that resonates with both domestic and international audiences.

From an industry perspective, TENACITY could influence chart dynamics this summer. Early pre‑order figures suggest the album may surpass 200,000 copies in the first week, a number that would place SF9 among the top‑selling acts on the Circle Album Chart for August 2026. If the lead single “Resilience” garners strong streaming numbers, it could re‑enter the Billboard Global Excl. US chart, echoing the group’s 2024 “Turn On” performance that peaked at #12.

Moreover, the multi‑platform streaming strategy—YouTube, Disney+, and Weverse—highlights the growing trend of cross‑regional distribution for K‑Pop comebacks. This approach maximizes exposure, especially in markets like the US, UK, Canada, and Australia, where fans frequently search “where to watch SF9 comeback” and “how to stream TENACITY.”

## What Fans Can Expect

Beyond “Resilience,” TENACITY offers 12 tracks that traverse genres from synth‑wave ballads (“Echoes of Time”) to Latin‑infused dance numbers (“Solar Flare”). Members have contributed lyrics to three songs, marking a deeper personal involvement in the creative process. The album’s packaging includes a hidden QR code that unlocks a virtual reality (VR) experience, allowing fans to explore a digital museum of SF9’s ten‑year history.

In the weeks following the release, SF9 plans a series of fan‑sign events in Seoul, Tokyo, and Los Angeles, with tickets expected to sell out within minutes. They will also embark on a “TENACITY World Tour” later in the year, with initial dates announced for major arenas in North America and Europe. Ticket sales will be handled through Ticketmaster and the official SF9 fan club platform, making it easy for global fans to secure seats.
