A new preview for the popular tvN variety show 'Amazing Saturday' has been released, featuring Yena, ITZY's Chaeryeong, and Lee Chae Yeon as guests. The episode will showcase the three idols dancing to songs by IVE and IZ*ONE. Yena and Lee Chae Yeon are former bandmates from the project group IZ*ONE, which adds an extra layer of excitement to the episode.
