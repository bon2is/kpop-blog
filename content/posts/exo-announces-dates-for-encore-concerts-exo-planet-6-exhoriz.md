EXO’s latest announcement has set the K‑Pop community buzzing: the boy‑band will stage three encore concerts in Seoul for their 2026 world tour, EXO PLANET #6 “EXhOrizon.” The shows, titled “EXhOrizon [dot],” will run from November 6 to November 8 at the iconic KSPO Dome, giving fans a final chance to experience the elaborate production before the tour moves on.

## Background

Since debuting under SM Entertainment in 2012, EXO has grown into one of the most influential acts in Korean music history. Originally split into EXO‑K (Korean) and EXO‑M (Mandarin) sub‑units, the group quickly amassed a massive following across Asia and the West with hits like “Growl,” “Call Me Baby,” and “Love Shot.” Their discography spans nine full‑length albums, numerous EPs, and a string of record‑breaking sales on both the Circle (formerly Gaon) and Hanteo charts. In 2023, EXO made a triumphant return after a two‑year hiatus with the album *EXIST*, which topped the Billboard 200’s World Albums chart and produced the viral single “Obsession.”

The group’s 2026 world tour, EXO PLANET #6 – “EXhOrizon,” was first announced in May 2024 and has already visited major markets including Los Angeles, London, Tokyo, and Sydney. The tour is notable for its futuristic stage design, immersive AR visuals, and a setlist that blends classic hits with tracks from *EXIST* and the newly released EP *Infinity*. Critics have praised the production as one of the most technically ambitious in K‑Pop, drawing comparisons to the scale of BTS’s “Permission to Dance” tour and BLACKPINK’s “Born Pink” stadium shows.

## What Happened

On August 20, SM Entertainment released an official poster confirming the encore dates for the Seoul leg of EXhOrizon [dot]. The three‑night run will take place at the KSPO Dome (formerly known as the Olympic Gymnastics Arena), a venue that can accommodate up to 15,000 fans per show. The announcement came alongside a teaser video that showcases a snippet of the stage’s holographic backdrop, featuring a pulsating galaxy motif that aligns with the “EXhOrizon” concept of exploring new frontiers.

In a brief statement, EXO’s leader Suho expressed gratitude to fans: “Seoul has always been our home, and we can’t wait to share this special encore with the fans who have supported us from day one.” The group’s management also hinted that the encore performances will include exclusive stage cuts not seen on the regular tour dates, as well as a surprise collaboration with a Western artist—though no further details have been disclosed.

Tickets will go on sale next Friday via the official Yes24 ticketing platform and will also be available on Interpark for international fans through a partnership with K‑Ticket. Early‑bird packages include a limited‑edition photobook, a digital download of the encore’s exclusive track, and a chance to win a backstage pass. No streaming or live‑broadcast options have been announced yet, but SM Entertainment typically partners with platforms like YouTube and V Live for post‑concert highlights, so fans can likely expect a recap video after the shows.

## Why This Matters

The addition of encore dates underscores EXO’s sustained commercial power nearly 15 years after debut. In a year where many second‑generation groups have either disbanded or shifted focus to solo activities, EXO’s ability to sell out a venue of KSPO Dome’s size for three consecutive nights signals robust demand. According to ticketing data released by SM, the initial Seoul dates sold out within 30 minutes, prompting the need for additional shows.

From an industry perspective, the encore also highlights a growing trend: K‑Pop acts are extending world tours with localized encore legs to maximize revenue and fan engagement. This mirrors strategies employed by BTS and Stray Kids, who added extra stadium dates in North America after initial sell‑outs. Moreover, the “EXhOrizon [dot]” concept aligns with SM’s broader push toward high‑tech concert experiences, potentially setting a new benchmark for future productions.

Chart-wise, EXO’s recent releases continue to perform strongly. Their latest single “Eclipse” debuted at #3 on the Billboard Global 200 and topped the Circle Digital Chart for three weeks. The tour’s setlist, which heavily features “Eclipse,” has contributed to a spike in streaming numbers on Spotify and Apple Music, reinforcing the symbiotic relationship between live performances and digital consumption.

## What Fans Can Expect

Beyond the three encore nights, fans should anticipate a few special elements. First, the setlist is rumored to include a brand‑new song debuted exclusively for the Seoul encore—potentially a teaser for a future EP slated for early 2027. Second, the production team has confirmed an upgraded visual segment, incorporating 3D mapping on the dome’s interior walls, which will create an immersive “space‑travel” experience.

Additionally, the surprise Western collaboration could bring an English‑language performance to the stage, further expanding EXO’s global appeal. While the identity of the guest artist remains under wraps, speculation points toward a pop or EDM act that aligns with the futuristic theme. Finally, fans who secure VIP tickets will receive a limited‑edition “EXhOrizon [dot]” merch bundle, featuring a holographic jacket and a signed mini‑photocard set.
