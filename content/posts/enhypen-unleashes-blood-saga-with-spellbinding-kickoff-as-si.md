## Background

ENHYPEN is a South Korean boy group formed by Belift Lab, a joint venture between Big Hit Entertainment (now HYBE) and CJ E&M. The group consists of six members: Heeseung, Jay, Jake, Sunghoon, Sunoo, and Jungwon. ENHYPEN made their debut in November 2020 with the single 'Given-Taken', and have since released a string of successful albums and singles, including 'Border: Day One', 'Border: Carnival', and 'Dimension: Dilemma'. With their unique sound, which blends genres such as pop, rock, and hip-hop, ENHYPEN has quickly established themselves as one of the most promising new groups in the K-Pop scene.

The group's name, ENHYPEN, is derived from the word 'hyphen', which represents the connection between different worlds and dimensions. This concept is reflected in their music and visuals, which often explore themes of identity, community, and the blurring of boundaries. ENHYPEN's members have also been praised for their impressive vocal and dance skills, as well as their charismatic stage presence.
