SEVENTEEN’s multi‑talented dancer Dino has once again turned heads, dropping the electrifying music video for “LIKE IT” on August 24 at 6 p.m. KST. The track, taken from Picheolin’s debut mini‑album “吉BOARD,” blends heartfelt lyrics with a kinetic dance routine that showcases Dino’s signature precision and charisma. Fans worldwide are already streaming the video on YouTube, where it quickly amassed millions of views, and the song is now available on major platforms such as Spotify, Apple Music, and Amazon Music.

## Background

Born Lee Chan‑hyuk, Dino debuted with SEVENTEEN in 2015 under Pledis Entertainment (now part of HYBE Labels). As the group’s main dancer and sub‑rapper, he quickly earned a reputation for his explosive stage presence and flawless technique, earning him the nickname “Dance Machine.” SEVENTEEN’s self‑producing ethos—writing, choreographing, and producing their own material—has propelled them to global stardom, with hits like “Very Nice,” “Don't Wanna Cry,” and the chart‑topping “HOT.” The group’s 2023 album “FML” topped the Billboard 200 and broke multiple records on South Korea’s Circle Album Chart.

While SEVENTEEN’s collective activities dominate headlines, individual members have increasingly explored solo ventures. In 2022, Dino released his first solo single “Dinosaur,” which peaked at #12 on the Circle Digital Chart and entered Billboard’s World Digital Song Sales chart. His solo work has been praised for its blend of hip‑hop swagger and melodic pop, allowing him to experiment beyond SEVENTEEN’s group sound.

In late 2023, Dino introduced an alter‑ego named Picheolin—a playful persona that lets him explore a different musical aesthetic. Picheolin’s concept leans toward bright, synth‑driven pop with a whimsical visual style, offering a contrast to Dino’s usual edgy image. The upcoming mini‑album “吉BOARD” marks Picheolin’s first official release, positioning Dino as both a group member and a solo artist with a distinct brand.

## What Happened

On August 24, Dino’s agency released the official MV for “LIKE IT,” the lead single from “吉BOARD.” The video opens with a burst of neon lighting and rapid cuts that immediately highlight Dino’s intricate footwork and fluid body rolls. Throughout the three‑minute visual, he dances across a futuristic set that merges street‑style graffiti with sleek, holographic panels—an aesthetic that mirrors the song’s lyrical theme of feeling alive when dancing for someone special.

The track’s lyrics, co‑written by Dino and Picheolin’s production team, convey a sincere confession: “I feel truly alive when I dance for you.” Musically, “LIKE IT” blends a punchy bassline, bright synth arpeggios, and a catchy chorus that leans into the K‑pop dance‑pop formula while allowing Dino’s vocal tone to shine. The song debuted at #3 on the Circle Digital Chart and entered the Billboard World Digital Song Sales chart at #8, indicating strong international reception.

In a brief statement posted to his Instagram, Dino said, “Creating Picheolin has let me express a side of me that loves pure joy and movement. ‘LIKE IT’ is my love letter to fans who’ve supported every step of my journey.” The MV’s choreography was co‑created with SEVENTEEN’s renowned choreographer Hoshi, ensuring the dance retains the group’s signature synchronicity while spotlighting Dino’s individual flair.

The mini‑album “吉BOARD” is now available for streaming worldwide on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, and YouTube Music. Physical copies can be pre‑ordered through HYBE’s official store, and limited‑edition photobooks featuring Picheolin’s concept art are slated for release in early September. Fans can also purchase the album on Korean platforms such as Melon and Genie, where it is expected to chart strongly.

## Why This Matters

Dino’s solo endeavor underscores a growing trend in K‑pop where group members launch distinct alter‑ego projects to diversify their artistic portfolios. By separating his solo identity from SEVENTEEN’s brand, Dino can experiment with genre, visual concepts, and songwriting without diluting the group’s core image. This strategy mirrors successful solo ventures by other HYBE artists, such as BTS’s J-Hope’s “Jack In The Box” and ENHYPEN’s Heeseung’s collaborations, which have expanded fan bases and generated additional revenue streams.

From an industry perspective, “LIKE IT” achieving a high placement on both domestic (Circle) and international (Billboard) charts demonstrates the global appetite for solo K‑pop releases. The track’s streaming numbers also contribute to the broader Hallyu wave, reinforcing the notion that individual members can act as cultural ambassadors independent of their groups.

Moreover, the MV’s high production value—featuring cutting‑edge VFX, elaborate set design, and intricate choreography—signals HYBE’s continued investment in solo projects. This level of support not only benefits Dino’s artistic growth but also sets a benchmark for other agencies looking to develop solo activities for their idols.

## What Fans Can Expect

Following the “LIKE IT” MV drop, HYBE has hinted at a series of promotional activities. A dance practice video is scheduled to release on YouTube within the week, giving fans a closer look at the choreography’s technical nuances. Additionally, Dino is slated to appear on several Korean music shows—including M Countdown and Inkigayo—where he will perform “LIKE IT” with a live band and backup dancers, further showcasing his versatility.

Looking ahead, the “吉BOARD” mini‑album contains four additional tracks, each exploring different facets of Picheolin’s bright aesthetic. Fans can anticipate a potential follow‑up music video for the album’s B‑side “Neon Nights,” rumored to feature a collaboration with a prominent EDM producer. Internationally, the album will be promoted through curated playlists on Spotify’s “K‑Pop Daebak” and Apple Music’s “New Music Daily,” ensuring maximum exposure to Western audiences searching for fresh K‑pop releases.
