## Background


LE SSERAFIM is a South Korean girl group formed by SOURCE MUSIC and HYBE, debuting on May 2, 2022, with their single "FEARLESS". The group consists of six members: Kim Chaewon, Sakura, Huh Yunjin, Kazuha, Kim Garam, and Hong Eunchae. Each member brings their unique talent and experience to the table, with Kim Chaewon being one of the key vocalists and performers. Before joining LE SSERAFIM, Kim Chaewon gained recognition through her participation in the reality competition show "Produce 48" in 2018, where she finished tenth and later became a member of the project girl group IZ*ONE. Her experience and charisma have been invaluable to LE SSERAFIM, contributing to the group's rapid rise in the K-Pop scene.


The group has been actively releasing music and performing since their debut, with notable releases such as "ANTIFRAGILE" and "UNFORGIVEN". ## What Happened


The update on Kim Chaewon's health was announced by SOURCE MUSIC, stating that she would be taking a temporary break from activities due to neck pain. This decision was made following medical advice for her to rest for a certain period. Unfortunately, this means she will not be able to join the rest of LE SSERAFIM at the 2026 Weverse Con Festival, an event that many fans were looking forward to. The Weverse Con Festival is a significant platform for K-Pop groups to perform and connect with their global fanbase, making Kim Chaewon's absence notable. However, the health and recovery of the members are always the top priority, and fans have been understanding and supportive of this decision.


The specific details of Kim Chaewon's condition and the exact duration of her break have not been fully disclosed, respecting her privacy and focusing on her recovery.
