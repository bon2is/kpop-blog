Chae Jong Hyeop and Won Ji An are in talks to star in a new historical drama called 'Unnamed Lamp'. The drama is based on a webtoon and will air on KBS2. Both actors' agencies have confirmed that they are positively reviewing the offers to star in the drama.

## Our Take

This news is significant as it marks a potential collaboration between two rising stars in the K-Drama scene. 

 Chae Jong Hyeop and Won Ji An have both gained recognition for their roles in previous dramas, and their involvement in 'Unnamed Lamp' could bring a fresh and exciting energy to the historical drama genre. 

 The K-Drama industry has seen a surge in popularity in recent years, and the combination of a compelling webtoon-based story and talented leads like Chae Jong Hyeop and Won Ji An could make 'Unnamed Lamp' a must-watch for fans. 

 If the casting is confirmed, this drama could be a major milestone in the careers of both Chae Jong Hyeop and Won Ji An, potentially leading to more high-profile projects and increased international recognition.
