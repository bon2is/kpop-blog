MBC's new drama 'Perfect Crown' starring IU has achieved the 3rd-highest premiere ratings of any MBC Friday-Saturday drama in history. The drama tells the love story of a chaebol heiress and a grand prince in an alternate universe. With its strong start, 'Perfect Crown' is expected to gain a large following.
---

## Our Take

IU's involvement in 'Perfect Crown' has been highly anticipated, and the drama's strong premiere ratings are a testament to her enduring popularity. 

 The drama's unique setting, which combines elements of modern-day Korea with a constitutional monarchy, offers a fresh take on the traditional romance genre. 

 The success of 'Perfect Crown' is also a significant milestone for MBC, as it demonstrates the network's ability to produce high-quality dramas that resonate with audiences. 

 As the drama continues to air, fans can expect to see IU's character navigate the complexities of royal life, and the show's ratings will likely remain a topic of interest in the K-Pop and K-Drama communities.
