Lee Junho, a member of the K-Pop group 2PM, has received an offer to star in the drama 'Buy King' and is currently reviewing it. The drama already has Ju Ji Hoon in talks to join, and Lee Junho's potential participation could bring significant attention to the project. This marks another potential acting role for Lee Junho, showcasing his versatility as an artist.
---

## Our Take

The potential casting of Lee Junho in 'Buy King' is significant because it highlights the growing trend of K-Pop idols transitioning into acting roles. 

 This move into drama is not unprecedented for Lee Junho, who has already demonstrated his acting chops in various projects, but it does indicate a broader strategy to expand his career beyond music. 

 For fans of Lee Junho and 2PM, this news is exciting as it promises more opportunities to see their favorite artist in a different light, potentially reaching a wider audience and gaining more recognition for his talents. 

 The involvement of both Lee Junho and Ju Ji Hoon in 'Buy King' could elevate the drama's profile, attracting both fans of K-Dramas and those interested in K-Pop, thus bridging two popular Korean entertainment genres.
