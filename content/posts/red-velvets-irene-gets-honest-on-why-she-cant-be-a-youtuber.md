In a refreshing display of candor, Red Velvet's Irene recently shared her genuine thoughts on why she cannot pursue a career as a YouTuber, a path many of her peers have taken to connect more intimately with their fans and explore their creativity beyond music. This revelation came during an engaging conversation with Lee Hyeri on her YouTube channel, I Am Lee Hyeri, where Irene, along with fellow Red Velvet members Seulgi and Yeri, delved into various topics, including their interests and aspirations outside of their group activities.


## Background


Red Velvet, formed by SM Entertainment, has been a pivotal figure in the K-Pop landscape since their debut in 2014. Known for their unique sound that blends genres like pop, rock, and R&B, the group has garnered a significant following worldwide. Irene, Seulgi, Yeri, Joy, and Wendy have each brought their distinctive talents and personalities to the table, making Red Velvet a versatile and beloved group. With hits like 'Bad Boy', 'Psycho', and 'Red Flavor', they have consistently pushed the boundaries of K-Pop, experimenting with different styles and themes. Beyond their music, the members have also ventured into solo activities, including acting, variety shows, and, for some, creating content on YouTube.


The K-Pop industry has seen a surge in idols turning to YouTube as a means to showcase their personalities, hobbies, and daily lives, offering fans a more personal glimpse into their world. This trend has been particularly notable among female idols, with many using the platform to share their passions for beauty, fashion, cooking, and more. Seulgi, a member of Red Velvet, has also embarked on this journey, launching her own YouTube channel where she shares various aspects of her life and interests with her fans.


## What Happened


The conversation that led to Irene's revelation about not becoming a YouTuber was sparked by Lee Hyeri's mention of Seulgi's YouTube channel. Hyeri, known for her friendly and open demeanor, created a comfortable atmosphere where the Red Velvet members felt at ease discussing their thoughts and feelings. Irene's response, though surprising to some, highlighted the careful consideration and self-awareness that goes into deciding how and when to share personal aspects of their lives with the public. Her honesty not only sheds light on the pressures and expectations that K-Pop idols face but also underscores the importance of maintaining a healthy work-life balance and prioritizing personal well-being.


The video, uploaded on August 7th, has generated significant interest among fans and non-fans alike, prompting discussions about the realities of being a K-Pop idol and the sacrifices that come with fame. Irene's decision, while respected by many, also sparks curiosity about the potential content she could have created, given her known interests and talents. However, her choice also serves as a reminder of the diverse paths that idols can take, both within and outside of the entertainment industry, and the need for supporters to respect and appreciate these individual journeys.


## Why This Matters


Irene's statement holds considerable significance within the context of the K-Pop industry, where the lines between the public and private lives of idols are often blurred. The decision to share personal content on platforms like YouTube can be daunting, especially considering the intense scrutiny and criticism that idols may face. By choosing not to pursue YouTube, Irene is, in a way, asserting her boundaries and prioritizing her personal space, a move that can be seen as empowering and responsible.


This conversation also touches upon the broader theme of mental health and the pressures of constant visibility in the digital age. As K-Pop continues to gain global traction, the demands on idols to constantly produce content, engage with fans, and maintain a public image can be overwhelming. Irene's choice, and the discussion it has sparked, serves as a timely reminder of the importance of prioritizing well-being and setting realistic boundaries in the pursuit of a career in entertainment.
