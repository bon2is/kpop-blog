Kim Young Dae's agency OUTERUNIVERSE announced that he will enlist in the military this April to fulfill his duty as an active-duty soldier. His discharge is expected around October 2027. This news marks a significant pause in Kim Young Dae's acting career, as he takes a break to serve his country.
---

## Our Take

The news of Kim Young Dae's military enlistment is significant as it affects his acting career and upcoming projects. 

 This break may impact his current momentum, but it's a necessary step for him to fulfill his civic duty. 

 In the context of the K-Pop and K-Drama industry, military enlistment is a common practice for male celebrities, and fans generally support their favorite artists during this time. 

 For Kim Young Dae's fans, this means a wait of about two years before they can see him return to the screen, but they can look forward to his future projects after his discharge.
