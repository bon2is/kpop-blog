In a testament to his growing popularity as a solo artist, BTS's j-hope has reached a significant milestone with his music video for 'Killin' It Girl (Solo Version)' surpassing 100 million views on YouTube. This achievement not only underscores j-hope's versatility and talent but also highlights the strong support of the BTS fan base, known as the ARMY. As one of the most successful K-Pop groups globally, BTS members' solo endeavors are closely watched, and j-hope's success in this realm is particularly noteworthy.

## Background

j-hope, whose real name is Jung Ho-seok, is a member of the global phenomenon K-Pop group BTS, formed by Big Hit Entertainment in 2013. Since then, BTS has broken numerous records and achieved unparalleled success, becoming one of the most influential musical groups worldwide. j-hope, alongside the other members, has been instrumental in the group's songwriting and production processes, showcasing his talent not only as a performer but also as a creator. His solo work, including his debut solo mixtape 'Hope World' released in 2018, has been well-received, offering fans a glimpse into his personal artistic vision and style.

j-hope's career highlights include his participation in BTS's critically acclaimed albums, such as 'Map of the Soul: 7' and 'Proof', as well as his solo releases. The song 'Killin' It Girl (Solo Version)' is part of his solo discography, which has been expanding since his debut mixtape. This solo version of 'Killin' It Girl' was initially released on June 16, 2025, as a 'Choreography version' music video, which has now achieved the milestone of 100 million views. The success of this solo single demonstrates j-hope's ability to connect with fans on a personal level, beyond his work with BTS.

The history of BTS is marked by numerous achievements, from topping the Billboard Hot 100 chart to selling out world tours. The group's impact on K-Pop and the global music scene has been profound, paving the way for other K-Pop acts to reach international audiences. As a member of BTS, j-hope has been part of this journey, contributing to the group's success while also exploring his solo career. His solo endeavors, such as 'Killin' It Girl (Solo Version)', reflect his personal growth as an artist and his desire to express himself in different ways.

## What Happened

On June 1 at around 3 a.m. KST, the music video for j-hope's 'Killin' It Girl (Solo Version)' reached the 100 million view milestone on YouTube. This 'Choreography version' of the music video was originally released on June 16, 2025, and its success indicates the strong and enduring support of the ARMY for j-hope's solo projects. The achievement is significant not only for j-hope but also for the K-Pop industry, as it shows the global appeal of solo artists from popular groups. The music video's choreography, which is a focal point of the 'Choreography version', has been particularly praised by fans and critics alike, showcasing j-hope's dancing skills and the meticulous work that goes into creating such high-quality visual content.

The announcement of the music video surpassing 100 million views was met with excitement from fans worldwide, who took to social media to congratulate j-hope on this achievement. The support for j-hope's solo work is a testament to the loyalty of the BTS fan base, which has been instrumental in the group's success and now extends that support to the members' individual projects. As j-hope continues to explore his solo career, achievements like this milestone demonstrate the potential for K-Pop solo artists to make a significant impact on the global music scene.
