## Our Take

HYBE's confirmation of Manon's Coachella appearance is a testament to the company's strategic plans for its artists. This move indicates HYBE's intent to expand its global reach and cement its position in the international music industry. 

 The K-Pop industry has seen a surge in global popularity, with many artists performing at prestigious festivals like Coachella. Manon's appearance alongside KATSEYE is a prime example of this trend, showcasing the genre's growing influence worldwide. 

 For fans, this news is a dream come true, as they get to witness their favorite artists share the stage with international acts. This exposure not only benefits the artists but also contributes to the growth of K-Pop as a whole, introducing the genre to new audiences and solidifying its place in the global music landscape. 

 As HYBE continues to make waves in the industry, it will be exciting to see how Manon's Coachella appearance impacts the company's future endeavors and the artist's career trajectory.
