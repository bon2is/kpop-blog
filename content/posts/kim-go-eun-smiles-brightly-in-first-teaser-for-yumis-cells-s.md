TVING has confirmed the return of its hit original series Yumi’s Cells Season 3, with Kim Go-eun reprising her role as Yumi. The series is set to premiere soon, with a teaser poster released on March 11. The poster features Kim Go-eun's bright and cheerful smile, offering fans a first glimpse of the upcoming season.
