Kim Ji Woong and Sung Han Bin of ZEROBASEONE have opened personal Instagram accounts, allowing fans to get closer to the artists. This move is expected to increase their individual and group's popularity. The official announcement was made through ZEROBASEONE's official Instagram account on March 24.

## Our Take

The decision of Kim Ji Woong and Sung Han Bin to have personal Instagram accounts matters because it signifies a shift in how K-Pop idols engage with their fans. 

 This move is contextualized within the broader K-Pop industry trend where idols are increasingly using social media to connect with fans and share aspects of their personal lives. 

 By having personal accounts, Kim Ji Woong and Sung Han Bin can share their individual interests, hobbies, and experiences, potentially attracting new fans and deepening their connection with existing ones. 

 For ZEROBASEONE as a group, this could translate into increased visibility and popularity, as fans are likely to follow both the group's and individual members' accounts, thereby amplifying their reach and influence.
