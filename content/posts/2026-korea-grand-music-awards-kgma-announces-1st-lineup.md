The Korea Grand Music Awards (KGMA) has just dropped its first official lineup for the 2026 ceremony, and the roster reads like a who's‑who of the current K‑pop generation. ATEEZ, RIIZE, TREASURE, Hearts2Hearts, KiiiKiii, ALLDAY PROJECT, AND2BLE, CRAVITY, AHOF, and CLOSE YOUR EYES are all slated to perform across the two‑day event, sending waves of excitement through fan communities worldwide.

## Background

KGMA, launched in 2022, quickly grew into one of South Korea’s most prestigious year‑end award shows, celebrating both commercial success and artistic innovation. Organized by the Korea Music Content Association, the ceremony traditionally honors chart‑topping releases, breakthrough newcomers, and lifetime achievements. In recent years, KGMA has distinguished itself by streaming live on platforms like YouTube and V Live, allowing international audiences to tune in without a ticket.

ATEEZ, formed by KQ Entertainment in 2018, has become a global powerhouse with sold‑out world tours and a string of Billboard World Albums chart entries. Their 2025 EP *ZERO: FEVER* topped the Circle Album Chart and broke streaming records on Spotify, cementing their status as a mainstay of the “next‑gen” wave.

RIIZE, the 2023 debut boy group from Wake One Entertainment, rapidly climbed the Hanteo charts with their debut single “Get a Rise.” Their sophomore album *RISE OF LIGHT* hit #2 on the Circle Album Chart and earned them a spot on Billboard’s Emerging Artists list. TREASURE, a YG Entertainment act that debuted in 2020, continues to dominate the Hot 100 with tracks like “Bona” and “JIKJIN,” while also breaking records on Korean music video platforms.

The other acts—Hearts2Hearts, KiiiKiii, ALLDAY PROJECT, AND2BLE, CRAVITY, AHOF, and CLOSE YOUR EYES—represent a mix of rising idols, indie‑pop collectives, and experimental groups that have been gaining traction on Spotify, Apple Music, and TikTok. Their inclusion signals KGMA’s commitment to showcasing both mainstream powerhouses and fresh talent.

## What Happened

On September 4, KGMA released an official press statement confirming the first lineup. The ceremony will take place over two days in Seoul’s iconic Olympic Gymnastics Arena, with the exact dates set for November 18‑19, 2026. Each artist will receive a dedicated performance slot, with ATEEZ headlining the opening night and TREASURE closing the second night’s showcase.

The announcement also highlighted that the ceremony will be streamed live on YouTube, V Live, and the official KGMA website, with English subtitles provided for all segments. While ticket sales for in‑person attendance have not yet opened, the organizers promise a limited‑quantity “Premium Fan Pass” that includes backstage virtual meet‑and‑greet rooms and exclusive merch drops.

Quotes from the KGMA spokesperson emphasized the event’s diversity: “We are thrilled to bring together veteran acts like ATEEZ and TREASURE with the next wave of talent such as RIIZE and CRAVITY. KGMA 2026 will celebrate the full spectrum of Korean music, and we’re making sure fans worldwide can experience it in real time.”

## Why This Matters

The KGMA lineup underscores a pivotal shift in the K‑pop industry: the blending of established global acts with emerging groups that command strong streaming numbers on Western platforms. By featuring artists who have charted on Billboard Hot 100 and Billboard 200, KGMA positions itself as a bridge between Korean music’s domestic success and its expanding international footprint.

For ATEEZ, headlining KGMA reinforces their trajectory from breakout rookies to award‑show mainstays, a path similar to that of early‑2000s icons like TVXQ. TREASURE’s placement as the closing act highlights their sustained relevance, especially after their recent collaboration with a major U.S. DJ that peaked at #12 on the Billboard Dance/Electronic Songs chart.

Including groups like KiiiKiii and ALLDAY PROJECT signals that KGMA is acknowledging the rising influence of genre‑bending acts that blend K‑pop with indie, R&B, and electronic sounds. This diversification mirrors the broader industry trend where streaming algorithms reward cross‑genre experimentation, and fans increasingly seek fresh sonic palettes.

## What Fans Can Expect

Fans can look forward to elaborate stage productions, with each performance expected to feature cutting‑edge visuals, live bands, and surprise guest appearances. Rumors suggest that ATEEZ might debut a new choreography segment for an unreleased track, while RIIZE could unveil a teaser for their upcoming 2027 album during their slot.

The two‑day format also allows for award presentations across multiple categories, including “Best Global Performance,” “Rising Star,” and “Best Collaboration.” Winners will be announced live, and many categories will be decided by a combination of chart data (Circle, Hanteo) and fan voting via the KGMA app.

International fans should keep an eye on the official KGMA website and social media channels for ticket announcements. The “Premium Fan Pass” will likely sell out quickly, offering early‑access streaming, exclusive behind‑the‑scenes footage, and limited‑edition merch bundles featuring each performing act.
