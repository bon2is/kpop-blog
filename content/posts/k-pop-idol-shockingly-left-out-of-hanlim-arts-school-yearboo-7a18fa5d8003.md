A K-Pop idol was notably excluded from the yearbook of Hanlim Arts School, which has generated outrage among fans and observers. This incident has prompted discussions about the treatment of students in the K-Pop industry, particularly in educational contexts.

## Our Take

The exclusion of a prominent K-Pop idol from the Hanlim Arts School yearbook is more than just a simple oversight; it highlights deeper issues within the K-Pop industry and its educational institutions. Hanlim Arts School is known for nurturing many successful idols, and its role in shaping these young talents cannot be understated. When a student, especially one with significant public recognition, is omitted from such a crucial memento, it raises questions about the school's inclusivity and values. Fans are understandably outraged, as this incident not only reflects on the individual involved but also on the perception of how idols are treated within their educational environments. It may also set a precedent for how schools manage the balance between academic life and the intense scrutiny that comes with fame. This situation calls for a reevaluation of how institutions like Hanlim support their students, especially those who are navigating the challenging duality of education and celebrity status. The backlash serves as a reminder that every student, regardless of their future career path, deserves recognition and respect within their educational journey.
