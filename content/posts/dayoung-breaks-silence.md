## Our Take

Dayoung's solo comeback is significant as it marks her return to the music scene after a successful debut. 

 This comeback is crucial for Dayoung as it will determine the trajectory of her solo career and potentially open up more opportunities for her as a solo artist. 

 The K-Pop industry is highly competitive, and a successful solo comeback can greatly impact an artist's career, making Dayoung's 'What's a girl to do' a highly anticipated release. 

 The music video teaser has already generated buzz, and fans are eagerly waiting for the full release, which is expected to showcase Dayoung's growth and artistry as a solo performer.
