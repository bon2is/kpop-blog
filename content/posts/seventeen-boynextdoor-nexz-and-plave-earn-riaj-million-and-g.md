K-Pop fans around the world are celebrating as SEVENTEEN, BOYNEXTDOOR, NEXZ, and PLAVE have achieved significant milestones in Japan, earning RIAJ million and gold certifications for their latest releases. The news was announced by the Recording Industry Association of Japan (RIAJ), which recognizes outstanding sales and shipments of music albums in the country. SEVENTEEN's 2023 Korean mini album 'SEVENTEENTH HEAVEN' has been certified as a million seller, with over 1 million units shipped in Japan, a remarkable feat that demonstrates the group's immense popularity not only in Korea but also globally.


## Background


SEVENTEEN is a South Korean boy group formed by Pledis Entertainment in 2015. The group consists of 13 members, divided into three sub-units: Hip-Hop Unit, Vocal Unit, and Performance Unit. With their unique blend of genres, captivating performances, and heartfelt lyrics, SEVENTEEN has gained a massive following worldwide. Their discography includes several successful albums and singles, such as 'Adore U', 'Mansae', 'Pretty U', and 'Don't Wanna Cry', which have topped various music charts, including the Circle Chart and Billboard's K-Pop Hot 100. In recent years, SEVENTEEN has been focusing on showcasing their musical growth and experimentation, exploring different sounds and themes in their releases.


BOYNEXTDOOR, on the other hand, is a newer group that has been making waves in the K-Pop scene. Formed by KOZ Entertainment, the group consists of six members: Sunwoo, Riwoo, Jaehyun, Taesan, Woonhak, and Hyeongjun. With their debut single 'Butterfly', BOYNEXTDOOR has already garnered attention for their fresh energy and talents. NEXZ and PLAVE are also rising stars in the K-Pop world, each with their unique style and sound. As these groups continue to release new music and perform, fans are excited to see their growth and evolution as artists.


The RIAJ certifications are a significant achievement for these groups, as they recognize the commercial success of their music in Japan. According to the RIAJ's certification thresholds, albums are certified gold at 100,000 units, platinum at 250,000 units, and million at 1,000,000 units. These certifications not only reflect the groups' popularity in Japan but also demonstrate the growing influence of K-Pop worldwide.
