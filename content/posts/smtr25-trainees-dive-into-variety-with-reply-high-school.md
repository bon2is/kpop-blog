Mnet has unveiled a main teaser for 'Reply High School,' a new variety show centering on 15 trainees from SM Entertainment's SMTR25. The show will showcase their growth in a school setting, with support from established artists like Super Junior and Changmin from TVXQ.

---

## Our Take

The introduction of 'Reply High School' highlights a significant trend in the K-Pop industry where trainee programs increasingly blend variety formats with traditional training methods. This approach not only humanizes the trainees, allowing fans to connect with them on a personal level, but also showcases their personalities, which is crucial in a market that values idol image as much as musical talent. With guidance from seasoned artists like Super Junior and Changmin, the trainees get a unique opportunity to learn from those who have successfully navigated the challenges of stardom. Furthermore, engaging in variety shows can enhance their performance skills and charisma, key components for thriving in K-Pop's competitive landscape. As the industry continues to evolve, 'Reply High School' may set a precedent for future trainee shows by focusing on holistic development rather than just musical prowess, ultimately shaping how new idols prepare for their debut.
