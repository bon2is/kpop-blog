My Royal Nemesis, the SBS romantic comedy starring Lim Ji Yeon and Heo Nam Jun, has been making waves in the Korean drama scene with its unique blend of humor, romance, and historical fiction elements. On May 22, the show reached a major milestone, soaring to its highest viewership ratings yet with an average nationwide rating of 9.5 percent. This impressive feat has left fans and critics alike wondering what's behind the show's surging popularity.


## Background


Lim Ji Yeon and Heo Nam Jun, the lead actors of My Royal Nemesis, have both had notable careers in the Korean entertainment industry. Lim Ji Yeon, known for her versatility as an actress, has appeared in a wide range of dramas and films, including High Society and Welcome to Waikiki. Heo Nam Jun, on the other hand, has made a name for himself with his charming on-screen presence in shows like About Time and Monarch – Owner of the Mask. The chemistry between the two leads has been a major factor in the show's success, with fans praising their witty banter and romantic moments.


The show itself is set in a fictional historical context, with a unique blend of comedy, romance, and social commentary. The story follows the lead characters as they navigate the complexities of royal life, all while dealing with their own personal struggles and relationships. With its lighthearted tone and engaging storyline, My Royal Nemesis has managed to capture the hearts of audiences and establish itself as one of the most popular dramas currently airing in Korea.


My Royal Nemesis premiered on April 17, 2023, and has been steadily gaining momentum since its debut. The show's ratings have been consistently high, with each episode building on the previous one's success. The show's writers have done an excellent job of crafting a narrative that is both entertaining and engaging, with well-developed characters and unexpected plot twists that keep viewers on the edge of their seats.


## What Happened


On May 22, My Royal Nemesis achieved its highest ratings yet, with an average nationwide rating of 9.5 percent. This marks an impressive jump of 3.5 percentage points from the previous episode, and cements the show's position as one of the most-watched dramas currently airing in Korea. According to Nielsen Korea, the show's ratings have been consistently high, with a peak rating of 11.1 percent in the Seoul metropolitan area.


The show's success comes despite the premiere of new competition, Fifties Professionals, which aired its first episode on the same day. The fact that My Royal Nemesis was able to maintain its momentum and even increase its ratings in the face of new competition is a testament to the show's enduring popularity and the loyalty of its fan base. The show's writers and producers have expressed their gratitude to fans for their support, and have promised to continue delivering high-quality content in the episodes to come.


The show's ratings have been steadily increasing, with each episode building on the previous one's success. The show's success has also been reflected in its online presence, with the show's official social media accounts and fan forums buzzing with activity and discussion.
