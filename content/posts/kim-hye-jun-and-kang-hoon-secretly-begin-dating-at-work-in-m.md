The latest promotional stills from tvN’s upcoming drama **My Bias, My Boss** have set fans’ hearts racing. In the newly released images, Kim Hye Jun’s character Nam Da‑reum and Kang Hoon’s character are caught in a secret office romance, confirming the series’ central love‑triangle and sparking a wave of online speculation.

## Background

Kim Hye Jun first burst onto the Korean entertainment scene as a child actress, gaining recognition for her heartfelt performance in the 2015 drama *The Legend of the Blue Sea*. Over the past few years she has transitioned smoothly into adult roles, most notably in the romantic comedy *Love in Contract* (2022) and the melodrama *The Girl Who Sees Scents* (2023). Her versatility and natural charisma have earned her a growing fanbase both domestically and internationally, with her recent projects consistently trending on platforms like V Live and YouTube.

Kang Hoon, meanwhile, is a rising star known for his strong supporting roles in popular series such as *Extraordinary Attorney Woo* and the 2024 hit *Alchemy of Souls 2*. Though still early in his career, his on‑screen presence and chemistry with co‑stars have made him a fan‑favorite, especially among viewers who follow K‑Drama forums on Reddit and Discord. Both actors are represented by major agencies—Kim by Fantagio and Kang by JYP Entertainment’s acting division—giving them access to high‑profile projects and extensive promotional support.

*My Bias, My Boss* is adapted from a beloved webtoon that amassed over 10 million reads on Naver Webtoon. The story follows Nam Da‑reum (Kim Hye Jun), a fresh graduate who lands a job at a cosmetics firm hoping to meet her longtime idol, Lee Chan (Cha Woo Min). The series blends office comedy, youthful romance, and the classic “bias‑meeting‑reality” fantasy that resonates strongly with K‑Pop fans, especially those who follow idol‑centric dramas. The drama is set to premiere on tvN’s Thursday‑Friday slot, with episodes also slated for streaming on V Live’s global service and on the Korean platform TVING, making it accessible to overseas viewers through partner sites.

## What Happened

On August 28, tvN released a batch of new stills from the upcoming episode, showcasing a pivotal scene where Nam Da‑reum and Kang Hoon’s character, Kang Hoon‑jae, are seen sharing a quiet moment in the company’s rooftop garden. The caption accompanying the images reads, “When work and love collide, secrets become harder to keep.” This visual cue confirms that the narrative has progressed beyond a simple crush on Lee Chan, moving into a secret office romance between Da‑reum and Hoon‑jae.

The stills also reveal subtle details: Da‑reum is wearing a navy blouse with a soft, pastel scarf—a nod to her character’s shy yet hopeful personality—while Hoon‑jae sports a crisp white shirt and a loosened tie, indicating a more relaxed, after‑hours vibe. In the background, a whiteboard with the phrase “Team Project: Dream” hints at an upcoming joint work assignment that will likely test their hidden relationship.

In a brief press release, tvN’s marketing director, Lee Sun‑hee, stated, “We wanted to capture the tension and excitement that comes with a secret office romance. The chemistry between Kim Hye Jun and Kang Hoon is palpable, and we’re thrilled to share a glimpse of their journey with fans.” No official statements from the actors have been released yet, but both have hinted at the drama’s emotional depth in recent Instagram stories, posting behind‑the‑scenes photos with the hashtags #MyBiasMyBoss and #SecretOfficeLove.

## Why This Matters

The reveal of a secret dating plotline is significant for several reasons. First, it aligns with a growing trend in K‑Drama where workplace romances are used to explore power dynamics and generational gaps, a formula that has proven successful in series like *Crash Landing on You* and *Business Proposal*. By integrating a “bias‑meeting‑reality” angle, the drama taps into the lucrative crossover market of K‑Pop fans who are eager to see their favorite idols and actors in relatable, romantic scenarios.

Second, the pairing of Kim Hye Jun and Kang Hoon could boost both actors’ international profiles. Kim’s recent surge in popularity on TikTok, where clips of her previous dramas have amassed over 5 million views, suggests a strong demand for her next project. Meanwhile, Kang Hoon’s fanbase, though smaller, is highly engaged on platforms like Twitter and Discord, often discussing potential “shipping” scenarios. The buzz generated by these stills may translate into higher viewership numbers, potentially pushing the drama onto weekly top‑10 lists on the Korean streaming chart Circle (formerly Gaon).

Lastly, the drama’s distribution strategy—airing on tvN while simultaneously streaming on V Live and TVING—means it can reach a global audience without delay. For Western fans, the series will be available with English subtitles on V Live’s international platform, and episodes are expected to be uploaded to the official *My Bias, My Boss* YouTube channel within 24 hours of broadcast, following the standard K‑Drama release model. This accessibility can drive higher engagement metrics, which in turn influence advertising revenue and future production budgets for similar romance‑driven series.

## What Fans Can Expect

Following the newly released stills, fans can anticipate a dramatic escalation in the office romance storyline. The next episode is rumored to feature a “confession scene” where Da‑reum must choose between pursuing her idol Lee Chan or embracing her growing feelings for Hoon‑jae. Additionally, a subplot involving a corporate project deadline could force the couple to collaborate under intense pressure, testing their secret bond.

Beyond the main plot, viewers can look forward to cameo appearances by Cha Woo Min, who plays Lee Chan, adding another layer of tension. The drama’s soundtrack is also generating excitement; a ballad performed by popular K‑Pop artist IU is slated for release alongside episode 4, and will be available on Spotify, Apple Music, and the Korean platform Melon.
