Super Junior's Donghae has released his pre-release track 'Good Day' featuring Jay Park and 1iL. The song is a precursor to his first full-length solo album 'ALIVE', which captures his journey so far and the story going forward. 'ALIVE' is set to showcase Donghae's growth as an artist.

## Our Take

Donghae's solo endeavor is a significant milestone in his career, marking a new chapter in his artistic journey. 

 The collaboration with Jay Park and 1iL on 'Good Day' brings a fresh sound to the table, blending genres and styles that will likely appeal to a wide range of listeners. 

 This pre-release track sets the tone for 'ALIVE', which promises to be a deeply personal and introspective album. 

 As a member of Super Junior, Donghae's solo project will be closely watched by fans and industry insiders alike, and its success could pave the way for more solo endeavors from the group's members.
