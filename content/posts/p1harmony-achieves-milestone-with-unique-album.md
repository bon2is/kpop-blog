P1Harmony has made history with their latest mini album 'UNIQUE', achieving the highest first-week sales of their career. The album and its title track were released on March 12, and according to Hanteo Chart, they have surpassed 500,000 sales in the first week. This milestone marks a significant step forward in the group's career.
