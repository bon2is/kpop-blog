Despite the denials, the rumor persisted. ## Why This Matters

Dating rumors have long been a double‑edged sword in K‑pop. On one hand, they generate massive online traffic, boosting streaming numbers and social media engagement. On the other, they can jeopardize an idol’s carefully curated image, especially when agencies enforce strict “no‑dating” policies to maintain fan loyalty. In this case, the age gap and the cross‑genre nature of the alleged couple raise additional cultural questions about age dynamics and the blending of idol and hip‑hop cultures.

From an industry perspective, the incident underscores the power of fan‑generated content to shape narratives. The rapid spread of the screenshot illustrates how a single post can dominate headlines across both Korean and international media within hours. It also reflects the growing scrutiny on privacy; idols and rappers alike are increasingly vulnerable to fabricated evidence that can damage reputations.

Chart-wise, the rumor could have indirect effects. The idol’s group is currently sitting at #3 on the Circle Album Chart, and any negative publicity might impact physical album sales, which still constitute a major revenue stream in K‑pop. Conversely, the heightened attention may drive streaming spikes on platforms like Spotify and Apple Music, where the group’s recent single already enjoys a strong position on the Global Top 50.
