## Our Take

The concern for the TWICE member's well-being highlights the darker side of the K-Pop industry, where idols often prioritize their careers over their health. 

 The K-Pop industry is known for its demanding rehearsal schedules and strict beauty standards, which can take a toll on an idol's physical and mental health. 

 This incident may lead to a larger conversation about idol welfare and the need for better support systems. 

 As fans, it's essential to prioritize the well-being of our favorite idols and advocate for their health and safety above their careers.
