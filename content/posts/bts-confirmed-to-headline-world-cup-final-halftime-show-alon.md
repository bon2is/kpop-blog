Their discography boasts an impressive array of chart-topping albums and singles, including 'Love Yourself: Tear', 'Map of the Soul: Persona', 'Dynamite', and 'Butter'. With a message of self-empowerment and love, BTS has garnered a devoted fan base, known as the ARMY, spanning across the globe.

BTS's recent releases have seen them exploring new sounds and themes, such as their 2020 album 'Be', which featured the hit single 'Life Goes On'. The group has also been recognized for their philanthropic efforts, particularly in the area of education and mental health awareness. As a result, BTS has become one of the most influential and successful K-Pop groups of all time, breaking numerous records and winning countless awards.

The group's history is marked by their tireless work ethic, innovative music videos, and captivating live performances. From their early days performing in small venues in Seoul to selling out stadiums worldwide, BTS has come a long way. Their dedication to their craft and their fans has earned them a reputation as one of the most beloved and respected K-Pop groups globally.
