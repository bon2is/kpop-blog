NCT 127 has finally lifted the veil on the internal discussions that led to member Mark’s departure and the renewal of contracts for the remaining members. After roughly two years of back‑and‑forth talks, the group disclosed the timeline and emotional stakes involved, giving fans a rare glimpse into the often‑secretive world of K‑Pop contract negotiations.

## Background

Formed in 2016 under SM Entertainment, NCT 127 is the Seoul‑based sub‑unit of the larger NCT project, which boasts a rotating roster of members across multiple cities worldwide. Known for their experimental sound, intricate choreography, and strong global following, the unit has released chart‑topping albums such as *Regular* (2018), *Neo Zone* (2020), and *Fact Check* (2023). Their blend of hip‑hop, R&B, and EDM has earned them spots on Billboard’s World Albums chart and consistent placements on South Korea’s Circle Album Chart.

Mark Lee, the Canadian‑born rapper and vocalist, debuted with NCT 127 in 2016 and quickly became a fan‑favorite for his charismatic stage presence and songwriting contributions. Over the years, he has also participated in NCT’s larger projects, including NCT Dream and NCT U, expanding his influence across the brand. In recent months, rumors of his exit swirled online, but official confirmation remained scarce until the latest announcement.

Contract renewals are a pivotal moment for any K‑Pop act. Typically, groups sign seven‑year contracts, and the decision to stay or leave can reshape a group’s lineup and future direction. For NCT 127, the renewal period coincided with a busy schedule of world tours, album releases, and individual activities, making the negotiation process especially complex.

## What Happened

During a recent press briefing, NCT 127’s agency disclosed that discussions about contract renewals began roughly two years ago. The group’s management confirmed that all members, except Mark, opted to renew their contracts after a series of intensive meetings that addressed salary structures, creative control, and future promotional plans. Mark, however, decided to part ways with SM Entertainment, citing personal aspirations and a desire to explore solo opportunities outside the NCT framework.

The agency emphasized that the decision was mutual and amicable, noting that Mark’s contributions will continue to be celebrated in the group’s discography. A spokesperson added that the two‑year negotiation period involved “transparent communication” and “mutual respect,” underscoring the importance of maintaining strong relationships even when members choose different paths.

While no specific dates for upcoming releases were announced, the agency hinted that NCT 127 will continue promoting as a six‑member unit, with plans for new music slated for later this year. Fans can look forward to teasers on the group’s official YouTube channel and updates on SM Entertainment’s social media platforms.

## Why This Matters

Mark’s departure marks the first major lineup change for NCT 127 since its debut, a shift that could influence the group’s musical direction and performance dynamics. Historically, member exits in K‑Pop can lead to both challenges and opportunities: groups may need to re‑arrange choreography, redistribute vocal parts, and adjust branding strategies. However, they also open space for creative experimentation and fresh concepts.

From an industry perspective, the transparent disclosure of contract negotiations is notable. K‑Pop agencies often keep such discussions private, leading to speculation and fan anxiety. By revealing the two‑year timeline, SM Entertainment sets a precedent for openness that could foster greater trust between idols, agencies, and fans.

The renewal of contracts for the remaining members also signals stability for NCT 127’s future activities. With all six members committed to the group, fans can anticipate continued world tours, brand endorsements, and potential collaborations with Western platforms such as Spotify and Apple Music, where NCT 127’s tracks already enjoy substantial streaming numbers.

## What Fans Can Expect

In the coming months, NCT 127 is expected to roll out a new comeback, likely accompanied by a music video on YouTube and streaming availability on major services like Spotify, Apple Music, and Amazon Music. The group’s social media channels will also provide behind‑the‑scenes content, giving fans a chance to see how the six‑member formation adapts on stage.

Mark’s solo pursuits remain a point of excitement. While details are still under wraps, industry insiders suggest he may explore collaborations with international artists and release solo material on platforms such as SoundCloud and Apple Music. Fans should keep an eye on Mark’s personal Instagram and Twitter accounts for teaser updates.
