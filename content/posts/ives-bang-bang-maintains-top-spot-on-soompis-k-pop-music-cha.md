IVE's 'BANG BANG' remains at the top of Soompi's K-Pop Music Chart for the third week in a row. The song has managed to hold off strong competition from other popular groups, with Hearts2Hearts' 'RUDE!' rising to second place and BLACKPINK's 'GO' falling to third. Onew's 'TOUGH LOVE' has debuted at number nine on the chart, marking a notable entry into the top ten.
---

## Our Take

IVE's continued dominance of the chart is a testament to their enduring popularity and the strength of their latest release. 

 The K-Pop industry is highly competitive, with numerous groups vying for attention and chart success. In this context, IVE's ability to maintain their position at the top of the chart is a significant achievement, demonstrating their dedication to their craft and their connection with fans. 

 For fans of IVE, this news is undoubtedly exciting, as it confirms the group's status as one of the leading acts in K-Pop. The success of 'BANG BANG' also bodes well for the group's future career prospects, potentially paving the way for further releases and collaborations. 

 The debut of Onew's 'TOUGH LOVE' at number nine on the chart is also noteworthy, as it highlights the artist's ability to produce compelling music that resonates with listeners.
