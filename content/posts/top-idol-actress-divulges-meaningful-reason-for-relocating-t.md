On the recent MBC variety show broadcast, the idol-actress shared that her decision to move to Jeju Island was motivated by a desire to reconnect with nature and find a sense of peace and tranquility. The beautiful and serene environment of Jeju Island, with its lush green landscapes and stunning coastlines, provided the perfect backdrop for her to unwind and focus on her well-being.

The idol-actress also shared that she has been enjoying her time on the island, engaging in activities such as hiking, reading, and cooking, which have helped her to relax and discover new passions. She mentioned that the supportive community on the island has made her feel welcome and at home, and she is grateful for the opportunity to experience a simpler and more peaceful way of life. Her fans, who have been following her journey, have been inspired by her courage and willingness to prioritize her health and happiness.
