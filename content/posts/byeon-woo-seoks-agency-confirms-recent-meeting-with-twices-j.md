## Background


TWICE is a South Korean girl group formed by JYP Entertainment in 2015, consisting of nine members: Nayeon, Jeongyeon, Momo, Sana, Jihyo, Mina, Dahyun, Chaeyoung, and Tzuyu. With a distinctive sound that blends genres like bubblegum pop, dance-pop, and electropop, TWICE has established itself as one of the most successful and beloved K-Pop groups globally. Since their debut with the single 'The Story Begins', TWICE has released numerous hit songs and albums, including 'Cheer Up', 'TT', 'Knock Knock', 'Signal', 'Likey', 'Dance the Night Away', and 'Fancy'. The group has won numerous awards, including the Melon Music Award for Artist of the Year, the Mnet Asian Music Award for Best Female Group, and the Golden Disc Award for Disc Daesang.


Jeongyeon, whose real name is Yoo Jeong-yeon, is a vocalist and visual of TWICE. Born on November 1, 1996, in Suwon, Gyeonggi Province, South Korea, Jeongyeon began her training at JYP Entertainment in 2010 and was eventually selected as a member of TWICE through the reality show 'Sixteen' in 2015. As a member of TWICE, Jeongyeon has been an integral part of the group's success, showcasing her impressive vocal range and captivating stage presence. In addition to her work with TWICE, Jeongyeon has also made appearances in various television shows and music programs, including 'Inkigayo' and 'Weekly Idol'.


Varo Entertainment, on the other hand, is a relatively new agency that has been making waves in the Korean entertainment industry. Founded in 2020, the agency has already signed several talented actors, including Byeon Woo Seok, who is known for his roles in dramas like 'Record of Youth' and 'Twenty-Five Twenty-One'. With its growing roster of talented artists, Varo Entertainment is quickly establishing itself as a major player in the industry, and its potential signing of Jeongyeon could be a significant coup for the agency.
