Chen, Baekhyun, and Xiumin of EXO have reportedly notified INB100 of their intention to terminate their contracts. The members sent a formal notice in late March, citing unpaid settlements and other issues. This move comes as a significant development in the K-Pop industry, sparking discussions about artist rights and contract fairness.

## Our Take

The decision by Chen, Baekhyun, and Xiumin to terminate their contracts with INB100 marks a significant moment in the K-Pop industry, highlighting the ongoing issues with contract fairness and artist rights. 

 This move is not entirely unexpected, given the historical tensions between K-Pop idols and their management agencies over contract terms and financial transparency. 

 The implications of this decision are far-reaching, potentially paving the way for other idols to re-evaluate their contracts and demand better treatment.
