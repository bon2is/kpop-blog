LNGSHOT, a rookie boy group from Jay Park's label MORE VISION, has announced the official name of their fan club as 'SHOTTIES'. ## Our Take

The announcement of an official fan club name is a significant step for any K-Pop group, as it formalizes the relationship between the artists and their supporters. 

 This move by LNGSHOT indicates their commitment to engaging with their fans and fostering a sense of community among 'SHOTTIES'. The name itself suggests a close, companionable relationship, which could help in strengthening the bond between the group and their fans. 

 In the context of the K-Pop industry, having a named fan club can boost fan loyalty and participation in activities such as concerts, album sales, and voting for awards. For LNGSHOT, this could be a crucial factor in their growth and success, especially as they are part of a competitive market with many established and emerging groups. 

 As 'SHOTTIES' embark on this journey with LNGSHOT, they can look forward to more exclusive content, fan engagements, and possibly even fan club events, which will further enhance their experience as supporters of the group.
