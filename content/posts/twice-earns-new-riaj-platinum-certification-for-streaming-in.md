TWICE’s latest streaming milestone in Japan has fans buzzing— the nine‑member girl group just secured a Platinum certification from the Recording Industry Association of Japan (RIAJ) for exceeding 100 million streams on a single track. The achievement underscores TWICE’s continued grip on the Japanese market, where they have consistently topped charts since their debut.

## Background

Formed by JYP Entertainment in 2015, TWICE quickly rose to fame with their catchy hook‑song “Cheer Up” and the iconic "shy shy shy" line that became a cultural phenomenon across Asia. Over the past eight years, the group has released multiple Japanese studio albums, including *#TWICE* (2017), *BDZ* (2018), and *&TWICE* (2019), each debuting at number one on the Oricon Weekly Albums Chart. Their Japanese singles—"One More Time," "Fanfare," and the recent "Celebrate"—have repeatedly broken streaming and sales records, earning them multiple RIAJ Gold and Double‑Platinum certifications for physical shipments.

TWICE’s success isn’t limited to physical sales; they have embraced digital platforms early on. Their presence on Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube has translated into massive streaming numbers worldwide, and Japan has become a key market for their digital strategy. In 2020, the RIAJ introduced a separate certification system for streaming, recognizing the shift from physical to online consumption. Under this system, songs receive Silver at 30 million streams, Gold at 50 million, and Platinum at 100 million.

## What Happened

According to the latest RIAJ report, TWICE’s Japanese single "Celebrate"—released in June 2024 as the lead track for their upcoming Japanese album—has surpassed the 100 million streaming mark, earning the group a Platinum certification for streaming. The certification was announced on the RIAJ website and subsequently reported by Soompi, confirming the milestone without specifying the exact date of the certification, but noting it was awarded in July 2024.

In a brief statement released through JYP Entertainment’s official channels, TWICE expressed gratitude to Japanese fans, saying, “Your love and support make every milestone possible. We’ll keep bringing you music that makes you smile.” The group’s management also highlighted that the streaming numbers were accumulated across major platforms, including Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube, reflecting a broad digital reach.

## Why This Matters

The Platinum streaming certification is significant for several reasons. First, it validates TWICE’s ability to thrive in a market that heavily values physical sales; achieving 100 million streams demonstrates a strong digital fanbase that complements their CD sales. Second, the milestone places TWICE among a select group of K‑Pop acts—such as BTS, BLACKPINK, and ENHYPEN—that have reached similar streaming heights in Japan, signaling the group’s parity with the industry’s biggest names.

From an industry perspective, the RIAJ’s streaming certifications are becoming a benchmark for measuring an artist’s long‑term relevance in Japan’s evolving music consumption landscape. As streaming continues to outpace physical formats, a Platinum certification can influence promotional strategies, radio play, and even placement on Billboard Japan Hot 100 and Oricon Digital Singles charts. Indeed, "Celebrate" already debuted at #2 on the Billboard Japan Hot 100, bolstered by its streaming surge.

For TWICE, the achievement reinforces their strategic focus on the Japanese market. Their frequent Japanese releases, localized content, and participation in Japanese TV shows have cultivated a dedicated fanbase. The streaming success suggests that fans are not only buying CDs for collectibles but also actively engaging with the group’s music on digital platforms, which can lead to higher visibility on algorithm‑driven playlists and greater opportunities for sync licensing in Japanese media.

## What Fans Can Expect

With the Platinum certification secured, TWICE is set to capitalize on the momentum. The group’s upcoming Japanese album, slated for an October 2024 release, will feature "Celebrate" as its title track along with several new B‑sides. Fans can look forward to a series of music‑show performances on NHK’s *Music Japan* and TV Asahi’s *Music Station*, as well as a potential Japanese arena tour announced later this year.

International fans eager to stream the track can find "Celebrate" on Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube worldwide. The song is also expected to appear on major K‑Pop playlists such as Spotify’s “K‑Pop Rising” and Apple Music’s “K‑Pop Essentials,” ensuring continued exposure beyond Japan.
