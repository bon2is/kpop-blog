## What Happened


So, what are the top fashion tips from K-Pop stars to stay cool this summer? According to BTS's j-hope, hats are a must-have accessory for any summer outfit. From baseball caps to fedoras, hats can add a stylish touch to any look while also providing protection from the sun. j-hope has been spotted wearing a variety of hats in recent years, from bold and colorful designs to more understated and classic looks. Another K-Pop idol who swears by hats is EXO's Baekhyun, who has been known to wear a variety of hats on stage and off.


Sunglasses are another essential accessory for any summer outfit, and K-Pop idols are no exception. BLACKPINK's Jennie has been spotted wearing a variety of stylish sunglasses in recent years, from sleek and sophisticated designs to more bold and eclectic looks. According to Jennie, sunglasses can add a cool and effortless touch to any outfit, while also providing protection from the sun. Other K-Pop idols who have been spotted wearing sunglasses include BTS's Jimin and EXO's Kai, who have both been known to wear stylish and trendy designs.


In addition to hats and sunglasses, K-Pop idols are also swearing by lightweight and breathable clothing to stay cool this summer. From cotton t-shirts to linen pants, these idols are opting for comfortable and practical clothing that can keep them cool even in the hottest temperatures. BTS's Jungkook, for example, has been spotted wearing a variety of lightweight and breathable clothing in recent years, from sleek and sophisticated outfits to more casual and relaxed looks.
