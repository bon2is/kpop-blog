## Our Take

IVE's video clip has sparked a major conversation about the well-being of K-Pop idols. 

 The K-Pop industry is notorious for its intense training regimens and demanding schedules, which can take a toll on an idol's physical and mental health. 

 This incident highlights the need for better support systems for idols and more awareness about mental health in the industry. 

 As a result, fans are calling for greater care and protection for their favorite idols, and IVE's reassurance has helped to ease some of the concern.
