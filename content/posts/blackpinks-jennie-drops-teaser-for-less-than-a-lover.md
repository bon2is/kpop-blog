## Background

Jennie is a member of the popular K-Pop girl group BLACKPINK, formed by YG Entertainment in 2016. The group consists of four members: Jennie, Lisa, Rosé, and Jisoo. BLACKPINK has been known for their unique blend of genres, from hip-hop to pop, and their high-energy performances. Jennie, in particular, has been praised for her versatile skills as a rapper, singer, and dancer. She has been a key component of the group's success, with hits like 'DDU-DU DDU-DU' and 'Kill This Love'. 

In recent years, the members of BLACKPINK have been exploring their solo activities, with Jennie being one of the first to release solo music. Her debut solo single 'SOLO' was released in 2018 and was a huge success, topping various music charts in Korea and internationally. The song's music video also broke records, surpassing 10 million views on YouTube within the first 24 hours of its release. Jennie's solo activities have been highly anticipated, and fans have been waiting for new music from her.

With all the members pursuing their individual passions, BLACKPINK's fan base, known as BLINK, has been spoiled with a wide range of content to enjoy.
