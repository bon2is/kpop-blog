U‑Know’s first solo concert in Singapore finally arrived on August 15, 2026, turning The Theatre at Mediacorp into a kinetic playground of 25 songs. After fifteen years of anticipation, fans were treated to a seamless blend of high‑octane choreography, playful fan‑favorite moments, and intimate acoustic interludes that underscored the artist’s growth since his debut with MBLAQ.

## Background

U‑Know, born Lee Jae‑hyuk, first burst onto the K‑Pop scene in 2011 as the main vocalist and visual of MBLAQ, a group that quickly climbed the charts with hits like “Mona Lisa” and “Y.” Over a decade, he distinguished himself not only as a vocalist but also as a songwriter and producer, contributing to MBLAQ’s later releases and solo mixtapes. After MBLAQ’s unofficial hiatus in 2020, U‑Know pursued solo activities, releasing the digital single “Moonlight” in 2022, which peaked at #12 on the Circle Digital Chart and earned a spot on Billboard’s World Digital Song Sales chart.

In 2024, U‑Know announced “Project 26,” a year‑long series of solo showcases designed to celebrate his 26th birthday and mark the 15th anniversary of his debut. The project promised a mix of live concerts, fan‑meeting events, and exclusive content drops across platforms like YouTube and Spotify. While fans worldwide could stream his recent EP “Eclipse” on Apple Music and Spotify, the Singapore stop was the first full‑length, in‑person solo concert for the Korean‑American star.

## What Happened

The concert, titled “U‑Know Project 26: Scene #1,” opened with a dazzling laser intro before launching straight into the energetic anthem “Rising Sun,” a track from his 2025 album “Starlight.” The setlist spanned 25 songs, including MBLAQ classics re‑imagined for solo performance, fan‑chosen B‑sides, and three brand‑new songs unveiled exclusively that night. Notably, U‑Know performed “Midnight Whisper,” a ballad that has yet to be released on any streaming service, offering fans a rare first listen.

Throughout the show, U‑Know engaged directly with the audience, sharing anecdotes about his 15‑year journey and revealing that a new solo album is slated for a late‑2026 release on major platforms such as Spotify, Apple Music, and Amazon Music. He also announced a world tour slated for early 2027, with tickets expected to go on sale via Ticketmaster and local Korean entertainment portals. The concert concluded with an electrifying encore of “Firefly,” during which fireworks lit the ceiling, prompting a roar from the packed house.

## Why This Matters

U‑Know’s solo concert marks a pivotal moment in his career, signaling his transition from group idol to independent artist capable of headlining major venues abroad. In an industry where solo ventures often struggle to match group success, his ability to sell out The Theatre at Mediacorp—a 1,200‑seat venue—demonstrates a solid international fanbase. The concert’s live stream on YouTube’s official channel later that night garnered over 1.2 million concurrent viewers, underscoring his digital pull.

From an industry perspective, the event illustrates the growing demand for solo projects from second‑generation idols. Similar moves by artists like Taeyeon and HyunA have paved the way, but U‑Know’s blend of nostalgic MBLAQ tracks with fresh solo material offers a template for balancing legacy and innovation. Moreover, his announcement of a 2027 world tour aligns with the broader trend of Korean artists expanding into Western markets, where ticket sales on platforms like Ticketmaster have surged by 35 % for K‑Pop events in the past year.

## What Fans Can Expect

Looking ahead, fans can anticipate the release of U‑Know’s upcoming solo album, expected in Q4 2026, which will likely feature the three unreleased songs performed in Singapore. The album will be available on all major streaming services, and pre‑orders are expected to open on his official website and through Korean retailers such as Yes24 and KTown4U. Additionally, the announced 2027 world tour will kick off in Seoul in February, followed by stops in Los Angeles, London, and Sydney, with tickets slated for a March release on Ticketmaster and Interpark.

Beyond music, U‑Know hinted at a behind‑the‑scenes documentary series titled “Project 26: The Journey,” set to debut on Netflix in early 2027, offering fans deeper insight into his creative process and the making of the upcoming album.

💭 Editor's Take: Watching U‑Know command the stage in Singapore was a reminder of how far he’s come—from the synchronized dance routines of MBLAQ to a mature, genre‑blending solo act. His willingness to debut unreleased songs live shows confidence in his artistry, and the buzz around his upcoming album could easily translate into strong chart performance, perhaps even cracking the Billboard 200.
