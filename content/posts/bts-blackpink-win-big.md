The 2026 iHeartRadio Music Awards took place on March 26, with K-Pop artists such as Rosé, Jennie, Stray Kids, and BTS members j-hope, Jimin, and Jungkook winning awards. The soundtrack to the animated film 'KPop Demon Hunters' also won three awards, including Favorite Soundtrack. The awards ceremony was held at the Dolby Theater in Los Angeles.

## Our Take

The wins at the 2026 iHeartRadio Music Awards mark a significant milestone for K-Pop in the global music scene. 

 The recognition of K-Pop artists and soundtracks at major awards shows like the iHeartRadio Music Awards demonstrates the growing influence and popularity of the genre worldwide. 

 This achievement is also a testament to the hard work and dedication of the artists, who have been consistently producing high-quality music and performances. 

 For fans, these wins are a celebration of their favorite artists' success and a validation of their passion for K-Pop, which has become a major force in the global music industry.
