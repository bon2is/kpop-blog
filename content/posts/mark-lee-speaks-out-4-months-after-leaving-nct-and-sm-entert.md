## Background


Mark Lee, whose full name is Mark Lee, is a Canadian singer, rapper, and songwriter. He was born on August 2, 1999, in Toronto, Canada, and later moved to South Korea to pursue a career in music. Mark was introduced as a member of SM Entertainment's pre-debut training team, SM Rookies, in 2013. He officially debuted as a member of NCT in 2016, becoming a vital part of the group's lineup. Throughout his time with NCT, Mark has been involved in various units, including NCT U, NCT 127, and NCT Dream, showcasing his versatility and talent as a performer.


As a member of NCT, Mark has been part of numerous successful releases, including 'The 7th Sense', 'Fire Truck', 'Cherry Bomb', and 'Superhuman'.
