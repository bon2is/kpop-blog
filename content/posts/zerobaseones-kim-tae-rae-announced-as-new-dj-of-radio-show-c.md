ZEROBASEONE’s Kim Tae Rae is set to spin a new kind of track—one that reaches listeners through the airwaves. On August 26, MBC FM4U confirmed that the 20‑year‑old vocalist will take over as the host of the beloved radio program, now renamed “ZEROBASEONE’s Close Friend.” Fans can tune in to hear Tae Rae’s candid stories, music picks, and exclusive updates from the group.

## Background

ZEROBASEONE debuted in July 2023 under ABONE Entertainment, quickly earning a reputation for their bright pop sound and tight choreography. The nine‑member ensemble—comprising members like Sunwoo, Jihun, and vocalist Kim Tae Rae—has already racked up impressive milestones: a debut EP that topped the Circle Album Chart, a breakout single “Dreamscape” that entered the Billboard Global 200, and a sold‑out debut showcase in Seoul. Their youthful image and relatable lyrics have resonated with both domestic fans and the growing international K‑Pop community, earning them a spot on Spotify’s “New Music Friday” playlists and a growing presence on YouTube Shorts.

While many K‑Pop idols appear on variety shows, radio hosting remains a coveted platform for deepening fan interaction. Programs like “SBS Inkigayo” and “MBC Radio’s Close Friend” have historically served as springboards for idols to showcase personality beyond the stage. Past hosts include veterans such as IU, EXO’s Chanyeol, and Red Velvet’s Irene, each using the mic to share behind‑the‑scenes anecdotes and curate music selections for listeners. The show’s format blends talk segments, listener letters, and live music performances, making it a vital conduit between artists and their audience.

## What Happened

The announcement came via a press release from MBC FM4U and was quickly amplified on Soompi and Korean entertainment portals. Kim Tae Rae will officially begin her duties on September 5, taking over from the previous host, who had led the program for three years. The show will retain its weekly broadcast schedule—every Friday at 10 PM KST—but will now carry the title “ZEROBASEONE’s Close Friend,” signaling a partnership between the group and the station.

In a brief interview, Tae Rae expressed her excitement: “I’ve always loved listening to ‘Close Friend’ when I was younger. Being able to give back and share my own stories feels like a dream.” She also hinted that the program will feature exclusive ZEROBASEONE content, including unreleased tracks, behind‑the‑scenes clips, and live acoustic performances. The station confirmed that the show will be streamed live on MBC’s official YouTube channel and will be available for replay on the MBC Radio app, making it accessible to international fans via YouTube’s global platform.

## Why This Matters

For ZEROBASEONE, securing a high‑profile radio slot is a strategic move that expands their media footprint beyond music shows and social media. Radio exposure can boost streaming numbers; historically, idols who host shows see a measurable uptick in Spotify and Apple Music plays during their tenure. Moreover, the partnership aligns with the group’s push into the global market—MBC FM4U’s digital streams reach audiences in North America, Europe, and Southeast Asia, providing a direct line to the very fans who drive K‑Pop’s overseas chart success.

Industry analysts note that this appointment reflects a broader trend of rookie groups leveraging traditional media to cement legitimacy. While many newer acts rely heavily on TikTok virality, a radio gig offers a sense of longevity and credibility. It also gives the station fresh content: ZEROBASEONE’s youthful energy and growing fanbase can attract younger listeners, potentially boosting the show’s ratings and advertising revenue.

From a cultural standpoint, the rebranding of “Close Friend” underscores how K‑Pop idols are becoming multimedia personalities. The show will likely feature cross‑promotions for upcoming comebacks, merchandise drops, and concert tickets—creating a synergistic marketing loop that benefits both the group and the broadcaster.

## What Fans Can Expect

Listeners can look forward to a mix of music recommendations curated by Tae Rae, personal anecdotes about life in ZEROBASEONE, and surprise guest appearances from fellow idols or industry veterans. The host has promised a special segment called “Friend’s Choice,” where fans submit song requests that will be played live on air. Additionally, the show will debut a limited‑time podcast version, allowing fans to download episodes on Spotify and Apple Podcasts for on‑the‑go listening.

The timing is also crucial: ZEROBASEONE is slated to release their second mini‑album in early November. Expect teasers, track previews, and perhaps an exclusive acoustic version of the title track to premiere on “ZEROBASEONE’s Close Friend” just weeks before the official drop. Fans eager to secure concert tickets should keep an ear out, as the host may announce pre‑sale codes or early‑bird discounts during the broadcast.
