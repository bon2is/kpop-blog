## What Happened

This impressive feat has nearly tripled their previous first-week sales record, solidifying XLOV's growing popularity and commercial success. According to Hanteo Chart, 'I,God' has achieved a remarkable sales milestone, further cementing XLOV's position as a rising force in the K-Pop industry.
