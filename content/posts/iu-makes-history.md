IU stars as Seong Hui Ju, a chaebol heiress, in the new series 'Perfect Crown', which premiered tonight. The show is set in an alternate universe where modern-day Korea is a constitutional monarchy. IU's character requests a royal audience with Grand Prince Ian, played by Byeon Woo Seok.
---

## Our Take

The premiere of 'Perfect Crown' marks a new era in IU's acting career, solidifying her position as a leading lady in K-Drama. 

 The show's unique blend of modern-day Korea and a constitutional monarchy offers a fresh take on the traditional romance genre, sparking curiosity among fans. 

 With IU's dedicated fan base and the show's intriguing premise, 'Perfect Crown' is expected to garner significant attention and viewership. 

 As the series progresses, it will be interesting to see how IU's character navigates the complexities of royal life and her romance with Grand Prince Ian, played by Byeon Woo Seok.
