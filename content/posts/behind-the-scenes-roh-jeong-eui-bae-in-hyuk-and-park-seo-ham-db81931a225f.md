This upcoming romance series promises to tug at your heartstrings as it tells the story of two in-laws who find themselves at odds, yet must come together under one roof to care for their 20-month-old nephew, Woo Joo, following the unexpected loss of their family member.

At the script reading, fans were treated to a glimpse of the chemistry among the cast members, including the talented Roh Jeong Eui, Bae In Hyuk, and Park Seo Ham. These actors not only displayed their acting chops but also demonstrated their warm personalities as they interacted with an adorable child actor who will portray Woo Joo.
