JYP Entertainment has announced that TWICE's Chaeyoung will be modifying her upcoming scheduled activities due to health concerns. Chaeyoung experienced discomfort in her lower back while preparing to depart, leading to the decision. The exact nature of the schedule changes has not been detailed, but the announcement ensures that Chaeyoung's health is the priority.
