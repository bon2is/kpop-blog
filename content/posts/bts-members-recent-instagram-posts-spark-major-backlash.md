## Background

Their music often explores themes of self-empowerment, love, and mental health, resonating with fans from diverse backgrounds and age groups.




BTS's career highlights include numerous awards and accolades, such as the Melon Music Award for Artist of the Year, the Mnet Asian Music Award for Best Male Group, and the Billboard Music Award for Top Duo/Group. They have also broken multiple records in the music industry, including becoming the first K-Pop group to top the US Billboard 200 chart and the first Korean act to perform at the Grammy Awards. The group's recent releases, such as 'Butter' and 'Permission to Dance', have further solidified their position as a leading force in the global music scene.




The group's history is marked by a strong bond between the members, who have been together since their trainee days.
