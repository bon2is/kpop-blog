Rosé's song 'APT.' has become the second song by a K-Pop artist to go triple platinum in the UK, with the British Phonographic Industry (BPI) announcing the certification this month. The song, a collaboration with Bruno Mars, has achieved this milestone at record speed. This achievement is a testament to Rosé's growing global popularity and the increasing influence of K-Pop in the international music scene.
