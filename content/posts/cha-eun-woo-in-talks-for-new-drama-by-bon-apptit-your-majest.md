Cha Eun Woo, a renowned South Korean singer, actor, and member of the popular boy group ASTRO, has begun reviewing his potential comeback project with only five months remaining until his military discharge. His performances have earned him numerous awards and nominations, solidifying his position as a talented and sought-after actor in the Korean entertainment industry.


Cha Eun Woo's military discharge is highly anticipated, and fans are eager to see what projects he will undertake next. The collaboration between Cha Eun Woo and this talented director is expected to result in a high-quality drama that will captivate audiences and leave a lasting impression.

On August 11, a media outlet reported that Cha Eun Woo has selected 'Seorabeol Knife' as his first project after his discharge.
