## Background

WayV is a Chinese boy group formed by SM Entertainment in 2019 as a sub-unit of the popular K-Pop group NCT. The group consists of six members: Kun, Ten, WinWin, Xiaojun, Hendery, and Yangyang. WayV made their debut on January 17, 2019, with the single 'The Vision', and have since released several successful albums and singles, including 'Take Off', 'Take Over the Moon', and 'Kick Back'. The group's music style is a unique blend of genres, ranging from hip-hop and R&B to pop and electronic dance music. WayV has gained a significant following not only in China but also globally, with fans appreciating their energetic performances and catchy songs.

WayV's career has been marked by several notable releases and achievements. In 2020, the group released their second mini album 'Take Over the Moon', which debuted at number five on the US Billboard World Albums chart. The group has also collaborated with other artists, such as the Chinese singer R1SE, and has performed at various music festivals and concerts. WayV's members have also pursued solo activities, with Kun and Xiaojun releasing solo singles and participating in variety shows. The group's versatility and talent have earned them a reputation as one of the most exciting and promising acts in the K-Pop scene.
