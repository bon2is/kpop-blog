An SM Entertainment idol recently confessed to frequently visiting clubs during his trainee days, describing himself as 'really rude' back then. This confession was made on the channel 'Study King Genius Hong Jin Kyung'. The idol's honesty has raised eyebrows and curiosity about the lifestyles of K-Pop trainees.

## Our Take

The news of an SM Entertainment idol visiting clubs often during his trainee days is significant because it humanizes K-Pop idols, often portrayed as having strict, disciplined lifestyles. 

 This confession comes at a time when the K-Pop industry is under scrutiny for its training regimes and the personal freedoms allowed to its idols. The fact that an idol from a major entertainment company like SM Entertainment felt comfortable enough to share this aspect of his past suggests a shift towards more openness and honesty in the industry. 

 For fans, this revelation might change their perception of their favorite idols, making them seem more relatable and down-to-earth. 

 The implications of this confession on the idol's career could be minimal, given the changing attitudes towards K-Pop idols' personal lives, with fans increasingly seeking authenticity and humanity in their idols.
