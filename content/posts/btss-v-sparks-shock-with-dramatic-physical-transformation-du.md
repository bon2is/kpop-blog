## Background

BTS, or Bangtan Sonyeondan, has been at the forefront of the global K-Pop phenomenon, breaking records and shattering cultural barriers since their debut in 2013. With a diverse discography that spans genres from hip-hop to pop and rock, the group has managed to appeal to a wide and dedicated fan base. V, whose real name is Kim Tae-hyung, is one of the vocalists and visual members of the group, known for his deep, soulful voice and striking appearance. Throughout BTS's career, V has been an integral part of the group's musical and visual identity, contributing to songwriting, composition, and even photography.

BTS's journey to international stardom has been marked by numerous milestones, including topping the Billboard 200 chart with several of their albums, such as 'Love Yourself: Tear', 'Map of the Soul: Persona', and 'Map of the Soul: 7'. Their music often carries themes of self-empowerment, love, and the complexities of growing up, resonating deeply with listeners from diverse backgrounds. V, alongside the other members, has been pivotal in crafting this narrative through his musical contributions and artistic vision.
