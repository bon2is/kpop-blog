## What Happened

'VIRAL' is an intense, addictive track with a strong beat and memorable hooks, and its music video has been praised for its high-quality production and captivating visuals. Six hours after the album's release, 'VIRAL' had climbed to No. 5 on Melon's Top 100 chart, a significant achievement for the group.
