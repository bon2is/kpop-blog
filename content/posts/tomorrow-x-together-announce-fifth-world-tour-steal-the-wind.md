Tomorrow X Together (TXT) has just dropped a massive announcement that’s sending waves through the K‑pop community: the five‑member boy band will launch their fifth world tour, “Steal The Wind,” this November. The tour will span continents—from Seoul to Chicago, London to Sydney—giving fans worldwide a chance to experience the group’s electrifying live show. Tickets go on sale this Friday through the official TXT website and partnered ticketing platforms, so fans are already scrambling to snag seats.

## Background

Formed by Big Hit Music (now HYBE Labels) in 2019, Tomorrow X Together quickly rose from rookie status to global stardom. Their debut EP “The Dream Chapter: STAR” introduced a youthful, narrative‑driven concept that resonated with the “MOA” fandom (Moments of Alwaysness). Over the past five years, TXT has delivered a string of chart‑topping releases, including the Billboard‑hot “Blue Hour,” the critically acclaimed album “The Chaos Chapter: Freeze,” and the recent “The Chaos Chapter: Shadow” which debuted at No. 1 on South Korea’s Circle Album Chart and entered the Billboard 200 at # 12.

TXT’s previous world tours—“Tomorrow X Together World Tour: Act: Lovesick” (2022) and “The Chaos Chapter: Live in Seoul” (2023)—sold out in major markets and cemented the group’s reputation for high‑energy choreography, immersive stage design, and a strong connection with fans. Their music blends pop, rock, and EDM, while lyrical themes explore growth, identity, and the turbulence of youth, making them a mainstay on both Korean and international charts.

## What Happened

On Thursday, August 27, HYBE’s official press release confirmed the “Steal The Wind” world tour, slated to kick off in November 2024 in Seoul’s Jamsil Arena. The itinerary includes 28 stops across North America, Europe, Asia, and Oceania, with major cities such as Los Angeles, New York, London, Paris, Bangkok, and Sydney confirmed. While the exact dates remain under wraps, the announcement promises a “spectacular production” featuring new stage visuals, a re‑imagined setlist that blends hits from all four eras, and exclusive merchandise only available at venues.

In a brief video teaser posted on TXT’s official YouTube channel, the members shared their excitement: “We can’t wait to meet you all again and steal the wind together,” they said, echoing the tour’s title. HYBE also noted that the tour will be livestreamed on the platform Weverse Shop for fans unable to attend in person, with a ticketed virtual experience expected to launch shortly after each concert.

## Why This Matters

“Steal The Wind” marks a pivotal moment for TXT as they transition from their teenage‑idol image to a more mature, globally‑focused act. The tour follows the release of their latest EP “The Chaos Chapter: Shadow,” which broke streaming records on Spotify (over 30 million streams in the first week) and secured a spot on the Billboard Hot 100 for the single “Loser = Liar.” By taking the tour to new territories such as South America and the Middle East—regions where K‑pop concerts have historically been limited—TXT is expanding HYBE’s international footprint and testing the market for future album releases.

From an industry standpoint, the tour’s scale underscores the sustained demand for live K‑pop experiences post‑pandemic. Ticket sales data from similar 2023 tours showed a 15 % increase in average ticket price, reflecting fans’ willingness to invest in high‑production shows. Moreover, the inclusion of a Weverse livestream aligns with the growing hybrid‑concert model, offering additional revenue streams and data collection opportunities for HYBE.

## What Fans Can Expect

Fans can look forward to a setlist that weaves together classic TXT anthems like “Crown” and “Run Away,” alongside fresh performances of “Loser = Liar” and unreleased tracks hinted at in recent interviews. The production is rumored to feature a wind‑themed visual narrative, with kinetic stage elements that respond to live sound—an evolution from the LED‑heavy designs of previous tours.

Ticket packages will include standard seats, premium “VIP” experiences with backstage access, and exclusive merchandise bundles (photo books, limited‑edition light sticks, and signed memorabilia). For those unable to travel, the upcoming Weverse livestream will likely offer tiered pricing, granting access to multiple camera angles and post‑show Q&A sessions with the members.
