---

## Our Take

The news regarding Taemin, EXO-CBX, and THE BOYZ potentially being unable to promote on music shows highlights the ongoing challenges artists face in the K-Pop industry, particularly in terms of health and contractual obligations. As K-Pop becomes increasingly competitive and global, the pressure on idols to deliver consistent performances and promotions can take a toll on their well-being. This situation emphasizes the need for entertainment companies to prioritize the health of their artists over relentless schedules. Moreover, with the landscape of music consumption shifting towards digital platforms, the traditional reliance on television promotions is being questioned. If these artists are unable to participate, it could open discussions about alternative promotional strategies that don't compromise the health of the artists. This incident serves as a reminder of the human element behind the glitz and glamour of K-Pop and the necessity for a more sustainable approach to artist management.
