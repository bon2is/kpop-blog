In a recent exclusive interview with Soompi, virtual K-Pop group PLAVE sat down to discuss their latest comeback with 'Caligo Pt.2', their fourth mini album. This release has marked another successful milestone in their journey as a virtual group, and they shared valuable insights into their music, creative process, and the path they are taking in the K-Pop industry.


## Background


PLAVE, as a virtual group, has been making waves in the K-Pop scene with their unique concept and captivating music. Although the group is virtual, each member brings their own distinctive talents and strengths to the table. With their formation, PLAVE aimed to push the boundaries of what a K-Pop group could be, experimenting with digital avatars and online presence. Their career highlights include the release of their debut single, which quickly gained attention for its innovative music video and the group's fresh sound. Since then, PLAVE has been consistently releasing music, with their 'Caligo' series being a particular focus. This series has seen the group explore darker, more introspective themes, resonating deeply with their fanbase.


The 'Caligo' series, which includes 'Caligo Pt.1' and the recently released 'Caligo Pt.2', showcases PLAVE's ability to create cohesive, themed music that tells a story. Each part of the series offers a glimpse into the group's musical versatility and their willingness to experiment with different genres and styles. PLAVE's music often features a blend of genres, from hip-hop and R&B to pop and electronic elements, making their sound both contemporary and distinctive. The group's recent releases have been well-received by fans and critics alike, with many praising the group's creativity and the depth of their music.


PLAVE's history as a virtual group is also noteworthy, as they have managed to build a strong fanbase despite the challenges that come with not being a traditional, in-person group. Their online presence has been key to their success, with the group engaging regularly with fans through social media and live streams. This direct interaction has helped foster a sense of community among fans and has been instrumental in PLAVE's growth as artists. Their approach to fan engagement is personalized and inclusive, making their fanbase feel valued and connected to the group.


## What Happened


In their exclusive interview with Soompi, PLAVE discussed their latest comeback with 'Caligo Pt.2', delving into the creative process behind the album and the themes they explored. The group talked about the challenges of working as a virtual entity, including the logistical aspects of recording and producing music remotely. Despite these challenges, PLAVE expressed their satisfaction with the outcome of 'Caligo Pt.2', highlighting the growth and development they have undergone as artists. They also shared insights into their individual strengths as members and how these strengths contribute to the group's overall sound and dynamic.


The interview provided a candid look at PLAVE's approach to their music and their experiences as a virtual group. The members discussed their inspirations for the 'Caligo' series, citing personal experiences and observations about the world around them. They also touched upon the importance of their fanbase, expressing gratitude for the support they have received and acknowledging the role fans have played in their success. PLAVE's appreciation for their fans is evident in their dedication to creating music that resonates with them and in their efforts to maintain a strong, personal connection with their audience.
