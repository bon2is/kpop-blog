## Our Take

This news matters as it marks a significant change in Ten's career, allowing him to explore new opportunities while still being part of NCT and WayV. 

 The K-Pop industry has seen several artists leave their management companies in recent years, citing reasons such as creative differences or desire for more control over their careers. 

 Ten's decision to remain with NCT and WayV suggests that he values his relationships with his group members and fans, and is committed to continuing his work with the groups. 

 This move is likely to be closely watched by fans, who will be eager to see how Ten's career evolves and what new projects he will undertake outside of SM Entertainment.
