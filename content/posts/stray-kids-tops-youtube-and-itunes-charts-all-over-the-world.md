## Background

Stray Kids is a South Korean boy group formed by JYP Entertainment in 2017. The group consists of nine members: Bang Chan, Woojin, Lee Know, Changbin, Hyunjin, Han, Felix, Seungmin, and I.N. Stray Kids made their official debut on March 25, 2018, with the EP 'I Am Not' and have since released several successful albums and singles, including 'I Am Who', 'I Am You', and 'Clé 1: Miroh'. The group is known for their energetic and addictive songs, as well as their impressive choreography and captivating music videos. With their unique blend of genres and styles, Stray Kids has gained a loyal fan base not only in Korea but also globally, with fans from all over the world eagerly anticipating their new releases.

With their consistent releases of high-quality music and their engaging performances, Stray Kids has established themselves as one of the leading K-Pop groups of their generation.
