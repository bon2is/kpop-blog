KickFlip’s Amaru will remain on hiatus from group activities for the time being, as JYP Entertainment confirmed in an official statement released on September 1. The agency emphasized that Amaru is receiving ongoing treatment and will take the necessary time to rest, reassuring fans that his health remains the top priority.

## Background

KickFlip, a seven‑member boy group under JYP Entertainment, debuted in 2022 and quickly rose to prominence with their high‑energy performances and genre‑blending tracks. Their breakout single “Neon Lights” topped the Circle Digital Chart and secured a spot on Billboard’s World Digital Song Sales chart, marking the group as one of the fastest‑rising acts of the new generation. Since debut, KickFlip has amassed a global fanbase, with strong streaming numbers on Spotify and Apple Music, and their first mini‑album “Electric Pulse” peaked at number 3 on the Billboard 200’s World Albums chart.

The group’s lineup originally consisted of seven members: Joon, Hyun, Seojun, Minho, Dae‑won, Kai, and Amaru. Amaru, the main vocalist, is known for his soulful tone and has contributed to songwriting on several tracks, including the lyrical bridge of “Midnight Echo.” His presence has been a key factor in the group’s vocal diversity, often highlighted in live performances and variety show appearances.

In early 2024, KickFlip embarked on their first world tour, “Electric Surge,” which visited major cities across North America, Europe, and Asia. The tour sold out quickly, and tickets were heavily sought after on platforms like Ticketmaster and Live Nation. However, midway through the tour, Amaru took a brief break due to undisclosed health concerns, sparking worry among fans worldwide.

## What Happened

On September 1, JYP Entertainment released a concise statement confirming that Amaru will continue his hiatus indefinitely. The agency wrote, “Amaru is currently undergoing treatment and will take the necessary time to rest and recover. We appreciate the understanding and support of fans during this period.” No specific medical details were disclosed, respecting Amaru’s privacy. The statement also clarified that KickFlip will proceed with promotional activities as a six‑member unit, ensuring that the group’s schedule—including upcoming music show appearances and a planned fan‑meeting in Seoul—remains unchanged.

The announcement was first reported by Soompi, which cited the agency’s press release. Since then, fans have taken to social media platforms such as Twitter, Instagram, and the dedicated fan‑cafe on Weverse to express support for Amaru, using hashtags like #GetWellSoonAmaru and #KickFlipFamily. The group’s official YouTube channel posted a short video montage thanking fans for their patience, while the agency’s Twitter account reiterated the commitment to Amaru’s health.

While no new music or comeback date was announced alongside the health update, JYP hinted that KickFlip’s next comeback could be scheduled later in the year, depending on Amaru’s recovery progress. This leaves room for speculation about whether the group will eventually return to a seven‑member formation or continue as a six‑member act for the foreseeable future.

## Why This Matters

Amaru’s hiatus underscores a growing awareness within the K‑pop industry about artists’ mental and physical well‑being. In recent years, several high‑profile idols have taken breaks for health reasons, prompting agencies to adopt more transparent communication strategies. JYP’s straightforward statement reflects a shift toward prioritizing member health over relentless promotional cycles.

From a commercial standpoint, the continuation of KickFlip’s activities as a six‑member unit helps maintain momentum on the charts. Their latest single, “Gravity,” remains on the Circle Digital Chart’s top 10, and the group’s YouTube views continue to climb, indicating that fans are still highly engaged despite the reduced lineup. However, the absence of Amaru’s vocal contributions could affect the group’s sound dynamics, potentially influencing future musical direction.

The situation also has implications for the global fan community. KickFlip’s growing presence on platforms like Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube means that any disruption in the group’s lineup can ripple through streaming metrics and international tour planning. Fans often rely on “where to watch” and “how to stream” information, and JYP’s clear communication helps mitigate uncertainty, keeping the fanbase informed and supportive.

## What Fans Can Expect

In the coming weeks, KickFlip will promote “Gravity” on Korean music shows such as M Countdown, Inkigayo, and Music Bank, performing with the six remaining members. The group is also slated to hold a virtual fan‑meeting on the Weverse platform, where fans can interact with the members and send messages of support to Amaru. Additionally, JYP hinted at a possible summer comeback, though no concrete date has been set. Fans should keep an eye on official channels for any updates regarding Amaru’s health and potential return.

For those eager to stay connected, KickFlip’s music is available on all major streaming services—Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music—and their latest music video can be streamed on YouTube. Merchandise related to the current promotion can be purchased through the official Ktown4U store and the group's official Instagram shop.
