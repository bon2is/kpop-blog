Namkoong Min and NCT's Jaemin continue to show their support for each other. Recently, Jaemin sent a thoughtful gift to Namkoong Min on the set of his new drama. The two stars had previously appeared together on the YouTube show 'SELF-ON KODE', where they noted their uncanny resemblance.

## Our Take

This heartwarming gesture highlights the strong bond between Namkoong Min and Jaemin. 

 Their friendship, which began when fans noticed their similar looks, has blossomed into a beautiful display of mutual support and admiration. 

 The K-Pop and K-Drama industries often see collaborations and friendships between artists, but this one is particularly endearing. 

 For fans of both Namkoong Min and NCT, this exchange is a delightful reminder of the positive relationships that can form between artists from different genres.
