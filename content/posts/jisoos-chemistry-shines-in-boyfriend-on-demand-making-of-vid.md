Netflix's 'Boyfriend on Demand' has released a making-of video showcasing Jisoo's chemistry with co-stars Seo In Guk, Kim Sung Cheol, and Seo Kang Jun. The romantic comedy series follows Seo Mi Rae, a webtoon producer who uses a virtual dating simulation service. Jisoo is set to make her acting debut in the series, which is directed by Kim Jung Sik.
