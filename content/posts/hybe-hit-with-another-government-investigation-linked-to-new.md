## Background
NewJeans is a South Korean girl group formed by ADOR, a subsidiary of HYBE. The group debuted in 2022 with their self-titled EP, which garnered significant attention for its unique blend of genres and the members' youthful energy. NewJeans consists of five members: Minji, Hanni, Danielle, Haerin, and Hyein. Each member brings their own distinct charm and talent to the table, making the group a compelling watch for fans of K-Pop. Before their debut, the members underwent rigorous training, and their dedication to their craft is evident in their polished performances and music videos.
