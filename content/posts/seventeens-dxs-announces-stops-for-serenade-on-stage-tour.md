SEVENTEEN’s unit DxS, comprising Dokyeom and Seungkwan, has announced the dates and locations for their upcoming 'SERENADE' ON STAGE tour, kicking off in Incheon from April 17 to 19. The tour will include stops in various cities across Asia, including Chiba and Macao, allowing fans to experience their unique musical performances live.
---

## Our Take

This news is significant for both SEVENTEEN and their dedicated fanbase, known as CARATs, as it marks a continuation of the group's commitment to engaging with their fans worldwide. The addition of the DxS unit's tour demonstrates the versatility of SEVENTEEN, showcasing individual members while maintaining the collective group's presence.

In the broader context of the K-Pop industry, unit tours have become increasingly popular as they allow groups to explore different musical styles and concepts. For SEVENTEEN, this tour will not only strengthen their brand as a versatile group but also provide a platform for members to shine individually, which can lead to new opportunities in their careers.

For fans, the 'SERENADE' ON STAGE tour represents a chance to connect with their favorite members in a more intimate setting. The anticipation surrounding these performances reflects the fans’ unwavering support and enthusiasm for SEVENTEEN's diverse talents, ultimately enhancing their loyalty and engagement with the group.

As SEVENTEEN continues to expand their touring schedule and engage with international markets, it solidifies their standing as a leading force in the K-Pop scene. This tour could potentially set the stage for even bigger projects and collaborations in the future, making it an exciting time for both the artists and their fans alike.
