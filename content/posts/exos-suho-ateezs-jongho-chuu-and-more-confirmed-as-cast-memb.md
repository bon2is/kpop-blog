The sixth season of SBS’s beloved busking series “The Listen” just got a star‑studded boost. On September 2, the network unveiled a lineup that includes EXO’s charismatic leader Suho, ATEEZ’s powerhouse vocalist Jongho, former Loona member Chuu, and eleven other acclaimed singers. Fans are already buzzing about where to watch the upcoming episodes, which are expected to stream on SBS’s official YouTube channel and be available for replay on V Live and the Korean streaming platform TVING.

## Background

“The Listen” (Korean title: “The Listen: The Season We Stayed”) debuted in 2020 as a music‑reality program that blends street‑style busking with high‑quality production. Unlike typical idol variety shows, the series focuses on pure vocal talent, inviting singers from various backgrounds—soloists, group members, and even indie artists—to perform acoustic sets in public spaces across Seoul. The concept resonated with viewers, earning strong ratings and a dedicated fanbase on YouTube, where past seasons amassed millions of views per episode.

Over its five previous seasons, the show has featured a mix of veteran idols and rising stars. Notable alumni include IU’s early‑career performances, Red Velvet’s Wendy, and soloist Baekho of NU’EST, each delivering intimate renditions that highlighted their vocal prowess beyond polished stage productions. The program’s success also translated into chart impact; songs performed on “The Listen” often see spikes on the Gaon Digital Chart and even appear on the Billboard Global Excl. US chart, underscoring the show’s influence on streaming numbers.

The upcoming season arrives at a pivotal moment for many of its cast members. EXO’s Suho, who debuted in 2012, has recently completed his mandatory military service and is gearing up for a solo mini‑album slated for early 2025. ATEEZ’s Jongho, known for his soaring high‑notes, is preparing for the group’s world tour “The Fellowship: Map of the Soul”. Meanwhile, Chuu, who left Loona in early 2024, launched a solo career with the digital single “Dreaming” that charted on the Circle Digital Chart’s top ten. Their participation in “The Listen 6” offers a platform to showcase new material and reconnect with fans in a more personal setting.

## What Happened

SBS released an official press statement on September 2, confirming the full roster of 14 vocalists for “The Listen 6”. The list includes:

- Lee Young‑hyun (former “Produce 101” trainee)
- Tei (Rising R&B singer)
- DK (Former “Superstar K” contestant)
- Lee Seung‑gi (Veteran actor‑singer)
- Suho (EXO)
- Jongho (ATEEZ)
- Chuu (former Loona)
- Kim Jae‑wan (Soloist)
- Yoon Mirae (Legendary hip‑hop/R&B artist)
- Baekho (NU’EST)
- Lee Hi (YG Entertainment)
- Park Ji‑hoon (Former Wanna One)
- Kim Min‑seok (Indie folk)
- Hwang Min‑hyun (NU’EST)

The program will film in various iconic Seoul locations—Hongdae’s bustling streets, the historic Gwanghwamun Plaza, and the scenic Hangang River banks—allowing each artist to craft a unique acoustic set that reflects the environment. According to the SBS production team, episodes will air weekly on Saturdays at 22:00 KST, with full‑length performances uploaded to SBS’s YouTube channel within 24 hours of broadcast. International fans can also catch subtitled highlights on the V Live app, which often provides English captions for major K‑pop shows.

In a brief interview, Suho expressed his excitement: “Returning to the stage after service feels surreal, and ‘The Listen’ gives me a chance to sing in a raw, stripped‑down way that fans haven’t seen in years.” Jongho added, “I love busking vibes; it’s just me and the mic, and I can truly connect with listeners.” Chuu, who recently signed with a new agency, said, “This is a perfect platform to show my growth as an artist and share my new music with both Korean and global fans.”

## Why This Matters

“The Listen” has become a barometer for vocal credibility in the K‑pop industry. By inviting idols known primarily for choreography and performance—like Suho and Jongho—the show underscores a shift toward recognizing vocal talent as a core component of idol identity. Their participation signals confidence in their singing abilities and offers a strategic promotional boost ahead of upcoming releases and tours.

From an industry perspective, the program’s multi‑platform distribution maximizes global reach. YouTube’s algorithm favors music content with high watch‑time, and past “The Listen” clips have consistently trended in K‑pop categories, driving additional streams for the featured songs on Spotify and Apple Music. Moreover, the show’s partnership with TVING ensures domestic viewers can re‑watch episodes on-demand, while the V Live subtitles cater to the English‑speaking market—a key factor in attracting high‑CPM advertising revenue.

Chart-wise, performances on “The Listen” have historically translated into measurable boosts. For example, Wendy’s acoustic rendition of “When I Was…“ in Season 3 saw a 38% increase in digital sales the following week, and Baekho’s live cover of “Love Scenario” propelled his solo single into the top 20 of the Billboard World Digital Song Sales chart. With Suho, Jongho, and Chuu on board, the upcoming season is poised to generate similar spikes, potentially influencing the Circle Album and Digital charts as fans stream the full performances.

## What Fans Can Expect

Viewers can look forward to a blend of classic ballads, contemporary R&B, and surprise genre‑crossovers. Suho is rumored to perform a piano‑driven version of EXO’s “Love Shot” alongside a brand‑new solo track yet to be announced. Jongho may showcase his vocal range with an acoustic arrangement of ATEEZ’s “Wonderland” and a soulful cover of Sam Smith’s “Stay With Me”. Chuu is expected to debut a stripped‑back rendition of her latest single “Dreaming”, possibly accompanied by a surprise duet with fellow cast member Lee Hi.

Beyond individual performances, “The Listen 6” will feature collaborative stages where artists pair up for duets or group harmonies—an element that has become a fan‑favorite in previous seasons. The production hints at a special finale episode that could include a mega‑collaboration featuring all 14 singers, a moment that would likely trend worldwide and become a viral highlight on TikTok and Instagram Reels.

For fans eager to watch, the first episode drops on September 14, 2024. Mark your calendars, set a reminder on YouTube, and follow SBS’s official social media accounts for behind‑the‑scenes clips and live‑chat events.
