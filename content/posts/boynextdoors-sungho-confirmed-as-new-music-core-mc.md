BOYNEXTDOOR’s Sungho has just landed a coveted spot on one of South Korea’s most iconic music programs. The rookie idol was confirmed on August 28 as the newest MC of MBC’s **Music Core**, a role that not only boosts his personal profile but also shines a spotlight on his group’s rising momentum.

## Background

BOYNEXTDOOR (often stylized as BOYNEXTDOOR) debuted under KOZ Entertainment, a subsidiary of HYBE, on May 30, 2023 with the EP *Underdog*. The seven‑member boy group—comprising Sungho, Jinsu, Seungmin, Youngjae, Sangwoo, Taesan, and Daehyun—quickly earned a reputation for sharp choreography, emotive vocal lines, and a youthful, “next‑door” charm that resonated with both domestic and international fans. Their debut single “But I Like You” charted within the top 20 on the Circle Digital Chart and earned them a rookie award at the 2023 Asia Artist Awards.

Since their debut, BOYNEXTDOOR has been steadily climbing the charts. Their first full‑length album *Ready to Fly* (released March 2024) peaked at #4 on the Circle Album Chart and sold over 120,000 copies in its first week, a notable feat for a group less than a year old. The title track “Fly High” also broke into the Billboard World Digital Song Sales chart, landing at #12 and marking the group’s first entry on a U.S. chart. Their growing discography now includes two EPs, one studio album, and a string of successful collaborations, including a recent duet with label‑mate LE SSERAFIM’s Chaeyeon that topped iTunes charts in over 30 regions.

Beyond music, the members have been active on variety shows, web dramas, and brand endorsements. Sungho, the group’s main vocalist and visual, quickly became a fan‑favorite for his bright personality and strong stage presence. He previously appeared on the variety program *Idol Room* and the web drama *Love in the Time of K‑Pop*, showcasing his acting chops and widening his appeal beyond the music sphere.

## What Happened

On August 28, an official spokesperson for MBC’s *Music Core* confirmed rumors circulating on social media that Sungho had been selected as the show’s next MC. The statement read, “It’s true that Sungho will be taking on the role of a new MC.” The announcement came alongside a brief teaser video featuring Sungho introducing himself in the iconic *Music Core* studio, complete with the program’s neon backdrop and live band.

*Music Core* has a storied history of pairing idols with seasoned hosts. Former MCs include EXO’s Suho, NCT’s Taeyong, and Red Velvet’s Irene—each bringing a blend of charisma and industry insight. Sungho joins the current hosting duo of actress Kim Mi‑ji and idol Jang Won‑young (formerly of Stray Kids). His addition is expected to begin with the September 2 broadcast, where he will co‑host the weekly live performances, interview artists, and introduce special segments.

In a brief interview with Soompi, Sungho expressed his gratitude: “I’ve always admired *Music Core* as a platform that celebrates all genres of Korean music. To be trusted with this role is a huge honor, and I hope to bring fresh energy and connect with both artists and fans.” The *Music Core* team also highlighted Sungho’s “natural hosting ability” and “strong fanbase” as key factors in their decision.

## Why This Matters

Sungho’s appointment is significant on multiple fronts. For BOYNEXTDOOR, it marks the group’s first major foray into mainstream broadcast media, a milestone that often correlates with increased album sales and higher visibility on music charts. Historically, idols who become MCs see a spike in streaming numbers; for example, when IU hosted *Inkigayo* in 2015, her solo releases experienced a 35% bump in digital sales within a month.

From an industry perspective, the move reflects MBC’s continued strategy of integrating younger, up‑and‑coming idols into their flagship programs to attract a younger demographic and keep the show culturally relevant. By selecting a member of a HYBE‑affiliated group, *Music Core* also aligns itself with the current powerhouses of K‑Pop, ensuring that the show remains a key venue for debut stages and comeback performances.

Moreover, Sungho’s role underscores the growing trend of idols expanding their personal brand beyond music. Hosting duties provide a platform for honing public speaking, live improvisation, and cross‑media exposure—skills that are increasingly valuable in the era of global streaming and multi‑platform content.

## What Fans Can Expect

Fans can look forward to Sungho’s charismatic presence on a weekly basis, starting with the September 2 episode. Expect him to introduce the latest comebacks, conduct live interviews, and possibly bring BOYNEXTDOOR’s own music into special segments. The show often releases highlight clips on YouTube and the official *Music Core* app, making it easy for international fans to catch Sungho’s moments even if they can’t watch the live broadcast.

In addition, BOYNEXTDOOR is slated to release a summer special single in October, and Sungho’s MC duties could provide a promotional boost. The group’s agency, KOZ Entertainment, hinted that the upcoming track may be performed live on *Music Core* with Sungho taking a lead role in the introduction—an exciting prospect for fans eager to see their idol shine on a major stage.
