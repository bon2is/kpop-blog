TREASURE is back on the radar with a jaw‑dropping dance performance video for “DANGER,” the fierce B‑side from their August mini‑album NEW WAV. Dropped at the stroke of midnight KST on August 24, the visual showcases the group’s signature high‑energy choreography and has already ignited a wave of reactions on social media.

## Background

Formed by YG Entertainment in 2020, TREASURE quickly rose from reality‑show trainees to one of the most dynamic fourth‑generation boy groups. Their debut EP THE FIRST STEP: CHAPTER ONE in 2020 introduced a blend of hip‑hop swagger and pop sensibility, but it was the 2021 single “BOY” that cemented their reputation for powerful performances. Since then, TREASURE has amassed a devoted global fanbase—known as “Treasure Hunters”—and consistently broken into the Billboard 200 with releases like THE SECOND STEP: CHAPTER ONE (2022) and THE THIRD STEP: CHAPTER ONE (2023).

The group’s most recent project, NEW WAV, arrived in August 2024 as a five‑track mini‑album, featuring the title track “NEW WAV” and the hard‑hitting B‑side “DANGER.” While “NEW WAV” highlighted a bright, synth‑driven sound, “DANGER” offered a darker, trap‑infused edge, giving fans a glimpse of TREASURE’s versatility. The album debuted atop the South Korean Circle Album Chart and entered the top‑10 of the Billboard 200, confirming the group’s growing international pull.

## What Happened

At precisely 00:00 KST on August 24, TREASURE’s official YouTube channel premiered the full dance performance video for “DANGER.” Filmed in a gritty warehouse set with neon lighting, the video emphasizes synchronized footwork, intricate hand‑gestures, and a fierce stage presence that matches the track’s aggressive beat. The choreography was crafted by renowned YG choreographer Mike Song, who previously worked on BLACKPINK’s “Kill This Love” and SEVENTEEN’s “HOT.”

In a brief caption accompanying the upload, YG Entertainment thanked fans for their “endless support” and encouraged viewers to stream the video on YouTube and share their reactions using the hashtag #TREASUREDanger. The group also promoted the video on their official V Live channel and posted teaser clips on TikTok, where the #DANGERChallenge quickly trended, prompting fans worldwide to replicate the signature moves.

The release coincided with TREASURE’s appearance on the streaming drama Idol Festa Attack on Viki, where they performed a short snippet of “DANGER” during a behind‑the‑scenes segment. While the drama itself is not a music release, the cross‑promotion helped drive additional traffic to the performance video, especially among viewers who discovered TREASURE through the show.

## Why This Matters

Performance videos are a critical tool for K‑pop groups to showcase choreography, boost song streaming numbers, and maintain visibility between comebacks. For TREASURE, releasing “DANGER” as a dance video serves several strategic purposes. First, it extends the promotional cycle of NEW WAV, keeping the mini‑album in public conversation beyond the initial title‑track push. Second, the high‑production value underscores YG’s confidence in TREASURE’s performance capabilities, positioning them alongside senior label mates like BIGBANG and BLACKPINK.

From an industry perspective, the rapid accumulation of views—over 5 million within 24 hours—signals strong fan engagement and contributes to YouTube’s algorithmic promotion, potentially attracting casual listeners. Moreover, the video’s release aligns with a broader trend of K‑pop groups issuing B‑side performance videos to capitalize on streaming metrics that feed into chart calculations such as the Circle Digital Chart and Billboard Hot 100.

TREASURE’s global chart performance also benefits from this visual content. The group’s recent entry into the Billboard 200 was driven largely by streaming and album sales outside Korea. By making “DANGER” readily accessible on YouTube (and subsequently on Apple Music’s video library and Spotify’s Canvas feature), YG maximizes the song’s exposure across the platforms that feed Western chart data.

## What Fans Can Expect

Following the “DANGER” video, TREASURE is set to embark on a series of fan‑sign events in Seoul and Tokyo later this month, with tickets expected to go on sale through YG’s official fan‑club portal. Additionally, the group hinted at a possible “NEW WAV” world‑tour in a recent interview on Mnet’s M Countdown, though official dates have yet to be announced. Fans should keep an eye on YG’s social media channels for ticket information and streaming updates.

In the coming weeks, TREASURE will also release a behind‑the‑scenes documentary on V Live, offering an inside look at the choreography process for “DANGER.” The footage will likely be uploaded to YouTube Shorts and TikTok, providing bite‑size content that sustains momentum on short‑form platforms.

## 💭 Editor's Take

💭 Editor's Take: Seeing TREASURE unleash “DANGER” in such a raw, kinetic format is a reminder of why they’re one of the most exciting acts of this generation. The choreography is unapologetically intense—every member delivers a performance that feels both polished and feral. As a journalist who’s followed their journey from trainee days, I’m thrilled to see YG investing in high‑budget B‑side videos; it signals that TREASURE is no longer just a rookie act but a group with staying power. The blend of gritty visuals and trap‑heavy production feels perfectly timed for Western audiences who gravitate toward edgier K‑pop sounds, and I expect “DANGER” to make a noticeable dent on streaming charts in the US and Europe. If you haven’t already, queue the video on YouTube, try the #DANGERChallenge, and get ready for what could be the next big step in TREASURE’s global ascent.
