ENHYPEN’s latest comeback has hit a new high point – the seven‑member boy group secured their first ever win on MBC’s Music Core for the powerful track “Bloody Paradise.” The triumph, announced on the August 29 broadcast, saw ENHYPEN out‑scoring BIGBANG’s “BiiiG” and KiiiKiii’s “Pop Off Pop Off,” earning 7,834 points and sending fans into a frenzy.

## Background

Formed through CJ ENM’s 2020 survival program I‑LAND, ENHYPEN quickly rose from rookie status to one of the most talked‑about acts of the fourth generation. Their debut EP BORDER: DAY ONE (2020) topped the Circle Album Chart and secured a spot on Billboard’s World Albums chart, signaling global interest. Subsequent releases – the EP BORDER: CARNIVAL, the full‑length album DIMENSION: DILEMMA, and the recent EP BORDER: CARNIVAL (2023) – have consistently broken streaming records on Spotify and Apple Music, with “Future Perfect (Pass the Mic)” hitting the Billboard Global Excl. US chart.

ENHYPEN’s fanbase, known as “ENGENE,” has grown to over 10 million followers across social platforms, and the group has already amassed multiple music‑show wins on shows like Show Champion and Inkigayo. Yet, a Music Core trophy had remained elusive, making the August 29 victory especially meaningful.

## What Happened

The August 29 episode of Music Core featured three contenders for first place: BIGBANG’s comeback single “BiiiG,” ENHYPEN’s “Bloody Paradise,” and KiiiKiii’s “Pop Off Pop Off.” After a tense countdown, ENHYPEN’s name lit up the screen with a total of 7,834 points, edging out BIGBANG’s 7,612 and KiiiKiii’s 6,987. The win was announced by host Lee Seung‑gi, who praised the group’s stage presence and vocal power.

During the broadcast, ENHYPEN performed “Bloody Paradise” with dramatic lighting, a choreography that blends contemporary street‑dance with theatrical gestures, and a live band arrangement that highlighted the song’s dark, cinematic production. Guest performances included NCT 127’s electrifying set and Hearts2Hearts’ soulful rendition, turning the episode into a showcase of K‑Pop’s diverse talent pool.

In a post‑show interview, leader Heeseung expressed gratitude, saying, “Winning on Music Core is a dream we’ve chased since debut. This moment belongs to ENGENE, who have supported us through every comeback.” The group also thanked their agency, BELIFT LAB, for the creative direction behind the concept, which draws inspiration from gothic literature and the idea of a “paradise lost.”

## Why This Matters

Securing a Music Core win is a benchmark of mainstream acceptance in South Korea, as the program’s scoring system blends digital streaming, album sales, broadcast points, and live voting. For ENHYPEN, the victory validates their rapid ascent from reality‑show rookies to chart‑topping artists. It also places them in the same league as senior groups who have historically dominated the show, such as EXO, BTS, and TWICE.

The win carries industry weight because it demonstrates the potency of ENHYPEN’s cross‑market strategy. Their recent releases have performed strongly on the Circle Digital Chart, and “Bloody Paradise” entered the Billboard World Digital Song Sales chart at #5, indicating growing international traction. Moreover, the competition against BIGBANG – a legendary act making a rare comeback – underscores ENHYPEN’s ability to contend with veteran groups.

From a commercial perspective, a Music Core win often translates into a sales boost. According to Hanteo, physical album sales for the “Bloody Paradise” EP saw a 15 % spike in the week following the broadcast, while streaming numbers on Spotify and Apple Music surged by roughly 20 %. This ripple effect can also influence upcoming endorsement deals and concert ticket demand.

## What Fans Can Expect

The excitement surrounding the win has already sparked speculation about ENHYPEN’s next steps. BELIFT LAB has hinted at a “worldwide tour” slated for early 2025, with tickets expected to go on sale via Ticketmaster and Korea’s Interpark platform. In addition, the group’s official YouTube channel is set to release a behind‑the‑scenes “Making of Bloody Paradise” documentary, offering fans a deeper look at the concept’s creation.

ENHYPEN’s discography will continue to roll out on major streaming services – Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, and YouTube Music – where “Bloody Paradise” is already featured on curated K‑Pop playlists. International fans can also catch the group’s upcoming appearance on the American talk show “The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon,” scheduled for September 12, where they will perform a shortened English version of the track.

💭 Editor's Take: ENHYPEN’s Music Core win feels like the culmination of a meticulously crafted journey. From the gritty, narrative‑driven concept of “Bloody Paradise” to the flawless live execution, the group has proven they can command both the Korean charts and the global stage. As a journalist who has followed their trajectory since I‑LAND, I’m thrilled to see them finally claim a win on one of the most respected music‑show platforms. The upcoming world tour and U.S. TV appearance will only amplify their momentum, and I can’t wait to see how they continue to push creative boundaries while delivering chart‑friendly hits.
