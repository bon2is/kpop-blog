BTS has made history by selling over 4 million copies of "ARIRANG" in just 3 days, dominating the Hanteo Chart weekly chart for the third week of March. This achievement sets a new record in K-Pop history, showcasing the group's immense popularity. The sales figure is a testament to BTS's enduring influence and dedicated fan base.
