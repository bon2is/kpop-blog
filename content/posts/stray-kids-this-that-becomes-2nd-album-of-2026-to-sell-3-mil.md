Stray Kids has once again proven their immense popularity and influence in the K-Pop scene with the remarkable success of their latest mini album 'THIS & THAT'. Released on August 7, the album has already sold over 3 million copies in its first week, a feat that only one other album has achieved in 2026. This news has sent shockwaves of excitement among fans and the music industry alike, as it underscores Stray Kids' consistent ability to break records and push boundaries.

## Background

Stray Kids is a South Korean boy group formed by JYP Entertainment in 2017. The group consists of nine members: Bang Chan, Woojin, Lee Know, Changbin, Hyunjin, Han, Felix, Seungmin, and I.N. Stray Kids made their official debut on March 26, 2018, with the EP 'I Am Not' and have since become known for their energetic performances, meaningful lyrics, and genre-bending music style. Over the years, the group has released numerous successful albums and singles, including 'District 9', 'God's Menu', and 'Back Door', which have topped various music charts in Korea and abroad.

Stray Kids' career has been marked by continuous growth and experimentation. The group has explored a wide range of genres, from hip-hop and rock to electronic and pop, showcasing their versatility and creativity. Their music often deals with themes of self-empowerment, individuality, and the challenges of youth, resonating deeply with their young fan base. In recent years, Stray Kids has gained significant international recognition, performing at festivals and concerts worldwide, including the prestigious Music Bank in Chile and the Jingle Ball in the United States.

## What Happened

The news of 'THIS & THAT' selling over 3 million copies in its first week was announced by Hanteo Chart on August 12. This achievement is particularly noteworthy given that it makes 'THIS & THAT' the second album of 2026 to reach this sales milestone. The album's title track, also named 'THIS & THAT', has been receiving praise for its addictive melody and thought-provoking lyrics. The music video for the song has also garnered millions of views on YouTube, further demonstrating the group's global appeal.

The success of 'THIS & THAT' can be attributed to Stray Kids' dedicated fan base, known as STAY, who have been eagerly anticipating the group's comeback. The album's release was preceded by a series of teasers and behind-the-scenes content, which helped build anticipation and excitement among fans. Upon its release, 'THIS & THAT' quickly rose to the top of various music charts, including the Circle Chart and the Billboard Korea Chart.
