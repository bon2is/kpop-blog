## Our Take

The passing of ASTRO's Moonbin was a significant loss for the K-Pop community, and it's clear that his memory still lingers in the hearts of his colleagues. 

 SEVENTEEN's Seungkwan and Billlie's Moon Sua have shown that even after three years, Moonbin's legacy lives on through the memories they shared with him. 

 This outpouring of love and respect is a testament to the strong bonds that exist between K-Pop idols, and it highlights the impact that Moonbin had on those around him. 

 As the K-Pop industry continues to evolve, it's essential to remember and honor the artists who have come before us, and Moonbin's memory will undoubtedly continue to be celebrated by fans and fellow idols alike.
