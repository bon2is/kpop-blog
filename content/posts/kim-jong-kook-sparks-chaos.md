SBS Plus’s new variety show 'Manly Men’s Way of Traveling' has released posters featuring Choo Sung Hoon, Kim Jong Kook, and Daesung. The show is a travel reality series that follows three men with type P MBTI on an unfiltered adventure in Japan. The posters highlight the chemistry and energy between the three stars.
---

## Our Take

The release of these posters has generated excitement among fans, who are looking forward to seeing the dynamic between Choo Sung Hoon, Kim Jong Kook, and Daesung. 

 The concept of 'Manly Men’s Way of Traveling' is unique, focusing on the raw and instinct-driven travel experiences of the three leads, which is expected to bring a fresh perspective to the travel variety show genre. 

 This show could be a great opportunity for the three stars to showcase their personalities and build a stronger bond with their fans, potentially leading to increased popularity and new projects. 

 The combination of Choo Sung Hoon, Kim Jong Kook, and Daesung's energetic and adventurous personalities is likely to create an entertaining and engaging viewing experience, making 'Manly Men’s Way of Traveling' a must-watch for fans of Korean variety shows.
